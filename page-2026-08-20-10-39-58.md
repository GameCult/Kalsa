## V – Prophecy

Ostensibly, Analysis had the same sort of problem that Sorcery did, but in a different way. It evolved to be a very focused trait, much more directly useful than Luck. While Sorcery provided paths and Analysis provided information, both did so only on very small scales. The next step in the breeding program would be to increase the scale of Analysis’ effects.

## Evolution

As Analysis provided breeding rights to individuals capable of perceiving small-scale systems, the next game needed to require more long-scale perception. The gene pool was of mixed heritage, Analysis and Shamanism. The target, instead of being entirely virtual, became instead economic. Prometheus also finally recognized the value of subtlety here. Most previous games would require only a particular aspect of fitness in the individuals it selected for, but this narrow focus tended to quickly produce individuals that lacked other forms of fitness. This new game,  in order to avoid the pitfall of atrophy which Analysis fell into, was by needs more subtle. The selection pressures were socially instead of divinely enforced: culture and aesthetics were changed to place an emphasis on wealth, so that individuals incapable of manipulating the market were culled out. The already systems-savvy Analysis phenotype was mutated into something more like long-term prediction. The weak prescience that Luck gave access to in dreams combined with the clarity of perception afforded by Analysis to form Prophecy.

## Magic

Prophecy is exactly what it says on the tin. Those that have it are capable of performing accurate long-term prediction without a need for knowledge of cause. However, it must always be taken into consideration that the situations and relations they perceive are not on any sort of deterministic time-line. They perceive not the absolute future, but any number of possible future states. A good prophet sees more than one possible future, and works backward by understanding the relationship between the desired future-state and the present. Prophets are the only magic users with a magical presence on such scales that can consciously perceive (without the use of shaman-trance) and thus they are the only ones capable of detecting the presence of Luck, Shamans and other Prophets; by interpolating over the gaps between predictions, they can deduce the trails left behind in time by large chaos beasts. The large amount of magical potential necessary to generate conscious prescience makes Prophets very desirable to Gods, who can greatly boost their own power by recruiting prophets to their cause.

## Attributes

Prophets have many of the same elfin features that define Analysts, but the less narrow scope of their breeding has somewhat weakened the extent of atrophy. Their bloodlines are also rather diluted in the general populace, but noble houses appeared early on that refused to breed with the control group, thereby salvaging their genes. Noble houses, therefore, produce very capable prophets whose distinctive features include an oversized head with large eyes (like the Grays from popular conspiracy theories).

## Culture

The City of Prophets landed almost perfectly in the middle of the terminator, making it a city of sunset where sunlight just manages to scratch at the tops of large buildings, making sunlight a valuable and scarce commodity. Because of this, their cultural identity places great worth in the availability of sunlight, so much that having a tan is a very powerful mark of status, signifying that the individual has both the leisure time and money to obtain copious amounts of sunlight. Prophets being very attractive to Gods, the nations capable of producing Gods prioritized early on the acquisition of this city. Once contact was established between the City of Prophets and the City of Analysis, the latter decided quickly to expand its burgeoning empire to include it. However, the relationship with the conquered Prophets was never more than tributary, as the culture barrier between the two nations never quite dissolved despite extensive diffusion over the borders. When the Nation of Sorcery broke free of its climactic barrier and began building its own empire, they also made the City of Prophets a primary goal. Sorceror Gods equipped with Prophets gain enormous power, and the City of Prophets was divided as Jerusalem was, reflecting the greater conflict between Analysis and Sorcery on a smaller scale.

## Wonders

Every nation solved the problem of what to do with the raw material of the Ring Spokes. Most simply took them apart piece by piece and reshaped the material into a conventional city. The City of Prophets had a rather unique solution. As an interesting parallel to the natives’ custom of building cities that grow taller as they grow sunward, The City of Prophets rearranged their Spokes into a huge sunward wall with casts an enormous shadow over half of the city. Further up above than humans can safely breathe, the wall contains vertical farms, enough to sustain the entire population of the huge city.
