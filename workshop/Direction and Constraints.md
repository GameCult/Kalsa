# Kalsa Direction and Constraints

This vault develops **Kalsa** as a high-fantasy setting for **Ghostlight Dungeon**. The exported teenage notes are the seed and historical witness, not a finished canon and not disposable embarrassment. New work must preserve their strange specificity while replacing unsupported assertion with institutions that can actually reproduce themselves.

## Product direction

- The setting must support persistent play in Ghostlight Dungeon: places, factions, offices, obligations, conflicts, rituals, material flows, and histories should generate situations rather than merely decorate them.
- Institutional depth should eventually match the neighboring `AetheriaLore` vault. This is a structural benchmark, not permission to import Aetheria's lore, voice, ontology, or assumptions.
- Obsidian is the canonical human authoring surface.
- Quartz is a published projection of the vault, not a second source of truth.
- Agent memory is a bounded project-memory surface. It records durable judgment, decisions, provenance, open questions, and iteration state; it does not silently become setting canon.

## Motivating setting direction

Prometheus repeatedly imposes clean formal structures on human systems. History turns those structures into culture.

The language history is the clearest model for this process:

- Prometheus imposed Lojban as a supposedly more logical, communicative prestige language alongside rationalized measures and timekeeping.
- High Lojban can survive in law, liturgy, treaties, technical manuals, priesthoods, scholarship, and old machine interfaces.
- Ordinary use should produce regional descendants through simplification, reanalysis, substrate influence, sound change, idiom, ellipsis, metaphor, and social signaling.
- Magical practice should exert domain-specific pressure on speech: prophecy on evidentiality, sorcery on hierarchy, Sarxe life on collective reference, and Analyst practice on technical causality are candidate directions, not yet blanket canon.
- Contact after the Fall should produce trade languages, koine forms, mixed prestige registers, and once-condemned constructions that become normal grammar.
- Techpriests may preserve formulae they only partly understand because surviving machinery still recognizes them.
- Elite performance of “correct” High Lojban may function as class power even when elites speak a descendant language at home.

The durable theme is not “logical language fails.” It is that a designed system becomes alive when people inherit, misuse, repair, ritualize, weaponize, localize, and love it.

## Institutional depth standard

A mature institution should answer, in setting terms:

1. What material or social need does it satisfy?
2. Who possesses authority, and what makes that authority legible?
3. How are members recruited, trained, ranked, paid, disciplined, and replaced?
4. What resources, records, places, tools, and supply chains keep it alive?
5. What formal doctrine does it claim, and what informal practice actually governs it?
6. Who benefits, who pays, who is excluded, and who can refuse or exit?
7. What other institutions constrain, fund, fear, imitate, or parasitize it?
8. How does it differ by class, region, lineage, language, and magical capacity?
9. What historical crisis created its current form?
10. What internal contradiction generates playable conflict now?
11. What changes when a player helps, harms, captures, exposes, or reforms it?
12. Which claims are established canon, attributed belief, present inference, or unresolved design?

Lists of offices and proper nouns do not count as depth by themselves. Depth means causal machinery: authority, reproduction, material support, contested legitimacy, and consequences.

## Iterative critique and repair

Each deepening pass must leave an inspectable record:

1. **Select** one bounded institution, region, practice, or cross-cutting system.
2. **Inventory** source facts, later additions, inferences, contradictions, and unknowns.
3. **Model** owner, inputs, outputs, dependencies, beneficiaries, costs, failure modes, and interfaces with neighboring institutions.
4. **Critique** generic fantasy defaults, missing material constraints, false consensus, absent subordinate perspectives, and consequences that stop at the page edge.
5. **Repair** the canonical notes through the smallest coherent set of changes.
6. **Connect** the repair to people, places, events, resources, language, magic, and adventure pressures.
7. **Falsify** it with at least one hostile or marginal perspective and one historical stress case.
8. **Record** decisions, provenance, unresolved pressure, and the next highest-value gap in project memory.

A pass is complete only when the repaired material produces more specific downstream consequences without creating duplicate owners or silently erasing the seed.

## Provenance and canon

- Preserve the raw exported pages as immutable source witnesses.
- Distinguish `seed`, `adopted`, `provisional`, `contested`, `rumor`, and `deprecated` claims.
- Do not launder an assistant suggestion into canon by copying it without status and provenance.
- Contradictions should become explicit design questions, perspectival disagreements, or deliberate historical uncertainty—not accidental parallel truths.
- Public-facing pages may omit workshop apparatus, but publication filters must never decide canon.

## Guardrails

- No generic medieval-Europe paste with renamed nouns.
- No institution without material support, succession, enforcement limits, and an affected population.
- No culture treated as a personality type.
- No magic system isolated from labor, law, warfare, kinship, disability, trade, education, ecology, and state capacity.
- No formal language frozen outside history once ordinary people speak it.
- No repair loop that rewards page count over causal density and usable conflict.
- No agent memory, task ledger, or critique note published as lore by accident.

## Current unknowns

- The final public/private boundary for Ghostlight-sensitive material.
- The canonical present date and temporal distance from the Fall.
- Which seed contradictions should remain contested in-world rather than be resolved.
- The exact threshold and rubric for declaring parity with AetheriaLore.
- Hosting destination and deployment authority for the Quartz site.
