# The Room Kept Ready

At Hadrin's house, the room beside the kitchen already has a shelf for a child who does not exist. His grown daughter, Ilket, fitted it herself. She meant it for whatever small person might eventually visit. Hadrin has begun describing it as where her children will keep their cups.

The house stands in Bent Court, a proposed settled neighbourhood of Sarxe and mixed-descent families on Kalsa's habitable twilight belt. Hadrin repairs stools and sleeping frames; his sister Mav makes padded covers for them. Ilket earns her living fitting shutters and now shares a smaller home across the court with her partner, Dushem. They can hear Mav calling when food is ready. Ilket would like, occasionally, to do the calling.

Several homes here keep a **room ready**: a usable room furnished gradually for the household people hope to become. Someone replaces a curtain with a door, lowers a shelf, or finds a broad bed that two people can sit on while dressing. Neighbours come to try the furniture and offer opinions. The best visits end with everyone eating in a room that still smells of fresh shavings. Hadrin considers a house successful when nobody has to perch on a storage chest. Mav has spent much of her life perching on storage chests.

These settled [[Kalsa/Spoilers/Polities/Channeling - Sarxe|Sarxe]] households admire making room before need becomes urgent. The practice grew locally from relatives helping one another enlarge cramped homes. It persists because improvements are useful now, and because fitting a room together gives neighbours an enjoyable, affordable way to help with a large hope. A gifted cabinet is easier to offer than permanent care. People sometimes discover too late that the recipient heard both promises.

A ready room might anticipate an elder coming to live nearby, friends joining households, a partner, or children. Such possibilities fit the varied kinship described in [[Kalsa/Spoilers/Institutions/Saxfoldi Households|Saxfoldi Households]], but this particular custom belongs to Bent Court. Here a room can be lent for sewing or occupied by a guest while its intended future remains undecided. Poorer neighbours keep a cleared alcove and resent hearing that it does not count. Others spend their spare earnings making the home they already have more comfortable.

The court has no single ancestry or magical capacity. Dushem has Control ancestry and expresses no Gift; these are separate facts, as [[Kalsa/Spoilers/Polities/Null and Control Descendants|Null and Control Descendants]] explains. He makes Hadrin describe furniture changes aloud before they carry anything together. Close familiarity does not tell him which end Hadrin intends to lift. Children learn the same habit, along with asking whose future the adults are furnishing.

Ilket wants children, and she likes the shelf. She also wants a household where her father arrives as a welcome guest. Returning to the kitchen in which he still finishes her tasks would make her life easier in ways she does not want to lose, and smaller in ways she struggles to explain. Hadrin hears her affection clearly; he keeps mistaking it for a plan.

Mav wants the ready room for herself and her old friend Rul, whose hands increasingly need help with fastenings. They want to live together while they can still choose and furnish a home, rather than wait until somebody has to arrange a sickbed. Mav would gladly stop sewing covers for customers if the household could afford it. Hadrin's hoped-for expansion has never included losing her earnings.

Rul's neighbour Azhed wants the room Rul would leave for a workbench of his own. He offers to make the new door, quite sincerely wishing them happiness. Rul also cares for Azhed's little son during some of his work periods. Moving next door will preserve affection, but it will not preserve every useful arrangement. Rul has already declined to carry the child's spare bedding across with her.

## Hooks left

- Ilket invites the family to eat at her own home and asks Hadrin to bring the little shelf.
- Mav and Rul have found a bed they both like. The seller will hold it until their next visit.
- Azhed brings a finished door and asks where to hang it before Hadrin and Mav have agreed who will sleep behind it.
