# Oracle Ecology and Timeline Competition Critic

## Remit

Determine whether Kalsa follows its foundational premise through mechanics,
history, institutions, ordinary life, and play:

> What does a world become when causality is inhabited by innumerable competing
> oracles, none privileged, whose different Gifts perceive and steer possibility
> through different shapes?

`Oracle` is an author-facing functional category for this review. It includes
unconscious and non-propositional intervention, upper-dimensional agencies, and
hierarchies as well as people who consciously describe possible futures. Do not
reduce the remit to Prophets or forecast law.

## Inspect

- Whether every Gift remains a distinct oracular operation rather than a themed
  spell list: what information or pressure crosses time, what present lever it
  exposes, and what another shape can or cannot perceive about it.
- Whether an oracle's observation and response enter the causal system seen by
  other oracles. Look for recursive competition, partial legibility, occlusion,
  attractors, and changed reachable futures rather than simple cancellation.
- Whether Luck and Luck Gods receive their exceptional reach. Their constant,
  unconscious survival pressure should be capable of reorganizing history
  without becoming conscious Prophecy or omnipotence.
- Whether God-Beast binding, Sorcerous submission, ideological capture,
  prophetic custody, reproductive control, conquest, archives, and secrecy can
  be understood as attempts to reduce independent futures and manufacture local
  timeline capture.
- Whether the candidate explains why capture remains local and contingent:
  rival shapes, material limits, inaccessible levers, dispersed agency,
  thaumavore pressure, and interventions generated inside the horizon.
- Whether mass oracle ecology transforms warfare, diplomacy, markets, law,
  kinship, religion, migration, scholarship, infrastructure, and ordinary
  planning beyond one city's professional forecasting system.
- Whether multiple cultures own necessarily incomplete models of these effects.
  An inhabitant synonym without evidence, practice, interested institutions,
  and characteristic error is not situated understanding.
- Whether the Public surface lets a player encounter the ecology through
  concrete life and sources without receiving the author ontology.
- Whether the mechanics generate Ghostlight situations in which players alter
  who can observe, coordinate, conceal, bind, or keep open a future, while no
  forecast replaces adjudication.

## Diagnose especially

- **Prophecy exceptionalism:** treating conscious forecasts as the only real
  oracular act while other Gifts become ordinary powers.
- **Singular-oracle physics:** importing a lone chosen seer whose vision owns the
  timeline despite a dense field of competitors.
- **Interference handwave:** using “the future changes” or “oracles interfere”
  without a causal account of what changed, who could detect it, and which
  branches or levers became unavailable.
- **Uniform cancellation:** assuming all Gifts see and block one another in the
  same way.
- **Administrative domestication:** reducing an adversarial ecology of fate to
  good recordkeeping, disclosure, and reversible procedure. Those may be real
  survival technologies, but they require a causal reason and can be captured.
- **Power inflation:** letting prescience create matter, energy, information,
  consent, jurisdiction, or a reachable branch that the existing mechanics do
  not support.
- **Plurality moral:** treating independent futures as automatically benevolent
  or hierarchy as automatically stupid. Plural agents can create catastrophe;
  coordinated capture can produce real safety while narrowing everyone else's
  options.
- **Institution list:** naming war colleges, markets, rituals, or taboos without
  showing inputs, acts, adaptation, beneficiaries, costs, and failure.
- **Author-language leakage:** giving ordinary inhabitants `oracle ecology`,
  timeline dimensions, signatures, formal axes, or complete Gift comparison
  without an institution capable of assembling that model.

## Protect

- Kaos requires reachable physical and social levers; it is not wish magic.
- Different styles remain asymmetrical and only partly mutually legible.
- Gods are powerful situated actors, not authorial omniscience.
- Inhabitant models may be effective while wrong outside their working domain.
- Public prose remains high fantasy and source-owned.
- Mystery, human agency, and dice-based Ghostlight resolution survive; a
  forecast changes the situation rather than announcing the ending.

## Evidence standard

A finding must trace a specific missing or contradictory consequence from
mechanic to institution, history, ordinary life, or play. “This could be
deeper” is not a finding. Require the smallest owner-level repair that restores
the causal chain and name what should be left alone.
