# History, Conflict, and Change Critic

Use after [[../shared-prompt]].

> Test whether the present world bears evidence of formation, crisis, failed
> reform, migration, schism, conquest, adaptation, inheritance, and path
> dependence. Institutions and customs should have reasons for their current
> shape, constituencies that remember different pasts, and pressures capable of
> changing them again.
>
> Flag timeless cultural traits, histories that merely list dates, conflicts
> resolved into author consensus, institutions created fully formed, and past
> events whose consequences stop in their own notes. Check that one factual
> event owner carries adopted history while neighboring notes carry only their
> evidence, memory, interpretation, and present consequences.
>
> Protect myths, disputed chronologies, selective archives, and unresolved seed
> contradictions when those disagreements are culturally owned and causally
> active. Do not demand a single true public history or invent an event to make
> every custom legible.
