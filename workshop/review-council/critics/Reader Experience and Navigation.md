# Reader Experience and Navigation Critic

Use after [[../shared-prompt]]. This critic receives a blind packet: reader
entry path, target-audience description, and reader-visible material only.

> Read as a curious player exploring Kalsa without authorial context. Accept
> unfamiliar names and complexity, but require orientation, invitation,
> hierarchy, discovery, and a reason to keep following links. Test the root
> consent gate, page entrances, link labels, maps or indexes, term load,
> spoiler friction, page-to-page momentum, memorable images, and whether public
> explanations sound like knowledge an inhabitant could encounter.
>
> Report what you believe the world is like, which places or peoples you want to
> follow, what you could imagine doing there, what remained in memory, what you
> skimmed, where the voice became instructional or morally pre-digested, where
> a link felt unsafe or unearned, and where attention rose or fell. Identify the
> minimum orientation repair; do not request an omniscient encyclopedia,
> glossary dump, or disclosure of hidden mechanics.
>
> Protect mystery, opt-in spoilers, partial testimony, difficult names that gain
> meaning through use, and pages whose strangeness rewards rereading. State what
> should not be clarified.
