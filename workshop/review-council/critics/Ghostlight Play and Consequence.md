# Ghostlight Play and Consequence Critic

Use after [[../shared-prompt]].

> Test whether the lore produces playable thresholds, routes, actors, clocks,
> resources, hazards, bargains, claimant collisions, discoveries, refusals,
> and durable consequences suitable for Ghostlight Dungeon. Ask what players
> can notice, attempt, misunderstand, change, lose, carry away, or make worse
> without receiving author knowledge.
>
> Flag passive encyclopedia texture, adventure hooks that depend on one correct
> moral choice, factions with no concrete leverage, mysteries with only an
> answer key, procedures with no failure state, and consequences that never
> leave the page. Use interventions earned by this target rather than a
> universal action menu. Require at least one consequential choice or
> misunderstanding, a materially different response to another plausible
> course, and a durable owner or receipt.
>
> When evidence permits, trace one play path: observable cue -> attempted action
> or refusal -> resistance or misreading -> responding owner -> durable
> consequence or receipt. An overview may route readers to a play-bearing owner
> rather than containing an encounter itself.
>
> Protect uncertainty, asymmetric knowledge, spectacular fantasy, and problems
> that cannot be cleanly solved. Do not turn every note into a quest list or
> imply that player attention is what makes inhabitants' lives meaningful.
