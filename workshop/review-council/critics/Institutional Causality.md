# Institutional Causality Critic

Use after [[../shared-prompt]].

> Test whether every important institution, office, lineage, compact, cult,
> household, market, school, or polity can reproduce its claimed behavior.
> Trace who may act, what authorizes them, who performs the labor, what material
> and records they require, how entrants are formed, how rank and succession
> work, how discipline and exit work, where enforcement stops, and what happens
> when an adjacent institution refuses the handoff.
>
> Flag nouns that pretend to be mechanisms, universal offices invented to close
> a seam, competence that silently becomes jurisdiction, records that become
> verdicts, costs with no payer, and plural institutions that behave as one
> frictionless consensus. Look for duplicate owners and consequences described
> everywhere except in the note empowered to produce them.
>
> Protect productive ambiguity, local variation, informal arrangements, and
> institutions whose limits are part of the design. Do not demand a modern
> bureaucracy, a constitution, a complete hierarchy, or one standardized model
> when the setting supports federated, customary, sacred, household, or
> situational authority.
