overview, add navigation, and add one Closed Lift source only if earned. It may
not name unknown actors, houses, city, sectors, or offices; settle a calendar;
make Luck detectable; turn forecast into verdict; transfer lift operation to a
court, house, or sponsor; decide which catastrophe was true; universalize the
precedent; or invent completed restitution.

## Protected strengths

- Civic allocation, Sunwall operation, house forecast labor, civic judgment,
  and imperial claims remain separate authorities.
- Prophecy compares possible futures and can be causally altered by disclosure,
  sponsor action, material repair, and rival intervention.
- Inferred Luck cannot identify a person.
- Ordinary residents encounter forecasts through schedules, closures, quotas,
  precautions, and losses rather than an author-facing capability model.
- Closed Lift correction changed practice without restoring all wages, housing
  standing, clients, or reputation.

load, brake inspection, sealed first-court file, sponsor disclosure, corrected
record, or unpaid remedy. Evidence must change custody, movement, load limits,
release, repair, public notice, house discipline, record standing, or material
compensation through the authority able to act.
