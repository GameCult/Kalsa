# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** Public preserves the right authority boundaries but presents them as
a constitutional silhouette. A reader cannot follow a forecast into sponsor
disclosure, material inspection, appeal, release, technical operation, revised
movement, or unpaid remedy. The adopted Closed Lift case can reproduce that
body without adding a court or office.

## Protected successes

- Civic allocation and Sunwall operation are already separate: the Allocation
  Table schedules shares while stewards own safe operation.
- Prophetic houses train and report without owning civic judgment, and ordinary
  residents meet prophecy through work, food, lifts, quotas, and closure.
- Public Prophecy remains mutable after disclosure and does not identify a Luck
  bearer from forecast deformation.
- The hidden event preserves occupation and incomplete remedy after successful
  correction.

## Findings

### IC37-01 — The divided authorities never perform a shared handoff

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/World/Peoples and Polities.md`, `## The divided
  city`, especially the first paragraph and the absence of an onward source.
- **Evidence:** The Closed Lift sequence moves from two sponsored forecasts and
  a first-court detention through mixed appeal, disclosure, material
  inspection, release, staged grain movement, repair, notice, and a new
  forecast. Civic officers execute release and movement orders; Sunwall
  stewards execute operating limits.
- **Effect:** Courts, houses, imperial sectors, allocators, and stewards remain
  parallel nouns. The reader cannot tell which institution can actually change
  custody, movement, load, repair, or public standing.
- **Repair direction:** Add one bounded Public Closed Lift source carrying the
  event from first order through appeal and partial aftermath, then link it
  from the overview and Public index. Keep every act with its existing owner.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not invent a universal court, settled armistice,
  native office, house, or authority able to command every handoff.
- **Dependency:** Event owner and Ghostlight Play and Consequence.
- **Confidence:** High; the event already owns the complete sequence.

### IC37-02 — Forecasts and material evidence have no decision path

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/World/Magic and Miracles.md`, Prophecy references,
  and `Peoples and Polities.md`, final Divided City paragraph.
- **Evidence:** The Ju'onai-sponsored forecast, Terjamna-sponsored forecast,
  house witness's Luck inference, sponsors' concealed interventions, and brake
  inspection answer different questions. The Mixed Bench excludes the identity
  claim and chooses reversible action after those inputs change.
- **Effect:** Public says forecasts are comparisons but does not show who tests
  disclosure, what present evidence can contradict them, or how a possible
  future becomes a bounded order rather than a verdict.
- **Repair direction:** In the event source, introduce each report, sponsor
  plan, and inspection when its claim is tested. Show the bench owning the
  finding while the Sunwall retains technical judgment.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not average rival forecasts into generic uncertainty,
  let the brake prove which catastrophe was true, or turn inferred Luck into
  identification.
- **Dependency:** Culture and Situated Knowledge; Material Life and Ecology.
- **Confidence:** High.

### IC37-03 — Public omits the procedure the failure reproduced

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** end of the Public Divided City section and current Public
  navigation.
- **Evidence:** The appeal changed intervention disclosure, Luck-inference
  limits, reversible precaution, duration and review, independent record
  custody, technical actuation, and correction duties. It did not make the
  civic authority sovereign or the empires irrelevant.
- **Effect:** Public institutions can correct one order but cannot reproduce
  the correction across later cases. The historical pressure stops at the page
  edge.
- **Repair direction:** Carry the narrow precedent as later practice inside the
  bounded source: disclosure, material inquiry, release, operating limits,
  record correction, house discipline referral, and named enforcement owners.
- **Collateral risk:** One case is not a city constitution, a universal forecast
  code, or proof that later authorities still honor every part.
- **Dependency:** History, Conflict, and Change.
- **Confidence:** High.

### IC37-04 — Record correction is detached from the harmed claimant

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Peoples and Polities.md`, sentence beginning `Most residents
  are not house prophets`, and the missing Closed Lift route.
- **Evidence:** Detention cost the non-house captain wages, housing standing,
  clients, and reputation. Release and a corrected record did not automatically
  restore them; lift closure also displaced grain, labor, ration, and security
  pressure.
- **Effect:** Ordinary residents remain a cost category rather than parties
  whose injury determines remedy, enforcement, and unfinished claims.
- **Repair direction:** End the source with the captain's corrected record
  beside the unresolved material remedies and the changed city systems. Let
  later evidence alter compensation or standing through the authority able to
  act.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not invent completed restitution, a named captain, or
  a heroic vindication that erases other households affected by closure.
- **Dependency:** Culture and Situated Knowledge; Ghostlight Play and
  Consequence.
- **Confidence:** High.
