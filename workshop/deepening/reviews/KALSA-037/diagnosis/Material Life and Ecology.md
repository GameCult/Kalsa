# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** All 26 raw SHA-1 hashes match the baseline packet. Hidden canon has
a complete chain from Sunwall harvest and cargo movement through forecast-led
closure, brake inspection, displaced grain and labor, staged repair, and
unrestored livelihood loss. Public names the resources and authority boundary
but omits every event-bounded material handoff.

## Protected successes

- The Sunwall is both granary and wound: high vertical farms catch permanent
  light while the wall deepens the inhabited shadow below. Lifts, pressure
  refuges, galleries, routes, and trained crews make altitude material.
- Bright hours remain specific abundance rather than mere wealth: they can be
  agricultural capacity, medicine, ceremony, work exposure, and prestige.
- Civic allocation and Sunwall operation remain separate. A table may assign
  grain, light, cargo, refuge, and reserve; stewards own safe operation and can
  stop work without owning those shares.
- The Closed Lift correction combines prophecy disclosure with material
  inspection and reversible operation, then leaves wages, housing standing,
  clients, and reputation incompletely restored.

## Findings

### MLE37-01 — The Sunwall's material failure chain is absent from Public

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/World/Peoples and Polities.md#The divided city`,
  first paragraph; `Kalsa/Public/World/Kalsa.md`, first section and closing
  examples.
- **Evidence:** Public says farms and lifts are operated by Sunwall stewards and
  imagines a clerk comparing reports before scheduling a lift. The Closed Lift
  owner establishes a harvest cargo lift, a contested grain load, a sequence of
  improbable faults, a fatigued brake assembly, material inspection, and the
  Stewardship's continuing operational custody. Rival forecasts and sponsor
  plans changed the causal system but did not repair the brake.
- **Effect:** Public presents food movement and technical authority as
  governance features rather than infrastructure with loads, wear, inspection,
  failure, and trained operators. Prophecy risks appearing to reveal or solve
  the material problem by itself.
- **Repair direction:** Carry the cargo load, fault sequence, competing
  forecasts, concealed sponsor interventions, brake inspection, and Sunwall
  operational response in one bounded Closed Lift source.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not invent the lift, brake, load limits, inspection
  method, grain amount, route, crew, sector, or maintenance schedule beyond the
  event owner.
- **Dependency:** Closed Lift event; Sunwall polity owner; Forecast Evidence for
  sponsor disclosure.
- **Confidence:** High; the complete chain is adopted and wholly absent from
  Public.

### MLE37-02 — Closure has no visible payer or displaced flow

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/World/Peoples and Polities.md#The divided city`,
  both paragraphs.
- **Evidence:** Public lists grain, cargo priority, emergency stores, farms,
  lifts, refuges, and ordinary workers, then says residents live beneath
  decisions. The event shows the actual transfer: sealing the lift delayed
  grain and shifted labor, ration, and security pressure elsewhere. Detaining
  the non-house captain removed paid work and damaged housing standing and
  client relationships before appeal.
- **Effect:** Uneven access remains an abstract social invoice. The reader
  cannot see which food route closed, which work moved, or how a reversible
  precaution reached one person's shelter and livelihood.
- **Repair direction:** In the same event source, carry lift closure into
  displaced grain and support work, then preserve the captain's wage, housing,
  and client losses as consequences of the first order.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not invent a destination for the grain, ration
  amounts, replacement routes, detention length, housing law, dependants, or a
  complete lower-shadow economy.
- **Dependency:** Event owner; civic allocation and court-remedy owners.
- **Confidence:** High.

### MLE37-03 — Recovery and partial remedy disappear after the warning

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** the complete Public Divided City route across `World/Kalsa.md`,
  `World/Peoples and Polities.md`, and `World/Magic and Miracles.md`.
- **Evidence:** The adopted response disclosed planned crew diversion and
  public seizure; excluded Luck identification; released the captain; limited
  loads; divided grain movement into reversible stages; ordered repair, public
  notice, and a new forecast after the material system changed. Sunwall workers
  executed operating limits while civic officers executed release and
  movement. The record was corrected, but lost wages, housing standing, and
  clients were not automatically restored.
- **Effect:** Public has no recovery chain. It asserts separated authority but
  never shows repair changing forecast conditions, food movement resuming, or
  procedural correction failing to pay the material debt it created.
- **Repair direction:** Close the bounded source on staged movement, repair and
  a new forecast, the distinct actuation owners, and the captain's still-unsettled
  losses.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not decide which catastrophe was true, imply that
  prevention proves a forecast, universalize the precedent, or invent completed
  restitution.
- **Dependency:** Forecast Evidence and Courts; Prophetic Houses; history and
  institutional causality.
- **Confidence:** High.

carrier does not need crop species, lift geometry, air-pressure engineering,
maintenance intervals, waste streams, exact light quotas, named sectors, or a
full ration economy. Do not add mundane scarcity merely to counterbalance the
wonder of cultivated sunlight, and do not answer the source of forecast
deformation or inferred Luck.
