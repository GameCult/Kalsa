# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** All 14 root/Public raw SHA-1 hashes match the baseline packet. The
Divided City has memorable resources and authority boundaries, but a reader
can reach only an architectural survey. There is no situated prophetic source,
historical carrier, or onward route that shows a forecast entering sponsorship,
allocation, infrastructure work, and material consequence.

## Protected successes

- `Kalsa/index.md` remains a clear consent gate, and `World/Kalsa.md` gives the
  Divided City a concrete first image: bright hours are medicine, ceremony,
  agricultural capacity, and privilege.
- Preserve the separation between the civic allocation table and Sunwall
  stewards. The stewards operate farms and lifts and can stop unsafe work; they
  do not assign public shares.
- Preserve the city as a contested condominium rather than resolving it into
  one kingdom, and preserve the ordinary workers who farm galleries, maintain
  lifts and refuges, carry grain, keep records, and serve garrisons.
- `Magic and Miracles.md` usefully limits prophecy: announced futures change,
  forecast pressure does not identify its source, and no single schoolbook
  explains every working.

## Findings

### REN37-01 — Curiosity reaches a section, then loses the road

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Public/index.md`; `World/Kalsa.md`, first section and footer;
  `World/Peoples and Polities.md#The divided city`.
- **Evidence:** Public index offers no Divided City or prophetic source. The
  world survey links `Divided City` to the overview section, but that section
  has no dedicated source or place link and ends in a general footer.
- **Effect:** Bright-hour privilege and disputed forecasts invite inspection,
  but the route stops before a reader meets a record, claimant, conflict, or
  consequential choice.
- **Repair direction:** Add one bounded Divided City source linked from the
  overview and Public index, with return links to the overview and Public home.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not turn the city into the mandatory opening route or
  expose backstage prophecy mechanics.
- **Dependency:** Public navigation and the owner of any adopted event or
  situated source.
- **Confidence:** High; no existing Public note completes the route.

### REN37-02 — Forecast practice has no inhabitant owner

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `World/Peoples and Polities.md#The divided city`, second
  paragraph; `World/Magic and Miracles.md`, opening and `What witnesses can
  know`.
- **Evidence:** Prophetic houses compare possible futures; their service can
  avert famine or make a patron's preference sound inevitable; prophetic
  records can show deformation without naming its source. No particular house,
  record keeper, sponsor, witness, rival reading, or affected household carries
  a forecast in Public.
- **Effect:** The reader receives a good epistemic warning but cannot tell what
  a forecast looks like in use, who paid for it, who may inspect it, or which
  claim remains arguable after events change.
- **Repair direction:** Let one declared source show a forecast request,
  bounded comparison, sponsor claim, preserved record, and competing reading.
  Use only owners already available to the source.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not publish a universal prophetic method, identify
  the source of forecast pressure, or make every house practice alike.
- **Dependency:** Prophetic-house and event ownership.
- **Confidence:** High.

### REN37-03 — Allocation and operation are separated but not connected

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `World/Peoples and Polities.md#The divided city`, first
  paragraph.
- **Evidence:** A civic table schedules grain, bright hours, cargo priority,
  pressure refuge, and emergency stores. Sunwall stewards operate farms and
  lifts and can stop unsafe work without assigning shares. Public does not show
  who submits or approves a forecast, how it changes the table, how a stop
  reaches the allocation process, or who loses access when those authorities
  disagree.
- **Effect:** The authority boundary reads as a design diagram rather than a
  lived handoff. Material consequences are named as resources but never arrive
  in a room, body, farm, refuge, or queue.
- **Repair direction:** Carry one forecast through sponsor, record, allocation,
  operational response, and a visible consequence for at least one affected
  group.
- **Collateral risk:** Preserve the allocation/operation split. Do not invent a
  complete constitution, universal table, central court, or technical farm and
  lift schematic.
- **Dependency:** Institutional and material owners.
- **Confidence:** High.

### REN37-04 — The city offers no historical memory or playable evidence

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** the complete Divided City route across `World/Kalsa.md`,
  `Peoples and Polities.md`, and `Magic and Miracles.md`.
- **Evidence:** Public names ongoing imperial tension, forecast patronage,
  allocation, infrastructure, and ordinary labor, but no Divided City event,
  precedent, source dispute, surviving damage, altered practice, or residual
  claim.
- **Effect:** A player can imagine generic court intrigue but cannot enter
  through a forecast copy, allocation mark, cargo order, stop record, damaged
  lift, refuge claim, or historical debt whose custody matters.
- **Repair direction:** Make the proposed source an event-bounded carrier with
  inspectable evidence and consequences that remain actionable after the
  immediate decision.
- **Collateral risk:** Avoid a hook list, solved heroic route, named history
  invented for convenience, or a balanced symposium of every faction.
- **Dependency:** History and Ghostlight play.
- **Confidence:** High.

### REN37-05 — The prose exposes an authority-map scaffold

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `World/Peoples and Polities.md#The divided city`.
- **Evidence:** Three compressed paragraphs use polished systemic oppositions:
  neither conqueror can command; allocation and operation remain separate;
  prophecy can avert famine or naturalize preference; most residents are not
  prophets. Abstract institutional and resource lists carry nearly all detail.
- **Effect:** The section is clear but sounds like a completed governance
  review rather than language grown from a city. Its caution is more legible
  than its people.
- **Repair direction:** Keep concise bearings in the survey and move causal
  sequence, records, objects, and disagreement into the situated source. Do not
  merely vary sentence rhythm.
- **Collateral risk:** Preserve the useful distinctions and ordinary labor;
  do not replace clarity with ornamental dialect or invented dialogue.
- **Dependency:** AI-Isms and Prose.
- **Confidence:** High; the contrast with the existing Public sources is
  immediate.

## Pattern or dependency map

The missing reader chain is: `sponsor or public need -> bounded forecast and
record -> competing interpretation -> civic allocation -> Sunwall operation or
stop -> bright hours, grain, cargo, refuge, and stores -> affected workers and
households -> later memory or claim`.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not write a complete Divided City constitution, explain prophecy from the
author's view, make Ju'onai and terjamna positions symmetrical, name unknown
offices or historical actors, or answer what deforms the forecasts. One
situated carrier is more useful than an encyclopedia expansion.

## Ranked actions

1. Add one bounded Divided City source and link it from the overview and Public
   index.
2. Carry a forecast through sponsorship, evidence, allocation, operational
   authority, material consequence, and surviving claim.
3. Compress the overview around that source while preserving bright-hour
   specificity, ordinary labor, contested rule, and bounded prophetic mystery.
