# Ghostlight Play and Consequence

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** All 26 raw SHA-1 digests match the frozen packet. The hidden owners
already support a strong Ghostlight situation: a closed lift, displaced grain,
a detained worker, rival forecasts, separately held records, scoped responders,
and consequences that outlive the ruling. Public exposes only an overview of
the institutional arrangement. A character can understand that the city is
divided, but cannot yet meet a threshold, obtain evidence, choose among
supported interventions, provoke a target-backed response, or carry away a
durable receipt. One bounded Closed Lift carrier is earned; a quest-hook list is
not.

## Protected successes

- `Kalsa/Spoilers/Events/The Closed Lift Appeal.md` offers several concrete
  points of contact without making any one mandatory: the closed cargo lift,
  detained captain, displaced grain, brake assembly, sealed first-court file,
  sponsor disclosures, corrected record, and unpaid remedy.
- `Kalsa/Spoilers/Polities/Prophecy - The Divided City.md` and
  `Kalsa/Spoilers/Institutions/Forecast Evidence and Courts.md` keep allocation,
  technical operation, civic judgment, house discipline, record custody, and
  imperial claims separate. That separation makes different actions produce
  materially different responses.
- Prophecy remains actionable without becoming an answer key. Disclosure,
  repair, staging, and rival action alter the futures; inferred Luck cannot
  identify the captain; preventing catastrophe proves no forecast correct.
- The captain's corrected record does not restore wages, housing standing,
  clients, or reputation. That unfinished remedy supplies a durable consequence
  without requiring player attention to make the captain's life meaningful.

## Findings

### GPC37-01 — The Public route has no playable threshold

- **Finding ID:** GPC37-01
- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/World/Peoples and Polities.md`, `## The divided
  city`; `Kalsa/Public/index.md`, `## Read another argument`; current absence
  from `Kalsa/Public/Sources/`.
- **Evidence:** The overview names lifts, grain, garrisons, schedules, closures,
  and authorities, but gives no particular pressure a character can encounter.
  The attested event owner supplies a bounded threshold and the packet
  explicitly permits one Closed Lift source.
- **Effect:** The divided city remains passive encyclopedia texture. Readers
  can admire its authority map but cannot tell where character knowledge begins
  or what immediate fact invites action, refusal, or misunderstanding.
- **Repair direction:** Add one situated Closed Lift carrier and link it from
  both the divided-city overview and Public source index. Let the closed lift,
  displaced load, captain's custody, or surviving remedy provide the opening;
  the source need not advertise jobs.
- **Collateral risk:** A list of commissions would turn every record and worker
  into a hook dispenser. Naming protected actors, houses, city, sectors,
  offices, or dates would invent canon merely to dress the threshold.
- **Dependency:** Reader Experience and Navigation for the direct route;
  Culture and Situated Knowledge for an inhabitant-owned source form.
- **Confidence:** High; the complete Public route contains no comparable point
  of contact, while the event owner contains several.

### GPC37-02 — Evidence cannot yet reach the owner able to respond

- **Finding ID:** GPC37-02
- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/World/Magic and Miracles.md`, `## What witnesses
  can know`; `Kalsa/Public/World/Peoples and Polities.md`, `## The divided
  city`; hidden basis in `Kalsa/Spoilers/Events/The Closed Lift Appeal.md`,
  `## Appeal and disclosed interventions` and `## Adventure consequences`.
- **Evidence:** The event distinguishes forecast reports, sponsor plans, brake
  inspection, first-court file, corrected record, and wage or tenancy records.
  It also distinguishes their responders: the Mixed Bench can rule on evidence
  and release, civic clerks preserve and correct records, the Sunwall
  Stewardship inspects and operates the lift, houses discipline reports, and
  imperial parties answer only inside their claims. Public states the general
  epistemic limits but exposes none of this evidence-to-owner path.
- **Effect:** A player-facing intervention would have to rely on author
  knowledge or generic persuasion. Recovering a brake, a disclosure, and a wage
  record would seem interchangeable even though each supports a different act.
- **Repair direction:** In the single source, make two or three evidence scopes
  legible through custody and consequence rather than an action menu. Material
  evidence may change inspection, repair, load limits, or reopening; sponsor
  disclosure may change admissibility, release, correction, or house
  discipline; wage and tenancy copies may support remedy without deciding the
  forecast or operating the lift.
- **Collateral risk:** Letting one dramatic object prove the whole case would
  make the mystery an answer key. Letting the court, house, or sponsor reopen
  the lift would erase the Stewardship's technical authority.
- **Dependency:** Forecast Evidence and Courts owns evidentiary response;
  Prophetic Houses owns discipline; the polity owns civic, imperial, and
  Sunwall boundaries; Material Life and Ecology may test whether the brake and
  grain pressure remain materially credible.
- **Confidence:** High; the owner notes explicitly name both the evidence and
  the scoped responders.

### GPC37-03 — Public offers no divergent consequence horizon

- **Finding ID:** GPC37-03
- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** prospective Public Closed Lift source close; hidden basis in
  `Kalsa/Spoilers/Events/The Closed Lift Appeal.md`, `## Outcome and precedent`,
  `## Institutional consequences`, and `## Adventure consequences`.
- **Evidence:** Inspection and repair can change safe capacity; disclosure can
  undo an identity finding; staged movement can avert both forecast branches;
  a corrected record can coexist with unpaid loss; imperial recognition can
  remain contested. The current Public overview ends before any of these
  consequences become reachable.
- **Effect:** Even if a source merely recounts that the appeal succeeded, play
  pressure would collapse into one correct moral choice and one clean ending.
  Players need to see that reopening, release, record correction, discipline,
  grain movement, and restitution are related but non-substitutable outcomes.
- **Repair direction:** Preserve at least one consequential choice or
  misunderstanding and show a materially different owner response to another
  plausible course. Close on a durable receipt or surviving burden—corrected
  record, operating limit, public notice, inspection copy, or unpaid wage and
  tenancy claim—without requiring complete restitution.
- **Collateral risk:** Declaring one forecast vindicated, making brake fatigue
  disprove all magical influence, or paying every loss would close the very
  horizons that support later play.
- **Dependency:** History, Conflict, and Change for the precedent's afterlife;
  Institutional Causality for distinct response owners; Reader Experience for
  keeping the consequence legible without tutorial prose.
- **Confidence:** High; the target models divergent outcomes and durable
  receipts in detail, all currently hidden from Public.

## Pattern or dependency map

One evidence-supported play trace is already available without inventing a
quest:

**Observable cue:** a cargo lift remains closed or restricted while grain is
displaced, and the captain's corrected record does not settle lost work or
tenancy. -> **Attempt:** carry an inspection component or independent record
across the occupied boundary to the forum able to use it. -> **Resistance or
misreading:** a sponsor treats the avoided catastrophe as proof, a sealed first
finding still circulates, or the party is told that possession of the brake
settles guilt. -> **Responding owner:** the Stewardship can inspect and alter
operation; the Mixed Bench can limit evidence and remedy; clerks can preserve a
correction; a house can discipline its report. -> **Durable consequence:** the
lift may reopen under limits and grain may move while the captain's record is
corrected, yet compensation or imperial recognition can remain unpaid or
contested.

An alternative course—recovering wage and tenancy records instead of the brake
record—can strengthen restitution without changing technical custody. That is
the useful claimant collision: neither course is the universal solution.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not turn the source into “six adventures in the City of Prophets,” and do
not make freeing the captain the single righteous route. The event earns one
inhabited pressure surface, not a hook quota. It must also resist the tidier
errors: detectable Luck, a brake that answers every metaphysical question, a
forecast declared correct, a court that operates machinery, a universal
precedent, or invented restitution.

## Ranked actions

1. **Essential:** Add and directly route one bounded Closed Lift source with a
   concrete threshold and character-accessible evidence tied to the authority
   able to act on it.
2. **High-value:** Preserve divergent interventions and consequence horizons,
   including at least one durable receipt and the captain's unfinished remedy.
3. **Optional:** Add no general action menu, faction roster, or further source.
   Leave protected identities, dates, sectors, ultimate forecast truth, and
   later restitution unresolved.
