# History, Conflict, and Change

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** The hidden owners contain a strong causal history: conquest produces
the condominium, and the Closed Lift Appeal turns a bad first judgment into
bounded changes in evidence, disclosure, operation, and imperial claims. The
Public route presents only the resulting arrangement. It therefore makes a
historically improvised authority structure look timeless, leaves its decisive
procedural scar inaccessible, and suppresses both the rival memories and the
captain's unrepaired losses that keep the episode from becoming a progress
fable.

## Protected successes

- `Kalsa/Public/World/Peoples and Polities.md` already keeps civic allocation,
  Sunwall operation, house forecast labor, and imperial power separate. Repair
  must not give any one of them retrospective ownership of the whole city.
- `Kalsa/Spoilers/Events/The Closed Lift Appeal.md` preserves an unusually good
  change mechanism: an abusive first ruling, sponsor-action disclosure,
  material inspection, staged precautions, and a later precedent whose limits
  remain visible.
- The event does not decide which forecast was true. Disclosure, repair,
  staging, and changed intentions alter the branch set; later Ju'onai,
  Terjamna, house, and lift-worker memories remain interested rather than
  converging on an authorial verdict.
- The corrected record releases the captain without restoring work, housing
  standing, clients, or reputation. That incomplete repair is load-bearing
  historical consequence, not a loose end to tidy away.

## Findings

### HCC37-01 — The condominium arrives without its formation

- **Finding ID:** HCC37-01
- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/World/Peoples and Polities.md`, `## The divided
  city`, both paragraphs; hidden basis in `Kalsa/Spoilers/Polities/Prophecy -
  The Divided City.md`, `## The divided sovereignty`.
- **Evidence:** The owner establishes a sequence: Ju'onai conquest first
  reduced the city to tribute, later Terjamna contest prevented exclusive
  control, and local civic bodies survive because neither occupier can cheaply
  abolish the authorities and dependencies the others also use. The Public
  account names an unstable condominium but none of this formation.
- **Effect:** Readers meet a static governance diagram. The division of powers
  reads like a designed settlement rather than path-dependent survival under
  repeated conquest, so present conflict has no inherited pressure.
- **Repair direction:** Add the smallest Public formation statement that
  preserves the order of conquest, later contest, and contingent civic
  survival. Keep formal armistice, borders, dates, names, and exact sectors
  unresolved.
- **Collateral risk:** A careless sentence could invent a founding compact,
  imply equal co-sovereignty, or promote civic mediation into independence.
- **Dependency:** The polity owner controls the formation claim; Reader
  Experience controls how much of it the overview can carry without becoming a
  hidden ontology dump.
- **Confidence:** High; the causal sequence is explicit in the attested owner
  and absent from the complete Public route.

### HCC37-02 — Public has no carrier for the crisis that changed practice

- **Finding ID:** HCC37-02
- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/World/Peoples and Polities.md`, `## The divided
  city`; `Kalsa/Public/index.md`; absence from `Kalsa/Public/Sources/`; hidden
  basis in `Kalsa/Spoilers/Events/The Closed Lift Appeal.md`, `## Failure and
  first judgment` through `## Institutional consequences`.
- **Evidence:** The Closed Lift record joins forecast rivalry to brake fatigue,
  a false Luck identification, detention and closure, sponsor disclosures,
  material inspection, staged grain movement, release, repair, and later
  limits on evidence and operating authority. No Public source or navigation
  path carries that formation crisis.
- **Effect:** Public institutional limits appear as narrator-supplied rules.
  Readers cannot see who exceeded authority, what evidence contradicted the
  first judgment, or how conflict made later practice possible.
- **Repair direction:** Add the permitted single bounded Public source and
  route it directly from the divided-city overview and Public source index.
  Organize it around the first ruling, distinct disclosed/material records,
  the appeal's reversible measures, and the narrow later changes; do not supply
  protected names, dates, city, house, sector, or office particulars.
- **Collateral risk:** Turning the source into a clean appellate victory would
  erase occupation, convert a narrow precedent into universal law, and let the
  court or houses absorb the lift workers' operational competence.
- **Dependency:** Event owner for sequence and omissions; Forecast Evidence
  and Courts for evidentiary limits; Prophetic Houses and the polity owner for
  sponsor, house, civic, imperial, and operating-authority boundaries.
- **Confidence:** High; the hidden event is fully modeled and the attested
  Public corpus contains no equivalent carrier.

### HCC37-03 — Rival afterlives and unrepaired harm are missing

- **Finding ID:** HCC37-03
- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Spoilers/Events/The Closed Lift Appeal.md`, `## Outcome
  and precedent` and `## Institutional consequences`; prospective Public
  Closed Lift source close.
- **Evidence:** Ju'onai officials remember foresight, Terjamna officials
  deterrence, houses disciplined comparison, and lift workers brake repair.
  The captain's record is corrected, but lost wages, housing standing, clients,
  and reputation are not automatically restored; later compensation and reach
  of the precedent remain unknown.
- **Effect:** If Public reports only the appeal and its new rules, change will
  read as consensus and successful reform. The people who paid for the first
  ruling disappear exactly when the institution learns from them.
- **Repair direction:** End the bounded source with asymmetrical, attributed
  memories and the captain's surviving losses. Let those carriers disagree
  without balancing them into a four-viewpoint symposium or deciding which
  forecast was correct.
- **Collateral risk:** Invented restitution would close the live consequence;
  evenly weighted testimonies would turn interested memory into a schematic
  debate; a triumphant ending would overstate both civic sovereignty and the
  precedent's reach.
- **Dependency:** Culture and Situated Knowledge for credible carriers;
  Ghostlight Play and Consequence for whether the surviving losses remain
  actionable without becoming a compulsory hook.
- **Confidence:** High; both the divergent memories and incomplete repair are
  explicit, and neither is reachable in Public.

## Pattern or dependency map

Promethean economic selection and the Sunwall's hierarchy -> Ju'onai conquest
-> later Terjamna contest -> contingent survival of civic bodies -> Closed Lift
first-court overreach -> sponsor disclosure plus brake inspection -> bounded
appeal and staged operation -> narrow later practice plus unrepaired personal
loss.

The Public repair should expose only the middle historical chain that has an
inhabitant carrier. It need not publish the Promethean origin or resolve the
unknown particulars in order to make present authority historically legible.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not make the Closed Lift Appeal the moment the divided city discovered fair
government. It neither ends occupation nor proves one forecast, detects Luck,
transfers lift operation to a court or sponsor, universalizes the precedent,
or compensates the captain. A neat reform story would destroy the conflict that
makes this history useful.

## Ranked actions

1. **Essential:** Give the divided-city overview its minimal conquest sequence,
   then add and directly route one bounded Closed Lift source that carries the
   first ruling, evidence collision, reversible appeal, and narrow procedural
   afterlife.
2. **High-value:** Preserve rival attributed memories and close on the
   captain's surviving losses and the precedent's unresolved reach.
3. **Optional:** No further historical expansion. Leave dates, names, sectors,
   houses, exact offices, calendar, later compensation, and ultimate forecast
   truth unresolved.
