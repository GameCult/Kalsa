# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** Public protects the Divided City's authority boundaries but gives
their explanation to an unnamed competent narrator. The Closed Lift case
already contains house, imperial, civic, Sunwall, lift-worker, and lower-shadow
carriers with different evidence and interests. One incomplete source can make
that plurality inhabitant-owned without publishing the hidden constitution.

## Protected successes

- Public presents Prophecy as comparison among futures changed by disclosure,
  not an author-facing capability table or inevitable fate.
- Allocation, operation, forecast labor, civic judgment, and imperial claims
  remain separate even under occupation.
- Most residents are visibly workers, carriers, clerks, families, and garrison
  dependents rather than a people made entirely of prophets.
- Luck remains inferred and cannot identify a person; the Closed Lift outcome
  leaves both causation and restitution incomplete.

## Findings

### CSK37-01 — The city's explanatory owner is an unnamed narrator

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/World/Peoples and Polities.md`, `## The divided
  city`, especially the first paragraph.
- **Evidence:** Hidden canon distributes knowledge through ward and court
  records, house archives, imperial claims, Sunwall operating records, worker
  testimony, and lower-shadow memory. Public states the condominium and its
  safeguards as a complete synthesis without naming a text, institution, or
  comparison of sources that owns it.
- **Effect:** A contested city speaks one polished institutional dialect. Its
  authority boundaries look like neutral constitution rather than practices
  people preserve because rivals keep crossing them.
- **Repair direction:** Route the overview to one incomplete Closed Lift Public
  source whose surviving files and accounts demonstrate the separation. Keep
  the overview as bearings rather than inventing a universal civic textbook.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not invent a city archive, constitution, armistice,
  native office name, or consensus about why the city remains divided.
- **Dependency:** Event owner.
- **Confidence:** High; the current overview names no inhabitant carrier.

### CSK37-02 — Prophetic houses appear as one frictionless tradition

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Peoples and Polities.md`, paragraph beginning `Prophetic
  houses train`, and `Magic and Miracles.md`, Prophecy references.
- **Evidence:** Houses preserve different archives, patrons, assignments, and
  report corrections. In the event, imperial sponsorship shapes two forecasts,
  a house witness supplies the Luck inference, and later house discipline owns
  report completeness but not civic remedy.
- **Effect:** Training and service become generic house capacities without a
  transmission path, sponsor interest, internal correction, or characteristic
  blind spot.
- **Repair direction:** Let forecast reports enter the event source with their
  sponsors, intended interventions, civic copies, and later correction duties.
  Do not catalogue houses or pretend the case represents all house practice.
- **Collateral risk:** No unnamed house should become the representative
  culture, and no archive should become complete truth merely because it
  trained the prophet.
- **Dependency:** Institutional Causality.
- **Confidence:** High.

### CSK37-03 — Rival accounts and their blind spots do not reach Public

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** the Public Divided City route as a whole; no situated source is
  linked from the overview or Public index.
- **Evidence:** Ju'onai officials credit foresight; Terjamna officers credit
  deterrence; houses credit disciplined comparison; lift workers credit the
  brake crew; lower-shadow accounts preserve the captain's unpaid injury.
  Sponsor plans and brake fatigue alter what each account can honestly claim.
- **Effect:** Public disagreement stops at `possible futures` and never becomes
  incompatible social memory. Readers cannot see which knowledge remains useful
  after neither predicted catastrophe occurs.
- **Repair direction:** Frame the source through the first-court file, sponsor
  disclosures, brake inspection, appellate copy, corrected record, and
  lower-shadow aftermath. Introduce each carrier where its evidence matters,
  not as a balanced five-view symposium.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not decide which catastrophe was real, let brake
  fatigue explain every forecast deformation, or turn later interpretation
  into the event's factual owner.
- **Dependency:** History, Conflict, and Change; Material Life and Ecology.
- **Confidence:** High.

### CSK37-04 — Non-house residents are burdens, not knowledge carriers

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Peoples and Polities.md`, paragraph ending `they may never be
  permitted to inspect`.
- **Evidence:** The non-house captain bears detention, wage loss, housing
  damage, and client loss; lift workers preserve material knowledge; closure
  shifts grain, labor, ration, and security pressure; lower-shadow accounts
  preserve the unfinished remedy.
- **Effect:** Ordinary people appear only as the population acted upon by
  prophetic institutions. Their work knowledge and memory do not reproduce
  correction or provide a route into the city.
- **Repair direction:** Make the captain's corrected record, lift-worker account
  of the brake, and lower-shadow memory of unpaid loss carry the source's
  aftermath. Preserve the wider households affected by closure without
  inventing testimony.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not turn the captain into a named hero, grant workers
  forecast or appellate authority, or make lower-shadow memory unanimous.
- **Dependency:** Ghostlight Play and Consequence.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not write an equal-voice dialogue, complete house catalogue, universal court
primer, native terminology, detected Luck, or a narrator's final answer about
which institution saved the city. Keep the existing ordinary-life paragraph
and allocation/operation boundary; give them one consequential source rather
than more explanatory bulk.

## Ranked actions

1. Add one incomplete Closed Lift carrier with declared records, omissions, and
   return links.
2. Preserve house, imperial, civic, technical, worker, and lower-shadow
   knowledge as asymmetric evidence with different blind spots.
3. Carry the captain's corrected record and unpaid remedy without making one
   case the origin or full expression of Divided City culture.
