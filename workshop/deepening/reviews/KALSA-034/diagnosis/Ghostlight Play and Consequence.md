# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

Block. The hidden owners already produce a strong Ghostlight situation: shared
water fails, evidence is perishable, armed crossings threaten, an adopted
keeper is accused through an undetectable Gift, and several authorities can
restore different parts of the damage without owning the whole answer. The
event has materially different courses and receipts that survive repair. Public
reduces that pressure to an outsider/insider summary and the principle that
coincidence is not proof. Readers receive the correct conclusion but no
threshold, inspectable evidence, responding owner, or consequence chain through
which to use it.

All 21 Git blob hashes in `KALSA-034-baseline-01` were verified and match.

## Protected successes

- `Kalsa/Spoilers/Events/The Broken Windtrap Settlement.md` §§ “Failure and
  accusation” and “Restraint and repair” provides an immediate clock and
  collision: injured workers, contaminated stored water, threatened armed
  crossings, emergency stores, an accused keeper, a prophet who refuses false
  identification, and wreckage that repair could destroy as evidence.
- The event supports several defensible courses rather than one moral solution.
  Families can prioritize retaliation, water continuity, evidence preservation,
  independent inspection, or protection of the accused; each course exposes a
  different person or resource to loss.
- `Luck Harm and Restitution.md` §§ “Opening a case,” “Evidence standards,”
  “Reciprocal restraint,” and “Review, refusal, and exit” supplies usable owners
  and resistance. A compact can open stores and preserve access; maintainers
  can expose material failure; parties can refuse unsafe inspection or
  indefinite restriction; failed settlement can still close boundaries or
  become feud.
- The repair succeeds without an answer key. Corrosion and missed maintenance
  support material findings, while prophetic bias and the keeper's presence do
  not establish whether Luck acted or who bore it.
- The durable consequences are unusually good: corrected liability does not
  restore contracts, family affiliation, lost work, or consumed reserves.

### GPC34-02

- **Finding ID:** GPC34-02
- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/World/Peoples and Polities.md` § “Selza'a,” final
  paragraph; `Kalsa/Public/World/Magic and Miracles.md` §§ “What people
  compare” and “What witnesses can know.”
- **Evidence:** Public says coincidence is not proof and that families can
  disagree about protection or predation. It does not show what players or
  inhabitants can inspect instead: corrosion, access, maintenance schedules,
  forecast horizon, planned intervention, household loss, pool contribution,
  or corrected record. Nor does it distinguish the compact that restores water,
  the worker who inspects the windtrap, the prophet who reports bias, and the
  case table that assigns material responsibility.
- **Effect:** The epistemic limit is correct but inert. Suspicion can only be
  accepted or rejected as theme; there is no honest investigative route between
  accusation and unknowable magic, and no way for a party to make matters
  better or worse without receiving author knowledge.
- **Repair direction:** Let the event carrier demonstrate the evidence
  hierarchy and owner handoff rather than expanding both surveys into a
  procedure manual: restore baseline water and preserve wreckage; record the
  prophet's bounded uncertainty; inspect corrosion and missed work; assign
  proven maintenance breaches separately from unresolved magical cause.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not create a Luck detector, make prophecy the final
  authority, or imply material explanation disproves magical involvement.
  Evidence should change action without closing the mystery.
- **Dependency:** Institutional causality and situated knowledge; exact
  authority remains with the hidden compact, worker, prophet, and restitution
  owners.
- **Confidence:** High. The hidden notes state each evidence boundary and Public
  currently supplies only the conclusion.

### GPC34-03

- **Finding ID:** GPC34-03
- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Spoilers/Events/The Broken Windtrap Settlement.md` §§
  “Restraint and repair” and “What remained broken”; prospective Public source
  permitted by the packet.
- **Evidence:** The event's strongest play consequence is not the discovered
  corrosion but the mismatch between repair layers. The risk pool restores
  water and care; maintenance findings shift future contributions; a corrected
  record clears the keeper; none restores contracts, affiliation, poor
  households' reserves, or lost work. Later compacts change because those
  failures remain legible.
- **Effect:** A careless Public reconstruction could end at “inspection found
  the real cause,” turning a layered conflict into a solved mystery and making
  material evidence the single correct moral choice. That would discard the
  setting's durable receipt and erase the reason to challenge a remedy after a
  technically successful repair.
- **Repair direction:** Carry one complete consequence spine into the source:
  reciprocal restraint and emergency water -> independent inspection -> shared
  maintenance finding and pooled repair -> shifted contributions and corrected
  record -> keeper's lost standing and poor households' uncompensated losses ->
  later pressure for lost-work support and fuller maintenance records.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not append further hooks or invent a later appeal.
  Preserve the known incomplete remedy and let future play decide what another
  compact does with the precedent.
- **Dependency:** Material-life consequences and Public prose compression.
- **Confidence:** High. Every link in the trace is explicitly adopted by the
  event owner.

## Pattern or dependency map

Observable cue: repeated faults, failed anchor, contaminated water, injured
workers -> attempted response: blame the keeper and prepare armed crossing, or
open stores, preserve wreckage, and accept restraint -> resistance/misreading:
prophetic bias is demanded as identity while all three families hide their own
missed work -> responding owners: compact, independent worker, prophet, case
table, and risk-pool stewards -> durable consequence: restored water, shifted
contributions, corrected record, changed inspection practice, lost contracts,
changed affiliation, and uncompensated household labor.

## Tempting edit to resist

- Do not reveal whether Luck contributed or whether the keeper carried it. A
  mystery with actionable evidence is stronger than an answer key.
- Do not turn `Peoples and Polities.md` or `Magic and Miracles.md` into a list
  of all permitted interventions. One situated source should carry the case;
  the surveys only need to route and frame it.
- Do not add a named citywide court. The compact and mutually accepted case
  table are effective precisely because their authority is bounded.

## Ranked actions

1. **Essential:** Add and link one Broken Windtrap Public reconstruction that
   opens on the material threshold and shows the evidence-owner handoff.
2. **High-value:** Preserve the incomplete restitution as the durable receipt,
   so successful repair does not become total resolution.
3. **Optional:** Add no new hooks, current named site, or magical explanation.
   The adopted event already contains enough pressure.
