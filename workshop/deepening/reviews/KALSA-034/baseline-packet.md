# KALSA-034 Selza'a Windtrap Knowledge Packet

## Objective and allowed pressure

Diagnose whether Selza'a knowledge is culturally situated, materially grounded,
historically formed, playable, and readable in Public. Repair may change the
two relevant Public surveys and add one situated Public source only if earned
by the adopted event. It may not invent a family, person, Luck detector,
citywide authority, quotation, native legal term, or magical verdict.

## Authority and provenance

- Seed witnesses are immutable evidence, not prose owners.
- The hidden polity, family-territory, restitution, and event notes own facts.
- `The Broken Windtrap Settlement.md` alone owns its event sequence and
  protected omissions.
- Public owns presentation only. The existing `Outsiders say... Selza'a
  arguments...` construction is an explicit prose and epistemic fault to test.

## Protected strengths

- Luck remains real, constant, indirect, and impossible to detect directly.
- Selza'a family territories remain plural, distributed, materially
  interdependent, and capable of both refuge and coercion.
- Prophetic bias remains evidence of uncertainty, not identification.
- The windtrap settlement restores water and narrows liability without fully
  restoring the accused keeper or poor households.
- No author taxonomy, named family, citywide court, complete legal system, or
  answer about leaving the city enters Public.

## Ghostlight lens

- **Contact:** a damaged shared windtrap, contaminated water, an accused
  keeper, a threatened crossing, an emergency store, or a family record.
- **Evidence:** wreckage, corrosion, work schedule, access, forecast bias,
  witness account, household loss, pool contribution, and corrected record.
- **Interventions:** preserve the wreckage, keep water moving, escort an
  inspector, protect the accused, compare work records, restrain a crossing,
  carry aid, or challenge the finding's remedy.
- **Consequences:** water, injury care, lost work, family standing,
  affiliation, compact contribution, feud, and future inspection practice. No
  intervention identifies Luck directly.
