## New defects within remit

### AIP38-07 — The archival waiver was rearranged rather than broken

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** first two paragraphs of `After the High Windtrap Broke`, and
  first paragraphs of `What the Three Ledgers Kept`, `Who Kept the Heat`, `The
  Missing Relief Cohort`, and `The Closed Lift Appeal`.
- **Evidence:** Windtrap now opens with later citation, but immediately follows
  with compact records that `have lost every name, the place, and the date`.
  Three Ledgers inventories three records before `cult, god, district, and year
  have been lost`. Ash-Valve inventories four surviving document types before
  saying they cannot be placed in a named branch, ward, storm, campaign, or
  year. Shield Levy inventories store marks, shift papers, testimony, and a
  military claim before naming the missing campaign, authorities, and
  communities. Closed Lift inventories forecasts, orders, disclosures,
  inspection, and corrected record before saying city, date, houses, and
  captain lost their names.
- **Effect:** the sentence shapes differ, but readers still meet the same
  two-column intake form: what survived / what is missing. Distinct archives
  remain less audible than the author's provenance protocol.
- **Repair direction:** keep the missing information, but stop making it the
  universal opening transaction. One source can place its loss later when the
  absence matters; another can identify an interested present-day user rather
  than enumerate every carrier; another can begin with the physical document
  in use without immediately completing the surviving-media list.
- **Collateral risk:** no invented metadata, archivist, quotation, or document
  form. The reader must still be able to distinguish unnamed history from named
  fact.
- **Confidence:** High; the paired inventory remains in five of seven source
  notes.

### AIP38-08 — Windtrap turns the keeper's loss into a clipped checksum

- **Priority:** Optional
- **Location:** `After the High Windtrap Broke`, final paragraph: `Their
  corrected record followed them. Their contracts did not.`
- **Evidence:** the revision removes the prior explanatory coda, but replaces
  it with two syntactically mirrored fragments whose final negation supplies a
  polished reversal.
- **Effect:** the material loss lands as an authored epigram. It calls attention
  to the writer's control and repeats the correction-theatre habit at miniature
  scale.
- **Repair direction:** keep both the portable correction and lost contracts,
  but let them occur in one unbalanced sentence or through a compact's later use
  rather than a setup/punchline pair.
- **Collateral risk:** do not restore the old catalogue of consequences or
  imply that the keeper's correction repaired their livelihood.
- **Confidence:** High; the reading effect is local and exact.

### AIP38-09 — One-office/one-limit choruses still expose the authority map

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `What the Three Ledgers Kept`, last paragraph of `The body
  leaves the shrine`; `Gods and the Dead`, opening exposition; `The Closed Lift
  Appeal`, last paragraph of `The corrected copy`; and, more lightly, `Peoples
  and Polities` under `The Ju'onai Hegemony`.
- **Evidence:** Three Ledgers gives covenant witnesses one `could` sentence,
  stewards another, and mortuary witnesses a third, each paired with the
  decision it cannot own. Gods repeats `A covenant witness records`, `A miracle
  steward records`, and `A mortuary witness records`, then gives interpreters a
  chorus and closes with what none can establish alone. Closed Lift gives
  Houses, Sunwall stewardship, civic judgment, and imperial claims one equally
  weighted scope sentence each. The Ju'onai survey similarly assigns schools,
  Houses, and Measured Offices their rights and denials in sequence.
- **Effect:** different institutions sound like entries in the same authority
  matrix. The structure is lucid but bloodless: the reader can see the workshop
  model through the paragraph, and the repeated negative scopes tell them the
  approved institutional interpretation.
- **Repair direction:** preserve every ownership boundary while allowing fewer
  sentences to state it abstractly. In sources, let custody of the body,
  ledger, stores, lift, or release order demonstrate the split. In surveys,
  give one concise bearing and route the reader to the case instead of reciting
  every office's permitted verb.
- **Collateral risk:** do not merge custodians, imply consensus, or let one
  office acquire another's decision. This is a presentation repair, not an
  invitation to simplify the institution.
- **Confidence:** High; the same parallel scope grammar recurs across divine,
  civic, and imperial material.

### AIP38-10 — One textbook `can ... and still ...` balance survives in Relics

- **Priority:** Optional
- **Location:** `Relics and Ruins`, final paragraph of `Techpriesthoods`.
- **Evidence:** `A town may need that judgment and still refuse the claim that
  repair grants ownership. Rival lineages may disagree honestly, dishonestly,
  or both.` Need is immediately balanced by refusal, then every moral valence is
  covered by the polished `or both` coda.
- **Effect:** this is the exact staged-nuance cadence the prompt identifies. It
  briefly turns a sharp fact—condemning a conduit can change a harvest or
  siege—into a generic assurance that complexity has been considered.
- **Repair direction:** let a town's refusal or a rival lineage's conduct
  follow from the condemned conduit, harvest, or siege. The paragraph needs the
  leverage/ownership distinction, not the symmetrical moral bookkeeping.
- **Collateral risk:** preserve the town's ability to reject priestly ownership
  and the possibility of sincere as well as self-interested technical
  disagreement.
- **Confidence:** High; the pattern is exact and locally dispensable.

### AIP38-11 — The Public source route repeats catalogue blurbs

- **Priority:** Optional
- **Location:** `Public/index`, `Read another argument`.
- **Evidence:** five consecutive entries use `follows` or `reconstructs` and
  summarize each page through three-to-six coordinated nouns: ledgers;
  `water, accusation, and repair`; `stores, a copied record, and an appeal`;
  four evidence types; then `forecasts, a detained ... plans, and a fatigued
  brake`.
- **Effect:** the reader-facing invitation becomes a metadata catalogue. The
  blurbs make distinct forms—dialogue, schism history, compact case, refusal,
  military review, appeal—share one exhaustive promotional rhythm.
- **Repair direction:** retain routing clarity but vary the invitations. One
  may ask a concrete question, another name a disputed object, another state
  only the initial pressure. Each description need not prove that the source
  contains every valued dimension.
- **Collateral risk:** do not make navigation coy; readers still need enough
  subject information to choose a route.
- **Confidence:** High; recurrence is concentrated in one short navigation
  section.
