# Round 01 Disposition

All six findings are accepted. They concern repeated presentation machinery,
not disputed lore. The repair therefore changes only the Public projections and
preserves their claims, source limits, technical findings, material costs, and
metaphysical uncertainty.

## AIP38-01 — Accepted

The five case histories now enter through different carriers and uses: an armed
crossing and its later citation, three surviving ledgers, a copied valve page,
store marks and shift papers, and two sponsored forecasts. Missing names,
dates, and methods remain explicit without five versions of the same opening

summary syntax were replaced by objects and actions. Terms attached to actual
courts, compacts, records, and Ju'onai doctrine remain because they name local
practices rather than the workshop's voice.

## AIP38-06 — Accepted as the governing repair

The Ash-Valve passage keeps the knocks, the Hearth reading, the copied page,
and the independent inspection. It drops the paired skeptical explanation, the
neutral appeal checksum, and the sentence explaining which question the knocks
did not answer. Windtrap and Closed Lift also stop after their material

survey leaves the families arguing after the case table has acted.

`At the Basin Table` and `Four Readings of a Broken Sluice` remain unchanged:
their scene and teaching-dialogue forms earn the disagreement they stage.
