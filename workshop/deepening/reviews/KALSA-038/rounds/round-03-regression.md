## New defects within remit

### AIP38-12 — Three Ledgers still explains the distinction after performing it

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `What the Three Ledgers Kept`: final paragraph of `The anchor
  dies`; final paragraph of `Grain before succession`; and opening paragraph of
  `What survived the hearing`.
- **Evidence:** after the preceding paragraph shows the merged register turning
  covenant, death inventory, and miracle ledger into the successor's service
  and assets, the narrator adds `No single false sentence made the register
  dangerous. The copy made three different questions answer to the same
  custodian.` Later, `The ledger could show where the grain went. It could not
  show whether the god had refused the successor.` The hearing grants office
  and rites, followed by `It did not certify divine preference.`
- **Effect:** action and custody already carry each distinction. The repeated
  explanatory corrections make the page sound like commentary on its own
  case study, with a lesson checksum after every institutional turn.
- **Repair direction:** trust the merged register, moving grain, and terms of
  the hearing. Keep whichever limit cannot be recovered from those acts, but
  remove or absorb the repeated `what this proves / what it does not prove`
  sentences rather than preserving all three.
- **Collateral risk:** the successor must not acquire divine certification;
  the miracle ledger must not become an oracle; and the danger of merging the
  three records must remain visible.
- **Confidence:** High; the same explanatory correction recurs at three major
  turns in one source.

### AIP38-13 — Gods and the Dead still runs wonder through balanced limitation

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Gods and the Dead`, opening exposition, `Covenant and
  submission`, and `Hungry holy places`.
- **Evidence:** `A god can perceive dangers ... and remain ignorant`; `It can
  preserve a city through war while misunderstanding`; `A god-cult may own a
  magnificent shrine and lack the grain`; and `The closure can protect a wider
  route while taking healing and burial away`. Each sentence presents power or
  competence and immediately balances it with ignorance, scarcity, or lost
  access. The closure then adds a three-part list of household demands.
- **Effect:** this is the original praise-then-invoice cadence in its most
  polished form. The facts are valuable; their recurring balance makes every
  divine wonder arrive with the narrator already holding its ethical receipt.
- **Repair direction:** preserve divine limits and household costs through
  uneven examples or sequence. Let a cheating storekeeper, the unanswered
  rival shrine, an empty relief store, or a household journey carry one side of
  the relation without every sentence formally balancing both.
- **Collateral risk:** do not make gods all-knowing, turn shrine wealth into
  reliable provision, or erase the cost of closing a hungry holy place.
- **Confidence:** High; four close variants occur within one short survey.

### AIP38-14 — The Sarxe survey compresses a people into consecutive inventory
clauses

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Peoples and Polities`, both paragraphs under `Sarxe and
  Saxfoldi`, especially the second.
- **Evidence:** households first carry `people, stores, records, routes, and
  care`; settled communities then inventory `Clinics, graves, farms,
  workshops, and mixed households`; divided families carry `messages,
  medicine, claims, and resentment`; diaspora workers become `mediators,
  guides, guards, salvagers, hunters, and mercenaries`. The final clause neatly
  balances those who sought the road against those assigned its danger.
- **Effect:** dispersal, finite route capacity, settled life, family division,
  and unequal danger are strong. Four consecutive completeness lists turn them
  into a demographic schema and reproduce the exact sophisticated
  wonder-then-cost/list-clause voice this pass exists to cut.
- **Repair direction:** keep every necessary pressure, but let one movement
  across a closing route carry several of them. Reduce parallel occupational
  coverage and state unequal assignment without the polished
  `some chose / others were assigned` balance.
- **Collateral risk:** do not reduce Sarxe life to the moving center, erase
  settled communities and mixed households, or imply all diaspora labor is
  voluntary.
- **Confidence:** High; the congestion is concentrated and the reading effect
  survives independent of any individual noun.

### AIP38-15 — Magic and Miracles presents traditions in repeated survey rows

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Magic and Miracles`: the paragraphs beginning `Other workings
  take months`, `Field and shield records`, `Household and route testimony`,

- **Evidence:** the opening samples Papsenai, prophet, Sarxe, and exceptional

- **Effect:** the page reads like a completed comparison matrix. Cultural

  Not every section needs one sentence from each magical polity.
- **Collateral risk:** do not imply a universal theory, demote Sarxe knowledge
  to rumor, or make the Ju'onai schools the default explanatory authority.
- **Confidence:** High; the row structure repeats across all three movements of
