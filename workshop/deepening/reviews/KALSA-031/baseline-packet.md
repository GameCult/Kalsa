# KALSA-031 Divine Witness Practice Packet

## Objective and allowed pressure

Diagnose whether Kalsa gives inhabitants adequate, institutionally distinct
ways to test divine testimony and whether Public delivers those practices
without leaking backstage cosmology. The repair may change existing owners and
may add one situated Public source only if the council finds that route earned
by adopted evidence. It may not invent a god, cult, universal school, factual
transcript, or Schism answer.

## Authority and provenance

- Seed witnesses are immutable evidence, not a prose owner.
- `Projections, God Beasts, and Thaumavores.md` owns author mechanics and divine
  cognitive limits.
- Covenant, allocation, and mortuary notes own their distinct records,
  training, actions, and remedies.
- `The Three Ledgers Schism.md` alone owns event facts; its cult, god, place,
  date, and manifestations remain unresolved.
- Public owns presentation only. The phrase “cult schools call this the limit
  of delegated attention” currently has no hidden inhabitant owner and is an
  explicit fault to test, not a license to create a school.

## Protected strengths

- Gods are real, powerful, and capable of consequential testimony.
- Divine claims can transform an investigation without ending it.
- Covenant, allocation, and mortuary authority remain separate.
- The Schism has competing coalitions, partial settlement, incomplete repair,
  and no clean moral side.
- Public remains familiar high fantasy; no author taxonomy, signature, sequence,
  or metaphysical solution crosses the boundary.
- Existing Low Sere, Basin Table, Broken Sluice, and Relics prose are outside
  the repair surface.

## Ghostlight lens

- **Contact:** a covenant, petition, failed miracle, death inventory, disputed
  manifestation, or surviving Schism copy.
- **Evidence:** terms and entry origin; petitions and material inputs; grants,
  failures, beneficiary order, leakage reports; bodily death and last
  statements; duplicate or conflicting copies.
- **Interventions:** protect or compare a record, witness a claim, carry an
  appeal, restore material relief, resist merged custody, or accept bounded
  emergency action.
- **Consequences:** welfare access, burial, office, succession, severance,
  record custody, material restitution, and physical protection. No action
  establishes a god's author-level intention.
