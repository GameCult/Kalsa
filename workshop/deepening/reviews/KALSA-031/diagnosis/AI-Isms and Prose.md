# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** `Gods and the Dead` contains vivid Kalsan images, but its load-bearing explanation is spoken by a smooth, anonymous institutional narrator. The most serious sentence invents a balanced pair of “cult schools” around the phrase `limit of delegated attention`; the packet and hashed owners establish no such inhabitant owner. Around that fault, correction theatre, viewpoint chorus, and completeness lists make gods, cults, and mortuary traditions sound processed into one prudent essay voice.

The hidden owner notes legitimately use inventories, jurisdiction maps, and repeated authority limits. Those are functional author tools. The Public prose fault occurs where that register is summarized as cultural speech without a speaker, object, or event to own it.

## Protected successes

- Preserve the opening image that gods may “die badly enough to poison a district for generations.” It establishes danger and scale without leaking the backstage taxonomy.
- Preserve the concrete contrast of a cult with a magnificent shrine but no stores. It turns institutional weakness into an object a reader can see.
- Preserve the dead imagery: names in sealed rooms, gestures in smoke or ash, and a burial ground returning memory, appetite, warning, or accusation.
- `At the Basin Table` is a useful counterexample to the diagnosed voice. The drain key, name cloth, copied warnings, wages, and knock carry authority and uncertainty through action. Its closing record of the knock is not a checksum to delete; it is the basin table performing its actual function.
- `Four Readings of a Broken Sluice` uses parallel argument deliberately as Ju'onai teaching rhetoric. Its repetitions are culture-specific and speaker-contested, not generic narrator smoothing.

## Findings

### AIP31-01

- **Finding ID:** AIP31-01
- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/World/Gods and the Dead.md`, opening paragraph beginning `Covenant ledgers, prophetic records...`, especially `Some cult schools call this the limit of delegated attention; others call the apparent failure a human inability to recognize divine purpose.`
- **Evidence:** The baseline packet explicitly identifies `limit of delegated attention` as lacking a hidden inhabitant owner. The hashed foundation explains divine limits through available relations, surviving memory, intervention style, rivals, evidence, doctrine, appetite, and political need. None of the relevant owners names this phrase, a school that teaches it, or the polished opposing school supplied by Public.
- **Effect:** Generic attribution impersonates cultural depth. The symmetrical `some schools / others` construction makes an author summary sound like established in-world discourse and gives both sides the same explanatory rhythm. The reader is asked to trust a neat debate that no institution presently owns.
- **Repair direction:** Remove the unsupported schools and carry the necessary distinction through owner-grounded evidence: a named or explicitly bounded record tradition, witness practice, surviving copy, or situated dispute. Retain the questions of what the god witnessed, received through servants, and merely asserted, but do not coin a cultural doctrine to package them.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not leak `God Beast`, Projection, binding topology, style taxonomy, or the author explanation for divine cognition. Do not reduce gods to unreliable narrators whose testimony is merely decorative; their claims must remain consequential evidence.
- **Dependency:** `Projections, God Beasts, and Thaumavores` owns the cognitive limits; covenant, allocation, mortuary, and Schism owners constrain any Public carrier.
- **Confidence:** High; the packet names the fault and exact-source search across all relevant owners confirms the attribution has no owner.

### AIP31-02

- **Finding ID:** AIP31-02
- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/World/Gods and the Dead.md`, page entrance and `## Covenant and submission`
- **Evidence:** The page stacks corrective frames: gods are `not metaphors`; no tradition has established omniscience; two rites can look alike while preserving different histories; a ledger can establish one thing but cannot prove another; cults are material `as well as` sacred. The same narrator habit recurs in `Magic and Miracles` (`Visible effect is not complete explanation`) and `Relics and Ruins` (`Discovery makes these claims visible. It does not erase them`; rites are `not empty theatre`; a town can need judgment and `still refuse` ownership).
- **Effect:** Nuance arrives as repeated anticipatory correction. Unrelated subjects sound written by one tireless institutional essayist rebutting an imagined careless reader before the world has spoken. The distinctions are sound; the staged cadence makes them feel processed.
- **Repair direction:** On the KALSA-031 repair surface, let one concrete divine claim, record conflict, or failed intervention prove several distinctions at once. Keep only the antitheses that still do work after the evidence is present, and vary the rhythm rather than replacing every sentence with a new formula.
- **Collateral risk:** The prose still must establish that gods are real, power is not omniscience, covenant origins differ, and records have limited evidentiary authority. The opening antithesis is vivid enough to survive if surrounding correction theatre is reduced.
- **Dependency:** Public presentation; any situated carrier depends on the relevant institutional owner.
- **Confidence:** High; the cluster recurs across Public survey narration, while the source pages demonstrate that the same nuance can be carried without it.

## Pattern or dependency map

- The hidden owners need inventories and repeated authority limits because they map distinct jurisdictions under uncertain metaphysics.
- Public compresses those maps into balanced lists, paired corrections, and generalized schools.
- The compression erases provenance while preserving workshop rhythm, producing the institutional-essayist voice.
- Low Sere and the two Public sources show the viable alternative: keys, cloth, ledgers, grain, mud, and named refusals make the same boundaries legible through action.
- Therefore the prose repair should translate owner functions into a bounded carrier, then cut the summary clauses made redundant by that carrier.

## Tempting edit to resist

- Do not run a vocabulary purge against `claim`, `record`, `authority`, `bounded`, or antithesis. Covenant and mortuary records are real setting objects; `bounded system`, `opening`, `aftermark`, and the repeated frames in the Ju'onai teaching dialogue belong to a specific learned register. Diagnose unattributed diffusion, not the tokens. Also leave the hidden owner inventories and authority maps intact.

## Ranked actions

1. **Essential:** Remove or ground the ownerless `cult schools / delegated attention` attribution while preserving consequential, non-omniscient divine testimony.
2. **High-value:** Use one asymmetrical situated carrier to replace stacked correction theatre, viewpoint chorus, and the Public cult-supply checklist; keep the specific distinctions and material consequences.
3. **Optional:** After that carrier exists, remove any checksum conclusion it has made redundant. Stop before flattening culture-specific technical rhetoric or useful mystery.
