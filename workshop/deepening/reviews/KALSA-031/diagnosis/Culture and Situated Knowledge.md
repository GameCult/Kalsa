# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** The hidden candidate contains several strong situated divine
knowledge traditions, but Public does not use them consistently. Its opening
invents anonymous “cult schools” to carry a translated version of the author
model, then treats priests, prophets, champions, and enemies as if they learned
one shared critical method. Its hungry-place section likewise balances several
interpretations without owners, transmission, evidence habits, or incompatible
interests. The smallest coherent repair is to attribute these explanations to
existing covenant, allocation, mortuary, household, and sect carriers. It does
not require an invented god, cult, school, or universal synthesis.

## Protected successes

- `Gods and the Dead` keeps gods real and consequential while denying that age,
  power, or worship makes them omniscient. It asks readers to treat divine
  speech as important evidence rather than converting uncertainty into
  disbelief or authorial dismissal.
- `Gods and the Dead#The unquiet dead` already demonstrates useful situated
  plurality. Kin, an ancestral household, a release sect, a ruler, and a
  mortuary witness perform incompatible acts around the same body. The local
  division of custody is presented as a safeguard with a place and limit, not a
  universal Kalsan code.
- `Soiru'i Sects` is the packet's strongest cultural knowledge body. High-
  branch, Hearth, unbound, and release traditions recruit differently, keep
  different records and stores, remember the same crises for different
  reasons, provide real welfare and sanctuary, and reproduce characteristic
  forms of domination as well as resistance.
- `The Three Ledgers Schism#Path dependence` preserves social memory without a
  clean moral victor. High-branch accounts remember defended continuity;
  Hearth accounts remember stores; unbound accounts remember carried copies;
  release traditions remember living exits. Each memory travels through a
  body that still has reason and capacity to preserve it.

## Findings

### CSK31-01

- **Finding ID:** CSK31-01
- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/World/Gods and the Dead.md`, opening paragraphs,
  especially “Some cult schools call this the limit of delegated attention;
  others call the apparent failure a human inability to recognize divine
  purpose.”
- **Evidence:** The packet identifies “delegated attention” as an explicit
  unowned fault. No canonical inhabitant tradition uses that term or owns the
  two-school division. Hidden author mechanics describe gods perceiving through
  relations, memories, manifestations, conflicts, and political need. Existing
  inhabitants instead have covenant witnesses, clergy, potential stewards,
  mortuary workers, households, and sects with narrower evidence and interests.
- **Effect:** An author mechanism returns wearing a cultural synonym. The two
  anonymous schools have no culture, teachers, apprentices, rites, archives,
  material patrons, ordinary adherents, rivals, inherited memory, or
  characteristic failure beyond being the two sides of an explanatory
  paragraph. Public therefore presents a polished cosmological debate that no
  inhabitant body can actually reproduce.
- **Repair direction:** Remove the invented doctrine division or adopt the
  comparison through existing role owners. Let covenant, allocation,
  mortuary, household, and sect practices state what their records can support
  and why their position remains useful to them. If translated vocabulary is
  retained, its owner, teaching path, domain, and blind spot must live in canon
  first. Do not create a school merely to preserve “delegated attention.”
- **Collateral risk:** The repair could leak the complete God-Beast hierarchy,
  invent a named cult or continent-wide theology, or make every religious
  tradition converge on the same approved skepticism.
- **Dependency:** Public epistemic ownership and backstage/public boundary;
  `Projections, God Beasts, and Thaumavores` remains author truth, not a clergy
  curriculum.
- **Confidence:** High. The fault is explicit in the packet and the terminology
  has no hidden inhabitant owner.

### CSK31-02

- **Finding ID:** CSK31-02
- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/World/Gods and the Dead.md`, opening source list
  through “Priests, prophets, champions, and enemies still learn to ask what a
  god actually witnessed...”
- **Evidence:** Public lists covenant ledgers, prophetic records, hostile
  chronicles, and priestly commentaries, but no culture, institution, text,
  witness, or named synthesis owns the comparison among them. It then assigns
  the same learned question to actors whose reproduction paths and incentives
  differ sharply. Hidden canon already provides those paths: clergy learn
  doctrine and manifestation limits; potential stewards apprentice through
  petitions, inputs, outcomes, and confident refusal; mortuary workers learn
  bodily evidence and observation/interpretation boundaries; sect memory
  keepers compare biographies, submission records, household testimony, and
  adverse accounts.
- **Effect:** Distinct traditions all speak in one polished institutional
  dialect. Their evidence becomes an omnibus narrator's bibliography rather
  than knowledge made and transmitted by people who need different things from
  a god. The useful domains and blind spots disappear: a covenant witness can
  test entry origin but not miracle delivery; a steward can compare promises
  and outcomes but not divine intention; a mortuary witness can preserve a
  manifestation without deciding identity; a sect can preserve erased memory
  while benefiting from the interpretation it carries.
- **Repair direction:** Attribute the comparison to a bounded carrier or stage
  it as explicit comparison among existing carriers. Preserve their different
  teaching routes, evidence, uses, and self-interest rather than giving them
  one method. An in-place Public repair is sufficient if it makes those owners
  legible. A new Public source is earned only if it is clearly a situated later
  teaching or dispute grounded in adopted institutional memory, not an
  omniscient transcript.
- **Collateral risk:** A source-shaped repair could quietly invent a factual
  Schism dialogue, universal circulation, or a neutral scholar who owns every
  archive. Over-attribution could also turn a concise overview into a roster.
- **Dependency:** Reader presentation and institutional handoff; the Schism may
  supply adopted pressure but its unnamed participants and manifestations must
  remain unresolved.
- **Confidence:** High. The reproduction paths are explicit in hidden owners
  and absent from the Public synthesis.

### CSK31-03

- **Finding ID:** CSK31-03
- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/World/Gods and the Dead.md#Hungry holy places`,
  from “Cults disagree over whether these are hungry dead...” through
  “Desperate ones call the danger proof that the miracle matters.”
- **Evidence:** The paragraph presents hungry dead, rival gods, broken
  covenants, and combined danger as balanced alternatives, then divides
  unnamed practitioner lineages from unnamed desperate practitioners. Hidden
  canon contains stronger situated owners: potential stewards infer leakage
  from failed grants and route behavior; containment wardens control dangerous
  access but risk permanent custody; household witness networks preserve
  omitted dead but can erase dependants; cult continuity offices preserve
  welfare while pricing burial in obedience; release and Soiru'i traditions
  carry incompatible memories, stores, routes, and ritual aims.
- **Effect:** The disagreement has no reproduction path or stakes beyond
  abstract caution versus desperate faith. Readers cannot tell what evidence
  each interpretation notices, what action it enables, whose survival makes it
  attractive, or what its characteristic blindness costs. This is exactly the
  balanced-paragraph form of cultural flattening.
- **Repair direction:** Replace the anonymous balance with two or more existing
  role- or tradition-owned readings tied to evidence and consequence. Keep the
  metaphysical answer unresolved. The page needs enough attribution to show why
  a steward, mortuary network, continuity cult, household, or release
  tradition would preserve its account, not a complete taxonomy of sects.
- **Collateral risk:** Treating the Jamnai Soiru'i taxonomy as a continent-wide
  religious map would universalize one culture. Conversely, presenting every
  containment practice as elite fraud would erase real danger and the people
  who depend upon protected sites.
- **Dependency:** Material-life review may test the cost of dispersal and
  closure; this finding concerns the owner, transmission, evidence, and blind
  spot of the explanation.
- **Confidence:** High. The Public paragraph is anonymous while several exact
  bounded carriers are already adopted in the supplied owners.

## Pattern or dependency map

The live situated knowledge bodies can be kept distinct:

| Carrier | Reproduction | Useful evidence/domain | Characteristic blind spot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Covenant witnesses and clergy | clerkship, household advocacy, translation, temple schooling, rite, copied terms | entry origin, promised obligations, local doctrine, intermediary named in the record | cannot prove binding, divine acceptance, or later delivery |
| Potential stewards and auditors | supervised petition records, shortage work, failed grants, route observation, adverse outcomes | commitments, material inputs, beneficiary order, leakage and delivery patterns | cannot see the reserve directly or turn failure into divine intention |
| Mortuary witnesses and household networks | burial work, apprenticeship, kin testimony, duplicate inventories, sanctuary practice | bodily death, last statements, custody, observed manifestation, omitted persons | cannot settle continuing identity; kin and custody interests can erase rivals |
| Soiru'i sect traditions | shrine ritual, biography, household and battlefield testimony, stores, championing, roads and sanctuary | remembered submission/refusal, manifestation reports, mutual aid, living consequences of dead authority | may put living command into a dead mouth or bind descendants to inherited memory |

Public currently collapses the first three rows into “cult schools” and a
shared method, then renders the hungry-place conflict as unattributed belief.
Repair should reveal selected differences without publishing the backstage
answer or pretending this table is one Kalsan curriculum.

## Tempting edit to resist

- Do not invent a universal School of Divine Witness, a neutral comparative
  priesthood, or a named god and cult to carry one useful paragraph. That would
  centralize knowledge the setting has already made productively plural.
- Do not turn the Three Ledgers Schism into a factual Public transcript. Its
  cult, place, speakers, manifestations, and surviving copies remain
  unresolved. Later institutional memories may be situated; an omniscient scene
  may not be smuggled in as pedagogy.
- Do not make every witness sound like the same modern procedural skeptic.
  Protection, reverence, inherited obligation, hunger, memory, rank, and fear
  are legitimate reasons for people to preserve incompatible accounts.

## Ranked actions

1. Remove or canonically owner-back the anonymous “cult schools” and
   “delegated attention” doctrine.
2. Give the Public divine-testimony comparison an identifiable carrier or an
   explicit comparison among existing carriers, with their reproduction paths,
   useful domains, and blind spots intact.
3. Replace the anonymous hungry-place belief balance with a small number of
   existing situated readings tied to evidence and stakes.
