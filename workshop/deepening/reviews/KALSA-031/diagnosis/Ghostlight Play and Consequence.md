# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

Block. The hidden owners already supply strong claimant collisions, material
leverage, refusals, appeals, records, and incomplete consequences. What they do
not yet own cleanly is the first playable step: how a reported divine statement
is captured with enough provenance that covenant, allocation, and mortuary
authorities can test different claims about it. Public then collapses those
distinct practices into an unowned reference to “cult schools” and advice to
ask good questions. Players can understand that gods are fallible, but cannot
yet see who can preserve the evidence, what each office can decide, or what
changes when a divine claim is believed provisionally rather than rejected.

All 19 Git blob hashes in `KALSA-031-baseline-01` were verified and match.

## Protected successes

- `Kalsa/Spoilers/Events/The Three Ledgers Schism.md` §§ “The three
  coalitions” through “The settlement” gives each claimant concrete leverage:
  shrine and champions, stores and beneficiary rolls, or body, inventory, and
  sanctuary routes. None can replace the others, and the mixed settlement
  preserves that asymmetry.
- The Schism produces durable, non-clean consequences. Distribution resumes
  unevenly; contractual exit costs welfare; deprivation, displacement, and the
  identity of the manifestations remain unrepaired. The setting does not
  reward one morally correct button press.
- `Death and Binding Law.md` §§ “Thaumavores, testimony, and recognition” and
  “Discipline, audit, and appeal” already model the right epistemic posture:
  manifestations matter, corroboration changes action, and even agreeing
  records do not settle metaphysical identity.
- `Kalsa/Public/World/Gods and the Dead.md` protects spectacular divinity and
  uncertainty at once. Gods act, demand, and poison places; disbelief does not
  make them harmless, while power does not make their accounts author truth.

## Findings

### GPC31-01

- **Finding ID:** GPC31-01
- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Spoilers/Institutions/God-Cult Covenants.md` §§ “Offices
  and authority,” “Records that make a cult governable,” and “Discipline,
  contractual exit, and appeal”; `Kalsa/Spoilers/Institutions/The Potential
  Economy.md` §§ “No common measure” and “Audit, discipline, and appeal”;
  compare `Kalsa/Spoilers/Institutions/Death and Binding Law.md` §§ “The death
  inventory” and “Thaumavores, testimony, and recognition.”
- **Evidence:** Covenant practice records human entry claims, obligations, and
  adverse manifestations. Allocation practice records petitions, inputs,
  commitments, outcomes, and leakage. Mortuary practice distinguishes observed
  manifestation from interpretation. None clearly owns the initial record of
  a god's reported testimony: the observer or intermediary, exact words or
  effect, conditions of contact, competing observations, transmission chain,
  claimant interest, and action taken in reliance upon it.
- **Effect:** At the table, “the god said” arrives as GM knowledge or clerical
  assertion. Players can inspect downstream books but cannot discover where a
  revelation changed hands, protect the first receipt, or expose an
  interpretation that became a command. The three institutions are distinct
  after intake but blur at the point where divine evidence enters play.
- **Repair direction:** Give the relevant existing owners a small, linked
  witness-practice boundary. Preserve observation, reported wording or effect,
  channel/intermediary, circumstances, interpretation, and resulting act as
  separable claims. Covenant authority should decide the lower-world terms and
  origin implicated; allocation authority should test promised intervention
  against material inputs, grants, failures, and beneficiary order; mortuary
  authority should test identity and post-mortem claims against body, last
  statements, manifestations, and duplicate records. No office should certify
  author-level intention.
- **Collateral risk:** A universal evidence code would flatten local cults and
  make revelation feel like paperwork with incense on it. Preserve direct,
  frightening divine contact and institutional variation; add provenance and
  bounded authority, not a machine for disproving gods.
- **Dependency:** Institutional ownership and provenance; the three owner notes
  must agree on handoff without merging custody.
- **Confidence:** High. The downstream procedures are explicit, while the
  intake chain is absent across the named owners.

### GPC31-02

- **Finding ID:** GPC31-02
- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/World/Gods and the Dead.md`, opening discussion of
  divine knowledge immediately before § “Covenant and submission,” especially
  “Some cult schools call this the limit of delegated attention” and “still
  learn to ask”; current navigation from `Kalsa/Public/index.md` § “Range
  outward.”
- **Evidence:** Public names covenant ledgers, prophetic records, hostile
  chronicles, and priestly commentaries, then offers three useful questions.
  The packet establishes that “cult schools” has no hidden inhabitant owner.
  The page does not show which existing actor can ask those questions, what
  evidence they can carry, what provisional action follows, or what material
  consequence survives correction. Its only play-bearing link is Low Sere,
  whose Basin Table records an ambiguous knock but does not deliver the
  covenant/allocation/mortuary distinction under review.
- **Effect:** The reader receives correct doctrine but no honest entry route.
  Divine fallibility becomes encyclopedia texture rather than a pressure the
  party can notice, misunderstand, resist, or leave altered. The unowned school
  phrase also makes the apparent route impossible to follow.
- **Repair direction:** Replace the unowned school claim with concise,
  inhabitant-owned practices drawn from the repaired hidden boundaries. The
  packet has also earned one situated Public carrier from the adopted Three
  Ledgers pressure: a bounded teaching case, disputed record comparison, or
  other explicitly situated account that lets covenant, allocation, and
  mortuary evidence produce different provisional actions and consequences.
  It need not name the unresolved cult or reproduce a factual transcript.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not expose God Beast taxonomy, assign a correct
  manifestation, invent a universal school, or turn the source into three
  quest hooks. One complete dispute with several defensible courses is more
  useful than an action menu.
- **Dependency:** Public voice and spoiler safety; any source must remain a
  situated presentation of adopted owner facts.
- **Confidence:** High. The packet names the ownership fault, and the current
  Public page supplies no equivalent carrier.

### GPC31-03

- **Finding ID:** GPC31-03
- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Spoilers/Events/The Three Ledgers Schism.md` §§ “The
  initiating crisis,” “Escalation,” “The settlement,” and “Dungeon and campaign
  consequences.”
- **Evidence:** The event contains a complete historical play path, but its
  presentation never condenses that path into the evidence a GM or player can
  follow. The final section offers several possible expedition premises while
  all surviving copies, sites, offices, and successor traditions remain
  unresolved.
- **Effect:** The event is excellent campaign pressure but currently requires
  the GM to invent both the present contact and the evidentiary spine. Its
  strongest consequence risks being read as a menu of hooks rather than one
  causal case that teaches how choices propagate.
- **Repair direction:** In the owner or the earned situated Public carrier,
  preserve one trace explicitly: contradictory manifestations and failed rites
  -> protection or comparison of separated records -> seizure, cordon, or
  suspended distribution -> response by the owner of the affected record -> a
  durable ruling, corrected copy, welfare loss, custody limit, or unresolved
  appeal. This need not adopt a surviving site; it must make clear which parts
  are historical evidence and which are present possibilities.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not resolve which manifestation was genuine or adopt
  a current relic merely to create an adventure start. The historical event is
  allowed to remain larger than player attention.
- **Dependency:** GPC31-01 and GPC31-02. A clear witness receipt makes the
  Public carrier playable without additional lore bulk.
- **Confidence:** Medium-high. The causal chain is strongly supported; whether
  it belongs as a compact owner addition or solely in Public presentation is a
  coordinator decision.

## Pattern or dependency map

`divine contact` -> **missing owned capture/provenance step** -> covenant,
allocation, and mortuary records test different consequences -> claimant acts
through archive, store, body, shrine, sanctuary, or champion -> review leaves a
durable receipt without settling divine intention.

Evidence-supported play trace already latent in the Schism: contradictory
manifestations and failing rites -> record keepers refuse a merged succession
register and move copies/body -> champions impose a cordon while stores reroute
or stop -> a mixed hearing preserves separate custody and narrows force -> the
successor keeps public rite, some households exit and lose welfare, and the
dead anchor's wishes remain unknown.

## Tempting edit to resist

- Do not create a universal “divine witness school,” standardized truth test,
  or reliable sign of godhood. That would repair an unowned phrase by building
  the flattening institution it accidentally implied.
- Do not name the Schism's god, cult, place, or true manifestation to make the
  source feel concrete. Concrete evidence and consequences are enough; the
  answer key is neither required nor authorized.

## Ranked actions

1. **Essential:** Assign and connect the divine-testimony capture boundary
   across covenant, allocation, and mortuary owners; then replace Public's
   unowned school-language with those bounded practices and an honest route.
2. **High-value:** Use one Schism-derived situated carrier or compact causal
   trace to show a consequential choice, materially different responses, and a
   durable receipt without inventing present survival.
3. **Optional:** No additional hook lists. Preserve the existing dungeon
   pressures and let later place work adopt a current site only when another
   pass earns it.
