# Regression Report

## Final verdict

Pass. All 20 Git blob hashes in `KALSA-031-repaired-01` match. The repaired
Public surface now exposes who preserves each kind of divine evidence, routes
readers directly into one bounded conflict, and carries that conflict through
refusal, resistance, adjudication, and durable material consequences. It does
so without assigning a correct manifestation, inventing a universal tribunal,
or turning the surrounding lore into an adventure-hook inventory.

## Finding rechecks

### GPC31-01 — Resolved

`Kalsa/Public/World/Gods and the Dead.md` now gives the witness handoff a
visible owner boundary: covenant witnesses preserve the promise and its origin;
miracle stewards preserve petitions, committed stores, beneficiaries, and
delivery; mortuary witnesses preserve bodily death, last statements, and later
answers. Interpretation remains available to clergy, prophets, champions, and
enemies, but no one record inherits authority over the others.

`Kalsa/Public/Sources/What the Three Ledgers Kept.md` then demonstrates the
boundary before institutional action. Witnesses agree on the body while rites
and manifestations conflict; the covenant book, miracle ledger, and death
inventory preserve different evidence; the successor's merged copy becomes an
inspectable transfer of authority rather than “the god said” arriving as GM
truth. The repair correctly stops short of inventing exact divine words the
event owner does not establish.

### GPC31-02 — Resolved

The abstract route is gone. `Kalsa/Public/index.md` links the new source from §
“Read another argument,” and `Gods and the Dead.md` links it immediately after
the role-attributed witness handoff and again at the close. The source provides
actors, resources, a shortage clock, claimant collision, refusal, force, and a
responding mixed hearing.

The source also offers materially different plausible courses without choosing
a moral answer for the reader. Supporting the successor restores defended
continuity but risks hereditary service and merged custody. Supporting the
stewards keeps an existing household minimum alive while new petitioners and
outer settlements bear delay. Protecting mortuary removal preserves the body,
last statements, and severance claims while leaving public succession and
relief unresolved.

### GPC31-03 — Resolved

The new source selects one causal spine rather than reproducing the owner's
menu of possible expeditions:

contradictory rites and manifestations -> successor merges three records ->
stewards refuse certification and route the household minimum -> champions
attempt custody while mortuary witnesses move body and inventory -> mixed
hearing separates offices and narrows the successor's claim -> distribution
resumes unevenly, exits cost welfare, witnesses remain displaced, and divine
preference stays unknown.

The durable receipts are explicit: original covenant origins survive, the
miracle ledger stays with stewards and is audited with stores, and the death
inventory remains under separate custody. Consequences therefore leave the
scene without pretending the records solve metaphysics.

## Protected successes retained

- Gods remain spectacular, dangerous actors whose testimony can transform an
  investigation without ending it.
- The Schism retains asymmetric leverage: shrine and champions, distributed
  relief and stores, or body, inventory, and sanctuary routes.
- No faction is the clean player solution. Each preserves something necessary
  and imposes costs another constituency bears.
- The cult, god, place, date, true manifestation, and divine preference remain
  unresolved; Public contains no backstage taxonomy.
- `Gods and the Dead.md` § “Hungry holy places” adds one bounded care pressure:
  interruption can protect a miracle route while removing healing and burial
  from households unable to travel, who can demand reopened petitions, failure
  copies, or alternate aid. This is consequence, not a quota of hooks.
