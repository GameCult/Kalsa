## Pattern or dependency map

The repaired path is coherent:

`divine act, claim, or manifestation`
→ covenant witness records origin and terms
→ miracle steward records petition, material commitment, beneficiary, and
observed delivery
→ mortuary witness records body, last statements, and manifestation conditions
→ clergy, prophet, champion, enemy, household, or sect offers an attributed
interpretation
→ the office competent over covenant, allocation, body, site, force, or public
danger takes only its bounded action
→ copies, refusal, petition, audit, appeal, and mixed hearing preserve review.

`What the Three Ledgers Kept` proves the chain under succession pressure. The
hungry-place section proves its smaller operational form under site pressure.
Neither path grants the records author-level knowledge or combines their
custodies.
