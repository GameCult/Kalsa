# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Pass.** `MLE31-01` is resolved in the frozen repaired candidate. Public now
completes the material chain it previously cut short: repeated divine work
creates a dangerous concentration; situated observers preserve different
evidence; shrine networks interrupt or disperse work; healing and burial move
out of reach for households unable to travel; and those households have
bounded demands for reopened petitions, copied failure records, or alternate
aid. The new Three Ledgers reconstruction also makes divine testimony depend on
harvest, transport, store labor, medicine, carriers, guards, credit,
beneficiary order, and uneven recovery. No new ecological mechanism, universal
cleanup body, or automatic scarcity tax has been introduced.

## Pattern or dependency map

Two repaired chains now meet cleanly.

**Hungry-place chain:**

repeated miracles near old death
→ shrine, mortuary, and steward observations
→ a local shrine network interrupts or disperses work
→ the wider route gains protection
→ travel-limited households lose nearby healing and burial
→ petitions reopen, failure records travel, or aid is moved away from the site.

**Three Ledgers chain:**

failed transport and poor harvest
→ pressure on sanctuary, miracle reserve, stores, carriers, and healers
→ a merged succession register attempts to redirect obligations and assets
→ stewards and store workers preserve the existing household minimum while new
work waits
→ outer settlements, displaced witnesses, and exiting households bear
different losses
→ a mixed hearing restores distribution unevenly without restoring every
person, service, or relationship.

These are specific mechanisms with choices and recovery paths. Neither implies
that divine abundance is false or that scarcity must accompany every working.

## Tempting edit to resist

- Do not add casualties, depleted villages, toxic residue, or a mundane price
  to every divine act. The repaired chain is about repeated concentration near
  dangerous dead and a historically stressed succession, not a universal
  misery tariff on miracles.
- Do not add a standardized leakage-cleanup order, contaminated-zone map, or
  common funeral logistics code. Local cult, mortuary, household, site, and
  polity bodies already own those variations.
