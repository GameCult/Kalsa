# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** Jamnai's two-paragraph Public entry is almost entirely explanatory
machinery: one definition cascade, one symmetrical moral ledger, one function
inventory, and one polished binary. Each sentence does useful work, but their
cluster produces the same tireless institutional essayist the council exists
to catch.

## Protected successes

- `coldward storm country`, heat from a `vast undercity`, and Soiru'i dead give
  the passage a peculiar physical and religious silhouette.
- The prose correctly refuses to equate every dependency with one magical
  relation and preserves genuine protection alongside coercion.

## Findings

### AIP35-01 — The definition cascade impersonates inhabited knowledge

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Peoples and Polities.md`: `submission, but that word covers
  several different relations:` followed by four abstract categories.
- **Evidence:** No speaker, oath, order, record, or consequence carries the
  distinction; the hidden event already contains all of those.
- **Effect:** The sentence reads like an author ontology with friendly wording.
- **Repair direction:** Delete the crosswalk and make the distinction legible
  through the Ash-Valve order, ward relation, steward's office, and appeal.
- **Collateral risk:** The distinction itself is necessary and must survive in
  action rather than disappear.
- **Dependency:** Institutional Causality.
- **Confidence:** High.

### AIP35-02 — Rank receives a polished virtue-and-invoice couplet

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Rank can mobilize defence... It can also turn every dependency
  into obedience.`
- **Evidence:** The paired `can` frames distribute one benefit and one harm at
  identical abstraction and weight.
- **Effect:** The narrator performs fairness before the reader has met a person,
  valve, household, or order. Nuance becomes cadence.
- **Repair direction:** Delete the couplet. Let the dangerous diversion, later
  removal, ward heat, and levy refuse a neat moral balance.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not replace it with a `not X but Y` aphorism.
- **Dependency:** History and Material Life.
- **Confidence:** High.

### AIP35-03 — The sect inventory and final binary manufacture completeness

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Soiru'i sects preserve... feed... shelter... sponsor... and
  quarrel over whether... strengthens... or places a limit...`.
- **Evidence:** Five functions and two ideological poles occupy one sentence;
  none has a scene, object, cadence, or cost.
- **Effect:** A vivid oddity becomes a checklist and closes on a seminar-ready
  dialectic.
- **Repair direction:** Keep only enough overview bearing to invite the source;
  let stores, copied record, champion, shrine knocks, and inspection arrive in
  sequence. End the source on an unresolved material claim, not its lesson.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not construct three equally weighted account
  paragraphs. Asymmetrical survival in documents and practice matters.
- **Dependency:** Culture and Reader Experience.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Pattern or dependency map

The Jamnai cluster repeats patterns still visible elsewhere in Public, but the
issue is concentration rather than token presence: explanation -> moral balance
-> exhaustive list -> checksum binary. The Selza'a, divine-witness, and Ju'onai
sources show that Kalsa reads better when records, damaged things, acts, and
residual losses carry the distinction.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not purge every list or antithesis in Public. The Papsenai watches and Sarxe
route inventory serve concrete play and material orientation. This diagnosis is
about Jamnai's complete reliance on the pattern cluster.

## Ranked actions

1. Delete the definition cascade and balanced rank couplet.
2. Break the sect inventory into event-bearing action.
3. Resist a checksum conclusion or symmetrical three-view chorus.
