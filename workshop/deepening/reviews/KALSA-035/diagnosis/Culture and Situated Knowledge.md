# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** Public protects several important distinctions, but delivers them in
the narrator's voice. The Ash-Valve event already supplies branch, Hearth,
tributary, technical, and shrine carriers with different evidence and blind
spots; those should displace the current frictionless taxonomy and sect chorus.

## Protected successes

- Public refuses to equate magical defeat, oath, dependency, and tribute.
- Jamnai's protection is real without becoming consent, and Soiru'i practice
  remains contested rather than metaphysically settled.
- The adopted event preserves disputed testimony and incomplete repair.

## Findings

### CSK35-01 — The four-relations list is an unowned author taxonomy

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Peoples and Polities.md`, first Jamnai paragraph.
- **Evidence:** Hidden owners distinguish the relations, but no Public
  registry, sect, treaty tradition, household, or synthesis owns the list.
- **Effect:** Conflict disappears inside a competent narrator.
- **Repair direction:** Replace the exhaustive crosswalk with concise bearings
  and let event records demonstrate the distinction.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not invent native categories or imply Jamnai shares
  the vault's exact taxonomy.
- **Dependency:** Hierarchy and event owners.
- **Confidence:** High.

### CSK35-02 — Soiru'i sects appear as one frictionless function chorus

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** the closing Jamnai sentence.
- **Evidence:** Hidden canon distributes capacities among divergent traditions;
  this event involved one Hearth-aligned sect.
- **Effect:** Incompatible material bodies become a balanced list.
- **Repair direction:** Identify the Hearth account and contrast its preserved
  evidence with high-branch memory inside the event carrier.
- **Collateral risk:** A four-sect catalogue would repeat the fault at greater
  length.
- **Dependency:** `Soiru'i Sects.md`.
- **Confidence:** High.

### CSK35-03 — Tributaries and dependants are objects, not knowledge carriers

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** entire Public Jamnai section.
- **Evidence:** The event grants tributary families a distinct memory and leaves
  fugitives and redistributed dependants outside the correction.
- **Effect:** Subordinate people become the cost column in the narrator's moral
  ledger.
- **Repair direction:** Preserve the families' account, levy experience,
  flight, and dependant transfer without inventing testimony.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not make tributary memory unanimous or grant it valve
  custody and branch judgment.
- **Dependency:** Event and tribute owners.
- **Confidence:** High.

### CSK35-04 — Divided accounts must not become neutral synthesis

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** prospective Public Ash-Valve source.
- **Evidence:** High-branch, Hearth, and tributary-family memories already exist
  with distinct records, uses, and blind spots.
- **Effect:** A tidy three-view symposium would let the narrator quietly own
  their final meaning.
- **Repair direction:** Frame an incomplete reconstruction through the branch
  order, custody record, store release, copied record, appeal, reported knocks,
  inspection, levy, flight, and dependant transfer. State missing particulars.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not make the accounts symmetrical or decide the
  manifestation.
- **Dependency:** Public presentation only.
- **Confidence:** High.
