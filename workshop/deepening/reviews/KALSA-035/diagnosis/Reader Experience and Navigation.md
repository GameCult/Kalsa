# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** The coldward undercity, several meanings of submission, and dead
warrior sects are strong hooks, but the Jamnai route ends at its overview. A
casual explorer receives a thesis without a door into heat, rank, refusal,
records, or consequence.

## Protected successes

- Jamnai's first sentence gives a clear physical and political silhouette.
- The overview protects plurality in submission and makes Soiru'i practice
  socially consequential rather than decorative necromancy.

## Findings

### REN35-01 — Jamnai has no specific onward route

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Public/World/Peoples and Polities.md`, Jamnai section and Public
  navigation.
- **Evidence:** Magic omits Jamnai; Gods discusses submission generally; the
  divided city presents terjamna only as a claimant. No Jamnai source exists.
- **Effect:** Reader curiosity reaches a painted door.
- **Repair direction:** Add and directly link one situated Jamnai source.
- **Collateral risk:** Preserve omission discipline in Magic; do not add a
  Sorcery slot only for symmetry.
- **Dependency:** Navigation and event owner.
- **Confidence:** High.

### REN35-02 — Heat has no visible chain

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `keep heat moving through crisis`.
- **Evidence:** Public shows no maintainer, route, allocation, failure, or first
  household cut.
- **Effect:** Heat becomes an abstract justification for hierarchy.
- **Repair direction:** Follow one diversion order through the threatened ward,
  steward, valve, stores, review, and later payment.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not supply a technical schematic or invented native
  office names.
- **Dependency:** Material Life.
- **Confidence:** High.

### REN35-03 — Submission is an omniscient taxonomy

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** the four-item submission sentence.
- **Evidence:** No oath, tribute demand, protection bargain, refusal, record, or
  speaker shows what the distinctions do.
- **Effect:** The reader cannot tell what refusal costs or protection owes.
- **Repair direction:** Let the event expose only the relations it actually
  touches; keep the wider hierarchy open.
- **Collateral risk:** One case must not become the whole Jamnai order.
- **Dependency:** Culture and Institutional Causality.
- **Confidence:** High.

### REN35-04 — Soiru'i practice is an institutional list, not an encounter

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** the closing Jamnai sentence.
- **Evidence:** The overview has functions but no shrine, store, household,
  champion, dependant, or refuser.
- **Effect:** Material support and the cost of shelter remain invisible.
- **Repair direction:** Put the source's sect store, copied record, steward's
  family, champion, and disputed shrine testimony on the path.
- **Collateral risk:** Leave the dead's metaphysics and sect-wide political
  meaning unresolved.
- **Dependency:** Culture and Material Life.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Pattern or dependency map

Public overview -> no Jamnai-specific link -> generic cross-setting notes ->
reader loses the live thread.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not answer the undercity's ultimate mechanism, define the full rank tree, or
settle whether loyalty to the Soiru'i strengthens or restrains hierarchy.

## Ranked actions

1. Add a direct Jamnai carrier and route.
2. Let heat and submission become inspectable through one crisis.
3. Keep the larger hierarchy and dead mysterious.
