# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** Public explains Jamnai in the present tense but omits the crisis that
made technical refusal durable and the incompatible memories that keep its
meaning politically live. The institution therefore appears designed whole.

## Protected successes

- Hidden history grows civil hierarchy from Sorcerous conquest, then forces it
  into dependence on inherited coldward infrastructure.
- The Ash-Valve outcome preserves a real reform without mistaking correction
  for equality or complete restitution.

## Findings

### HCC35-01 — Public has no formation crisis for the stop boundary

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Public/World/Peoples and Polities.md`, Jamnai section.
- **Evidence:** The adopted event records a severe storm, mobilization, unsafe
  diversion, refusal, detention, review, holder removal, and later red-line
  practice.
- **Effect:** The limits on rank look like narrator-designed nuance rather than
  an institution scarred into shape by failure.
- **Repair direction:** Publish a declared reconstruction of the Ash-Valve
  Refusal and its later procedural consequence.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not invent a date, campaign, ward, people, or current
  enforcement strength.
- **Dependency:** Event ownership.
- **Confidence:** High.

### HCC35-02 — Jamnai's historical memory has been replaced by consensus prose

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** the overview's `Rank can... It can also...` and closing sect
  dialectic.
- **Evidence:** High branches remember rank correcting rank; Hearth accounts
  remember work, stores, sanctuary, copies, and disputed dead testimony;
  tributary families remember that protection counted them late.
- **Effect:** A stable narrator balance displaces actors who use the same event
  for incompatible present claims.
- **Repair direction:** Give the three memories distinct carriers and concrete
  aftermaths without a representative chorus or final verdict.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not make each memory one symmetrical paragraph or
  give tributary memory automatic metaphysical truth.
- **Dependency:** Culture and AI-Isms.
- **Confidence:** High.

### HCC35-03 — The partial repair has no afterlife in Public

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** entire Public Jamnai route.
- **Evidence:** The ward kept heat but paid much of the levy; fugitives were not
  automatically restored; the removed holder's dependants were redistributed.
- **Effect:** Holder removal risks becoming a clean reform ending.
- **Repair direction:** Carry at least levy, flight, and dependant transfer into
  the source's aftermath and later uses of the precedent.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not invent the full levy, present claimants, or a
  completed restoration movement.
- **Dependency:** Material Life and Ghostlight.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Pattern or dependency map

Sorcerous conquest -> branch hierarchy -> coldward technical dependence ->
tributary expansion -> Ash-Valve protection failure -> bounded stop precedent
and divided memory.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not turn the Refusal into Jamnai's democratic awakening or the Hearth sect
into a timeless opposition. The hierarchy corrected itself and survived.

## Ranked actions

1. Put the formation crisis and procedural scar in Public.
2. Preserve high-branch, Hearth, and tributary uses of the event.
3. End with live unpaid consequences, not institutional redemption.
