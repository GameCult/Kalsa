# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** The hidden Jamnai body keeps branch allocation, technical custody,
sect aid, appeal, testimony, inspection, and challenge distinct. Public reduces
that body to a taxonomy and two balanced abstractions. The reader cannot follow
an order into refusal, support, evidence, judgment, or aftermath.

## Protected successes

- Jamnai's heat-bearing undercity, conquest hierarchy, and Soiru'i sects are
  immediately distinctive.
- The hidden owners distinguish real protection from equal authority and keep
  technical competence outside combat rank.

## Findings

### IC35-01 — Public cannot reproduce the institutional handoff

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Public/World/Peoples and Polities.md`, `## Jamnai and the terjamna`.
- **Evidence:** The Ash-Valve owner records diversion, stop, detention, aid,
  duplicate evidence, appeal, disputed testimony, inspection, challenge, and
  partial repair; none reaches Public.
- **Effect:** Heat and submission remain themes rather than institutions a
  character can encounter, contest, or depend upon.
- **Repair direction:** Add one bounded Public source carrying the adopted
  Ash-Valve sequence and link it from the Jamnai overview.
- **Collateral risk:** A summary of every office would replace one diagram with
  another; keep the sequence attached to objects and decisions.
- **Dependency:** Event owner and all three institutional memories.
- **Confidence:** High.

### IC35-02 — The overview collapses different relations into a narrator list

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** the sentence beginning `Its hierarchy speaks the language of submission`.
- **Evidence:** Hidden canon distinguishes magical defeat, civil oath,
  shielded dependency, and external tribute by evidence and consequence.
- **Effect:** Public states the correct distinction but grants it no inhabitant
  carrier, record, refusal, or cost.
- **Repair direction:** Delete the four-item crosswalk. Let a tributary ward,
  steward, holder, champion, and superior expose the differences they need.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not imply all civil submission is magical or that one
  event exhausts Jamnai's relations.
- **Dependency:** Culture and Situated Knowledge.
- **Confidence:** High.

### IC35-03 — Sects appear to own every counterweight at once

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** the sentence beginning `Soiru'i sects preserve`.
- **Evidence:** The event assigns stores, sanctuary, copying, champion advocacy,
  shrine testimony, technical inspection, and final challenge to different
  actors.
- **Effect:** The sect inventory reads as frictionless benevolent capacity and
  obscures where aid stops and judgment begins.
- **Repair direction:** In the event carrier, place each act at its actual
  handoff and keep the material inspector and superior outside sect authority.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not make the Hearth sect the narrator's resistance
  party or erase high-branch dependence on sect care.
- **Dependency:** Soiru'i Sects and Material Life.
- **Confidence:** High.
