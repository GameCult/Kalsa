# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** Hidden canon contains a strong chain from geothermal flow through
stewarded configuration, allocation, failure risk, aid, inspection, and levy.
Public reduces it to `rank keeps heat moving` and a list of sect benefits.

## Protected successes

- The return-main failure is specific without pretending the vault has a full
  technical schematic.
- Technical custody, political allocation, sect aid, testimony, and challenge
  stay separate.
- The ward keeps heat but bears much of the levy; fugitives and dependants stay
  outside the clean repair.

## Findings

### MLE35-01 — Heat remains scenery

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Peoples and Polities.md`, Jamnai section.
- **Evidence:** Owners establish stewarded geothermal service, an allocation
  choice, return-main risk, operational stop, inspection, and safe continuation.
- **Effect:** Hierarchy appears to generate technical competence.
- **Repair direction:** Carry that event-bounded chain in a linked source.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not invent fuel, network design, inspection method,
  casualties, or named offices.
- **Dependency:** Relic Custody and event owner.
- **Confidence:** High.

### MLE35-02 — Public collapses every material handoff into rank

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Rank can mobilize defence and keep heat moving through crisis`.
- **Evidence:** Holder allocates; steward stops; sect sustains; inspector tests;
  superior removes.
- **Effect:** Labor, competence, evidence, and coercion become one
  benevolent-or-abusive hierarchy.
- **Repair direction:** Keep each act with its role and limit.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not let shrine, champion, sect, or superior substitute
  for inspection; do not grant the steward civic command.
- **Dependency:** Institutional Causality.
- **Confidence:** High.

### MLE35-03 — Aid has no payer, duration, or residual burden

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** Public sect sentence and the event aftermath.
- **Evidence:** The sect supplies the ward, shelters the steward's family, and
  copies the record; the ward later pays much of the levy; fugitives remain
  unrestored; former-holder dependants are redistributed.
- **Effect:** `feed` and `shelter` read as automatic virtue rather than stores
  buying time for appeal.
- **Repair direction:** Show the opened store and family shelter, then preserve
  the levy, flight, and redistribution without specifying missing contents or
  amounts.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not invent rations, levy medium, shelter site,
  destinations, or later conditions.
- **Dependency:** Soiru'i Sects and History.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Pattern or dependency map

`geothermal flow -> stewarded configuration -> branch allocation -> unsafe
diversion -> stop and detention -> stores, shelter, copied record, appeal ->
inspection -> challenge -> heat retained, levy imposed, people unsettled`

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not add fuel scarcity, frost casualties, ration quantities, or engineering
detail merely to make the crisis feel material.

## Ranked actions

1. Put the complete material chain in one bounded source.
2. Preserve acting roles and their limits.
3. Keep aid's residual burdens visible.
