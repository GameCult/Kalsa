# KALSA-035 Jamnai Ash-Valve Knowledge Packet

## Objective and allowed pressure

Diagnose whether Jamnai's submission order is materially causal, culturally
situated, historically formed, playable, and readable in Public. Repair may
change the relevant Public survey and navigation and add one situated Public
source if earned by the adopted event. It may not name unresolved people,
branches, wards, rites, offices, or machinery; prove the shrine knocks; grant
tributary equality; or turn one precedent into a universal constitution.

## Authority and provenance

- Seed witnesses are immutable evidence, not prose owners.
- The hidden polity, hierarchy, sect, and event notes own facts.
- `The Ash-Valve Refusal.md` alone owns the event sequence and omissions.
- Public owns presentation only. Its current four-relations list, rank
  virtue/vice balance, and sect-function chorus are explicit prose and
  epistemic faults to test.

## Protected strengths

- Magical defeat remains a real Sorcerous relation without making every civil
  oath, dependency, or tribute metaphysically identical.
- Jamnai remains an authoritarian conquest hierarchy that also performs real
  protection and depends on noncombat competence.
- Technical custody, political allocation, sect aid, appeal, and shrine
  testimony remain distinct.
- The shrine knocks stay disputed; material inspection independently decides
  the unsafe configuration.
- The holder is removed and the ward retains heat, while levy, flight, and
  dependant redistribution prevent a clean triumph.
- Event particulars and native terms remain unresolved.

## Ghostlight lens

- **Contact:** a valve or bypass, a detained steward, a copied custody record,
  a sect store, a shrine, an appeal in transit, or a tributary levy.
- **Evidence:** branch order, return-main condition, pressure behavior,
  custody record and copy, shrine knocks as testimony, inspection, challenge
  record, levy, flight, and dependant transfers.
- **Interventions:** protect or recover a record, sustain the ward, carry or
  obstruct appeal, escort a reviewer, inspect the bypass, contest the levy,
  find excluded fugitives, or decide what to do with the shrine.
- **Consequences:** ward heat, foundry output, branch office, steward safety,
  sect standing, dependants, fugitives, levy, later stop procedure, and future
  control of a live heat gallery.
