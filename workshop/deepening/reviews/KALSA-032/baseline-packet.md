# KALSA-032 Council Calibration Packet

## Objective and scope

- **Target:** the proposed Kalsa Lore Review Council, its seven remits, and its
  integration into every institutional deepening pass.
- **Process owner:** `workshop/review-council/README.md`.
- **Files in scope:** the council README, shared prompt, seven critic prompts,
  packet/report/synthesis templates, and the deepening README, pass template,
  benchmark, queue, project instructions, and KALSA-032 pass record.
- **Calibration corpus:** `Kalsa/Public/index.md`, all notes under
  `Kalsa/Public/World/`, and `Kalsa/Public/Sources/Four Readings of a Broken
  Sluice.md`.
- **Repair boundary:** this pass may repair only workshop process, project
  instructions, Body map, and project memory. Lore findings become named queue
  pressure for a later bounded pass.

## Authority and provenance

- Immutable seed evidence remains under `seed/original/` and is not a process
  edit target.
- Canonical subject notes retain lore authority. The council diagnoses; the
  pass coordinator adjudicates; Hands repairs only named owners.
- `Kalsa/Public/` owns situated presentation. `Kalsa/Spoilers/` holds author and
  GM truth. Workshop documents cannot settle setting facts.
- The protocol shape is adapted from AetheriaLore's Novella Council. Novel-only
  roles and a dedicated continuity critic are intentionally omitted.
- Canon fidelity, provenance, ownership, and spoiler safety are cross-cutting
  constraints and final verification duties rather than a council seat.

## Protected strengths

- Seven genuinely different lore-quality remits, not seven renamed general
  editors.
- Every critic speaks independently before repair and again after repair.
- A no-finding report is valid; ceremony and manufactured findings are not.
- Critics preserve successes, identify collateral risk, and name an edit to
  resist.
- Critics cannot write canon or decide by vote.
- Reader Experience uses a blind reader-visible packet when Public is in scope.
- The AI-Isms critic diagnoses recurrence and effect without detector scores,
  word bans, authorship claims, or regex tribunals.

## Known concerns

- Distinct specialties can still overlap into seven versions of institutional
  analysis.
- Requiring fourteen reports may become ceremony if packet and output contracts
  are vague.
- The AI-Isms prompt must catch Kalsa's recurring corrective antithesis,
  praise-then-cost ledger, list-clause density, repeated frames, checksum
  conclusions, viewpoint chorus, and workshop dialect without banning useful
  rhetoric or technical vocabulary.
- Lore-quality review must not drift into novel-specific continuity, scene,
  character-arc, or chapter-pacing machinery.
