# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Pass with findings.** The remit is distinct and useful: it tests formation,
path dependence, rival memory, and whether old conflicts still shape present
choices. It protects disputed chronology and does not demand one authoritative
public history. One packet-boundary gap prevents the factual-event ownership
check from being fully exercised.

## Protected successes

- The prompt requires one factual event owner while neighbors retain evidence,
  memory, interpretation, and consequence.
- `Peoples and Polities.md`, under Sarxe and Saxfoldi, carries path dependence
  from conquest and massacre into a mobile political center, distributed
  survival, and continuing mobile/settled conflict.
- `Gods and the Dead.md`, under Covenant and submission, makes visually similar
  rites carry incompatible histories of consent, inheritance, and conquest.
- `Four Readings of a Broken Sluice.md` preserves transmission, alteration, and
  disagreement while keeping the repair dispute alive.

## Findings

### HCC-01

- **Priority:** High-value.
- **Location:** council README Packet Contract; packet template Objective and
  scope and Authority and provenance; KALSA-032 calibration corpus.
- **Evidence:** The critic prompt requires checking that adopted event history
  has one factual owner. The calibration packet supplies Public presentation
  but no canonical event-owner notes for the Sarxe dispersals, divine
  conquests, Crown histories, or Divided City conquest.
- **Effect:** The seat can test public memory and present consequences, but
  cannot verify singular, duplicated, or absent factual ownership.
- **Repair direction:** When event ownership is under review, supply the
  relevant owner and neighboring memory/consequence notes, or explicitly state
  that ownership evidence is unavailable and outside the verdict.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not expose hidden canon to the blind reader, require
  every pass to invent an event note, or turn factual ownership into one public
  interpretation.
- **Dependency:** Process owner, provenance, and spoiler verification.
- **Confidence:** High.

### HCC-02

- **Priority:** High-value, deferred lore pressure.
- **Location:** `Peoples and Polities.md`, Papsenai and Ti'asantatca, The Ju'onai
  Hegemony, and The divided city.
- **Evidence:** These sections establish inherited shield service,
  conquest-backed administration, levy conflict, overlapping imperial power,
  and restricted prophetic knowledge, with little evidence of formation,
  crises, failed reforms, or rival memories.
- **Effect:** The conflicts are playable and material, but some institutions
  arrive as polished present-tense arrangements; their likely directions of
  change are harder to derive.
- **Repair direction:** Queue later bounded owner passes to recover one
  source-supported hinge, failed settlement, inherited compromise, or rival
  memory. Invent no event merely to satisfy the rubric.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not produce an omniscient timeline, symmetrical
  histories, or backstage explanation in compact Public pages.
- **Dependency:** Canonical owners; Culture and Situated Knowledge;
  Institutional Causality.
- **Confidence:** Medium-high; hidden owner notes were outside this packet.

## Pattern or dependency map

- Conquest or massacre -> Sarxe memory -> mobile Saxfoldi -> distributed
  survival and settled/mobile conflict.
- Chosen, inherited, or imposed covenant -> similar rite -> incompatible
  obligations and consent claims.
- Layered occupation and adaptation -> ruin infrastructure -> current water,
  burial, residency, and salvage claims.
- Present conflict without supplied formation -> Hegemony, Divided City, and
  Ti'asantatca -> strong pressure but weaker trajectory.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not reconcile Crown Cities, divine covenants, Sarxe dispersals, or broken
sluice traditions into one public chronology. The useful repair is culturally
owned memory tied to consequence, not an authorial master timeline.

## Ranked actions

1. No essential action.
2. Make event-owner evidence or its explicit absence part of the packet.
3. Defer Public historical pressure to later canonical-owner passes.
