# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Pass with findings. No blocking finding.** The spoiler consent gate is
unmistakable, the public path establishes a distinctive world quickly, and Low
Sere converts the setting into immediate playable pressure. The main weakness
is route hierarchy: the entrance foregrounds broad explanatory pages whose
repeated institutional cautions flatten momentum before stronger place and
source material.

The reader's working picture was a twilight world where settlements survive
through ancient infrastructure, negotiated miracle, inherited expertise, and
overlapping claims. Low Sere, Ti'asantatca's shield workers, the mobile Sarxe
center, and the divided City of Prophets created the strongest desire to follow.

## Protected successes

- `Kalsa/index.md` plainly distinguishes Kalsa for Readers from the Author and
  GM Vault without an accidental spoiler lure.
- `Public/Places/Low Sere.md` provides compact spatial orientation, unequal
  stakes, named actors, and an immediate adventure without resolving the dispute.
- `At the Basin Table.md` is the most memorable page. The drain key, name
  cloth, pale marks, and unexplained knock carry lore through people.
- `Four Readings of a Broken Sluice.md` rewards rereading; the keeper keeps a
  difficult scholarly dispute attached to missing seed and responsibility.

## Findings

### REN-01

- **Priority:** High-value, deferred lore pressure.
- **Location:** `Public/index.md`, Begin with the world as its people meet it.
- **Evidence:** Five broad survey pages precede one concrete place and two
  situated sources; Low Sere appears sixth.
- **Effect:** A top-to-bottom reader encounters abstraction before meeting
  anyone. Attention rises sharply at Low Sere and the Basin Table.
- **Repair direction:** Offer two routes: start with a live problem through Low
  Sere and its source, or survey the world through overview notes.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not demote overview material or imply Low Sere
  represents every culture.
- **Dependency:** Public navigation owner.
- **Confidence:** High.

### REN-02

- **Priority:** High-value, deferred lore pressure.
- **Location:** `Peoples and Polities.md`, especially Papsenai and
  Ti'asantatca, The divided city, and Sarxe and Saxfoldi.
- **Evidence:** Compelling places and conflicts have no reader-visible
  continuation for Ti'asantatca, the divided city, or Saxfoldi.
- **Effect:** Curiosity is raised and stranded; some links are painted doors.
- **Repair direction:** Link existing public material where it exists.
  Otherwise label glimpses and route to the nearest situated material rather
  than manufacture thin pages.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not expose hidden owners or demand link symmetry.
- **Dependency:** Public planning and future place/source owners.
- **Confidence:** High.

### REN-03

- **Priority:** High-value, deferred lore pressure.
- **Location:** recurring endings and framing across Public index, Peoples,
  Magic, Gods, and Relics.
- **Evidence:** Variants of “does not grant authority,” “does not erase claims,”
  “does not establish meaning,” and useful-yet-dangerous institutions recur in
  a uniform explanatory voice.
- **Effect:** The Public surface can sound like one morally pre-digested
  institutional handbook despite denying a universal schoolbook.
- **Repair direction:** Let named texts, offices, disputes, objects, and
  consequences carry more of the ambiguity; remove narrator conclusions where
  evidence already demonstrates them.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not replace careful limits with caricature or erase
  protection against essentializing peoples.
- **Dependency:** AI-Isms and Culture remits.
- **Confidence:** Medium-high.

### REN-04

- **Priority:** Optional, deferred lore pressure.
- **Location:** `Public/World/Kalsa.md`, geography and Roads through twilight.
- **Evidence:** Sunward/coldward is clear, but later destinations are not placed
  relative to one another beyond scattered cues.
- **Effect:** The climatic axis survives in memory; a travel-scale picture does not.
- **Repair direction:** Add a bounded traveller's route or itinerary rather
  than an omniscient atlas.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not settle disputed geography or expose author structure.
- **Dependency:** Geography and Public presentation owners.
- **Confidence:** Medium.

## Pattern or dependency map

`safe root gate -> survey-first Public index -> strong premise plus repeated
abstract cautions -> Low Sere -> highest attention in situated sources`

The Public surface is strongest when an inhabitant, office, object, or disputed
record owns the explanation.

## Tempting edit to resist

- Do not add a glossary; unfamiliar terms accumulate meaning through use.
- Do not explain the Low Sere knock, decide what the dead are, or reveal the
  Crown Cities' final nature.
- Do not build an omniscient atlas or one-page-per-people catalogue.
