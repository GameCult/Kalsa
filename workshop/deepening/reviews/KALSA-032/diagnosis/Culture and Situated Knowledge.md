# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Pass with findings. No blocking finding.** The council explicitly protects
culturally owned disagreement, incomplete but usable theories, opacity, and
non-omniscient public knowledge. Its strongest calibration example—the broken
sluice dialogue—proves that this can work. Two handoff defects could still pull
future passes toward formal institutions and magic-indexed cultures.

## Protected successes

- The critic directly tests whether people exist beyond magic category, polity,
  occupation, assigned psychology, and player arrival. It protects opacity and
  unresolved belief instead of equating incompleteness with failure.
- The Situated Knowledge Benchmark gives incomplete models a rigorous shape:
  evidence, owner, reproduction, useful domain, rival accounts, and
  characteristic blind spot. “A local synonym for an author label scores
  absent” defends against laundering backstage taxonomy.
- `Four Readings of a Broken Sluice.md` situates knowledge in circulating
  client-college copies, trade-speech translation, rival schools,
  administrative separation, and a keeper whose material objection survives
  the schools' explanations.
- `Magic and Miracles.md` distinguishes what traditions can notice from what
  they cannot establish; it grants none the author's final word.

## Findings

### CSK-01

- **Priority:** High-value.
- **Location:** `critics/Culture and Situated Knowledge.md`, paragraph beginning
  “For every public explanation”; calibration examples in `Kalsa.md`, Crown
  Cities and Roads through twilight.
- **Evidence:** The prompt requires every public explanation to have an
  inhabitant owner, then asks for its “teaching body, patronage, instruments or
  rites.” The corpus also contains diffuse knowledge: popular stories combine
  rival Crown accounts; travellers learn warning marks, threshold speech, host
  oaths, and route customs without a single school or patron.
- **Effect:** A literal critic may force household, oral, itinerant, customary,
  or anonymous accumulated knowledge into a formal institution-shaped model.
- **Repair direction:** Trace the actual reproduction path: household
  transmission, apprenticeship, oral repetition, seasonal gathering, market
  circulation, ritual, travel, testimony, or formal teaching. Patronage and
  instruments apply only where the model claims them.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not weaken the requirement for a legible inhabitant
  source or let “common knowledge” become an omniscient author voice.
- **Dependency:** Institutional Causality where a formal school or office exists.
- **Confidence:** High.

### CSK-02

- **Priority:** High-value.
- **Location:** `packet-template.md`, Authority and provenance;
  `pass-template.md`, Institutional model and Review council.
- **Evidence:** The benchmark asks for owner, evidence, reproduction, useful
  domain, rivals, blind spot, and unknowns, but the packet template offers only
  one line for Public inhabitant presentation. The pass record has no durable
  place to carry the tested boundary into adjudication and repair.
- **Effect:** The coordinator may repair a visible gap by completing the theory
  from author knowledge. The good criterion exists, but its handoff is lossy.
- **Repair direction:** For in-scope public explanatory models, record
  inhabitant owner or synthesis, evidence and reproduction path, useful domain,
  characteristic blind spot, and protected unknown. Carry accepted boundaries
  into protected material or the repair brief.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not inventory the whole vault, disclose hidden
  taxonomy, or turn folk observation into schema ceremony.
- **Dependency:** Reader Experience, provenance, and spoiler verification.
- **Confidence:** High.

### CSK-03

- **Priority:** Optional.
- **Location:** `queue.md`, KALSA-024 and KALSA-031; `Peoples and Polities.md`;
  `Magic and Miracles.md`, What different traditions say.
- **Evidence:** Public presentation still introduces most named peoples through
  polity and magical capacity. It contains useful counterevidence, but
  KALSA-031 queues remaining magical traditions while KALSA-024 defers concrete
  social variants until campaign demand.
- **Effect:** Successive passes could deepen magical explanations while leaving
  humor, inheritance, ordinary aspiration, translation, class idiom, and mixed
  lives as supporting texture around magic.
- **Repair direction:** Amend existing queued pressure: each situated-tradition
  pass should test one non-magical relation or practice owned by the same
  people, and KALSA-024 should include mixed and cross-boundary lives when play
  creates pressure.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not invent the unresolved Intuition polity, demand
  symmetrical coverage, or add generic festivals and cuisine.
- **Dependency:** History, Conflict, and Change; Material Life and Ecology.
- **Confidence:** Medium.

## Pattern or dependency map

The weak seam is transmission:

`bounded inhabitant model -> packet summary -> coordinator disposition ->
repair brief`

The packet and pass templates do not yet preserve the model's reproduction path
and protected limit. Queue selection also begins from magical traditions, so
ordinary culture depends on the critic pushing beyond the initial category.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not require every people to receive matching sections for humor, kinship,
ritual, class, food, dissent, and aspiration. That would replace magic-category
flattening with checklist-category flattening. Protect uneven emphasis, diffuse
knowledge, and the broken-sluice dialogue's refusal to provide a final page.

## Ranked actions

1. No essential action.
2. Broaden formal reproduction language and preserve each in-scope model's
   owner, evidence path, useful domain, blind spot, and protected unknown
   through packet and repair handoff.
3. Tie KALSA-031 and KALSA-024 together without demanding symmetrical cultural
   completion.
