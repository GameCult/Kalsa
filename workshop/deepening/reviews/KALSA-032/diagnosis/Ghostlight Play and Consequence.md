# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Pass with findings.** The council creates real Ghostlight pressure: it asks
what characters can perceive and alter, demands durable consequences, and
protects uncertainty and inhabitant agency. Two seams could still turn that
pressure into generic quest menus: packets lack a bounded player-access lens,
while benchmark and prompt repeat a standard suite of intervention verbs.

## Protected successes

- The prompt tests what players can notice, attempt, misunderstand, change,
  lose, carry away, or make worse without author knowledge.
- It protects uncertainty, asymmetric knowledge, unsolved problems, and lives
  that do not begin when players arrive.
- `Relics and Ruins.md` demonstrates thresholds, claimants, irreversible acts,
  incomplete evidence, refusal, rescue obligations, and durable receipts.
- `Four Readings of a Broken Sluice.md` provides a non-quest-shaped case: a
  failed work, partial explanations, missing repair authority, material loss,
  and a keeper who makes knowledge answer to a dry field.

## Findings

### GPC-01

- **Priority:** High-value.
- **Location:** packet template Objective and scope; council README Packet Contract.
- **Evidence:** Packets name target, owner, files, audience, and reader path,
  but not a plausible character-facing entry, accessible evidence, available
  kinds of intervention, or consequence horizon.
- **Effect:** The critic must assume capabilities from author context or produce
  free-floating “players could” hooks.
- **Repair direction:** Add a small Ghostlight lens: plausible point of contact,
  evidence available without backstage truth, and expected consequence horizon.
  Permit an overview to route to a play-bearing owner.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not create adventure briefs, prescribe party
  composition, or disclose GM answers.
- **Dependency:** Spoiler and knowledge boundaries; Reader Experience owns
  navigation, not character capability.
- **Confidence:** High.

### GPC-02

- **Priority:** High-value.
- **Location:** benchmark qualitative dimension 11; Ghostlight critic paragraph
  beginning “Flag passive encyclopedia texture”.
- **Evidence:** The benchmark asks what happens when players help, harm,
  capture, expose, or reform every target. The critic adds helping, exposing,
  stealing, joining, reforming, and destroying.
- **Effect:** Each target may answer the same verb checklist even when its real
  pressure is passage, testimony, maintenance, refusal, rescue, bargaining, or
  accepting irreversible cost.
- **Repair direction:** Test only target-supported interventions. Require one
  consequential choice or misunderstanding, a materially different response to
  another plausible course, and a durable owner or receipt.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not weaken branching consequences or leave
  institutions as untouchable encyclopedia objects.
- **Dependency:** Institutional Causality and this seat's player-visible remit.
- **Confidence:** High.

### GPC-03

- **Priority:** Optional.
- **Location:** Ghostlight critic opening paragraph; report template Findings.
- **Evidence:** The remit names routes, actors, clocks, resources, hazards, and
  bargains, but does not require one tested interaction.
- **Effect:** Reports may inventory missing ingredients without showing where
  play becomes inert.
- **Repair direction:** When evidence permits, trace observable cue -> attempted
  action or refusal -> resistance or misreading -> responding owner -> durable
  consequence or receipt.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not demand a complete encounter, balanced scenario,
  or single solution.
- **Dependency:** Institutional and Material mechanisms.
- **Confidence:** Medium.

## Pattern or dependency map

`orientation -> playable locus -> consequence-bearing interaction`

The council should test that progression rather than demand that every page
perform all three jobs.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not add Adventure Hooks headings, hook quotas, mandatory clocks, or a full
help/harm/join/destroy matrix to every lore note.

## Ranked actions

1. No essential action.
2. Add the bounded play-access lens and use target-earned interventions.
3. Calibrate findings around one evidence-supported play trace.
