# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

Pass with findings. No blocking finding. The remit clearly distinguishes
material mechanism from a generic suffering ledger. One high-value hardening
would prevent inventories of material nouns from masquerading as causal chains.

## Protected successes

- Preserve the explicit rejection of automatic misery ledgers. Requiring a
  mechanism, payer, place, response, and resulting choices directly addresses
  the calibration concern.
- Preserve attention to maintenance, residues, bodies, care work, seasons, and
  uneven access. These distinguish this remit from Institutional Causality.
- Preserve miraculous abundance, spectacle, and delight. Material scrutiny
  should explain this magic's consequences, not force mundane scarcity back
  into every wonder.

## Findings

### MLE-01

- **Priority:** High-value.
- **Location:** `critics/Material Life and Ecology.md`, first and second quoted
  paragraphs.
- **Evidence:** The prompt names strong inputs and rejects abstract misery
  accounting, but it does not say that naming workers, tools, inputs, and costs
  remains an inventory unless their handoffs form a causal chain. The corpus
  contains both noun-rich summaries and compact mechanisms such as incense
  revealing airflow and chants coordinating distant controls.
- **Effect:** A checklist-shaped report could approve a material-cost ledger
  because enough concrete nouns appear, or criticize only cadence while missing
  absent physical handoffs.
- **Repair direction:** Require each substantive finding to trace one evidenced
  chain—source/input -> labor or infrastructure -> allocation/use -> failure,
  residue, or recovery—and identify the exact broken or unavailable link.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not require every overview to carry a supply-chain
  monograph or imply all magic consumes mundane fuel.
- **Dependency:** Institutional Causality for jurisdiction; AI-Isms for cadence.
- **Confidence:** High.

### MLE-02

- **Priority:** Optional.
- **Location:** Material critic prompt as a whole, against a Public-only corpus.
- **Evidence:** Every lore file supplied for calibration is a presentation-only
  Public note; hidden owner notes are intentionally absent.
- **Effect:** A critic could overstate “the setting has no mechanism” when the
  packet establishes only that Public does not show it.
- **Repair direction:** Label a chain absent, outside the packet, delegated
  through a named link, or appropriately compressed for the page's function.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not let “perhaps hidden elsewhere” excuse unsupported
  public claims.
- **Dependency:** Packet design, provenance, and coordinator adjudication.
- **Confidence:** High.
