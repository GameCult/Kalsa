# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** The council's authority boundaries and specialist remit are sound,
but the current protocol does not preserve one immutable institutional body
across either review cycle. It also lacks stable finding identities through
adjudication and regression. Those defects make completion reproducible only by
trust in the coordinator's memory.

## Protected successes

- `workshop/review-council/README.md`, Authority Map through Repair Phase,
  cleanly separates diagnosis, adjudication, repair, and canonical ownership.
  Critics cannot decide lore, and majority agreement remains evidence rather
  than authority.
- `critics/Institutional Causality.md` tests authorization, labor,
  reproduction, enforcement limits, and failed handoffs without demanding a
  universal bureaucracy. The calibration corpus proves the remit is specific:
  `Relics and Ruins.md` separates technical competence from ownership, while
  `Four Readings of a Broken Sluice.md` asks who owns repair after several
  bounded authorities decline it.
- Independent reports, explicit no-finding outcomes, collateral protection,
  and coordinator dispositions give defects a plausible route without turning
  critics into canon writers.

## Findings

### IC-01

- **Priority:** Essential.
- **Location:** `baseline-packet.md`, Objective and scope and Candidate identity.
- **Evidence:** The KALSA-032 pass record is declared in scope, but
  `workshop/deepening/passes/KALSA-032-review-council.md` has no recorded blob
  hash. Its baseline hash is `82ceb03ec6cc95025833d80eccbafb24d8417cf4`;
  every listed candidate hash otherwise matches.
- **Effect:** Critics can unknowingly review different versions of the process
  model and authority map while the packet still claims an immutable shared
  baseline.
- **Repair direction:** Add the pass record to the frozen identity, or remove
  it from candidate scope and supply a frozen excerpt as context.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not create recursive hashing ceremony around the
  packet or later reports.
- **Dependency:** Packet and provenance verification.
- **Confidence:** High; the declared scope and manifest disagree directly.

### IC-02

- **Priority:** Essential.
- **Location:** `workshop/review-council/README.md`, Packet Contract, Regression
  Phase, and Completion Gate.
- **Evidence:** The diagnostic candidate must be frozen, but regression critics
  merely receive “the repaired candidate.” No repaired snapshot identity or
  mutation boundary is required; only Reader Experience receives a newly
  assembled packet.
- **Effect:** Sequential regression reports may inspect different repairs. A
  later change can invalidate earlier regressions while all seven reports still
  satisfy the completion gate.
- **Repair direction:** Freeze one repaired candidate identity before
  regression. Derive critic-specific packets, including the blind reader
  packet, from that snapshot. Any subsequent repair should invalidate the
  affected regressions and begin another bounded cycle.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not leak hidden material into the Reader Experience
  packet or require every critic to receive irrelevant files.
- **Dependency:** Reader Experience packet construction and final verification.
- **Confidence:** High; the first snapshot is explicit and the second is absent.

### IC-03

- **Priority:** High-value.
- **Location:** `report-template.md`, Findings; `synthesis-template.md`, Finding
  dispositions; `README.md`, Regression Phase.
- **Evidence:** Diagnostic findings receive no required identifier. The
  synthesis invents an `ID`, while regression asks in prose which findings were
  resolved.
- **Effect:** Split, merged, modified, and deferred findings can lose identity
  between critic, coordinator, repair brief, and regression. An essential
  defect can appear closed through ambiguous paraphrase.
- **Repair direction:** Require critic-local finding IDs at diagnosis; preserve
  those IDs through disposition, repair instructions, regression status, and
  queue deferral.
- **Collateral risk:** IDs must remain routing keys, not scores or canon authority.
- **Dependency:** Report and synthesis contracts.
- **Confidence:** High; the handoff fields do not share a stable key.

### IC-04

- **Priority:** High-value.
- **Location:** `workshop/review-council/README.md`, Authority Map and Repair
  Phase; `workshop/deepening/pass-template.md` frontmatter and Review council.
- **Evidence:** The coordinator adjudicates every finding and alone integrates
  canonical repairs, but the pass contract records no coordinator identity or
  explicit assumption of that role.
- **Effect:** The decisive office exists, but its occupant and handoffs are
  invisible. A critic or repair agent can silently become adjudicator,
  weakening independence and obscuring who exercised integration authority.
- **Repair direction:** Record the coordinator in the pass and synthesis
  artifacts. Permit one person or agent to hold multiple roles when necessary,
  but require that overlap to be explicit.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not confuse role provenance with a demand for
  managerial hierarchy or distinct human staffing.
- **Dependency:** Pass-record provenance.
- **Confidence:** Medium-high; authority is well described, but role occupancy
  is not.

## Pattern or dependency map

`frozen baseline -> critic finding ID -> coordinator disposition -> bounded
repair -> frozen repaired snapshot -> regression by finding ID -> closeout`

The candidate defines the actors and most authority boundaries well. Its
missing mechanisms occur at identity-bearing handoffs: baseline scope, report
routing, coordinator occupancy, and repaired-state regression.

The Public corpus demonstrates that the Institutional Causality seat can detect
the intended defects. Ti'asantatca names workers, cohorts, observers, tenders,
and supporting households without yet naming command or succession; the sluice
dialogue exposes several competent authorities unable to own repair. Those are
later lore pressures, not findings KALSA-032 may settle.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not empower this critic or the coordinator to invent a universal office that
resolves every failed handoff. `Four Readings of a Broken Sluice` gains force
from separated authority and unresolved responsibility. The council should
route that pressure to the owning lore pass, not answer it from workshop prose.

## Ranked actions

1. Freeze the complete diagnostic candidate and one repaired regression snapshot.
2. Add stable finding IDs and explicit coordinator identity across the report,
   synthesis, pass, and regression contracts.
3. Leave the seven specialist remits and their canon boundary intact; no
   omnibus institutional reviewer is needed.
