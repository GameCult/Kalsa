### GPC40-05 — The ceramic intervention has two material owners

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `The Ash-Halo Court`, `Site card`, `People at the court`
  (Sedren), and `What the party can reach` (source racks).
- **Evidence:** The site card makes Sedren the material owner who can refuse an
  unsafe heat draw. His actor entry says thin ceramic is sufficient for him to
  delay the second horn and that no other qualified custodian is present. The
  intervention text instead says removing the thin ceramic delays the third
  horn “unless another owner accepts the heat risk.” No other material owner is
  identified, and party authority explicitly does not command the forge.
- **Play effect:** A party choosing the best-defined safety intervention cannot
  know whether Sedren's stop holds, Iresa can override it, or an unnamed person
  can accept the danger. That ambiguity is not productive uncertainty about
  the fracture; it is split authority over the action's result.
- **Repair direction:** Give the risk decision to the existing owner. Either
  Sedren alone accepts a replacement configuration, or name the distinct
  authority that can overrule him and the consequence of doing so. Keep Iresa's
  power to remove him afterward separate from qualification to sign the live
  working.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not remove the political struggle around the stop or
  make the party's inspection self-executing. The repair should clarify who
  decides, not guarantee the safe outcome.
- **Confidence:** High.
