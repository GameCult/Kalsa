# Critic Report

## Packet verification

I verified all 16 raw SHA-1 identities in `KALSA-040-baseline-01`; every hash

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** The proposal's central material rule is sound and worth protecting:
Kalsan spectacle is constrained by mechanism rather than by modest scale.
Apparent materialization, sudden armament, radiance, levitation, summoning, and
city-breaking force can all be real when existing matter, energy, preparation,
accessible levers, and potential make the branch reachable.

The proposal then compresses the actual material chain into categories. Matter
comes from “stores or the surrounding environment”; energy comes from nearly
any physical source; prescience arranges precursors years ahead; interception
keeps feeders off the champion; residents bear the remaining cost. No owned
case says what was removed from where, which mechanism supplied energy, who
built and maintained the precursor system, which grants or bodies lost
protection, where the leakage perimeter ran, what residue remained, or who had
to recover the place. The result is a spectacular allowance followed by a
generic aftermath ledger.

A bounded event is essential. It should not exist merely to prove that the
proposal is possible. It must force the miracle through one complete chain:
source -> preparation and labor -> allocation -> transformation or movement ->
feeding and defense -> displaced protection and residue -> recovery. The event
can stay astonishing; its specific abundance and violence need specific
substrates.

## Protected successes

- Preserve “restrictive about mechanism, not visible scale.” The seed explicitly
  allows mountains rent and lakes steamed by linked chains. Material review
  should not turn Kalsa into a setting where every miracle is secretly a pulley
  and a poor harvest.
- Preserve apparent materialization and summoning as honest witness language.
  A champion's raiment or weapon may arrive too quickly for witnesses to see
  gathering, transformation, or transport even though the working keeps a
  material account.
- Preserve the distinction between magical potential and physical matter or
  energy. Potential commits a reachable branch; it does not itself become the
  cloth, metal, lift force, light, fuel, or blast.
- Preserve thaumavore feeding as a real concentration limit. Extended divine
  workings near old death create more than an abstract reserve penalty: they
  can produce manifestations, lost control, unsafe holy geography, and a
  fountain.
- Preserve `The Empty Bridge Vigil` as the packet's best existing material
  model. It names the reserve choice, withdrawn shelter grants, roof seams,
  smoke vents, wet fuel, floor stores, drainage work, injured refugees, and
  separate claims without making the miracle unreal.
- Preserve current Public materiality. `Gods and the Dead` and `Magic and
  Miracles` already say that shrines need stores and workers, dead answer near
  gathered power, and no miracle supplies its own material world.

## Canon boundary

### Strong enough to adopt under current owners

- `Kaos` can adopt apparent materialization and summoning as rapid location,
  gathering, separation, refinement, transformation, movement, assembly,
  revelation, or prepositioned arrival of existing matter using existing
  energy and reachable levers.
- A divine hierarchy can fund such a working with delegated potential while a
  champion or intermediary carries its lower-world act. The delegation does
  not replace material operators, stored inputs, transport, maintenance, or
  recovery.
- Prescient preparation may begin long before witnesses know its purpose. The
  precursor still has builders, carriers, custodians, land use, maintenance,
  spoilage, competing use, and a point at which the later working can fail.
- Concentrated potential creates a feeding opportunity and can force a God or
  steward to disperse, shorten, reroute, defend, or abort a miracle.
- Defending the champion's route can externalize danger. Feeders may find the
  source site, outer route, burial ground, unprotected beneficiaries, or
  downstream structures more accessible than the defended body.
- A large working can leave absences and dangerous residues: exhausted grants,
  failed ancestral protection, newly active manifestations, closed sites,
  damaged mechanisms, displaced stores, and recovery claims. These are
  observable conditions even when the nature of potential remains uncertain.

### Must remain unresolved or event-specific

- A universal conversion rate among potential, matter, energy, distance, speed,
  or visible scale.
- A generic “surrounding environment” from which any matter or energy can be
  taken without an identified extraction site and affected use.
- Whether potential is conserved, stored like a substance, or can literally
  lodge in a body or building rather than sustaining a changed relation there.
- Whether a century of small feedings accumulates as a durable thaumavore body,
  and what material or magical maintenance such persistence requires.
- A universal radius, threshold, or perimeter for thaumavore attraction and
  interception.
- Whether every flash leaves residue, where stripped potential goes, or whether
  the God recovers, disperses, loses, or redirects it.
- The bodily effect of delegated divine concentration on a champion. One case
  may establish heat, injury, exhaustion, sensory damage, protection, or no
  direct harm; the proposal cannot assign one cost to every style.
- A universal divine cleanup duty. Living institutions can make grants,
  closures, compensation, containment, and recovery claims without proving
  that the God recognizes or can be compelled by them.
- “City-killing destruction” as an ownerless capability. It remains possible
  only through an actual susceptible system such as stressed geology, extreme
  weather, fuel, relic infrastructure, stored weapons, or another named chain.

## Findings

### MLE40-01 — Apparent materialization has inputs but no material handoff

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Divine Manifestation and Thaumavore Interception Proposal`,
  **Apparent materialization and summoning**, especially the lists of possible
  matter, energy, and effects.
- **Evidence:** The proposal says raiment, armament, lift, light, summoning, and
  destruction can draw from carried stores or the environment and from
  physical, biological, atmospheric, geological, technological, or other
  accessible sources. `Kaos`, **Hard constraints**, requires actual matter,
  energy, lever, time, and potential. No proposed example selects one source,
  transformation, transport path, material operator, or residue.
- **Effect:** The phrases “surrounding environment” and “other causally
  accessible sources” do the work of ex nihilo creation. Readers receive a
  footnote saying the account exists, but the world contains no emptied store,
  cooled air, broken soil, diverted water, consumed fuel, altered structure,
  waste heat, tool wear, or claimant able to prove where the wonder came from.
- **Repair direction:** Keep the general permission in Kaos, but require the
  KALSA-040 case to name one complete transformation. Identify source matter,
  physical energy, extraction or release mechanism, preparatory work, operating
  custodian, what visibly vanishes or changes, and what remains after the
  spectacle. The event need not explain every microscopic step.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not make the miracle slow, visually modest, or
  reducible to ordinary craft. Kaos supplies impossible selection, timing,
  coordination, and counterfactual reach; the material chain supplies the body
  it acts through.
- **Dependency:** Mechanics and Causal Coherence should test reachability;
  History should keep one case from becoming every God's standard capability.
- **Confidence:** High. The proposal gives categories exactly where the material
  prompt requires one evidenced handoff.

### MLE40-02 — Long prescient preparation erases infrastructure and maintenance

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** Proposal, **Apparent materialization and summoning**, final
  paragraph: precursor conditions arranged hours, years, or generations ahead.
- **Evidence:** A precursor condition lasting generations implies land,
  materials, transport, storage, records, custodians, repair, secrecy, and
  repeated decisions not to consume or repurpose it. `The Potential Economy`
  already establishes granaries, hostels, workshops, routes, burial grounds,
  trusted suppliers, and trained delegates as part of miracle production. The
  proposal assigns all preparation to the god and names none of those hands.
- **Effect:** Preparation becomes free retroactive infrastructure. A God can
  appear to have always placed the right store or mechanism without the
  warehouse leaking, workers changing allegiance, descendants contesting the
  duty, a road failing, material spoiling, or another oracle entering the plan.
- **Repair direction:** In the bounded case, choose the precursor chain and
  give it lower-world continuity. Name who began it, who understood only a
  local purpose, who maintained or guarded it, what ordinary use it displaced,
  how its intended future survived succession, and which maintenance failure
  could still defeat the miracle. If the preparation is accidental rather than
  institutional, name the ordinary history that made the resource available.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not require every worker to know the divine plan. The
  strongest version is a comprehensible local work whose later use exceeds the
  builders' explanation.
- **Dependency:** Institutional Causality owns stewardship and succession;
  Oracle Ecology should test how the long plan remains vulnerable to other
  actors.
- **Confidence:** High. The proposal explicitly claims long duration while
  omitting the machinery that makes duration real.

### MLE40-03 — Feeding and interception lose the potential handoff

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** Proposal, **Thaumavore feeding pressure**, **Divine
  interception**, and **Aftermath and revelation**: accumulated scraps,
  interception, destroyed potential body, and potential lodged in bodies,
  structures, graves, or old routes.
- **Evidence:** The seed establishes that thaumavores steal passing potential
  to manifest and that a God risks a fountain. Current `The Potential Economy`
  calls potential finite routing capacity, not coin, and leaves conservation
  unresolved. The proposal treats it successively as a stored pile, a body, a
  diverted flow, a destroyed asset, and a residue that lodges in matter. It does
  not say whether interception returns capacity to the God, spends it, disperses
  it, transfers it, or leaves a route active elsewhere.
- **Effect:** Neither miracle reserve nor aftermath can be tracked. A steward
  cannot distinguish depleted grant from defended grant, continued leakage,
  dormant feeder, or dangerous new route. “Lodged potential” becomes generic
  magical pollution with no test, site boundary, or recovery act.
- **Repair direction:** Preserve the metaphysical uncertainty and specify
  observable state transitions in one case: expected grant weakens; a
  manifestation acquires lower-world agency; the champion's working flickers or
  changes; the hierarchy retracts or counter-routes; a named site continues to
  answer, heat, move, attract, or drain later grants. Give stewards and mortuary
  workers different records. Do not require a conserved substance account.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not smooth the fountain into bookkeeping. Once control
  fails, multiple claimants and dead identities should be able to make the
  reserve materially and politically ungovernable.
- **Dependency:** Identity review owns what a collapsed manifestation means;
  Oracle Ecology owns the competing acts; Potential Economy owns allocative
  evidence rather than metaphysical substance.
- **Confidence:** High. The proposal's nouns shift among incompatible material
  models while the owner marks the question unresolved.

### MLE40-04 — The defended champion has no leakage perimeter or bodily support

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** Proposal, **Divine interception**, first and final paragraphs;
  **Pity, shame, and divine policy**.
- **Evidence:** The God keeps feeding thaumavores “away from that champion,” may
  protect only the champion, may avoid burial landscapes, and may spend more to
  protect a wider community. No note defines which part of the route is
  defended, where redirected feeders go, what other bodies or sites become
  easier to reach, or what the champion needs before and after carrying the
  concentration.
- **Effect:** “Protection” becomes a binary aura. The miracle has no outer edge,
  no workers stationed along it, no bodies excluded from it, no relocation or
  closure cost, and no choice between a safer champion and a more dangerous
  district. The champion is also a bodiless nozzle rather than a person who
  needs transport, equipment, food, rest, medical attention, witnesses, and a
  way to stop.
- **Repair direction:** In the event, draw the practical perimeter through
  named sites and routes rather than a universal radius. Identify the champion
  station, source and transit points, nearby burial or fountain sites, the
  defended segment, warning posts or wardens, and the settlements or shrines
  outside it. Name champion preparation, an operator who may stop the working,
  and whatever recovery this specific delegation requires.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not assign generic exhaustion or injury merely to
  make the cost visible. The body's exact burden should follow the Gift,
  delegation, protection, and event evidence.
- **Dependency:** Ghostlight Play should test whether parties can move or alter
  the perimeter; God-Cult Covenants owns the champion grant and stop boundary.
- **Confidence:** High on the missing perimeter; medium on the champion's bodily
  cost, which should remain case-specific.

### MLE40-05 — Displaced protection and recovery remain an automatic misery ledger

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** Proposal, **Pity, shame, and divine policy** and **Aftermath and
  revelation**; integration request for containment, displaced harm, recovery,
  and cleanup.
- **Evidence:** Residents bear whatever the God declines to contain; overfed
  thaumavores remain; diverted potential lodges in places; familiar protections
  go silent. The proposal names no prior beneficiary, withdrawn grant, damaged
  store, closed route, shelter capacity, recovery crew, supply need, time
  interval, or office receiving the claim. `The Empty Bridge Vigil` shows the
  required specificity: three shelter grants become wet fuel, displaced stores,
  drainage work, reduced capacity, injured arrivals, and separate ledgers.
- **Effect:** The aftermath sounds morally serious while generating no ordinary
  choices. “Residents” pay in the abstract; institutions can promise cleanup
  without food, shelter, tools, burial access, transport, medicine, replacement
  protection, or a sequence for making the place usable again.
- **Repair direction:** Give the event a before-and-after allocation surface.
  Name the miracle's beneficiaries, reserve, protections suspended to fund or
  defend it, households or dead previously using the site, and the material
  service lost when familiar presences stop answering. Then name recovery work:
  closure or guarded access, evacuation or shelter, repair, recovery of stores
  and tools, mortuary witnessing, alternate minor grants, site monitoring, and
  the claims that remain unpaid. Let institutions dispute who owes each act.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not force every displaced ancestor to have supplied a
  useful service or every miracle to distribute harm symmetrically. Specific
  absences can be grief, lost testimony, failed protection, or danger without
  becoming a balanced ledger of good and bad.
- **Dependency:** Institutional Causality owns grant, cleanup, and appeal
  authority; Culture owns how mortuary and cult institutions value familiar
  dead; History should preserve later path dependence.
- **Confidence:** High. The proposal itself requests these consequences but
  supplies none of their handoffs.

### MLE40-06 — A bounded event is required before institutional integration

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** Proposal, **Request to the maintainer loop**; compare `The Empty
  Bridge Vigil` and the current Public route.
- **Evidence:** The integration request would place general mechanism in two
  foundations and downstream duties in multiple institutions. Without one
  actual case, each owner can repeat a different abstraction: Kaos lists
  sources, Potential Economy lists allocations, Covenants lists policy, Death
  Law lists testimony, and Public lists wonder. No packet note currently owns a
  spectacular materialization or summoning followed by feeder interception.
- **Effect:** The repair can pass link and prose checks while never proving that
  its source, infrastructure, allocation, perimeter, residue, and recovery fit
  together. The world would gain doctrine without a place whose people live
  through it.
- **Repair direction:** Adopt one event or current Ghostlight site as the sole
  factual owner of the complete chain. At minimum it needs: miracle objective;
  source matter and energy; prepared infrastructure and custodian; champion or
  intermediary; potential grant and displaced commitments; exposed burial or
  manifestation site; defended route and leakage perimeter; visible
  intervention; residue; recovery labor; and several unresolved outcomes or
  claims. Institutions inherit only their local procedures.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not build a seven-Gift showcase or a universal Divine
  Works Department. One site should pressure existing cult, potential,
  mortuary, material, and household owners.
- **Dependency:** History owns event propagation; Ghostlight Play owns the live
  decision surface; Reader Experience should receive a later blind Public
  projection rather than the hidden mechanism.
- **Confidence:** High. The missing event is the only reliable falsification of
  the proposed cross-owner chain.

### MLE40-07 — Public needs the event's material evidence, not its backstage answer

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/index.md`; `Kalsa/Public/World/Magic and
  Miracles.md`; `Kalsa/Public/World/Gods and the Dead.md`.
- **Evidence:** Current Public establishes the constraints accurately: divine

  linked source in which spectacular manifestation gathers matter, crosses a
  prepared route, defends one body, and leaves a recovery site. Empty Bridge is

- **Effect:** A reader can be told the rule but cannot inspect the missing
  stores, changed landscape, damaged mechanism, exhausted grant, silenced
  ancestor, or cleanup dispute that would make spectacle belong to ordinary
  life. Publishing the author mechanism would replace this absence with a
  spoiler.
- **Repair direction:** After the hidden event passes review, publish one
  situated source: a material operator's work record, mortuary absence list,
  champion station account, recovery petition, or explicit comparison of named
  records. It should show what appeared, what supplied it, what was lost, and
  what work continues without naming author axes, Projections, God Beasts, or a
  universal thaumavore explanation.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not turn the source into an austere invoice. Preserve
  the impossible arrival, light, sound, fear, worship, and delight alongside
  the material evidence.
- **Dependency:** Reader Experience and Culture should review the resulting
  situated Public owner.
- **Confidence:** High. The present surveys are sound but the phenomenon has no
  explorable Public body.

## Pattern or dependency map

The proposal currently skips the middle and last handoffs:

possible source categories -> divine spectacle -> generic displaced harm.

The repair needs the complete material chain:

named matter and energy -> prepared site, transport, tools, stores, and labor
-> stewarded grant and withdrawn commitments -> champion/intermediary operation
-> transformed or moved matter -> route-connected feeding -> defended segment
and displaced leakage -> observable residue -> shelter, repair, mortuary,
monitoring, and compensation work.

`MLE40-01` and `MLE40-02` establish the inputs and infrastructure. `MLE40-03`
and `MLE40-04` make the working and perimeter observable. `MLE40-05` gives the
aftermath bodies, stores, and labor. `MLE40-06` is the owner that proves those
parts can coexist. `MLE40-07` is its situated reader projection.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not repair materiality by reducing the miracle to a mundane supply chain
with decorative light. The divine working should still perform coordination,
selection, speed, precision, and counterfactual scale no ordinary crew could
achieve. Name the cloth, stone, fuel, fault, route, hands, and residue so the
wonder has a body—not so the body explains the wonder away.
