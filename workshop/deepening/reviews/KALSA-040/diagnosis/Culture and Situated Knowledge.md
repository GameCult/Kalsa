# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** All 16 raw SHA-1 identities match the frozen packet. The proposal
protects the correct reveal gradient in principle and routes its backstage
claims toward existing owners, but its `Aftermath and revelation` still jumps
from generic cult accounts and mortuary evidence to investigators completing
the horrifying answer. The adopted institutions already contain the people,
records, interests, and blind spots needed to discover, conceal, and contest
that relation; the proposal has not yet made them the actual carriers.

The causal-material claims can enter `Kaos`; feeding, interception, and any
surviving Projection belong under the divine/thaumavore foundation; allocation
and displaced harm belong in the potential, covenant, and mortuary owners.
Whether a feeding thaumavore can become a God Beast, and what identity survives
destruction of its accumulated active body, remain unresolved in the packet.
Neither claim may become common religious knowledge merely because the author
model adopts a bounded answer.

## Protected successes

- Preserve `Kalsa/Spoilers/Reference/Divine Manifestation and Thaumavore
  Interception Proposal.md`, **Aftermath and revelation**, where cult glory,
  mortuary disappearance, and momentary faces are different evidentiary layers
  rather than synonyms. Its closing instruction that Public receive only
  inhabitant-owned evidence is sound.
- Preserve `Kalsa/Public/World/Gods and the Dead.md`'s separation of covenant
  book, miracle ledger, and mortuary testimony. It lets clergy, store workers,
  kin, champions, and enemies disagree without allowing a divine claim or a
  matching archive to become author truth.
- Preserve `Kalsa/Public/World/Magic and Miracles.md`'s failure-led common
  knowledge: large miracles remain spectacular, while courts, shrines, and
  mortuary keepers carry different arguments about repeated workings near old
  death.
- Preserve the culturally unequal evidence already owned by `The Potential
  Economy`, `God-Cult Covenants`, and `Death and Binding Law`: registered
  ancestors and omitted war dead do not receive the same hearing; cults can
  destroy adverse manifestation records; household and sanctuary networks can
  keep rival copies; a manifestation remains testimony rather than automatic
  identity proof.

## Findings

### CSK40-01 — “Careful investigators” bypass the institutions that own the evidence

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Spoilers/Reference/Divine Manifestation and Thaumavore
  Interception Proposal.md`, **Aftermath and revelation**, especially the move
  from “mortuary records” and “careful witnesses” to a completed reveal;
  compare `Kalsa/Spoilers/Institutions/The Potential Economy.md`, **Leakage and
  magical fountains** and **Audit, discipline, and appeal**;
  `Kalsa/Spoilers/Institutions/God-Cult Covenants.md`, **Leakage, restraint,
  and holy geography** and **Discipline, contractual exit, and appeal**; and
  `Kalsa/Spoilers/Institutions/Death and Binding Law.md`, **Thaumavores,
  testimony, and recognition** and **Organized mortuary positions**.
- **Evidence:** Potential stewards can compare concentration, failed grants,
  altered voices, and recurrent loss but cannot see the reserve directly.
  Covenant clergy control doctrine, recognized miracle routes, and access to
  adverse manifestation records. Death witnesses, binding clerks, kin, and
  household witness networks can identify names, gestures, prior appearances,
  and later absences but cannot inspect a Projection. Shrine and containment
  wardens control sites and safety. The proposal instead names occupational
  categories without assigning any characteristic fragment of evidence,
  custody contest, or reproduction path to them.
- **Effect:** Discovery becomes an author synthesis performed by whoever is
  curious enough. Concealment becomes a generic cult lie rather than the
  consequence of archive, shrine, welfare, and champion control; contest loses
  the unequal material capacities that make these traditions culturally real.
- **Repair direction:** Distribute the discovery chain through the existing
  owners. Potential and operating records should preserve concentration,
  withdrawal, leakage, and cleanup; mortuary inventories and household copies
  should preserve named presences, familiar signs, and absences; covenant
  offices should carry glory/purification doctrine plus their control of
  access and suppression; shrine or containment workers should carry site
  recurrence and refusal. Name how each record is taught or copied and what its
  holder cannot establish. No one archive should contain the completed answer.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not create a universal inquisition, mortuary academy,
  or cross-cultural radiance bureau. Do not make poor, conquered, or
  unregistered dead appear only as ethical footnotes; their missing names and
  inaccessible copies are part of the evidence structure.
- **Dependency:** Institutional Causality for office authority and record
  handoff; History and Ghostlight for the bounded incident that makes the
  evidence discoverable.
- **Confidence:** High. All three adopted institution notes already expose the
  distinct capacities and blind spots that the proposal currently skips.

### CSK40-02 — Public has no situated carrier for the new radiance dispute

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/World/Gods and the Dead.md`, **Hungry holy
  places**; `Kalsa/Public/World/Magic and Miracles.md`, opening and **Work,
  schools, and testimony**; `Kalsa/Spoilers/Events/The Empty Bridge Vigil.md`,
  **Four preserved records**; compare the proposal's **Divine interception**
  and **Aftermath and revelation**.
- **Evidence:** Public currently knows that repeated miracles near old deaths
  can produce uncommissioned answers and that shrine keepers, mortuary
  witnesses, and stewards disagree. It does not contain an inhabitant-owned
  incident in which a spectacular radiance, a concentrated grant, recognizable
  brief manifestations, and later missing ancestral presences can be compared.
  The Empty Bridge gives a strong four-record model for divine concentration
  and displaced cost, but the frozen event does not establish radiance,
  interception, or vanished dead.
- **Effect:** A repair confined to foundations and institutions will leave the
  proposed spectacle outside lived culture. Adding the mechanism directly to
  either Public survey would solve that absence by leaking the backstage
  answer: readers would learn that divine light *is* a god destroying
  thaumavores, rather than encounter cult teaching, mortuary evidence, and a
  contested reconstruction.
- **Repair direction:** Give Public one bounded, situated source or incident
  whose owners can only carry evidence: for example a cult account of glory or
  purification, a potential record of concentration and loss, a witness's
  familiar face or gesture, and a household or mortuary list of presences that
  no longer answer. The source may route from a survey, but it must stop before
  the author taxonomy and before a universal verdict. Use the Empty Bridge only
  if the event owner independently earns those new facts; do not retrofit it
  merely because it is already in the packet.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not turn every divine spectacle into the same hidden
  atrocity, every halo into a diagnostic tell, or every Public page into a
  mystery advertisement. One evidence-rich carrier is enough.
- **Dependency:** Ghostlight Play and Consequence for an honest discovery
  threshold; Reader Experience for the spoiler-safe route; History for any
  adopted incident.
- **Confidence:** High. The present Public notes deliberately preserve
  disagreement but contain no carrier for this proposal's distinctive visible
  and mortuary evidence.

### CSK40-03 — Thaumavore ascent cannot become shared divine autobiography

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Spoilers/Reference/Divine Manifestation and Thaumavore
  Interception Proposal.md`, **Thaumavore feeding pressure** (“Many gods began
  as thaumavores”) and **Pity, shame, and divine policy**; compare
  `Kalsa/Spoilers/Foundations/Projections, God Beasts, and Thaumavores.md`,
  **God Beasts**, **Divine knowledge and performed certainty**, and
  **Thaumavores**.
- **Evidence:** Adopted canon says a Chaos Beast may become a God Beast through
  binding and that thaumavores include lost souls, ancestral presences, and
  carcasses of unascended Beasts. It explicitly leaves manifestation, identity
  continuity, memory, and release unresolved. The proposal itself asks the
  maintainer to adjudicate both the ascent path and what remains after an
  accumulated active body is destroyed. Existing divine-knowledge doctrine
  also makes gods interested, incomplete witnesses to their own origins.
- **Effect:** Canonizing “many gods began this way” without a bounded adopted
  mechanism would make cult origin stories converge on the author answer and
  give gods a shared memory they are not entitled to possess. Pity and shame
  would become assigned divine psychology rather than one possible policy or
  sectarian reading.
- **Repair direction:** Keep frequency, memory continuity, and universality
  unresolved unless the foundation owner can adopt a narrower mechanism from
  the seed. Even if a thaumavore-to-god route becomes backstage canon, give
  living cults and gods only situated memories, doctrines, observed wagers, or
  rival claims. Attach pity, warning, binding, or extermination policies to
  particular later divine/cult owners rather than to gods as a class.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not erase the seed's genuine possibility that the
  dead or unascended Beasts can seize a divine collapse and found something
  new. Productive uncertainty is not rejection of the spectacle.
- **Dependency:** Oracle Ecology and Timeline Competition for the ascent and
  identity mechanism; History for any tradition claiming descent from a
  survived interception.
- **Confidence:** High. The proposal marks these questions for adjudication,
  while the adopted owner explicitly preserves the relevant uncertainty.

## Pattern or dependency map

The safe knowledge route is:

**Backstage mechanism** (`Kaos` plus divine/thaumavore foundation) → **local
effects** (concentrated flow, flashes, interruption, residue, absence) →
**separate custodians** (potential ledger; covenant doctrine and restricted
record; mortuary inventory; household recognition; shrine/containment site) →
**interested reproduction** (clerical teaching, steward apprenticeship,
funerary testimony, copied kin lists, site warnings) → **Public disagreement or
bounded source**.

No current institution can honestly complete the whole arrow. That is the
protection: cult control can conceal records but not erase household memory;
mortuary comparison can establish patterned absence but not inspect the divine
route; stewards can show concentration and loss but not identify the dead; a
god can testify and still perform certainty.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not add a neutral Public paragraph explaining that crackling divine radiance
is the destruction of feeding thaumavores. Also resist giving every cult the
same purification doctrine and every mortuary tradition the same correct
counter-theory. The setting needs recognizable evidence and incompatible
stakes, not two standardized teams debating the answer key.

## Ranked actions

1. **Essential:** Assign discovery, concealment, and contest to the existing
   potential, covenant, mortuary, household, and site owners, each with bounded
   evidence, reproduction, interest, and blind spot.
2. **Essential:** Create or identify one bounded Public carrier for the
   radiance/absence dispute while withholding interception, taxonomy, and the
   complete reveal.
3. **High-value:** Keep thaumavore ascent frequency, surviving memory, and
   divine recognition unresolved or situated until their foundation mechanism
   is separately adopted.
4. **Leave alone:** Preserve the current Public surveys' plural witnesses,
   spectacular entrance, and refusal to make gods or records omniscient.
