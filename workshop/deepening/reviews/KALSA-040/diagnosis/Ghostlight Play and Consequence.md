# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** All 16 packet files match their declared raw SHA-1 identities. The
proposal supplies a spectacular phenomenon, several interested institutions,
and a promising ladder of partial evidence, but it does not yet produce a live
or bounded playable state. There is no site where a concentrated miracle is
underway, no named local custodians or affected claimants, no advancing pressure,
and no branch by which character action changes the miracle, the feeding dead,
or the people whose protection funds it. Its revelation currently culminates
in an author answer rather than in evidence that characters can contest without
receiving author knowledge.

The existing owners contain nearly all of the needed machinery. [[../../../../../Kalsa/Spoilers/Institutions/The Potential Economy|The Potential Economy]]
provides grant records, leakage reports, wardens, appeals, and beneficiaries;
[[../../../../../Kalsa/Spoilers/Institutions/God-Cult Covenants|God-Cult Covenants]]
provides champions, miracle sites, covenant claimants, and adverse
manifestation records; and [[../../../../../Kalsa/Spoilers/Institutions/Death and Binding Law|Death and Binding Law]]
provides witnesses, inventories, containment, and rival classifications. A
small current dungeon or another explicitly live situation should join those
interfaces. It must not settle the proposed ascent path, the identity surviving
interception, or one universal interpretation of divine radiance.

## Protected successes

- `Divine Manifestation and Thaumavore Interception Proposal` preserves visible
  high-fantasy scale while requiring matter, energy, preparation, and a causal
  route. The playable repair should keep the miracle genuinely spectacular,
  not shrink it into an accounting demonstration.
- The proposal's `Aftermath and revelation` section separates cult account,
  mortuary evidence, witness perception, and backstage explanation. That
  asymmetry is the right basis for investigation if the evidence is placed in
  character-accessible custody.
- `The Potential Economy`, `God-Cult Covenants`, and `Death and Binding Law`
  already give different actors concrete leverage over routes, stores,
  champions, bodies, records, classification, and containment. Repair should
  use those authorities rather than inventing a universal adventurer office.
- [[../../../../../Kalsa/Spoilers/Events/The Empty Bridge Vigil|The Empty Bridge Vigil]]
  demonstrates how one divine expenditure can leave several preserved records,
  material losses, and incompatible institutional lessons without granting a
  complete divine explanation. Its event facts should remain untouched.

## Findings

### GPC40-01 — No bounded playable owner for the proposed phenomenon

- **Finding ID:** GPC40-01
- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Divine Manifestation and Thaumavore Interception Proposal`,
  especially `Restrictive mechanism, spectacular result`, `Divine
  interception`, `Aftermath and revelation`, and `Request to the maintainer
  loop`; current Public route `Kalsa/Public/index.md` -> `World/Gods and the
  Dead.md#Hungry holy places`.
- **Evidence:** The proposal describes what a god, champion, feeding dead, and
  residents may do in general. It names no current site, declared miracle need,
  material source, local beneficiary, custodian, active manifestation, or
  unresolved starting state. Public offers dangerous miracle sites in the
  aggregate, while the only attested divine event is a completed flood history
  that does not own interception or apparent materialization.
- **Effect:** The material can explain a GM's answer but cannot begin play.
  Characters have no threshold to cross, no actor whose plan is already moving,
  and no state that can answer their arrival differently from their refusal.
- **Repair direction:** Add one bounded current situation under a play-bearing
  dungeon/site owner, or an equally concrete live-state note, where a named
  local cult intends a large miracle through an established champion or route.
  Specify the immediate need, available material lever, affected households or
  claimants, nearby dead, institutional custodians, and what has already begun.
  Preserve all proper names or quantities that remain genuinely unresolved;
  bounded does not require continent-wide law.
- **Collateral risk:** Retrofitting the Empty Bridge Vigil would make an adopted
  event silently own facts it never established. A generic “divine miracle
  dungeon” would also detach the phenomenon from the cult, mortuary, and
  allocation bodies that make it Kalsan.
- **Dependency:** Canon owner adjudication must first decide which proposal
  claims are available to the scene; Reader Experience can then test the
  Public route without seeing those backstage owners.
- **Confidence:** High. No attested note owns a present manifestation-and-
  interception state.

### GPC40-02 — Evidence layers lack reachable carriers and custody

- **Finding ID:** GPC40-02
- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** Proposal, `Aftermath and revelation`; `The Potential Economy`,
  `Leakage and magical fountains` and `Audit, discipline, and appeal`;
  `Death and Binding Law`, `Thaumavores, testimony, and recognition` and
  `Discipline, audit, and appeal`.
- **Evidence:** The proposal lists public glory, mortuary disappearance records,
  faces or gestures in flashes, and a final backstage relation. The institution
  notes establish miracle ledgers, leakage reports, death inventories,
  manifestation witnesses, claimant appeals, and separated custody. The packet
  does not place any particular trace with a reachable person, office, object,
  room, route, or hostile custodian.
- **Effect:** Investigation has an answer key but no evidence path. A GM must
  either hand characters the author model or improvise the entire chain of
  access, resistance, and misreading. The different institutions' interests do
  not yet press against one another at the table.
- **Repair direction:** Give the bounded situation a small evidence inventory
  distributed among existing authorities: for example, a steward's record of
  the grant and material inputs, a mortuary record of a familiar presence that
  went silent, a cult witness's account of hostile interference, a recoverable
  physical source or scar, and one brief manifestation that can be heard or
  protected. State what each carrier can establish, what it cannot establish,
  and who can deny, destroy, withhold, or reinterpret it. No character-facing
  source should simply disclose the proposal's author labels or complete
  interception account.
- **Collateral risk:** Making every clue converge cleanly would turn culturally
  situated evidence into a disguised omniscient exposition trail. Making only
  the cult account false would also reduce a real defensive collision to a
  routine cover-up.
- **Dependency:** Culture and Situated Knowledge should test the inhabitant
  carriers; Metaphysical Coherence must preserve the distinction between an
  observed absence, a manifestation, and destroyed identity.
- **Confidence:** High. The packet names evidence classes but no playable
  custody or access conditions.

### GPC40-03 — The flow has no clock and player interventions do not change it

- **Finding ID:** GPC40-03
- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** Proposal, `Thaumavore feeding pressure`, `Divine interception`,
  and `Aftermath and revelation`; `Oracle Ecology#Ghostlight consequences`.
- **Evidence:** The proposed miracle concentrates potential, attracts feeding
  attempts, may make a god defend its champion, and can leave displaced harm.
  `Oracle Ecology` requires resolution from live state, approach, material
  levers, competing interventions, and chance. The proposal supplies no phase,
  threshold, expiry, worsening condition, or consequential character action.
- **Effect:** The crackling can only happen as narrated spectacle. Characters
  cannot arrive early, misread it, refuse involvement, divert or disperse the
  working, protect an alleged intruder, preserve a material input, or prioritize
  an abandoned beneficiary and receive materially different responses.
- **Repair direction:** Tie an advancing clock to this particular miracle: its
  preparation, concentration, expenditure, retraction, or exhaustion should
  change what remains possible. Give actors target-specific interventions over
  the established levers—staging or interrupting the grant, moving or denying
  its material source, dispersing the route, warning or containing nearby dead,
  protecting a witness, negotiating a share, or moving people whose aid was
  withdrawn. At least two plausible courses, including refusal or delay, must
  produce different states for the miracle, claimants, and residents. Chance
  and competing action should remain able to frustrate a correct diagnosis.
- **Collateral risk:** Copying the Second Gate's three-bell device would make
  the clock ornamental. Letting a single skill check stop “the god” would erase
  the distributed material and institutional machinery that gives the scene
  leverage.
- **Dependency:** Material Life and Ecology should test the physical source and
  displaced cost; Institutional Causality should test who can commit, stop, or
  review each intervention.
- **Confidence:** High. The proposal specifies causal pressure but not a mutable
  encounter state.

### GPC40-04 — Aftermath has no durable receipt or branch-preserving return path

- **Finding ID:** GPC40-04
- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** Proposal, `Aftermath and revelation`; `The Potential Economy`,
  `Dungeon pressure`; `God-Cult Covenants`, `Dungeon pressure`; `Death and
  Binding Law`, `Dungeon pressure`.
- **Evidence:** The proposal predicts overfed manifestations, displaced
  potential, lodged effects, ancestral absences, and resident costs. Existing
  institutions can preserve allocation records, covenant claims, death
  inventories, containment reviews, stores, and altered site custody. Nothing
  in the proposal says which result survives play or which owner receives it.
- **Effect:** Even a strong improvised session can snap back to unchanged lore.
  The miracle's beneficiaries, the dead, affected households, and institutional
  claimants carry no durable difference, while an investigation risks ending
  only with the GM announcing the hidden mechanism.
- **Repair direction:** Define branch-preserving receipts for the bounded
  situation rather than one canonical ending. Record the changed miracle route
  or site, remaining material stores, champion condition or authority, named
  lost or protected aid, manifestation/absence testimony, containment status,
  and any appeal or claimant record generated by play. Each receipt must return
  to its rightful lore owner for post-session canon review; no result should
  automatically prove thaumavore ascent, complete erasure, divine guilt, or one
  claimant's metaphysical identity.
- **Collateral risk:** Prewriting the “true” successful investigation would
  turn choices into delivery mechanisms for canon. A single all-purpose quest
  log would also collapse the deliberately separated covenant, allocation, and
  mortuary records.
- **Dependency:** Ghostlight's world compiler/resolver must preserve the live
  branch; canon adoption remains with the subject owners, not runtime state or
  the Public projection.
- **Confidence:** High. Consequences are listed, but no durable local owner or
  receipt connects them to character action.

## Pattern or dependency map

```text
declared local miracle need + material lever + grant + nearby dead + affected people
    -> advancing concentration / expenditure / retraction pressure
    -> separated, interested evidence carriers
    -> character action, refusal, delay, protection, diversion, or misreading
    -> response from champion, steward, mortuary claimant, household, and manifestation
    -> materially distinct live states
    -> separate site, miracle-ledger, covenant, mortuary, and claimant receipts
    -> post-session owner review without automatic metaphysical verdict
```

The proposal can supply candidate mechanism only after owner adjudication.
`The Potential Economy`, `God-Cult Covenants`, and `Death and Binding Law`
supply the lower-world actors and receipts. A current site supplies mutable
state. Public may route toward inhabitant evidence but does not own the hidden
answer. Ghostlight may resolve a bounded occurrence; it must not convert that
occurrence into a universal law.

## Tempting edit to resist

- Do not write a revelation scene in which the party learns that every flash is
  a shredded thaumavore, proves that gods ascended through the same wager, and
  exposes the cult's account as a lie. That would settle the proposal's live
  unknowns, reward one correct interpretation, and replace institutional
  conflict with an authored confession. Do not retrofit these facts into the
  Empty Bridge Vigil or turn every hungry holy place into the same encounter.

## Ranked actions

1. **Essential:** After canonical adjudication, create one bounded current
   manifestation/interception situation with a declared need, material lever,
   local actors and claimants, mutable starting state, and no predetermined
   canon outcome (`GPC40-01`).
2. **Essential:** Place partial evidence in separate, reachable, interested
   custody without giving characters the backstage taxonomy (`GPC40-02`).
3. **Essential:** Add target-specific interventions and an advancing pressure
   whose plausible branches respond differently to action, refusal, and delay
   (`GPC40-03`).
4. **Essential:** Preserve each resulting branch through rightful site,
   allocation, covenant, mortuary, and claimant receipts, followed by explicit
   owner review (`GPC40-04`).
