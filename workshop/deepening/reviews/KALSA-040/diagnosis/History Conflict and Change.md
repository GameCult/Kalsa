# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** All 16 raw SHA-1 identities match the frozen packet. The proposal
offers a strong causal account of spectacular divine action and a plausible
source of conflict around concentrated miracles, but it presently has no
adopted historical owner. Its divine-policy section reads as a timeless menu,
and its claim that many gods survived the same thaumavore wager assumes both a
frequency and a continuity of divine memory that the adopted foundation leaves
unresolved.

One bounded event is needed if interception is to form present covenant,
potential, mortuary, or Public practice rather than remain a foundation
mechanism. That case must own only its facts and local aftermath. The Empty
Bridge Vigil demonstrates the correct historical shape—several records,
opposed later lessons, and no universal settlement—but its frozen facts do not
include radiance, feeding attempts, destroyed active bodies, or missing
ancestral presences. It may be extended only through explicit adoption of
those new facts; otherwise a separate event should carry them.

## Protected successes

- Preserve the seed's peculiar connection among concentrated divine power,
  hungry dead, catastrophic fountains, and the possibility that old ghosts or
  dead gods gain renewed agency. The spectacular scale is historical pressure,
  not an embarrassment to be reduced to minor haunting.
- Preserve `Kalsa/Spoilers/Events/The Empty Bridge Vigil.md`'s four-record
  structure. The cult, low-settlement households, upstream Channeler, and
  bridge keepers inherit different evidence and interests; later procedures
  remain partial and non-universal.
- Preserve the existing institutional phrasing in `The Potential Economy` and
  `God-Cult Covenants`: some stewards or cults cite an event, other factions
  draw the opposite lesson, and one settlement does not silently become
  continent-wide law.
- Preserve `Projections, God Beasts, and Thaumavores`, **Divine knowledge and
  performed certainty**. A god can be an ancient witness to its own formation
  without becoming a perfect historian of origin, schism, memory loss, or
  rival accounts.

## Findings

### HCC40-01 — Thaumavore ascent is presented as shared divine prehistory

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Spoilers/Reference/Divine Manifestation and Thaumavore
  Interception Proposal.md`, **Thaumavore feeding pressure** (“Many gods began
  as thaumavores”) and **Pity, shame, and divine policy** (“Gods that rose from
  thaumavore existence recognize the wager”); compare
  `Kalsa/Spoilers/Foundations/Projections, God Beasts, and Thaumavores.md`,
  **God Beasts**, **Divine knowledge and performed certainty**, **Thaumavores**,
  and **Release**.
- **Evidence:** The seed establishes that a Chaos Beast may found a God Beast,
  that dead Projections and unascended Beast carcasses can feed on passing
  potential, and that a divine collapse may briefly revive old ghosts and dead
  gods. The adopted foundation leaves manifestation, identity continuity,
  memory, release, and the survival rules of most divine styles unresolved.
  The proposal itself requests adjudication of both ascent and what remains
  after an accumulated active body is destroyed.
- **Effect:** “Many gods” turns a possible route into universalized ancient
  history without an event, lineage, archive, or surviving claimant. The next
  section then gives those gods coherent autobiographical recognition of the
  wager, making pity and shame look like consequences of settled memory rather
  than policies shaped by particular histories and doctrines.
- **Repair direction:** Retain the possibility of seizure and ascent while
  leaving its frequency and continuity unresolved unless the foundation owner
  adopts a narrower mechanism. Any god or cult claiming such an origin should
  do so through a named memory, tradition, rival account, or historical case;
  the claim must remain subject to the existing limits on divine knowledge.
  Divine policies of warning, trickle, binding, avoidance, or extermination
  belong to particular later owners once an episode explains their formation.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not erase the seed's real possibility that a feeder
  survives a fountain or diversion and becomes a new divine power. The repair
  should narrow historical certainty, not domesticate the danger.
- **Dependency:** Oracle Ecology and Timeline Competition for the ascent and
  identity mechanism; Culture and Situated Knowledge for how later divine and
  cult traditions reproduce the claim.
- **Confidence:** High. The proposal labels the two premises for adjudication,
  while the adopted owner explicitly preserves the uncertainties they would
  settle.

### HCC40-02 — Interception has no adopted event from which current practice could form

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Spoilers/Reference/Divine Manifestation and Thaumavore
  Interception Proposal.md`, **Divine interception**, **Pity, shame, and divine
  policy**, and **Aftermath and revelation**; `Kalsa/Spoilers/Institutions/The
  Potential Economy.md`, **Leakage and magical fountains** and **Audit,
  discipline, and appeal**; `Kalsa/Spoilers/Institutions/God-Cult Covenants.md`,
  **Leakage, restraint, and holy geography**; `Kalsa/Spoilers/Institutions/Death
  and Binding Law.md`, **Thaumavores, testimony, and recognition**.
- **Evidence:** Existing institutions already rotate sites, classify spirits,
  compare leakage, preserve adverse manifestations, hear mortuary testimony,
  and fight over containment. The new proposal would add a distinctive causal
  sequence—spectacular concentration, visible momentary feeders, deliberate
  interception, disappearance or residue, concealment, and later revelation—
  but no adopted event owns a single occurrence or the conflict that made any
  current response durable.
- **Effect:** If the mechanism is copied directly into institutional notes,
  their current procedures will appear to have arrived fully formed from the
  author ontology. If it remains only in foundations, it produces no historical
  pressure, constituency, archive, or changed practice in the world.
- **Repair direction:** Adopt one bounded factual episode that records a named
  or deliberately unresolved divine concentration, what witnesses could see,
  which familiar presences or protections ceased or changed afterward, which
  records survived, who controlled them, and what material cleanup or dispute
  followed. Neighboring institution notes should carry only their later
  evidence, memory, and present procedure. A Public source may preserve the
  incompatible accounts but not the backstage verdict.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not invent a date list or a canonical revelation
  council. One causally complete episode is enough; precise names and casualty
  counts may remain unresolved when no source earns them.
- **Dependency:** Institutional Causality for the record and cleanup handoffs;
  Ghostlight Play and Consequence for whether the surviving evidence creates a
  live threshold rather than a historical answer key.
- **Confidence:** High. No event in the exact packet owns interception-specific
  facts, while the proposal explicitly requests downstream institutional and
  Public consequence.

### HCC40-03 — The nearest precedent must not become universal radiance law

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Spoilers/Events/The Empty Bridge Vigil.md`, **Evidence
  inside the hierarchy**, **Four preserved records**, and **Consequences**;
  `Kalsa/Spoilers/Institutions/The Potential Economy.md`, **The Empty Bridge
  consequence**; `Kalsa/Spoilers/Institutions/God-Cult Covenants.md`, **The
  Empty Bridge consequence**.
- **Evidence:** Empty Bridge establishes a concentrated one-night grant,
  withdrawn shelter protection, external river intervention, four unequal
  records, and later procedures adopted by *some* cults and stewards. It
  explicitly leaves the god, cult, place, date, and divine style unresolved and
  states that no settlement made either lesson universal. It contains no
  evidence of crackling radiance, intercepted feeders, destroyed accumulated
  selves, or later ancestral silence.
- **Effect:** Treating the vigil as if it already proved the proposal would
  alter its factual owner by implication and let one famous case explain every
  cult's theology, mortuary rule, allocation procedure, and reading of divine
  light. The event's productive conflict would collapse into a canonical
  demonstration.
- **Repair direction:** If the coordinator uses Empty Bridge, explicitly adopt
  the smallest new event facts and keep their custodians separate from the four
  existing records. Let only named or scoped successor practices cite that
  evidence, and retain rival readings plus non-adoption elsewhere. If those
  additions overload the event's allocation conflict, create a separate
  bounded case instead.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not demand a second event merely to increase the
  archive. Reuse is coherent when the new causal chain genuinely fits; the
  defect is unearned or universalized precedent, not economy of notes.
- **Dependency:** Culture and Situated Knowledge for record traditions and
  Public attribution; AI-Isms and Prose if a repaired case begins carrying too
  many explanatory jobs.
- **Confidence:** High. The event's adopted facts and explicit scope limits are
  precise enough to distinguish compatible extension from retrospective
  overclaim.

## Pattern or dependency map

The required historical path is:

**Backstage possibility** → **one owned concentration/interception event** →
**separate cult, steward, mortuary, household, and site records** → **opposed
memories and selective concealment** → **local procedures with uneven adoption
and present dissent**.

The proposal currently moves from backstage possibility directly to a menu of
divine policies and a completed reveal. Empty Bridge already proves how to
preserve the middle: an event can be real, later practices can change, and
constituencies can still inherit incompatible lessons.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not declare that all divine radiance has always been recognized as the
slaughter of hungry dead, then invent a continent-wide mortuary reform to
explain why nobody says so publicly. That would replace history with a secret
universal theology. Also do not force Empty Bridge to found every later rule
merely because it is the available concentrated-miracle case.

## Ranked actions

1. **Essential:** Narrow or withhold the universal “many gods” origin and
   shared-memory claims until the ascent and identity mechanism is adopted.
2. **Essential:** Give interception one bounded adopted event with surviving
   records, conflict, aftermath, and at least one changed present practice.
3. **High-value:** Keep every precedent local and unevenly inherited; extend
   Empty Bridge only if its event owner explicitly earns the new facts.
4. **Leave alone:** Preserve the vigil's four conflicting records, non-universal
   consequences, unresolved proper history, and the current doctrine that gods
   remain interested rather than omniscient witnesses.
