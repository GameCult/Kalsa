## Concise diagnosis

**Pass with findings.** The proposal contains several exact images capable of
carrying spectacular divine action into Kalsa: raiment assembled from available
matter, faces or hands appearing in individual flashes, and familiar ancestral
protections going silent afterward. It also correctly demands layered discovery
and warns maintainers not to copy its sections wholesale.

Its explanatory architecture should not survive integration. The mechanism is
repeatedly defined against nearby misreadings, the proposed Public revelation
gives cults, mortuary records, careful witnesses, and investigators one rung
each, and the full section sequence could become an event built to demonstrate
every line of a design brief. The current Public route remains readable because
it does not yet contain that integration. The required repair is structural:
owner notes keep compact positive invariants, while one situated source carries
one spectacular act and its specific aftermath without narrating the complete
backstage answer.

## Protected successes

- `Apparent materialization and summoning` preserves causal spectacle. A
  champion's raiment can arrive with impossible speed while matter and energy
  still come from carried stores or the surrounding system.
- The interception image is sharp and strange: individual faces, hands, voices,
  shadows, cold, light, or static momentarily catch the flow. Preserve the
  perceptible fragments even if the complete interpretation remains hidden.
- “Familiar ancestral presences and protections may go silent” gives the
  aftermath an absence people can notice, mourn, dispute, and investigate. It
  is stronger than a generic statement that miracles have costs.
- The current Public `Gods and the Dead` route protects two good hard lines:
  “Death does not guarantee silence” and the refusal to treat gods as
  all-knowing. Both are followed by bodies, voices, ledgers, shrines, and
  practices rather than a backstage taxonomy.
- The proposal's final “Do not copy these sections wholesale” instruction and
  its functional integration checklist are sound workshop apparatus. Their
  checklist form is appropriate to the handoff.

## Findings

### AIP40-01 — The proposed mechanics argue through serial correction theatre

- **Priority:** High-value.
- **Location:** `Divine Manifestation and Thaumavore Interception Proposal.md`,
  especially `Restrictive mechanism, spectacular result`, `Apparent
  materialization and summoning`, `Thaumavore feeding pressure`, and `Divine
  interception`.
- **Evidence:** The explanatory spine repeatedly advances by rebuttal:
  “restrictive about mechanism, not about visible scale”; Kaos “does not create”
  and “does not grant”; divine whim “does not repeal causality”; summoning is
  “not retroactive rewriting”; feeding is “not an unrelated haunting”; and
  interception “does not necessarily erase” the Projection but “instead
  destroys” the accumulated body. Several hard distinctions are necessary, but
  the sequence keeps installing and correcting an imagined reader's next
  mistake. `Kaos#Hard constraints` already owns a positive causal inventory,
  while the divine foundation already distinguishes Projection, Beast, binding,
  manifestation, and release.
- **Effect:** If this rhetoric is distributed across those owners, the
  foundations will sound like one overprepared essay defending a magic-system
  pitch. The spectacular images become examples in a chain of disclaimers, and
  later Public prose will be tempted to repeat the same approved corrections in
  simpler words.
- **Repair direction:** Distill the accepted material into a few positive
  invariants under their existing owners. For example, Kaos can own that divine
  work assembles reachable matter through available energy and levers;
  Projections can own that concentrated flow gives nearby dead an opportunity
  to act; the identity owner can state exactly what interception destroys and
  leave the remainder unresolved. Keep one hard negation where it protects an
  actual adjudication boundary, then demonstrate the rest through an event or
  object rather than further rebuttal.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not purge negation from author constraints. “Kaos
  creates neither matter nor energy,” the absence of omniscience, and the
  unresolved relation between manifestation and identity remain functional
  limits. The repair is to stop paraphrasing them around every example.
- **Dependency:** Oracle Ecology and Timeline Competition for causal
  invariants; Identity, Continuity, and Personhood for the surviving remainder.
- **Confidence:** High. The recurrence spans four consecutive mechanism
  sections and matches a repaired Kalsa pattern rather than an isolated phrase.

### AIP40-02 — The proposed revelation ladder is a balanced cult/mortuary chorus

- **Priority:** Essential.
- **Location:** proposal `Aftermath and revelation`, especially the paragraph
  beginning “The public meaning of the crackling”; compare current
  `Kalsa/Public/World/Gods and the Dead.md`, `Hungry holy places`.
- **Evidence:** The proposal allocates one interpretive step to each position:
  cults call the crackling glory or purification; mortuary records reveal
  disappearing dead; careful witnesses identify faces or gestures; investigators
  complete the horrifying reveal. The next sentence turns the sequence into a
  five-part epistemic crosswalk: “Observed effects, cult accounts, mortuary
  evidence, restricted learned models, and backstage truth.” Current Public
  already contains a smaller shrine-keeper / mortuary-witness / steward triad in
  `Hungry holy places`. Adding this second, more complete chorus would make the
  neutral narrator the owner of how every institution fits together.
- **Effect:** Cult and mortuary differences would read like assigned seats in a
  comparison table. The final investigator step becomes a checksum that tells
  readers when they have reached the correct interpretation, while the complete
  sequence leaks the shape of the backstage reveal even if it withholds the
  word `thaumavore`.
- **Repair direction:** Carry Public discovery through one situated incident
  and its actual records. Let a miracle produce a perceptible flash, an absent
  familiar protection, a disputed body or shrine practice, and one action by a
  named local keeper or witness. Other institutions can enter only where they
  possess a different object, duty, or consequence. End on the surviving
  absence, damaged site, missing answer, or live decision; do not add an
  omniscient paragraph arranging cult account, mortuary evidence, learned model,
  and truth.
- **Collateral risk:** Preserve genuine cult concealment, mortuary comparison,
  deliberate faces in the flashes, and the layered spoiler structure. Do not
  make the cult obviously fraudulent, make mortuary evidence automatically
  correct, or reveal that gods began as feeders on the Public surface.
- **Dependency:** Culture and Situated Knowledge for the local carriers;
  Reader Experience and Navigation for the spoiler-safe route.
- **Confidence:** High. The proposal states the chorus explicitly, and the
  current survey already contains the neighboring structure it would amplify.

### AIP40-03 — A single integration event could become a mechanism checklist

- **Priority:** High-value.
- **Location:** the proposal's complete section sequence and `Request to the
  maintainer loop`; compare the present route from `Kalsa/Public/index.md` to
  `Gods and the Dead` and `Magic and Miracles`.
- **Evidence:** The proposal covers divine-scale material processing,
  materialization, summoning, levitation, radiance, armament, destruction,
  prescient preparation, feeding, interception, identity reduction, several
  divine policies, displaced harm, cult interpretation, mortuary evidence, and
  the gods' own origin. The `at minimum` list is a legitimate workshop checklist,
  but there is no current event or Public source that should be made to prove
  every item. The current Public route supplies only survey bearings and links
  to older disputes; its one sentence of spectacular divine action ends when
  witnesses begin a shrine before the smoke clears.
- **Effect:** Building one canonical case to satisfy the entire integration list
  would make the event feel reverse-engineered from review criteria. Every
  flash, disappearance, policy, office, and revelation would arrive on cue, and
  the wonder would read as a worked example of the author model rather than an
  incident inhabitants survived.
- **Repair direction:** Separate general owner integration from event design.
  Let the foundation and institution owners carry the reusable rules by link.
  Give the downstream event one visible miracle, one interception or feeding
  pressure, one material or personal aftermath, and only the records needed by
  its participants. The Public route should lead with a concrete image or
  question from that incident, not a summary of every mechanism it exemplifies.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not avoid checklist pressure by scattering several
  thin demonstration events across the vault. One causally dense case is enough
  if it is allowed to omit most of the proposal's menu. Preserve spectacle;
  divine-scale action need not become a restrained courtroom exercise.
- **Dependency:** History, Conflict, and Change and Ghostlight Play and
  Consequence for the event carrier; Reader Experience and Navigation for its
  Public entry.
- **Confidence:** High. The proposal's breadth is appropriate to a handoff but
  would be conspicuous if transferred into one narrative occurrence.

The functional checklist belongs in workshop. Its prose architecture does not
belong in canon.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not create a Public section with headings such as `Cult accounts`, `Mortuary
evidence`, `Learned theories`, and `What really happened`. That would turn the
spoiler boundary into a table of contents for the secret and reproduce the
balanced chorus more cleanly, not more safely.

Also resist lexical cleanup. Terms such as `material`, `record`, `claim`, and
`authority` remain useful when a named keeper, steward, court, or backstage
owner earns them. The defect is one rhetorical architecture diffusing across
unrelated voices.

## Ranked actions

1. Design the Public-facing discovery around one situated incident and remove
   the cult/mortuary/witness/investigator revelation ladder (`AIP40-02`).
2. Distill the proposal's accepted mechanics into compact positive owner
   statements instead of serial rebuttal (`AIP40-01`).
3. Keep the maintainer checklist in workshop while allowing the event to omit
   most of it (`AIP40-03`).
