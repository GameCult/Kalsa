# Critic Report

## Packet verification

I verified all 16 raw SHA-1 identities in `KALSA-040-baseline-01`; every hash
matched the exact file reviewed. I read the complete packet, both seed
witnesses, all named canonical owners, the three Public entry surfaces, project
direction, council protocol, shared prompt, specialist prompt, and report
contract. I did not read another critic report.

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** The proposal recovers a strong seed mechanism that current canon has
only partly developed: spectacular divine concentration creates a crowded
potential route, nearby dead can feed and manifest through it, and loss of
control can turn one miracle into a magical fountain from which new divine
competitors may arise. Apparent materialization also fits Kaos when it is
sourced rearrangement rather than creation.

The proposal currently changes that adversarial ecology into a God-owned visual
routine. A hierarchy “combines” several Gift faculties, every sufficiently
large flow provokes an attempt, God and feeder both know the odds, and the God
intercepts every diversion. The resulting halo then comes with a completed
identity and ascension explanation. That grants divine scale a fused
super-oracle, gives unlike thaumavores one common perception, and makes capture
automatic precisely where the seed says a God must retract or risk losing its
whole power base.

The smallest coherent repair is not less spectacle. It is one explicit
collision: distinct oracular inputs, an actual potential route, a feeder's
reachable lever, the hierarchy's partial detection and counter-intervention,
material and mortuary costs, failure states, and player actions. The universal
origin and identity claims should remain unresolved behind that event.

## Protected successes

- Preserve the proposal's core principle that mechanism is restrictive while
  visible scale can be obscene. The seed supports mountains rent and lakes
  steamed by linked power; current Kaos supports large effects through stressed
  or energetic systems. This is not a low-magic setting.
- Preserve apparent materialization and summoning as witness descriptions of
  rapid gathering, transformation, prepositioning, revelation, or arrival of
  existing matter through existing energy and causal routes.
- Preserve the seed's feeding pressure. Extended concentrated miracles attract
  nearby thaumavores, whose feeding limits how much power a God can hold in one
  place and for how long.
- Preserve the fountain danger. A God that loses control of a great
  concentration can create an interval in which dead identities and Beast
  remnants manifest, compete, and may find routes toward ascension.
- Preserve the proposal's visual horror as a possible event: distinct faces,
  gestures, voices, shadows, cold, light, or static flashing inside a divine
  working, followed by familiar dead who no longer answer.
- Preserve the existing Public boundary. `Gods and the Dead` already gives
  readers hungry holy places, recognizable gestures, divine uncertainty, and
  bounded mortuary testimony without publishing Projections, God Beasts,
  thaumavores, or the author model.

## Canon boundary

### Strong enough to adopt under current owners

- `Kaos` can adopt that apparent materialization, sudden raiment, radiance,
  levitation, armament, arrival, and catastrophic destruction remain possible
  when a working has a nonzero branch, sourced matter and energy, accessible
  levers, sufficient time and potential, and no rival that closes the route.
  Each actual case still owes a material account.
- `Projections, God Beasts, and Thaumavores` can retain and sharpen the
  seed-grounded rule that concentrated potential creates a feeding opportunity,
  thaumavores can use stolen flow to manifest and act, and a God risks losing
  control if it concentrates too much for too long.
- The same owner can adopt that a hierarchy may contest a feeding diversion by
  rerouting, retracting, dispersing, binding, or using a delegate against the
  feeder. Successful defense can collapse the current manifestation and remove
  its access to the flow. This is a possible interaction, not yet a universal
  visible sequence.
- `Oracle Ecology` can adopt that a great miracle is a crowded horizon in its
  own right. Scaling concentration increases the number of route-connected
  competitors and can make the hierarchy's defensive responses part of the
  miracle's causal system.
- The seed supports a bounded possibility that a magical fountain or stolen
  divine flow can open a route toward new God-Beast formation. It does not yet
  support a universal ascent procedure or a population claim about existing
  gods.
- `The Potential Economy`, `God-Cult Covenants`, and `Death and Binding Law` can
  carry the practical consequences: concentration estimates, leakage warnings,
  grant limits, route defense, mortuary witnesses, missing ancestral answers,
  containment, withdrawn beneficiaries, cleanup, and claims after failure.

### Must remain unresolved or attributed

- Whether potential is conserved or stored as a substance, and whether stolen
  potential constitutes a durable posthumous “body.”
- What a thaumavore retains from the dead person's or Beast's original Gift,
  which temporal relation lets it choose a diversion, and whether all feeders
  possess conscious strategy.
- Whether a feeder or God can perceive the odds of an attempted interception,
  identify the opponent, or distinguish one category of dead from another.
- The threshold, range, geometry, latency, and observability of feeding and
  counter-routing around different divine styles.
- Whether losing accumulated potential destroys a person, a partial
  continuity, an imitative manifestation, a Beast carcass, or only the current
  causal foothold; what memory and agency remain afterward.
- The exact path by which a feeder becomes a God Beast, including binding,
  root-strain requirements, stabilization, and the difference among a lost
  soul, ancestor, and unascended Beast remnant.
- Whether any, some, or many current gods began as thaumavores. “A collapsed
  God can create conditions in which new gods arise” is seed-grounded; “many
  gods survived this exact interception gamble” is not.
- Whether a risen God remembers that origin and therefore feels pity, shame, or
  fear. Those emotions can belong to a named God or cult account, not a
  mechanical consequence of ascension.
- Whether a stable halo is divine interception. One event may establish that
  explanation for its own evidence; the appearance is not a universal test.
- Any complete Public explanation of the crackling, the destroyed claimant, or
  divine origin.

## Findings

### OETC40-01 — Divine hierarchy is treated as a fused multi-Gift oracle

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Divine Manifestation and Thaumavore Interception Proposal`,
  **Restrictive mechanism, spectacular result**, especially “combine the causal
  faculties of several magical styles”; **Apparent materialization and
  summoning**, final paragraph.
- **Evidence:** `Magic Styles` says unlike Gifts do not add into one shared
  perception. `God-Cult Covenants`, **Oracular portfolios and divine strategy**,
  says a cult can recruit several Gifts without possessing one complete future.
  `Oracle Ecology` requires each operation to retain its own temporal
  transaction. The proposal instead lets an unspecified God acquire enough
  Clarity, Depth, and Scope, combine the faculties, and arrange precursors years
  or generations ahead.
- **Effect:** Divine scale becomes a universal Intuition-like superpower. The
  hierarchy can apparently borrow whatever horizon, resolution, or commitment
  the spectacle requires, erasing the partial legibility and internal
  competition that keep timeline capture local.
- **Repair direction:** Replace combination with coordination. A case must name
  which bound Prophet, opened reader, Sorcerer, shaman, Channeler, Luck relation,
  archived warning, or material operator supplies each piece; what reaches the
  God; which delegate acts; and what remains outside the hierarchy. Long
  preparation is available only through an appropriate style or relation and
  accessible precursor levers.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not demote the God into a passive battery. It can
  choose priority, route potential, preserve long memory, bind contributors,
  and coordinate response without receiving a view from outside every Gift.
- **Dependency:** Mechanics and Causal Coherence should verify the Kaos axes and
  reachability account; Culture should prevent a cult interpretation from
  becoming the backstage crosswalk.
- **Confidence:** High. Three current owners state the opposite boundary
  explicitly.

### OETC40-02 — The proposed interception is uniform cancellation rather than a causal collision

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** Proposal, **Thaumavore feeding pressure** and **Divine
  interception**: “necessarily attract attempts,” both parties perceiving the
  outcome, interception of “each attempted diversion,” and the stable halo.
- **Evidence:** The seed says an extended miracle attracts nearby thaumavores,
  the feeders begin consuming potential, and the God must retract or risk a
  fountain. It does not give the God perfect detection or automatic victory.
  Current Oracle Ecology says blocking requires contact with a route, lever,
  potential source, target, or question. The proposal names no retained feeder
  operation, detection route, counter-lever, cost, or failure before declaring
  every flash an intercepted feeder.
- **Effect:** The God stands outside the contest and deletes entrants one by
  one. Scale no longer increases recursive risk; it merely increases the
  brightness of a defense the proposal has already decided will work.
- **Repair direction:** Define the minimal loop. A route-connected feeder
  detects or exploits accessible flow through a retained relation or local
  foothold; its diversion changes the amount, destination, timing, or coherence
  of the grant; the hierarchy notices only the loss or deformation available
  through its route; a named intermediary, champion, boundary, retraction, or
  counter-routing act answers; that answer spends potential and changes the
  miracle. Preserve escape, hidden feeding, negotiation, binding, mutual
  damage, abort, and fountain as reachable outcomes.
- **Collateral risk:** Keep the rapid shredding sequence as one possible
  successful defense. Contingency should sharpen the spectacle, not sanitize it.
- **Dependency:** Mechanics should own contact and cost; Material Life should
  test the physical source and aftermath; History should require an event owner
  for any claimed standard response.
- **Confidence:** High. The proposal removes the exact loss-of-control branch
  protected by both seed and current canon.

### OETC40-03 — The feeder receives a generic oracular mind

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** Proposal, **Thaumavore feeding pressure**, paragraphs beginning
  “A thaumavore may have waited” and “The attempt is nevertheless tempting.”
- **Evidence:** The proposal gives a generic thaumavore century-scale planning,
  a canny all-in diversion, and perception of the likely result. The packet's
  thaumavore category contains lost souls, ancestral presences, and carcasses of
  unascended Chaos Beasts; current canon explicitly leaves identity and memory
  unresolved. No owner states that these entities share one Gift, conscious
  decision process, or access to future odds.
- **Effect:** Unlike posthumous entities become one species of desperate short-
  range Prophet. Their different origins cease to matter to how they enter the
  crowded horizon, and witnesses can infer intention from feeding behavior the
  mechanics do not actually expose.
- **Repair direction:** Keep the common capability narrow: accessible potential
  can support manifestation and action. Let one event identify the evidence for
  one feeder's retained style, remembered plan, appetite, repeated pattern, or
  deliberate choice. Other feeders may be opportunistic, partial, imitative,
  bound, dormant, or moved by a relation the witnesses cannot classify.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not make thaumavores mindless hazards. Some should be
  cunning persons or Beasts; the defect is universal cognition, not agency.
- **Dependency:** Culture and Situated Knowledge should own competing mortuary
  classifications; source and identity provenance constrain any factual event.
- **Confidence:** High. The category's heterogeneity and uncertainty are
  explicit in both foundation and death law.

### OETC40-04 — Interception silently decides which independent oracle survives

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** Proposal, **Divine interception**, especially “accumulated
  potential body,” “bare, powerless continuity,” and “genuinely been
  destroyed.”
- **Evidence:** `Projections` calls the Individual Projection the continuing
  person-pattern and leaves manifestation, identity, memory, and release
  unresolved. `Death and Binding Law` refuses to equate a manifestation with a
  complete returned person. The proposal divides the entity into an active
  accumulated self that is destroyed and a bare continuity that may remain.
- **Effect:** The timeline-capture consequence cannot be adjudicated honestly.
  If the independent oracle was killed, the God permanently narrowed the field;
  if its manifestation was stripped, it may remain a later competitor; if it
  was an imitation or Beast carcass, a different identity was affected. The
  proposal chooses the first moral and causal reading while admitting that
  memory survival is undecided.
- **Repair direction:** Canonize only the observable change: the contested flow
  is stripped or rerouted, the manifestation collapses, and its present causal
  foothold ends. Preserve evidence of subsequent silence and lost protection.
  Leave personhood, memory, dormancy, release, and capacity to return unresolved
  or owned by one later case.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not make interception harmless. Loss of testimony,
  protection, accumulated agency, and a familiar relation can be devastating
  without an author verdict that the person was erased.
- **Dependency:** Death/identity review owns the metaphysical boundary;
  institutional review should test who may classify the loss and claim remedy.
- **Confidence:** High. The proposal uses settled language for questions the
  packet repeatedly protects as unresolved.

### OETC40-05 — The God-origin reveal overstates a seed-supported possibility

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** Proposal, **Thaumavore feeding pressure**, **Pity, shame, and
  divine policy**, and **Aftermath and revelation**: “Many gods began as
  thaumavores that survived such an attempt.”
- **Evidence:** The seed says thaumavores seek recognition and ascension, a
  God-death fountain can bring dead powers into manifestation, and the death of
  a God Beast can mean the birth of others depending on the event's visibility.
  Current `Magic Styles` says only root strains normally found Gods; current
  `Projections` says a Chaos Beast becomes a God Beast by binding other Beasts
  and Projections. The proposal supplies neither binding nor a distinction
  among lost person, ancestor, and Beast remnant.
- **Effect:** One possible route becomes the common biography of gods. Divine
  pity and policy then arrive as mechanical consequences of an origin the
  setting has not shown, and the final reveal ends rather than deepens the
  reconstruction problem.
- **Repair direction:** Adopt the narrower seed claim: a magical fountain or
  captured divine flow can create a real opportunity from which a new God Beast
  may arise. Leave the binding and stabilization route unresolved. If a cult or
  god claims this origin, preserve its evidence and interests. Do not quantify
  how many existing gods share it.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not discard the taboo hypothesis. It is excellent
  restricted theology and campaign pressure when it remains contestable.
- **Dependency:** History must keep one event from becoming universal origin;
  Culture must supply the institutions able to preserve or suppress the claim.
- **Confidence:** High on the overstatement; medium on how much of the seed's
  “birth of many others” should be literalized before a concrete event tests it.

### OETC40-06 — The new ecology stops before a live Ghostlight state

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** Proposal, **Aftermath and revelation** and **Request to the
  maintainer loop**; compare `Oracle Ecology`, **Ghostlight consequences**, and
  `The Empty Bridge Vigil`.
- **Evidence:** The proposal supplies a retrospective reveal but no named
  miracle objective, active hierarchy evidence, feeding competitor, material
  source, route, concentration decision, clock, character knowledge, or
  reachable outcomes. Empty Bridge proves a God can act through partial
  evidence and finite potential, but it does not demonstrate apparent
  materialization or divine interception. Current Public contains no complete
  spectacular feeding collision.
- **Effect:** The new mechanism becomes an author cutscene. Players can discover
  what the halo meant after the God has already won, rather than alter who can
  observe, feed, defend, bind, escape, or keep the future open while the miracle
  is live.
- **Repair direction:** Give one current site or adopted event a complete
  crowded field. Name the desired miracle, source matter/energy, hierarchy
  evidence, distinct Gift inputs, champion or intermediary, exposed dead, grant
  route, feeder's reachable lever, institutional warnings, and clock. Let the
  party change concentration, route, warning, material preparation, witness
  access, negotiation, retraction, or containment. Preserve at least these
  outcomes: concentrated success with mortuary loss, dispersed partial aid,
  failed interception and stabilized rival, aborted miracle, redirected harm,
  and fountain.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not create a universal miracle minigame, require a
  Gifted party, identify the morally correct outcome, or let one event settle
  every feeder's identity.
- **Dependency:** Ghostlight Play should verify scenario usability; Material
  Life should test source and aftermath; Institutional review should assign
  grants, custody, cleanup, and appeal.
- **Confidence:** High. No packet owner currently contains the proposed live
  sequence.

### OETC40-07 — Public has the evidentiary floor but no situated spectacular case

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/index.md`; `Kalsa/Public/World/Magic and
  Miracles.md`; `Kalsa/Public/World/Gods and the Dead.md`.
- **Evidence:** Public correctly says that miracles near old death draw
  uncommissioned answers, familiar gestures may form, a mortuary witness records
  a voice without declaring a returned person, and gods are not omniscient.
  `Magic and Miracles` mentions storm, stone, and favor through a champion but
  gives no linked source for a spectacular divine working. The guided crowded-
  future route reaches Empty Bridge's finite protection, not manifestation or
  interception.
- **Effect:** Readers can understand the rule in survey prose but cannot explore
  its evidence, constituencies, and characteristic error through an inhabitant-
  owned source. Publishing the proposal's completed reveal would fix discovery
  by breaking the spoiler boundary.
- **Repair direction:** After the hidden event is owned and reviewed, add one
  Public source from a legitimate position: cult victory account, champion
  witness, mortuary disappearance list, site cleanup record, or an explicit
  comparison among named copies. Show the spectacle, distinct flashes, missing
  familiar presences, material cost, and dispute. Do not publish `thaumavore`,
  `Projection`, `God Beast`, axes, potential body, or the universal God-origin
  thesis.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not retreat into vague wonder. Named faces, lost
  protections, damaged structures, withdrawn grants, and disputed records are
  spoiler-safe when their evidence owner is clear.
- **Dependency:** Reader Experience needs a fresh blind packet; Culture must
  verify that the source's model is genuinely situated rather than relabeled
  author ontology.
- **Confidence:** High. The current Public route is accurate but lacks the
  proposed phenomenon as a source-owned encounter.

## Pattern or dependency map

`OETC40-01` and `OETC40-03` protect asymmetry: neither the God nor the feeder
may receive a convenient bundle of every temporal faculty. `OETC40-02` then
owns the contact loop between those unlike actors. `OETC40-04` decides what
remains in the causal field after the loop, while `OETC40-05` prevents one
successful case from becoming the biography of all gods. `OETC40-06` is the
required falsification surface: without one playable collision, the earlier
mechanics remain assertions. `OETC40-07` is the situated reader projection of
that case, not an independent explanation.

The intended causal chain is:

distinct hierarchy evidence -> bounded allocation -> sourced material working
-> route-connected feeding -> partial detection -> costly counter-intervention
-> changed miracle and competitor state -> material/mortuary aftermath ->
separate institutional records -> situated Public dispute.

No step may silently inherit the authority of the next.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not save the proposal by declaring that every great halo is a battle among
dead short-range Prophets and an omniscient God. That would produce a clean,
memorable rule by flattening Gift asymmetry, posthumous identity, divine
uncertainty, and the seed's actual fountain danger. One violently specific case
with unresolved identity is stronger than a universal answer.

## Ranked actions

1. **Essential:** Replace combined divine faculties with a named coordination
   path among distinct Gifts and owners.
2. **Essential:** Define the feeding/interception contact loop, costs, partial
   detection, and failure states without automatic cancellation.
3. **Essential:** Remove the generic feeder mind and preserve post-interception
   identity as unresolved beyond observable loss.
4. **Essential:** Build one current or adopted Ghostlight collision that tests
   the mechanism under live player intervention.
5. **High-value:** Narrow the God-origin claim to a seed-grounded possibility
   and keep exact ascension, prevalence, and memory unsettled.
6. **High-value:** Project the reviewed case through one inhabitant-owned Public
   source while retaining the current survey's spoiler-safe limits.

No Optional finding survives the evidence threshold.
