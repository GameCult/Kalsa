# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** The proposal has a sound owner-aware handoff request, and the
existing institutions already contain most required organs. They do not yet
perform one shared miracle-to-aftermath path. Divine choice, cult allocation,
material operation, interception, mortuary evidence, containment, and household
remedy are allowed to collapse into the god's act. The proposal also makes
institutional policy depend on unadjudicated thaumavore ascent and destruction
of an accumulated self. Its committed integration request then escaped the
KALSA-039 packet because no intake owner had to enumerate it.

All 16 raw SHA-1 identities match `KALSA-040-baseline-01`.

## Protected successes

- The proposal is explicitly `adopted-candidate`, owns only its proposal and
  integration request, names its rightful owners, preserves the Public spoiler
  boundary, and asks to be retired after adjudication.
- `The Potential Economy` already separates finite routing from material
  inputs, provides dispersal and leakage wardens, budgets containment and
  recovery, and supports audit, discipline, and appeal.
- `God-Cult Covenants` separates actual divine routing, clergy, stewards,
  champions, and household welfare rather than treating cult office as
  metaphysical position.
- `Death and Binding Law` separates body, record, manifestation, containment,
  testimony, and severance jurisdictions while refusing to let paperwork
  settle identity.
- `The Empty Bridge Vigil` preserves finite divine protection, withdrawn
  beneficiaries, an independent material intervention, and incompatible record
  owners without granting any of them the God's complete explanation.

## Findings

### IC40-01 — Divine whim collapses allocation, operation, and defense

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Divine Manifestation and Thaumavore Interception Proposal.md`,
  `## Restrictive mechanism, spectacular result`, especially `The god's mood
  can decide`, and `## Divine interception`, especially `can keep feeding
  thaumavores away`.
- **Evidence:** The proposal assigns selection, funding, champion protection,
  and repeated interception to the God. Existing owners instead distinguish the
  God Beast's actual routing, the potential steward's estimate and allocation,
  the delegate's bounded grant, mundane suppliers and material operators, and
  wardens who pause or move a hazardous flow. Oracle Ecology also requires
  contact with the rival operation rather than automatic divine anti-magic.
- **Effect:** A spectacular miracle reproduces itself through one competent
  divine noun. No record shows who exposed beneficiaries, committed reserve,
  prepared the material lever, authorized the delegate, detected loss, stopped
  unsafe continuation, or answered when an adjacent owner refused.
- **Repair direction:** Preserve divine refusal and actual routing, but make one
  scoped handoff legible: covenant claim and beneficiary list -> steward's
  allocation, withdrawals, reserve, and expiry -> delegate or champion's grant
  -> named material operator and site -> leakage observation and bounded
  stop/redirect authority. Each owner records only its act and failure.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not imply that a steward can command the God, create a
  universal miracle office, or give a champion jurisdiction merely because the
  God routed power through them.
- **Dependency:** Kaos; Oracle Ecology and Timeline Competition; Material Life
  and Ecology.
- **Confidence:** High; the existing owner notes state these separations
  directly.

### IC40-02 — “Cleanup” has costs but no reproducible claim path

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** proposal, `## Aftermath and revelation`, and `## Request to the
  maintainer loop`, bullet beginning `carry allocation, concentration,
  containment`.
- **Evidence:** The proposal assigns residents overfed manifestations,
  displaced potential, dangerous sites, and missing ancestral presences when a
  God declines containment. `The Potential Economy` already recognizes
  containment and recovery reserve, withdrawn beneficiaries, leakage wardens,
  audit, material restitution, and appeal. `Death and Binding Law` separately
  owns manifestation testimony, mortuary custody, emergency containment,
  duration, review, and claims through the dead.
- **Effect:** “Residents bear the cost” becomes atmospheric tragedy rather than
  an institutionally actionable injury. No actor must preserve the pre-working
  hazard, post-working losses, withheld protection, site custody, worker labor,
  or the difference between material remedy and a metaphysical finding.
- **Repair direction:** Route cleanup through existing plural owners: record
  likely leakage sites and withdrawn beneficiaries before concentration; name
  the site/material operator and containment reserve; preserve each diversion,
  stop, manifestation, absence, exposure, store loss, and labor claim afterward;
  give emergency custody an expiry and review; let households and mortuary
  claimants pursue material support without requiring the forum to decide
  divine intent or personal continuity.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not invent continent-wide divine liability, make
  compensation proof of metaphysical guilt, or let a potential ledger absorb
  the death inventory and covenant register.
- **Dependency:** Material Life and Ecology; Culture and Situated Knowledge.
- **Confidence:** High; every required organ already exists, but no shared path
  connects them to the proposed phenomenon.

### IC40-03 — Unresolved ascent and self-destruction would decide jurisdiction

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** proposal, `## Thaumavore feeding pressure`, claims that many
  gods began as thaumavores, and `## Divine interception`, claims that the
  accumulated active being is destroyed while a bare continuity remains.
- **Evidence:** `Projections, God Beasts, and Thaumavores` currently makes a
  Chaos Beast a God Beast through binding and explicitly leaves manifestation,
  identity continuity, memory, and release unresolved. `Death and Binding Law`
  bars mortuary records and containment from deciding whether a manifestation
  is the continuing person. The proposal's ascent history supplies motive for
  divine policy, while its destruction model determines whether interception
  is defense, irreversible killing, loss of current agency, or depletion.
- **Effect:** A cult could bind, destroy, mourn, prosecute, or deny remedy to an
  entity based on a candidate ontology that no owner has adopted. Success in a
  feeding attempt could manufacture its own divine legitimacy; failed
  interception could erase the claimant needed to appeal it.
- **Repair direction:** Adjudicate the metaphysical minimum in the foundation
  owner first. Until then, institutional owners may record only observable
  diversion, manifestation, loss of present agency, disappearance, and disputed
  continuity. If an ascent path is adopted, `God-Cult Covenants` owns later cult
  formation and social recognition; `Death and Binding Law` owns testimony,
  custody, and claims through the dead. Neither institution may prove ascent.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not resolve the unknown merely to complete procedure,
  call a surviving Projection an empty substrate, or make a dangerous
  manifestation immune from temporary containment.
- **Dependency:** Oracle Ecology and Timeline Competition; Culture and Situated
  Knowledge; History, Conflict, and Change.
- **Confidence:** High; the contradiction is explicit in owner boundaries and
  unresolved lists.

### IC40-04 — The repair has no bounded world carrier

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** proposal, `## Aftermath and revelation`; current
  `The Empty Bridge Vigil`; `Public/World/Gods and the Dead.md`, `## Hungry holy
  places`; and the absence of an onward Public source for this phenomenon.
- **Evidence:** Empty Bridge performs finite allocation, outside intervention,
  material labor, and displaced claims but owns no thaumavore interception.
  Public preserves generic reports of hungry shrines and moved aid but no
  bounded case in which allocation, spectacular working, interception evidence,
  mortuary loss, containment, and remedy meet. The proposal correctly asks for
  observed effects, cult accounts, mortuary evidence, restricted models, and
  backstage truth to remain distinct.
- **Effect:** Repair can stop at foundation doctrine. Institutions will say they
  can allocate, investigate, and clean up without any event or current site
  forcing adjacent owners to exchange records, labor, custody, and refusal.
  Readers and players receive either generic danger or the complete backstage
  answer.
- **Repair direction:** After the foundation minimum is settled, add one
  bounded adopted event or current site that owns the factual sequence and
  makes the existing roles perform the handoff. A situated Public carrier may
  then compare named cult, material, mortuary, and household evidence without
  naming the author taxonomy or deciding identity. Do not retrofit thaumavore
  facts into Empty Bridge unless its event owner explicitly adopts them.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not build a universal cleanup bureau, balanced
  five-voice symposium, adventure menu, or event whose only purpose is to
  demonstrate every rule.
- **Dependency:** Ghostlight Play and Consequence; Reader Experience and
  Navigation; History, Conflict, and Change.
- **Confidence:** High; the current event and Public routes can be inspected
  directly.

### IC40-05 — Proposal intake has no owner that can reproduce it

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `baseline-packet.md`, `## Operator direction`;
  `workshop/review-council/README.md`, `## Packet Contract`; and the proposal,
  `## Request to the maintainer loop`.
- **Evidence:** The proposal was committed during KALSA-039 with an explicit
  integration request, but that pass's selected packet omitted it. The packet
  contract requires provisional design to be distinguished after selection; it
  does not require the coordinator to enumerate newly committed candidate or
  integration-request notes before freezing scope.
- **Effect:** A pass can certify every file it selected while leaving a live
  handoff in the same changed jurisdiction unseen. The proposal has provenance
  but no receiver, queue transition, deferral receipt, or negative closeout
  check. Its request succeeds only if someone happens to remember it.
- **Repair direction:** Give the existing pass coordinator one deterministic
  intake step before packet freeze: enumerate changed or newly committed lore
  notes since the pass base and active `adopted-candidate` or
  `proposal-and-integration-request-only` surfaces in the touched jurisdiction;
  record each as in-scope, explicitly deferred with an owner, or rejected. At
  closeout, verify no unreceived integration request remains. Once accepted
  claims move, retire this proposal to a short provenance pointer so candidate
  prose cannot survive as a shadow owner.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not create a second proposal registry, scan every
  workshop idea as mandatory canon work, or let candidate status promote its
  own claims. Intake grants review, not truth.
- **Dependency:** Coordinator / council protocol; no lore owner.
- **Confidence:** High; the packet records the exact failure and the current
  protocol omits the producing step.

## Pattern or dependency map

```text
metaphysical minimum: Kaos + Projections + Oracle Ecology
  -> covenant claim and affected households
  -> potential allocation, withdrawals, reserve, and delegate grant
  -> material operator and bounded working
  -> interception / leakage evidence
  -> separate potential, covenant, mortuary, material, and household records

commit or candidate status
  -> coordinator intake and explicit disposition
  -> frozen council packet
  -> owner repair and regression
  -> proposal retirement or named deferral
```

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not solve the handoff by founding a universal Order of Divine Cleanup,
giving potential stewards metaphysical sight, making champions judges of every
manifestation they fight, or publishing the proposal's backstage reveal. The
existing plural offices are the strength; they need one shared case and record
path, not a new throne.

## Ranked actions

1. Repair proposal intake and require an explicit disposition before a packet
   can close; retire the proposal after integration.
2. Settle only the metaphysical minimum needed for interception, ascent, and
   persistence before institutional consequences rely on it.
3. Connect existing covenant, allocation, material-operation, containment,
   mortuary, and household-remedy owners through one scoped working and
   aftermath path.
4. Give that path one bounded event or current-site owner and only then derive
   a situated Public carrier.
