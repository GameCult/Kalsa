# Reader Experience and Navigation Diagnostic

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** The three attested Public files make divine uncertainty, evidence
custody, and ordinary consequences intelligible, but they do not make a
spectacular divine manifestation explorable. `Kalsa/Public/index.md` invites me
to `Gods and the Dead`; that survey gives me strong rules for distrusting a
claim and names several kinds of records, then sends me to a succession dispute
or back into broad surveys. `Magic and Miracles` supplies one memorable image—a
god pouring favour through a champion while witnesses begin a shrine before the
smoke clears—but no link lets me enter that event, inspect its evidence, or
follow what it costs afterward.

I believe Kalsa's gods are active but limited powers whose relations are
maintained by promises, stores, bodily custody, witnesses, and labour. I want to
play a mortuary witness separating a voice from a body, a store worker testing a
miracle claim, or someone caught between a spectacular appearance and the
institutions trying to own it. The dead rival answering at the victor's shrine,
the empty relief stores, and the shrine rising through smoke remained in
memory. Attention fell when the divine route looped from `Gods and the Dead` to
`Magic and Miracles` and then back without opening a concrete divine case. I
skimmed the latter page's school and crowded-future material because it was
interesting but no longer advanced the question that brought me there.

The three Public hashes match the packet. The vault-level consent gate was not
in the supplied route and is not certified here.

## Protected successes

- Preserve `Gods and the Dead`'s separation of covenant book, miracle ledger,
  and mortuary testimony. These holdings make evidence socially situated and
  contestable without telling the reader which theology is true.
- Preserve the material dependency beneath sacred authority: relief stores,
  aid carriers, body tending, guards, and preserved terms. The line about a
  magnificent shrine with empty relief stores turns divine power into a
  playable obligation rather than decorative spectacle.
- Preserve the explicit limit that no surviving institution has established
  divine omniscience, together with examples of ledgers and dead rivals
  contradicting divine claims. This creates curiosity without pre-solving it.
- Preserve the index's concise survey labels and its warning that the surveys
  are bearings across continuing in-world arguments. The entrance is inviting
  and does not masquerade as an omniscient encyclopedia.

## Findings

### REN40-01 — The manifestation claim has no situated public carrier

- **Priority:** Essential.
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/World/Gods and the Dead.md`, opening through the
  link to `What the Three Ledgers Kept`, and the page's final navigation.
- **Evidence:** The page establishes that gods protect, conquer, bless, refuse,
  and leave disputed evidence. It explains who keeps covenant, miracle, and
  mortuary records, but its only linked argument is a succession crisis. None
  of its onward links is presented as an inspectable case of a spectacular
  divine appearance.
- **Effect:** A reader can understand the epistemic doctrine but cannot test it
  against an event. Spectacular action remains an assertion made by the survey
  voice, with no named witnesses, conflicting evidence path, or consequence to
  follow into play.
- **Repair direction:** Add one bounded, situated Public carrier for a divine
  manifestation and give it a direct, descriptive link from `Gods and the
  Dead`. The carrier needs observable action, identifiable evidence holders,
  at least one unresolved interpretation, and a consequence borne by people or
  material support. It does not need to disclose the backstage cause.
- **Collateral risk:** A careless repair could certify one cult's explanation,
  reveal hidden divine mechanics, or let a neutral narrator reconcile records
  that should remain separately owned.
- **Dependency:** The relevant event, cult, mortuary, and material owners must
  bound what a Public witness can know.
- **Confidence:** High. The entire supplied Gods page and all of its onward link
  labels were inspected within the three-file route.

### REN40-02 — The vivid champion image opens into a survey loop

- **Priority:** High-value.
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/World/Magic and Miracles.md`, paragraph beginning
  `At larger scales`, and its final `See` navigation.
- **Evidence:** The champion, smoke, witnesses, and nascent shrine provide the
  route's clearest spectacular image. The sentence has no local link. The page
  then turns to schools, aftermarks, and crowded futures; its only divine-facing
  onward link returns to `Gods and the Dead`.
- **Effect:** Attention rises at exactly the desired subject and then loses its
  object. The reader enters a two-survey circuit rather than discovering whose
  testimony, property, safety, or obligation changed after the manifestation.
- **Repair direction:** Once a situated carrier exists, link this image or an
  adjacent short invitation directly to it. Prefer a precise link label over an
  additional explanatory paragraph.
- **Collateral risk:** Repeating the carrier's explanation here would make the
  survey an alternate owner and slow an otherwise strong page entrance.
- **Dependency:** REN40-01; navigation should point to an owned Public account,
  not invent one.
- **Confidence:** High. The direct `Gods and the Dead` → `Magic and Miracles`
  route was followed in full.

## Pattern or dependency map

`Kalsa/Public/index.md` promises worship, obligation, manifestation, and divine
uncertainty → `Gods and the Dead` supplies excellent evidence categories and
material consequences → `Magic and Miracles` supplies the spectacular visual
beat → its navigation returns to the Gods survey. The missing organ is not more
explanation; it is one situated event carrier joining spectacle to evidence and
consequence. REN40-02 therefore depends on REN40-01 rather than warranting a
separate lore expansion.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not explain what gods really are, whether the champion's manifestation was
genuine, what the dead voice truly represents, or how miracles work backstage.
Do not add a glossary of divine categories or make every cult keep identical
records. The public route needs a case readers can investigate, not a final
answer or a standardized theology.

## Ranked actions

1. **Essential:** Supply one situated manifestation carrier and link it
   descriptively from `Gods and the Dead` (REN40-01).
2. **High-value:** Turn the champion-and-smoke attention peak in `Magic and
   Miracles` into an honest onward route to that carrier (REN40-02).
3. **Optional:** None. The existing orientation, evidence distinctions, and
   mystery should be left alone.
