# Reader Experience and Navigation Regression

## Concise diagnosis

**Pass.** A casual reader can now encounter spectacular divine action through
ordinary navigation, inspect several kinds of evidence and consequence, and
return to the surrounding surveys without receiving a backstage answer. The
index offers a concrete invitation; `Gods and the Dead` places the account
inside its discussion of hungry holy places; `Magic and Miracles` links its
champion-and-smoke image directly to the case. `The Faces in the Ash Halo` then
delivers the promised spectacle, material preparation, separately held
testimony, later absences, and unresolved danger.

The account feels like knowledge people in Kalsa could actually preserve. The
registrar, death witness, forge workers, recognizing households, shrines, and
branch each hold different pieces. No narrator decides what entered the light
or what remained afterward. The most memorable sequence is the scarred hand,
warning mouth, and old champion's dress forming in the fire, followed by the
silence at three shrines and the new answer below the court. I can imagine
playing the witness carrying the opened roll, a burned forge worker making a
claim, a household copying a silent entry, or a cleanup crew returning to the
blocked drain. Nothing in the four-file route felt like an authorial reveal,
unsafe link, or morally pre-digested conclusion.

The packet begins at `Kalsa/Public/index.md`; the vault-level consent gate is
outside this certification.

## Protected successes

- Preserve the index invitation: “Iron crosses a furnace court in a divine
  flash while a mortuary roll remains sealed below.” It promises spectacle,
  evidence, and a withheld answer in two sentences.
- Preserve the source's division of evidence among the sealed roll, gestures
  witnessed in the fire, later shrine silences, the forge return, and household
  copies. These pieces can be compared without becoming a single omniscient
  account.
- Preserve the material preparation and aftermath: three shifts of forge work,
  named stores, surrendered shrine grants, burned workers, damaged lifting
  gear, and the blocked quench drain. The miracle changes work and obligation,
  not merely imagery.
- Preserve the final uncertainty and renewed court. “The old furnace court has
  been opened again” converts unresolved evidence into immediate play pressure
  without explaining the divine mechanism.
