## New defects within remit

No separate repair-introduced defect is needed. The remaining weakness is the
unresolved actor-and-access half of the original playable-owner and evidence-
custody findings. The excellent mechanical branch structure makes that absence
more visible; it did not cause it.

## Protected strengths

- The opening state is immediate and legible: the party holds a sealed answer
  roll, a face has appeared before the rite, and the first horn is sounding.
- Physical interventions alter actual propagation paths rather than applying a
  universal action menu. The drain, cistern, racks, ceramic, weights, water,
  heat, and source stores all earn their effects.
- The party has bounded authority. It can inspect, carry evidence, alter routes,
  bargain, and recommend, but cannot command the God, branch, forge, or dead.
- The result structure preserves refusal, misunderstanding, partial success,
  delayed harm, and a fountain. It does not reward one author-approved moral
  answer.

## Essential further change

Complete the current Court's social state without enlarging its mechanics:

1. Instantiate the current branch or local armament body and the heat-gate
   dispute closely enough that the assault has a concrete beneficiary and
   opponent pressure.
2. Give the branch patron, forge custodian, potential steward, intermediary,
   mortuary witness/sect witness, and champion concise current commitments and
   conditional responses at the horns. At minimum, establish who can actually
   delay or stop, who proceeds despite a warning, who can admit a share, and
   what each does when the party opens, copies, conceals, or carries the roll.
3. State the party's access or resistance path to the grant ledger, withdrawn-
   shrine entries, champion testimony, and branch/covenant record. Preserve a
   blind spot for every carrier; do not make compliance automatic.

This repair belongs principally in `The Ash-Halo Court`. The general
institutions already own the offices and procedures and should not absorb this
site's cast.

## Leave alone

- Leave the historical God, branch, champion, exact date, and siege identity
  unresolved in `The Ash-Halo Armament`; the present site needs current actors,
  not a retroactive solution to every historical blank.
- Leave the six reachable outcome families and separated receipt structure
  intact.
- Leave feeder identity, Gift, memory, intention, and post-interception survival
  unresolved.
- Leave the party unable to command institutional and divine owners. Add their
  responses; do not transfer their authority to the party.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not fix the missing actor pressure by adding a table of universally
cooperative quest-givers or a single villain who blocks every clue. The Court
needs particular commitments and access contests: owners who can each help,
refuse, misread, and defend a real jurisdiction.

## Verdict

**Block.** `GPC40-03` and `GPC40-04` are resolved, and most of the physical
play chassis for `GPC40-01` and `GPC40-02` is excellent. The exact candidate is
not yet a complete current Ghostlight situation because its deciding actors
and several evidence-access paths remain uninstantiated. One bounded site-level
repair can finish both findings without changing the successful mechanics.
