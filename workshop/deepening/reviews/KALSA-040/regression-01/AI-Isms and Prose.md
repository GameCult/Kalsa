## New defects within remit

### AIP40-04 — The institutional chorus has been distributed rather than removed

- **Priority:** High-value.
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Spoilers/Events/The Ash-Halo Armament.md`, `The records
  diverge` and `Descendant practice`, lines 82–105; downstream `The Ash-Halo
  consequence` sections in `Death and Binding Law`, `God-Cult Covenants`,
  `Soiru'i Sects`, and `The Potential Economy`; compare `Sorcery — Jamnai and
  the Terjamna`, `Ash-Halo branch politics`, lines 125–140.
- **Evidence:** The event first gives high-branch copies, the furnace return,
  mortuary copies, and Soiru'i recitation one balanced holding apiece. The next
  section gives potential stewards, mortuary offices, Soiru'i sects, and
  high-branch rejection one descendant response apiece, then closes with “No
  response became universal Jamnai law.” The downstream notes repeat the same
  event in their own consequence sections and repeatedly restore the
  high-branch counterposition. The polity note performs one more balanced
  disclosure/secrecy comparison before explaining that the dispute remains
  live.
- **Effect:** Individual claims remain sensible, but their recurrence exposes
  the review architecture. The event reads as a hub designed to give each
  institution its required reaction, and the owner notes sound as though they
  are checking their seats off afterward. High branch and mortuary/sect
  positions become a compulsory matched pair even where one owner's particular
  procedure could stand on its own material history.
- **Repair direction:** Preserve the event's actual records and the genuinely
  adopted descendant practices, but choose one owner for each full account and
  let links carry the rest. In downstream notes, retain only the procedure,
  object, or conflict that changes that institution's work. Do not repeat the
  complete victory-versus-silence balance in every destination. Uneven survival
  is desirable: one office may keep a docket, another a copied roll, while a
  third needs only the current access dispute.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not erase high-branch doctrine, pretend the mortuary
  account is neutral truth, or cut the historical consequences merely to make
  the event shorter. The defect is recursive arrangement, not plural politics.
- **Dependency:** Institutional Causality and History, Conflict, and Change for
  claim ownership and which descendants are factual.
- **Confidence:** High. The same event-and-counterposition sequence appears in
  the event, four institutional destinations, and the polity owner.

### AIP40-05 — The Public roll closes with an evidentiary checksum

- **Priority:** High-value.
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/Sources/The Faces in the Ash Halo.md`, `The opened
  roll`, lines 45–58.
- **Evidence:** The death witness already carries the roll to three sites; the
  pressure warning, burial signs, and champion shrine have gone silent; a new
  presence drives workers from the cistern. The paragraph then summarizes the
  approved evidence categories—earlier signs, gestures, later silence—and
  closes with the balanced correction: “It could not say what had entered the
  light or what remained afterward.” This reproduces the Vault's earlier
  record-preserves-dispute / narrow-question checksum in a cleaner form.
- **Effect:** The sentence is correct, but it makes the author audible at the
  precise moment when the absences should be allowed to trouble the reader. It
  explains the epistemic lesson after the witness's route and the silent sites
  have already carried it.
- **Repair direction:** Let the roll's physical entries, omissions, custody, or
  the witness's next act carry its limit. Preserve the unanswered identity and
  survival questions without adding a concluding sentence that classifies what
  the record can and cannot know.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not imply that the roll proves death, interception,
  identity, release, or survival. The spoiler-safe uncertainty is a protected
  fact; only its checksum delivery is at issue.
- **Dependency:** Reader Experience and Navigation only if the changed ending
  alters the source's route forward.
- **Confidence:** High. The paragraph repeats a specifically documented Kalsa
  construction, and the preceding concrete evidence already performs its
  necessary work.
