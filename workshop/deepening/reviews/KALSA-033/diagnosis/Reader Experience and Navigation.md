# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Pass with findings.** Public is spoiler-safe, distinctive, and strongly
playable once it reaches Low Sere. Route architecture stacks five surveys
before the first lived situation and leaves the final source feeling appended.

## Protected successes

- Root consent is unmistakable and opt-in.
- Low Sere and Basin Table convert abstractions into forty-one hearths, disputed
  warnings, wages, injury care, the black door, and the knock.
- Four Readings makes Ju'onai schools memorable through disagreement.
- Difficult names gain meaning through use; mysteries remain unanswered.

## Findings

### REN33-01

- **Priority:** High-value.
- **Location:** `Public/index.md`, navigation list through Relics and Ruins.
- **Evidence:** Five surveys precede Low Sere and six precede a dramatic source.
- **Effect:** Accurate orientation becomes skimmable before the reason to play arrives.
- **Repair direction:** Regroup the index around a short lived-entry route,
  with surveys as lateral exploration.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not make overviews trivia or Low Sere representative of all Kalsa.
- **Dependency:** Navigation owner.
- **Confidence:** High.

### REN33-02

- **Priority:** High-value.
- **Location:** Basin Table final navigation and Public index final item.
- **Evidence:** Basin Table returns to Low Sere/index; Four Readings requires
  backtracking despite appearing next in the advertised order.
- **Effect:** Momentum stops and the second source feels appended.
- **Repair direction:** Either frame an onward link or label Four Readings as a
  separate second reading rather than a route step.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not imply shared place, participants, or causation.
- **Dependency:** Navigation owner.
- **Confidence:** High.

### REN33-03

- **Priority:** Optional.
- **Location:** Public index close, Peoples opening, Magic traditions, Gods opening.
- **Evidence:** Overviews repeatedly explain that no people, school, god,
  miracle, or institution receives final interpretive authority.
- **Effect:** The editorial hand becomes visible and pre-digests the reading.
- **Repair direction:** Keep one firm frame, then let attributed institutions
  and examples carry it.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not restore essentialism, omniscience, or false consensus.
- **Dependency:** Situated presentation.
- **Confidence:** Medium-high.

## Pattern or dependency map

Abstract pages state epistemic limits; concrete pages demonstrate them. The
strongest route is root -> Public -> Low Sere -> Basin Table. Spoiler safety
passes and no Public link enters Spoilers.

## Tempting edit to resist

dead, Crown Cities, Intuition, or the Low Sere knock.

## Ranked actions

2. Clearly separate Four Readings as another situated source.
3. Reduce repeated disclaimers.
