# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** The strongest Public material is situated, but `Magic and Miracles`
reconstructs the complete backstage Gift sequence as one tradition per people.
Elsewhere, subordinate lives and religious disagreement arrive through the same
balanced narrator dialect. The essential defect is structural taxonomy leakage.

All inspected candidate and hidden-owner hashes matched the packet.

## Protected successes

- `Four Readings` gives Ju'onai language an owner, transmission path, rivals,
  practical domain, and blind spots; its earned technical terms must survive.
- `World/Kalsa`, Roads through twilight, reproduces informal knowledge through
  marks, speech, host oaths, weather, and customary passage.
- Low Sere and both sources let named inhabitants own material interests,
  memory, disagreement, class, refusal, and uncertainty.
- Peoples and Polities protects fluid membership, mixed households, and
  unresolved Intuition without equal-coverage demands.

## Findings

### CSK33-01

- **Priority:** Essential.
- **Location:** `Public/World/Magic and Miracles.md`, What different traditions
  say; negative-check owner `Spoilers/Foundations/Magic Styles.md`.
- **Evidence:** The hidden seven-family table is an author/adjudicator crosswalk
  not known across Kalsa. Public presents Selza'a, Jamnai, Papsenai, Ju'onai,
  Prophetic, Sarxe, and Intuition in the same complete order and one-bullet shape.
- **Effect:** Peoples become cultural faces of seven magic categories. “No
  uncontested schoolbook” is undermined by author-crosswalk completeness.
- **Repair direction:** Break the exhaustive sequence. Present only source-owned
  comparisons useful here, organized around encountered evidence or dispute,
  with omissions visibly ordinary rather than missing cells.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not erase local explanations, expose signatures or
  sequences, invent pan-Kalsan comparison, or demand one source per people.
- **Dependency:** Provenance negative check; Reader Experience.
- **Confidence:** High.

### CSK33-02

- **Priority:** High-value.
- **Location:** `Peoples and Polities.md`, Papsenai, Divided City, and Sarxe.
- **Evidence:** Three peoples receive one polished moral-accounting dialect.
  Hidden owners provide sharper, differently owned material: Shield Levy Winter,
  Closed Lift Appeal, and Sarxe history without a separate adopted event owner.
- **Effect:** Subordinate inhabitants certify narrator fairness rather than
  carrying distinct memory or argument.
- **Repair direction:** Repair asymmetrically: ground Papsenai in current Circle
  practice or supported event memory; use Closed Lift only if needed; preserve
  Sarxe workshops, graves, farms, mixed households without inventing an event.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not add parallel anecdotes, native names, unresolved
  details, or delete mixed lives merely to shorten.
- **Dependency:** History; AI-Isms.
- **Confidence:** High for Papsenai and cadence; medium for Divided City scope.

### CSK33-03

- **Priority:** High-value.
- **Location:** `Gods and the Dead.md`, The unquiet dead.
- **Evidence:** Kin, mortuary office, release sect, and ruler receive one
  balanced sentence before a narrator conclusion. Hidden law preserves
  incompatible ends: binding, ancestral manifestation, release, or severance.
- **Effect:** Consequential funeral disagreement becomes tidy epistemic positions.
- **Repair direction:** Preserve uncertainty while making one existing
  incompatibility concrete at rite or obligation level.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not settle identity, expose author terms, invent a
  universal code, or standardize traditions.
- **Dependency:** History and institutional remit if a jurisdiction is selected.
- **Confidence:** High.
