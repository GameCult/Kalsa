# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Pass with findings.** Factual event ownership is strong. Public uses it
unevenly: Low Sere and Sarxe visibly inherit their pasts, while Ti'asantatca,
Jamnai, the Hegemony, and Divided City often arrive as balanced present-tense
summaries despite adopted historical owners. No master chronology is needed.

- Crown Cities carry rival court, priestly, technical, and popular histories.
- `Four Readings` makes transmission historical through changing copies and a
  preserved hostile question.
