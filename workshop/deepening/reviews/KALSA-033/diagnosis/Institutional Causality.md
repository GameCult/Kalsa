# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Pass with findings.** Public is strongest when authority collides at an
actionable boundary. Three overview passages instead compress real institutions
into a cost list, a universal practice, or an infrastructure noun pretending to
govern. All can be repaired in Public without changing hidden canon.

## Protected successes

- Low Sere names participants, jurisdiction, unequal representation, technical
  limits, and refusal; `At the Basin Table` makes those authorities collide.
- The Broken Sluice source distinguishes certification, administrative use,
  compact remedy, records, and actual repair.
- `Relics and Ruins` correctly keeps technical competence from becoming land or
  water ownership and limits compacts to their participants.

## Findings

### IC33-01

- **Priority:** High-value.
- **Location:** `Public/World/Peoples and Polities.md`, Papsenai and
  Ti'asantatca.
- **Evidence:** Public lists workers, cohorts, observers, tenders, households,
  then balances wonder against exhaustion and service debt. `Stormshield
  Circles.md` supplies overlapping handoffs, stop authority, tribal provision,
  and separate civic shelter/evacuation authority.
- **Effect:** A continuously reproduced institution becomes a labor-and-harm
  ledger; readers do not see which failed handoff can break the city.
- **Repair direction:** Name the Stormshield Circles and replace part of the
  inventory with one supported handoff or jurisdictional boundary.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not import procedural detail, settle ranks/native
  titles, or imply one central Papsenai government.
- **Dependency:** Material Life and Ecology; AI-Isms and Prose.
- **Confidence:** High.

### IC33-02

- **Priority:** Essential.
- **Location:** `Public/World/Gods and the Dead.md`, The unquiet dead.
- **Evidence:** Public states that institutions decide what follows and that
  funerals separate five authorities. `Death and Binding Law.md` explicitly
  denies a universal code and describes overlapping jurisdictions; separation
  is a safeguard that funerals may fail to preserve.
- **Effect:** Contested local defense becomes universal narrator law and hides
  the live failure mode: one claimant may collapse body, testimony, property,
  danger, and binding into one authority.
- **Repair direction:** Attribute separation to named kinds of mortuary office,
  settlement, or reform practice, or describe it as a contested safeguard.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not expose metaphysical answers, invent a continental
  court, or imply procedure settles identity.
- **Dependency:** Culture and Situated Knowledge.
- **Confidence:** High.

### IC33-03

- **Priority:** High-value.
- **Location:** `Public/World/Peoples and Polities.md`, The divided city.
- **Evidence:** Public makes “the Sunwall” a governing actor. The hidden owner
  distinguishes the physical Sunwall, Civic Allocation Table, and Sunwall
  Stewardship; allocation and operation are separate.
- **Effect:** Infrastructure pretends to govern and obscures the city's defining
  seam: allocators cannot operate the wall, operators cannot decide allocation.
- **Repair direction:** Replace the Sunwall as actor with supported civic and
  stewardship authorities and expose one allocation/operation boundary.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not reproduce the office chart, settle native names,
  or make the condominium orderly.
- **Dependency:** Reader Experience and Navigation.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Pattern or dependency map

Successful Public causality follows `named actor -> scoped authority -> refused
handoff -> visible consequence`. The three findings weaken that into worker
list -> moral cost, universal procedure -> hidden failure, and structure name
-> governing authority.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not create a standardized institution page or miniature constitution for
every polity. One supported handoff is enough.

## Ranked actions

1. Qualify funeral separation.
2. Give Ti'asantatca one Circle handoff and distinguish Sunwall operation from allocation.
3. Leave Low Sere, the ruin synthesis, Ju'onai authority split, and sources structurally alone.
