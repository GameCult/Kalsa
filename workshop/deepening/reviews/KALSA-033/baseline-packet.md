## In-scope inhabitant model boundaries

- Common travel knowledge may reproduce through roads, hosts, warning marks,
  trade speech, stories, and custom without one formal school.
- Ju'onai opening language belongs to named schools, houses, offices, and the
  client teaching dialogue. Its `bounded`, `claim`, `record`, and causal terms
  are an earned register, not automatic workshop leakage.
- The ruin synthesis explicitly circulates among settlement councils, delving
  companies, shrine keepers, mortuary witnesses, and technical lineages. Its
  questions may be concise and procedural.
- Public uncertainty about Crown Cities, gods, the dead, coldward Intuition,
  and the Low Sere knock is protected. No narrator may settle it.

## Ghostlight lens

- **Point of contact:** the reader can begin at Low Sere's failing intake.
- **Accessible evidence:** warm-water dependency, wet lower rooms, the Grey
  Scald record, disputed warnings, the missing tool and folio, claimant speech,
  and the black door.
- **Supported interventions:** join or supply the descent, carry testimony,
  negotiate compact terms, compare procedure, refuse unsafe work, or follow the
  wider setting surveys.
- **Consequence horizon:** immediate water and heat, wages and injury care,
  mortuary claims, technical custody, appeal, and later settlement authority.
  Nothing in the Public packet reveals what ultimately knocks or the correct
  hidden route.

## Protected strengths

- Root consent gate and Public-only navigation.
- The unmoving-sun opening and familiar high-fantasy spectacle.
- Low Sere's forty-one hearths, black door, Grey Beds, basin authority, and
  unresolved knock.
- `At the Basin Table` and `Four Readings of a Broken Sluice` as situated voices.
- Ju'onai's earned scholarly register and the keeper's refusal.
- Difficult names, partial testimony, non-symmetric cultural coverage, and
  uncertainty that produces discovery rather than fog.
- Genuine functional inventories in the ruins synthesis and compact.

## Known concerns

- Public index offers five survey pages before the first concrete place and
  source.
- `Peoples and Polities.md` raises interest in Ti'asantatca, the Divided City,
  and Saxfoldi without a reader-visible situated continuation.
- Narrator-level versions of `does not establish`, `does not erase`, `may X and
  still Y`, and `not X; Y` recur across Public.
- Ti'asantatca uses the operator-identified praise-then-invoice construction:
  outsiders praise a wonder; inhabitants count exhaustion, missed field work,
  unequal shelter, and inherited service debt.
- Lists of abstract costs and balanced one-paragraph viewpoints can perform
  moral completeness without embodying a mechanism or distinct voice.
- Overview endings sometimes explain the point after the examples already
  carry it.
