# KALSA-039 Candidate 05

## Question

Does this exact candidate make Kalsa answer what a world becomes when
innumerable heterogeneous oracles compete inside the causal field, rather than
merely developing a professional institution for conscious Prophecy?

## Round-four repair

Candidate 05 responds to `OETC39-11` and `OETC39-12`. It assigns Sorcery's
immediate local orientation to the embodied coupled person-Beast relation,
distinguishes that reachable result from a superior's strategy, and preserves
coercion and divided agency as pressure rather than magical consent evidence.
It also adds a current three-bell Ghostlight site in which players can alter
information custody, route, target, material mechanisms, and rival responses.

## Protected limits

- No oracle or God has author omniscience; Kaos creates neither matter nor energy.
- Different Gifts remain asymmetric and partly mutually illegible.
- Luck is not consciously controlled or directly detected.
- Timeline capture is local and relational, not a literal end to possibility.
- Intuition, Gift frequencies, and inheritance remain unresolved.
- Coordination can save lives; independent action can produce catastrophe.
- Public readers receive situated high fantasy, not the author model.
- Forecasts change Ghostlight state and options rather than predetermining dice.
