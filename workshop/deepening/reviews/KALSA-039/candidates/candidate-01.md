# KALSA-039 Candidate 01

## Question

Does this exact candidate make Kalsa answer what a world becomes when
innumerable heterogeneous oracles compete inside the causal field, rather than
merely developing a professional institution for conscious Prophecy?

## Authority

- `Kalsa/Spoilers/Foundations/Oracle Ecology.md` owns interactions among
  differently shaped oracles and bounded timeline capture.
- `Kaos` still owns physical and causal reachability.
- `Magic Styles` still owns the author crosswalk among Gifts.
- `Projections, God Beasts, and Thaumavores` still owns entities, binding, and
  potential routing.
- Polities, institutions, and events own their situated responses and history.
- Public notes own presentation only.

## Source evidence

The critic may inspect the immutable witnesses named by the candidate notes,
especially the seed's statements that observation changes the future, every
person occupies possible volume, folds generate Chaos Beasts, God Beasts absorb
and route other oracles, Luck acts constantly at greatest reach toward survival,
and the seven Gifts expose different parts of causal intervention.

## Protected limits

- No oracle has author omniscience.
- Kaos creates neither matter nor energy.
- Different Gifts remain asymmetric and partly mutually illegible.
- Luck is not consciously controlled or directly detected.
- Timeline capture is local and relational, not a literal end to possibility.
- Intuition remains unresolved.
- Coordination can save lives; independent action can produce catastrophe.
- Public readers receive situated high fantasy, not the author model.
- Forecasts change Ghostlight state and options rather than predetermining dice.
