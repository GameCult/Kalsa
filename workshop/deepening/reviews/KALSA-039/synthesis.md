## Adjudication

All findings and exact repair owners are recorded in
`council/dispositions.md`. No critic report writes canon. Accepted repairs:

- established scale-dependent oracle density and six asymmetric temporal
  transactions;
- made timeline capture useful, local, materially contingent, and politically
  dangerous without making plurality automatically good;
- gave Luck a multigenerational attractor and collision rather than a disguised
  Prophet;
- created independent catastrophe, bounded command, divine partial evidence,
  and a current three-bell Ghostlight collision;
- assigned the courier charge an issuer, support, return copy, and bounded
  change-claim path while separating Nerin's reading from levy signature;
- gave withdrawn shelter grants a physical function and recovery burden;
- carried Sunwall, Narrow Shield, and Empty Bridge through bounded present
  institutions and cultural keepers;
- rebuilt Public navigation and source structures around unequal scenes,
  objects, work, and claims rather than archive-case templates and checksum
  conclusions;
- removed unsupported timing, institutional reform, cultural carrier, and
  continuity claims introduced during repair.

## Protected material

- No Gift becomes a renamed form of Prophecy.
- Luck remains indirect and has the widest reach without global sovereignty.
- Divine intent, Luck attribution, casualty totals, Intuition, Gift frequency,
  and exact chronology remain unresolved.
- Empty Bridge retains real divine protection and real upstream rescue without
  resolving their conflict.
- Narrow Shield retains the lifesaving and dispossessing effects of bounded
  command.
- The Second Gate has no preferred route or result, and Public receives only
  the courier threshold.
- Grain and Road retain their earned document and recitation carriers.

## Candidate history

Five Oracle Ecology saturation candidates each blocked and produced a repair.
The later full council then rejected candidates 01 through 06 for remaining
party-contract ownership, material shelter function, public repeated frames,
mechanical timing, unsupported historical reform, unowned cultural specificity,
and false continuity. Candidate 07 passed all eight seats on one exact body.

## Leave alone

Do not add an oracle census, universal anti-magic, a complete inhabitant theory,
a universal court for Second Gate claims, a detected Luck bearer, a moral verdict
on Empty Bridge, a permanent Terjamna recitation tradition, or chronology merely
to make the case histories sound continuous. Further change requires a new pass
and new evidence.

## Final decision

Adopt `KALSA-039-council-07`. The setting now answers its causal question at
foundation, institution, polity, history, Public, and current-play scales. No
accepted finding remains open.
