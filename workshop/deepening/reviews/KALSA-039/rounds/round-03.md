important competitors remain declarative at the point of action.

### OETC39-09 — Sorcery still lacks an explicit temporal transaction

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Spoilers/Foundations/Kaos.md`, **Observing Kaos**;
  `Kalsa/Spoilers/Foundations/Oracle Ecology.md`, **Differently shaped
  oracles**; `Kalsa/Spoilers/Polities/Sorcery - Jamnai and the Terjamna.md`,
  opening and **Office and branch life**; `Kalsa/Spoilers/Events/The Three
  Orders at Sunwall.md`, **The prepared victory** and **The third order**.
- **Evidence:** `Observing Kaos` now specifies the temporal return for Analysis,
  Prophecy, Channeling, Shamanism, and Luck but contains no Sorcery entry.
  `Oracle Ecology` says only that Sorcery “finds a focused intervention under
  immediate pressure” and can “force a locally improbable departure.” The
  polity note says an unconscious perception “selects precise interventions in
  local conflict,” without stating whether a later success, failure,
  counterfactual outcome, conflict pressure, or some other information returns
  to select the present lever. In the complete Sunwall event, the champion's
  Sorcery has not yet selected a point of attack; a Prophetic house tells him to
  take an expected opening, and the Stewardship removes it before he acts.
- **Effect:** Sorcery can still be read as supernatural tactical instinct plus
  branch-forcing rather than a distinct oracle. It changes futures seen by
  other Gifts but is not itself shown receiving anything from a possible
  outcome. This leaves one of the three root strains outside the causal
  comparison at precisely the point where Sorcerous submission is supposed to
  be the most explicit attempt to remove a rival oracle from the field.
- **Repair direction:** Name the smallest temporal transaction that selects a
  Sorcerous act: what near or counterfactual result bears upon unconscious
  present choice, what existing local opening or lever it selects, and what
  ends or defeats the relation. Preserve the absence of a report and conscious
  model. Then let one bounded adopted collision show the selection actually
  fire, so another shape can perceive only its branch deformation, used lever,
  or aftermark rather than the Sorcerer's private operation.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not turn Sorcery into a conscious glimpse of “the
  best move,” make it automatic victory, give it Prophecy's comparison among
  articulated branches, or let it create the opening, matter, and energy its
  intervention requires. Do not collapse it into Luck's continuous survival
  bias.
- **Dependency:** Mechanics and Causal Coherence should test the relation
  against Depth and the hard constraints; Jamnai and an event owner should
  supply the situated evidence and limits.
- **Confidence:** High. The specialist contract explicitly requires what
  information or pressure crosses time for every Gift, and Sorcery is the one
  developed Gift for which Candidate 03 still answers only with selection and
  effect.

## Pattern or dependency map

`OETC39-09` is the remaining Gift-level engine defect: Sorcery needs a temporal
input, present lever, and bounded failure before its effects can participate as
an asymmetric oracle rather than a themed force. `OETC39-10` is the aggregation
defect: God hierarchies already own routing and capture relations, but the
candidate needs one factual act to show how those relations think and fail in a
crowded field. They should not be repaired by making a Sorcerer God a complete
demonstration of both; that would make one showcase bear too much ontology and
leave ordinary Sorcery untested.

## Leave-alone guidance

  between one person and one or several Beasts unresolved.

  the material and institutional pattern that remains after causal certainty

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not repair both findings by making a Sorcerer God receive a crisp vision of
the winning move and relay it through a perfectly coordinated cult. That would
turn Sorcery into short-range Prophecy, make God hierarchy a super-oracle that
erases its component shapes, and demonstrate capture only by granting the
captor authorial competence.
