# Oracle Ecology and Timeline Competition — Round 02

## Packet verification

The candidate manifest contains 30 files. Every listed file was read in full,
along with the shared council prompt and the version-controlled Oracle Ecology
and Timeline Competition prompt. Every manifest SHA-1 matched the exact file
body reviewed.

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** Candidate 02 resolves all four round-one findings. The density model
now makes crowded horizons a normal scale-dependent condition without inventing
a census. The Narrow Shield supplies the missing case in which competent
independent interventions recurse toward catastrophe and bounded capture saves
the center at an outer cost. Its public witness breaks Prophecy's monopoly on
the reader's experience, and the shamanic court interface preserves unlike
evidence rather than translating it into forecast branches.

The next mechanical seam is underneath that success: Analysis and Channeling
are declared oracular, but their descriptions still stop at exceptional
present-time perception. The candidate does not say what information or
pressure crosses time for either Gift. Luck also remains the closest analogue
to dominant prescience only in author explanation; no owned historical case
tests its claimed generational reach against other oracles. Cross-polity
exchange remains a third substantive edge where the new density rule has not
yet acquired an institutional consequence.

## Regression of prior findings

### OETC39-01 — Resolved

`Oracle Ecology`, **Density without a census**, separates scarce strong
forecasting from weak Gifts, bound Projections, Beasts, divine routes, inherited
oracular decisions, and the actors reached by a question. Its scale ladder
states when household, road, campaign, and polity horizons should be considered
crowded while preserving speed, secrecy, isolation, exhaustion, and genuinely
new systems as sparse openings. It does not settle Gift frequency or make every
person a mage.

### OETC39-02 — Resolved

`The Narrow Shield` supplies the missing factual counterexample. Shamanic and
Channeler interventions protect their accepted bodies, return changed pressure
through one another, outrun separate signals, and make locally competent action
collectively catastrophic. The narrowing order succeeds by controlling
signals, targets, stores, shelter, and independent boundary changes for one
watch. Its survival benefit, outer losses, expiry, retained record power, and
later political misuse are all present.

### OETC39-03 — Resolved

The complete Public source `The Narrow Shield` contains no Prophetic report and
does not expose the author taxonomy. Circle tenders, outer households, and
Sarxe route keepers preserve different practical inheritances from one event.
The Public index and both surveys route readers to it.

### OETC39-04 — Resolved

`Forecast Evidence and Courts`, **A held future entering forecast litigation**,
gives the shaman or working group ownership of its target, material commitment,
sensed pressure, intervention, and release conditions. It states what the court
can infer, what the bond cannot prove, and how one material act can expire both
the bond account and a Prophetic report. The repair remains a bounded interface,
not a universal magic tribunal.

## Protected successes

## Findings

### OETC39-05 — Analysis and Channeling still lack a specified temporal return

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Spoilers/Foundations/Oracle Ecology.md`, **Differently
  shaped oracles**; `Kalsa/Spoilers/Foundations/Kaos.md`, opening and
  **Observing Kaos**; `Kalsa/Spoilers/Polities/Ju'onai Hegemony.md`, opening;
  `Kalsa/Spoilers/Polities/Channeling - Sarxe.md`, **Channeling and its
  boundary**.
- **Evidence:** Luck continuously biases variation toward later survival;
  Sorcery selects an improbable departure; Shamanism senses future conflict
  around a bonded target; Prophecy compares possible states. Analysis is
  described as exposing joins, dependencies, aftermarks, and present levers.
  Channeling perceives active energy, momentum, and magical influence as flows
  and predicts “from local dynamics.” None of the reviewed owners identifies
  what future information or pressure returns to the present for either Gift,
  how far ahead that return operates, or what distinguishes it from perfect
  causal comprehension and extraordinary real-time sensing.
- **Effect:** Calling every Gift oracular remains partly declarative. Analysis
  can be read as supernatural engineering and Channeling as flow perception
  plus redirection, with Prophecy, Luck, Sorcery, and Shamanism doing the actual
  work across possibility. The ecology then contains several magical powers
  that affect a Prophet's forecast rather than several differently shaped
  oracles.
- **Repair direction:** In the author foundations, state the minimal temporal
  transaction for Analysis and Channeling: what later state, imminent
  consequence, or counterfactual pressure informs the present perception; the
  present lever it exposes; and the horizon or boundary that defeats it. Then
  ensure each situated polity retains evidence appropriate to that mechanism
  without acquiring the crosswalk. One or two precise causal sentences per
  Gift are enough.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not turn an opening into a conscious Prophetic vision,
  let aftermarks identify hidden actors, let a Channeler sense remote or dormant
  pressure, or add a universal future-sense shared by all Gifts.
- **Dependency:** Mechanics and Causal Coherence should check the Kaos axis
  interpretation; the two polity owners should preserve their local models.
- **Confidence:** High. The specialist prompt requires the temporal crossing for
  every Gift, and the current descriptions provide it clearly for some but not
  these two.

### OETC39-06 — Luck's exceptional reach has no owned historical collision

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Spoilers/Foundations/Oracle Ecology.md`, **Luck and large
  attractors**; `Kalsa/Spoilers/Polities/Luck - Selza'a.md`, **Long fortune and
  territorial survival**, **Mutual defense**, and **Historical consequence:
  the Broken Windtrap Settlement**; `Kalsa/Spoilers/Reference/Timeline.md`.
- **Evidence:** The foundation claims that failures to kill a Luck Beast,
  meetings, storms, routes, descendants, and rivals' errors can accumulate into
  a vast historical attractor. Selza'a gives strong ordinary mechanisms—family
  survival histories, marriage networks, detours, feud, and an unconquered
  terrain—but its only named adopted consequence is a shared windtrap failure
  and restitution dispute. The timeline records Luck's emergence and the
  Luck-ring rebellion without attributing their outcomes to Luck, and it
  contains no event in which a large attractor encounters a rival oracular
  shape across generations.
- **Effect:** The Gift singled out as closest to life under dominant prescience
  remains the least falsified major claim in the candidate. Its reach is vivid
  in summary but has not reorganized a concrete history whose rival evidence,
  adaptations, beneficiaries, and displaced costs can be inspected. A reader
  can still treat Selza'a's survival as fog, mountains, competence, and author
  insistence that Luck also mattered.
- **Repair direction:** Give an existing Selza'a or cross-polity owner one
  bounded historical collision in which opponents adapt repeatedly to a
  territorial or lineage survival pattern and their adaptations become new
  levers for it. Preserve multiple records so nobody identifies Luck directly.
  The event should show a rival Gift perceiving deformation or causal scars,
  changing strategy, and still being unable to distinguish attractor from
  accumulated competence and geography.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not declare that Luck caused the Fall, prove which
  person carries it, make the Luck Crown unconquerable by metaphysical fiat, or
  erase material defense and ordinary skill.
- **Dependency:** Historical Depth should protect event ownership; Culture and
  Lived Difference should keep Selza'a traditions incompatible rather than
  resolving the attractor for them.
- **Confidence:** High. Candidate 02 gives the exceptional-reach claim more
  weight than any owned history currently tests.

### OETC39-07 — Cross-polity exchange has not inherited the crowded-horizon rule

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Spoilers/Foundations/Kaos.md`, opening; `Kalsa/Spoilers/
  Foundations/Oracle Ecology.md`, **Density without a census**; `Kalsa/Spoilers/
  Institutions/The Potential Economy.md`, **Brokerage, tribute, and conquest**;
  `Kalsa/Spoilers/Institutions/Prophetic Houses.md`, **Material support, pay,
  and assignment**; `Kalsa/Spoilers/Polities/Shamanism - Papsenai.md`, opening
  and **The Alliance source promise**.
- **Evidence:** Kaos names markets as susceptible systems, and the density model
  says harvests, dynastic marriages, imperial levies, and polity-scale plans are
  normally crowded. The Potential Economy explains brokerage inside divine
  allocation, not ordinary exchange. Prophetic Houses list merchant contracts
  as income without showing how disclosure and response alter a bargain.
  Papsenai surplus makes neighbors dependent, but the actual protector,
  compact, and market arrangements remain candidate or unresolved.
- **Effect:** Warfare, emergency infrastructure, law, religion, kinship, and
  migration now bear the premise. Trade and diplomacy can still operate like
  ordinary bargains with magical consultants attached. Yet prices, delivery
  routes, warranties, tribute offers, and treaty concessions are exactly the
  kind of announced future action that should enter rivals' causal fields.
- **Repair direction:** Choose one existing bounded exchange—merchant forecast
  service, Papsenai grain delivery, hosted oracle service, or miracle brokerage—
  and show its input, announcement, rival response, changed route, settlement,
  and characteristic failure. One functioning transaction is preferable to a
  catalogue of oracle-aware market offices.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not invent a continent-wide market, universal
  currency, working Alliance, futures exchange, or modern finance terminology.
- **Dependency:** Institutional Depth should own the transaction; Play
  Consequence should verify that players can alter information or delivery
  without negotiating reality.
- **Confidence:** High. The causal premise explicitly names markets and
  diplomacy, while the reviewed owners provide inputs and claims but no
  complete interaction.

### OETC39-08 — “No one predicts” briefly reinstates Prophecy's vocabulary boundary

- **Priority:** Optional
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/index.md`, final paragraph.
- **Evidence:** The index introduces the new non-Prophetic source with “No one
  predicts the Narrow Shield,” although Papsenai workers are holding possible
  futures and sensing pressure before outcome. `Magic and Miracles` uses the
  more exact “No Prophet appears.”
- **Effect:** The shorter entrance line can imply that prediction belongs only
  to Prophets and that shamanic future relation becomes prediction only in the
  author model. It is a small recurrence of the framing Candidate 02 otherwise
  repairs.
- **Repair direction:** Use the already accurate distinction from the survey:
  no Prophet issues a forecast, while the involved practitioners still work
  through what happens next.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not introduce `oracle`, `non-propositional`, or other
  author-facing comparative language at the Public entrance.
- **Dependency:** Reader Experience may decide whether the sentence actually
  misleads a blind reader.
- **Confidence:** Medium. The source itself corrects the impression immediately,
  so this is a navigation-level wording risk rather than a lore defect.

## Pattern or dependency map

`OETC39-05` concerns the engine: two Gifts need a temporal transaction before
the comparison is fully causal rather than taxonomic. `OETC39-06` concerns the
longest-range promised output of that engine. `OETC39-07` concerns an ordinary
cross-polity domain where the new density rule should become visible. They are
separate repairs; a Luck-history event should not be forced to double as the
setting's only market transaction.

## Leave-alone guidance

- Leave Gift prevalence, inheritance rates, and the person-to-Beast relation
  unresolved. The density model now does its job without those answers.
- Leave the Narrow Shield's casualty count, local names, Sarxe compact status,
  and exact storm behavior unresolved. The causal sequence does not need
  administrative completion.
- Leave the Three Orders at Sunwall intact. The new pro-capture stress case
  complements it; neither should be rewritten to make one authority design the
  universal answer.
- Leave Intuition unresolved and do not use it as a convenient explanation for
  cross-polity exchange.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not repair `OETC39-05` by saying every Gift “sees possible futures” and then
varying only its range. That would make the seven Gifts a themed ladder of
Prophecy, erase their distinct evidence, and undo the best work in this pass.

## Ranked actions

1. Specify the temporal return that makes Analysis and Channeling genuinely
   oracular while preserving their existing boundaries.
2. Give Luck's generational reach one owned, contested historical collision.
3. Make one cross-polity transaction recursively oracle-aware instead of
   adding a generic market system.
4. Replace the Public index's “No one predicts” with the narrower absence of a
   Prophet or forecast.
