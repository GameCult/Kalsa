# Oracle Ecology and Timeline Competition — Round 01

## Packet verification

The candidate manifest contains 26 files. Every listed file was read in full,
along with the shared council prompt and the version-controlled Oracle Ecology
and Timeline Competition prompt. Each manifest SHA-1 matched the exact file
body reviewed.

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** Candidate 01 establishes a coherent author model for heterogeneous
oracular interaction and distributes useful situated fragments into several
polities. It does not yet make the motivating plurality true at world scale.
The number and social distribution of oracles remain unspecified, while the
only fully adopted cross-oracle stress case demonstrates divided authority
resisting capture. Catastrophe caused by independent oracles and safety bought
through bounded capture remain assertions. The Public candidate consequently
still lets a reader understand Prophecy as the principal future-facing power
and the other Gifts as complications around it.

## Protected successes

- `Kalsa/Spoilers/Foundations/Oracle Ecology.md`, **Differently shaped
  oracles**, gives every developed Gift a distinct operation and follows that
  distinction into asymmetric collisions. The sentence limiting a block to an
  actually closed route, consumed or redirected potential, removed lever,
  changed system, or obsolete question is especially important. Preserve it.
- The same note's **Timeline capture** section gives capture a named horizon,
  material and informational requirements, and contingent failure conditions.
  It neither turns prescience into destiny nor treats hierarchy as inherently
  incompetent.
- The polity notes do not simply distribute a translation of the author term.
  Divided City comparers have crowded horizons, Papsenai distinguish held from
  unheld possibilities, Sarxe ask what crossed a boundary, Selza'a preserve
  contested survival histories, and Ju'onai schools fight over concordance and
  edges. These are genuinely situated partial models.
- `The Three Orders at Sunwall` makes material operation a causal intervention
  rather than scenery. The stewards do not win an oracle ranking; they change
  the loaded system upon which two predictions depended. That mechanism should
  survive every repair.

## Findings

### OETC39-01 — The candidate never establishes the population ecology promised by “innumerable”

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Spoilers/Foundations/Oracle Ecology.md`, opening through
  **Crowded horizons**; `Kalsa/Spoilers/Foundations/Magic Styles.md`,
  **Expression and descent**; `Kalsa/Spoilers/Institutions/Prophetic
  Houses.md`, **Material support, pay, and assignment**.
- **Evidence:** The foundation opens with “people, Projections, Chaos Beasts,
  God hierarchies, and institutions,” then defines a crowded horizon
  conditionally: it occurs when independent branch-sensitive actors can respond
  within it. The crosswalk leaves expression frequency and the relation between
  people and Beasts unresolved. Prophetic labor is explicitly scarce. No owner
  states how common weak expression, Beasts, divine routing, or indirect
  oracle participation is, nor why an ordinary polity-scale plan should expect
  many heterogeneous oracles rather than the occasional elite contest.
- **Effect:** The mechanics support a world containing rare magical specialists
  who sometimes interfere. They do not yet compel the “ten thousand Pauls”
  world that motivated the pass. Crowding can be read as an exceptional hazard
  around courts, gods, and wars instead of the normal causal condition from
  which Kalsan institutions evolved.
- **Repair direction:** Give `Oracle Ecology` the smallest qualitative scale
  model needed to distinguish strong conscious experts from the much larger
  field of weak, indirect, bound, unconscious, and institutionally coordinated
  interventions. State what kinds of horizon are normally crowded, what kinds
  can still be sparse, and why powerful actors cannot assume an unwatched
  population. Carry that model into one ordinary-life owner so it has an
  observable consequence outside elite forecasting.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not set numerical inheritance rates, decide whether
  every mage has a Beast, make every person Gifted, make strong Prophecy common,
  or erase places where isolation and surprise are possible.
- **Dependency:** Magic Styles owns inheritance uncertainty; polity and
  institution owners must supply the situated consequences.
- **Confidence:** High. The motivating question uses innumerability as the
  counterfactual change, while the candidate supplies interaction rules without
  a density rule.

### OETC39-02 — The concrete evidence makes plurality prudent and capture hypothetical

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Spoilers/Foundations/Oracle Ecology.md`, **Timeline
  capture**, **Keeping futures independent**, and **Historical and political
  pressure**; both versions of `The Three Orders at Sunwall`; `Kalsa/Spoilers/
  Polities/Prophecy - The Divided City.md`, **A horizon no claimant owns**.
- **Evidence:** The foundation explicitly says capture can provide real
  coordination and independent actors can converge upon catastrophe. Its only
  examples are conditional summaries: a city “may survive” under one defensive
  hierarchy, while the War of the Magi receives one paragraph without a factual
  event owner. The candidate's fully adopted stress case runs the other way:
  two imperial plans omit a material owner, the independent steward closes both
  routes, and later practice divides forecast, judgment, operation, and force.
  The closure causes ration pressure, but no bounded capture is shown saving
  lives and no independent oracle cascade is followed through to catastrophe.
- **Effect:** Despite its explicit caveat, the repaired setting teaches a
  plurality moral: divided authority generates the successful example, while
  coordinated capture belongs to the villain's toolkit or to abstract
  possibility. That blunts the premise's hardest consequence. In a dense
  oracle ecology, surrendering independent response should sometimes be the
  only reachable rescue, and preserving it should sometimes kill people.
- **Repair direction:** Add one factual stress mechanism to an existing owner
  in which several honest, locally rational oracular responses recursively
  worsen the field and a named, bounded hierarchy saves something by temporarily
  controlling information, levers, and response. Record what future that
  coordination forecloses, who pays for it, how its scope expires or persists,
  and why its success becomes evidence later captors can weaponize. This can be
  integrated into an existing event or polity history; it does not require a
  new continent-wide institution.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not turn capture into a benevolent technocracy,
  declare divided authority foolish, or imply that the successful commander
  possessed a truer view from outside the field.
- **Dependency:** Historical Depth should check the event ownership and later
  institutional inheritance; Play Consequence should test the resulting choice.
- **Confidence:** High. The asymmetry is visible between the candidate's stated
  protected limit and the kind of event it actually adopts.

### OETC39-03 — The Public encounter remains Prophecy-shaped

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/index.md`, opening and **Read another argument**;
  `Kalsa/Public/World/Magic and Miracles.md`, **When futures crowd**;
  `Kalsa/Public/Sources/The Three Orders at Sunwall.md`.
- **Evidence:** The entrance advertises that “Prophets dispute futures in
  court.” The survey does say other works reach through what happens next, but
  its shared comparison is the Prophetic Houses' term *crowded horizon*. The
  only complete public causal collision in this candidate begins with two
  sponsored house forecasts and teaches why their reports must retain other
  orders. Luck appears as a family living inside unlocatable protection;
  Sorcery, Shamanism, and Channeling receive one-sentence examples rather than
  a source-owned encounter.
- **Effect:** A casual player can still leave with Prophecy as the real
  timeline power and the other Gifts as magic that incidentally changes a
  prophet's forecast. The hidden author model is safe, but the public fantasy
  does not yet answer the premise through non-propositional experience.
- **Repair direction:** Give the public path one concrete situated source or
  ordinary-life passage in which no one issues a forecast: for example, rival
  survival histories, an inherited bond, a redirected crossing, or a forced
  opening recursively changes what several parties can do. Its owner should
  explain the event through local evidence and characteristic error, leaving
  the author comparison reconstructible rather than stated.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not publish the Gift crosswalk, name oracle ecology or
  timeline capture, make a local witness identify Luck directly, or turn the
  new source into a seven-Gift demonstration tableau.
- **Dependency:** Reader Experience should receive the repaired public note
  blind; Culture and Lived Difference should test the source owner.
- **Confidence:** High for this exact candidate. Other public files are outside
  the immutable manifest and cannot cure the reviewed path.

### OETC39-04 — Forecast law acknowledges the wider ecology but still governs only Prophecy

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Spoilers/Institutions/Forecast Evidence and Courts.md`,
  **The forecast record**, **Luck**, and **Unresolved**.
- **Evidence:** Reviewers are told to record known “prophets, Gods, shamans,
  Channelers, or other scoped distortions,” but every admissibility, rivalry,
  appeal, and remedy rule is built around a forecast report. Luck receives one
  careful negative evidentiary rule. The treatment of divine commands,
  shamanic bonds, Analysis reports, and Channeler observations when combined
  with prophecy remains explicitly unresolved.
- **Effect:** The candidate's deepest mature legal institution still reduces
  heterogeneous oracle competition to complications inside a professional
  Prophecy procedure. That is exactly the administrative domestication the
  pass is meant to outgrow. A party affected by a long-held field future, a
  redirected influence, or an Analysis intervention has no equally concrete
  evidentiary path.
- **Repair direction:** Do not build a universal magic court. Add one bounded
  interface rule showing how the existing court receives a non-prophetic
  oracular claim without pretending it is a forecast: who owns the observation,
  what material evidence travels with it, what it cannot prove, and how its
  intervention can expire the Prophetic record. Leave the other combinations
  unresolved.
- **Collateral risk:** Avoid a single evidentiary template that makes unlike
  Gifts commensurable or gives judges author-level categories.
- **Dependency:** Institutional Depth owns procedure; the relevant Gift polity
  owns its evidence practice.
- **Confidence:** High. The unresolved list names the missing interface
  directly.

## Pattern or dependency map

`OETC39-01` is the root. Without a qualitative density model, downstream notes
can treat cross-oracle interaction as an optional complication. `OETC39-02`
then controls the moral range of the engine: both independent catastrophe and
successful capture must become factual, not merely cautions. `OETC39-03` and
`OETC39-04` are two projections of the same remaining Prophecy exceptionalism,
one in reader experience and one in law.

## Leave-alone guidance

- Leave the formal Gift taxonomy, axes, and inheritance unknowns in the author
  surface. The repair does not need a new universal measure of oracle strength.
- Leave Intuition unresolved. Filling the seventh row merely to complete the
  ecology would convert an honest seed absence into false symmetry.
- Preserve the Sunwall steward's material competence and inability to decide
  the war. The missing counterexample should not be created by reversing that
  event's judgment.
- Preserve Luck's direct undetectability. Density must be inferred through
  historical and institutional exposure, not solved by a new detection power.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not answer `OETC39-01` with a population table, universal magical census, or
the claim that every ordinary person is secretly an oracle. Those moves would
settle inheritance questions, flatten weak and strong expression, and give the
author a legibility that the setting's powers themselves do not possess.

## Ranked actions

1. Establish the qualitative density and distribution that makes crowded
   causal fields normal at polity scale while preserving scarce strong Gifts.
2. Adopt one concrete case where recursive plurality causes catastrophe and
   bounded capture provides real safety at a real cost.
3. Give the Public path a non-propositional, non-Prophecy-owned encounter with
   competing futures.
4. Add one non-prophetic evidence interface to the Divided City's law instead
   of pretending a forecast form can contain every Gift.
