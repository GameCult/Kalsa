## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** Candidate 05 resolves both round-four findings without reopening the
first ten. Sorcery now has an adjudicable local goal owner that neither turns
orders into magical authority nor treats coercion as consent. The Ash-Valve
Refusal separates a successful reachable break from the superior strategy it
failed to advance. The Second Gate Understore supplies the missing current
Ghostlight field: three asymmetric oracular relations, several ordinary
authorities, material levers, information custody, a clock, and resolver-owned
outcomes.

The new site exposes two last owner-level defects in its information ecology.
Its Public direct-entry source gives a casual reader the exact later plans and
technical warning which the author dossier says no actor initially holds
together and which play is supposed to assemble or conceal. Inside the dossier,
the levy response is also keyed first to “the route named in the opened report”
and later to the announced route, although the sealed comparison names two
reachable routes and leaves route choice to the buyer. Those are different
information paths with different player consequences. The fifth loop therefore
finds the premise mechanically saturated but not yet safely and consistently
projected through this live site.

  is the hidden response list presented as the party's starting view.
- **Effect:** A casual Public reader receives the main discoveries that make
  information custody causal at the table. The site asks characters to assemble
  a crowded horizon while its spoiler-safe entry has already assembled it for
  their players. That weakens the exact live-play repair to OETC39-12 and crosses
  the repository's intended boundary between reader projection and the hidden
  state used by the resolver.
- **Repair direction:** Keep the direct high-fantasy entrance, but give the
  Public note one legitimate epistemic position at the first bell: the sealed

  the waiting carts, and the bell schedule. Do not solve the issue with a prose

- **Dependency:** Reader Experience and Navigation should test the revised page

  starting knowledge matches the handout.
- **Confidence:** High. The Public note and hidden dossier state incompatible
  starting information for the same second-person party.

## Pattern or dependency map

Both findings concern the same live invariant: information changes the field
only when a named actor receives it through a named path. `OETC39-14` leaves
that path ambiguous inside author state. `OETC39-13` then gives the combined
state to the Public reader without any path at all. Repair the hidden trigger
first, then project only the information a situated Public owner can carry.

controlling partial knowledge. It would also make the levy response easy by
fiat rather than giving it an observable signal path.
