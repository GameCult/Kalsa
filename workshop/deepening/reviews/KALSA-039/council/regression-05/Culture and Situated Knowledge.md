  recitation. Across the attested Public and hidden corpus, the phrase occurs

  recitation—without an adopted Terjamna keeper or reproduction path. That is
  the precise flattening risk this seat tests: cultures differentiated by decorative
  delivery labels rather than their owned social memory.

  account,” or equivalent—unless the coordinator deliberately adopts a
  marching recitation, its keeper/repetition context, and its characteristic
  limit in the proper cultural or event owner. The former is the minimum repair.

  leverage, or make the Ju'onai and Terjamna afterlives symmetrical in form or

  for the original repair boundary.
- **Confidence:** High. The exact candidate files support generic Terjamna
  accounts but not the newly named carrier, and repository-wide exact search
