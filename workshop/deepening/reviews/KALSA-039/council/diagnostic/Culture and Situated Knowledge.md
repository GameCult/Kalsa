# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Pass with findings.** The combined 40-file manifest matches
`KALSA-039-council-01`, including both replacement bodies. The author model is
correctly fenced inside `Oracle Ecology`, while the six developed Gifts acquire
different inhabitant vocabularies, institutions, evidence practices, and blind
spots. The five adopted histories and the live Second Gate situation generally
reproduce knowledge through specific archives, recitations, work records,
contracts, testimony, household memory, and current custody.

Two Public handoffs remain under-situated. `Magic and Miracles` briefly presents
the cross-Gift comparison from an unowned narrator before attributing “crowded
horizon” to house comparers. `The Empty Bridge Vigil` announces four surviving
records but leaves the ridge account's keeper unnamed, then gives only temples
a later interpretive afterlife. These are high-value presentation repairs, not
grounds to flatten the model or invent missing cultures.

## Protected successes

- `Oracle Ecology` is explicitly author-facing and repeatedly denies
  inhabitants its universal comparison. It owns interaction invariants without
  stealing polity vocabulary, evidence, or interpretation.
- The developed traditions do not merely rename one taxonomy. Ju'onai readers
  discuss “what will bear”; Jamnai champions dispute “the answer in the
  motion”; Papsenai records distinguish an unheld possibility from a future a
  household has begun paying for; Sarxe workers describe “arrival already in
  the crossing”; Divided City comparers use “crowded horizon”; Selza'a
  recitations refuse to settle Luck into one observable doctrine.
- `The Road That Returned` gives exemplary cultural reproduction: an imperial
  road-office archive stands beside three materially unequal plateau
  recitations from a family, guide lineage, and displaced household. Their
  disagreement changes route, water, wages, refuge, and claim.
- `The Narrow Shield` preserves different afterlives among Circle leads, outer
  communities, and Sarxe route practice. Shared catastrophe does not manufacture
  shared memory or equal stakes.
- `The Second Gate Understore` gives named people bounded knowledge and
  pre-existing interests. Pera's working group has reserve and witnesses;
  Sela has a ward and contract; Daro has a maintained gate body; Iven and Nerin
  own different reports rather than ethnic omniscience. The party enters their
  relations instead of causing them to exist.

## Findings

### CSK39-01 — The Public cross-Gift synthesis briefly lacks an inhabitant owner

- **Finding ID:** CSK39-01
- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/World/Magic and Miracles.md#When futures crowd`,
  first two paragraphs.
- **Evidence:** The section first groups Selza'a fortune, Jamnai championing,
  Papsenai held seasons, and Sarxe boundaries under the narrator's claim that
  Prophets are not the only people whose work reaches through what happens
  next. Only afterward does it attribute “crowded horizon” to Divided City
  house comparers. The hidden owners establish that house comparers can record
  other responsive actors inside a forecast, but also that this vocabulary can
  excuse a poor question, conceal briefing custody, and cannot classify every
  Gift. `Oracle Ecology` alone owns the complete cross-Gift comparison.
- **Effect:** Public readers briefly receive the pass's author model as neutral
  synthesis. The house term supplies local color but not a clear evidence or
  reproduction path for the four-polity list, so a bounded analogue risks
  inheriting author-wide scope.
- **Repair direction:** Make the cross-style paragraph explicitly one bounded
  comparison drawn by house comparers from named reports, or let the linked
  cases carry most of the list before introducing their term and characteristic
  limit. The smallest repair is attribution, not additional taxonomy.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not publish `Oracle Ecology`, invent a continent-wide
  magical council, make Divided City houses neutral experts on every Gift, or
  remove the useful “crowded horizon” term and its archive practice.
- **Dependency:** Public presentation owner; Divided City polity and Prophetic
  Houses for the situated model.
- **Confidence:** High; the owner boundary and house blind spot are explicit in
  the candidate.

### CSK39-02 — The ridge account loses its keeper and cultural afterlife

- **Finding ID:** CSK39-02
- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/Sources/The Empty Bridge Vigil.md`, opening claim
  of four records, “The ridge account,” and “The bridge account,” final two
  paragraphs.
- **Evidence:** The cult account has watchers and a divine intermediary; the
  upstream account has a mill settlement and Channeler; the bridge account has
  keepers and material records. The ridge account follows evacuees, shelter
  admission, incomplete lists, and deaths but does not state who preserved it.
  The aftermath then says what later temples copied. The factual owner already
  establishes that the low settlement preserves its warning, movement, and
  people missing at each shelter, while shelter households retain claims for
  stores, labor, and losses.
- **Effect:** The people whose route lengthened and whose dead remain missing
  become evidence inside a temple argument rather than custodians of their own
  account. This is the one source where subordinate testimony approaches a
  moral cost ledger instead of a reproduced social memory.
- **Repair direction:** Attribute the ridge account to the low settlement's
  surviving record or its route/shelter households, and carry that keeper or
  outstanding claim once into the aftermath. Use only the owner-backed warning,
  movement, missing-person, store, labor, and loss claims already present.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not name the unresolved settlement or shelters,
  supply a casualty count, decide whether the third shelter refused entry, or
  make the low-settlement record the complete verdict on the miracle.
- **Dependency:** Empty Bridge event owner and Public source presentation.
- **Confidence:** High; the hidden owner supplies the missing custodian without
  requiring invention.

## Pattern or dependency map

The healthy path is:

`author interaction invariant -> polity vocabulary and social interest ->
institutional evidence/reproduction -> event collision -> bounded Public
carrier`.

The two weak handoffs are exact:

- `Oracle Ecology cross-Gift comparison -> unowned Public narrator` before the
  house comparator is named.
- `low-settlement warning and missing-person record -> anonymous ridge account`
  before temple interpretations inherit the event.

Elsewhere the path holds: steward copies, house archives, Circle records,
family recitations, guide lineage memory, merchant delivery copies, covenant
ledgers, and the courier's live custody keep knowledge culturally located.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not give every Gift an equally polished native theory, force the six
traditions into a symmetrical symposium, or invent names, calendar, ritual,
humor, kinship structures, and offices merely to decorate the interaction map.
Do not settle which dimna acted, what the Soiru'i intended, which shield choice
was morally correct, whether the God chose well, or what Iven's sealed report
says. Opacity is working where evidence and consequence remain available.
