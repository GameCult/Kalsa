# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** The combined council manifest matches all 40 raw SHA-1 digests, including both council replacements. The institutional ecology is otherwise unusually strong: observation, judgment, material operation, custody, and force repeatedly remain with different owners, and their refusals create consequences instead of frictionless consensus. The blocking seam is narrower and sits at the player boundary. The current courier charge gives the party duties and limits without establishing who commissioned and supports them, who answers for extraordinary labor or damaged borrowed equipment, or where their preserved copies return. A second, nonblocking seam lets Nerin's analytical competence shade into levy-signaling jurisdiction without naming the office role that authorizes it.

## Protected successes

- Preserve the Second Gate's divided owner chain: Iven owns the comparison and expiry, Sela owns the buyer's route choice, Pera and her working-group witnesses own the held target and its release, Daro owns safe operation, and the watch captain makes a disputed coercive claim rather than receiving automatic legitimacy.
- Preserve the three-bell signal mechanism. Reed Ward's public slate changes porter movement and the levy response; withholding, correcting, or conflicting records have different institutional effects; no record becomes a verdict by existing.
- Preserve the cross-note separation between author comparison and situated institutions. `Oracle Ecology` names invariants, while the Divided City, Papsenai practices, Prophetic Houses, courts, Stormshield Circles, and event owners carry distinct procedures and historical consequences.
- Preserve local variation and failed handoffs: house response lists, Papsenai route/target distinctions, narrowing orders, covenant appeals, and potential audits are explicitly non-universal and retain unequal access, refusal, and enforcement limits.

## Findings

### IC39-01 — The party's charge has duties but no reproducing institution

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/Sources/At the Second Gate.md`, opening and paragraph beginning “Your written charge is short”; `Kalsa/Spoilers/Dungeons/The Second Gate Understore.md`, `Site card`, `What the party can change` (`Route` and `Material system`), and `Continuing consequences`; compare `Kalsa/Spoilers/Institutions/Forecast Evidence and Courts.md`, `Player and delving consequences`.
- **Evidence:** The threshold copy names the object, first recipient, custody limits, and several powers the escort does not possess, but it does not identify the issuer of the original charge, compensation or ordinary support, a safety limit, or a return forum. The hidden site then offers hand-carriage “outside the buyer's original pay record” and intervention using “someone else's claimed equipment.” Its aftermath says Reed Ward *may* owe wages or restitution and that the party's copies determine which claims can be heard, but names no initial payer, claim recipient, or institution obliged to hear those copies. Elsewhere in the same packet, a bounded adventurer commission is expected to name compensation and return forum.
- **Effect:** The grain, forecast, gate, bond, levy, and watch all have owners, but the party's own labor appears by narrative necessity. A resolver must invent who hired them, who feeds or pays them, who bears injury or equipment loss, and where compliance or breach is returned. This weakens the most immediate institutional bargain in the playable site and makes preservation of records procedural color until a forum is improvised.
- **Repair direction:** Give the charge the smallest situated institutional origin and settlement path: identify the commissioning owner or represented party, who initially owes agreed support/pay, where the charge and preserved changes are returned, and how extraordinary labor, injury, or use of another owner's equipment becomes a claim. Allocation can remain disputed after the fact; the site only needs a first responsible owner and handoff.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not turn the courier into a civic officer, grant ownership of the grain/report/gate, invent a universal adventurers' bureau, or resolve the city's wider legal forum. The Public copy should reveal only terms the escort would actually know; hidden consequences can retain contested liability.
- **Dependency:** Ghostlight Play and Consequence for whether the delivered terms are sufficient at the table; Public/hidden presentation ownership for spoiler-safe division of the charge.
- **Confidence:** High. The packet explicitly names out-of-record labor and claim-bearing copies while withholding the payer and return owner that would make either reproduce.

### IC39-02 — Nerin's opening and levy-signaling authority are not cleanly joined

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Spoilers/Dungeons/The Second Gate Understore.md`, `Site card`, `The live field`, `Intended responses`, and `What the party can change` (`Information`).
- **Evidence:** Nerin is introduced as the Ju'onai oracle who opened the weigh mechanism. The text correctly says his reading grants no authority over grain or gate. It then says “Nerin's levy office” holds the conditional order and that Nerin must send a signed correction through its runner, without stating whether Nerin is also a levy officer, is an authorized signatory for this order, or must carry another officer's correction. The Divided City and Ju'onai polity notes elsewhere insist that expertise, certification, office, and material custody are separate authorities.
- **Effect:** At the exact point where Analysis enters administration, the resolver can reasonably infer that seeing the mechanism makes Nerin competent to redirect the levy crew. That recreates the competence-to-jurisdiction leak the Ju'onai owner notes are built to expose, and makes a forged, refused, or unauthorized correction hard to adjudicate.
- **Repair direction:** State the narrow office relation that makes the correction operative: either Nerin holds a named bounded levy role/signature, or the correction must be obtained from and signed by the responsible levy officer. Preserve his ability to explain the mechanism and his lack of authority over buyer, grain, and gate.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not design the whole levy hierarchy, settle native office names, or make the office's requisition/inspection claim lawful merely by clarifying its signal path.
- **Dependency:** None beyond the existing Divided City/Ju'onai authority boundary.
- **Confidence:** High. The ambiguity occurs in the operative conditional order, not in harmless unresolved nomenclature.

## Pattern or dependency map

- **Working institutional chain:** house comparison → buyer choice → public slate → porter/crowd response; Papsenai bond → two working-group witnesses → release or retargeting; Daro's custody → operational stop; captain's announcement → disputed attempted requisition.
- **Broken party chain:** unnamed issuer → bounded courier charge → optional extraordinary labor and borrowed equipment → preserved copies → unspecified payer/forum.
- **Ambiguous levy chain:** Nerin's bounded opening → unspecified office capacity/signature → runner → expanded weigh crew. The first arrow needs an authorization boundary, not more magical explanation.

## Tempting edit to resist

- Do not regularize every house, tribe, cult, court, Circle, or imperial office into one standardized bureaucracy. The packet's strongest institutional result is that comparable problems produce local records, customary witnesses, scoped offices, coercive claims, and uneven appeals rather than a universal constitutional machine.

## Ranked actions

1. **Essential:** Complete the party's bounded commission by naming its issuer/represented owner, initial support or pay responsibility, return forum, and the handoff for extraordinary labor, injury, or borrowed-equipment claims.
2. **High-value:** Clarify whether Nerin possesses levy-signaling office authority or must obtain the responsible officer's signature.
3. **Optional:** None. Leave the wider legal forum, office endonyms, exact liability allocation, city identity, and non-universal institutional variation unresolved.
