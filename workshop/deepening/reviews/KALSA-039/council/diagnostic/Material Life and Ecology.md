## Concise diagnosis

**Pass with findings.** KALSA-039 usually makes oracle competition enter
material systems instead of floating above them. Grain has reserves, carts,
porters, gates, spoilage, ration clocks, and wage claims. Storm work has
targets, shifts, signals, shelters, seed, roads, and displaced water. Ju'onai
road correction leaves cuts, depots, damaged catchments, guide relations, and
unpaid work. The Ash-Valve turns a Sorcerous act on a real collar, lug,
handwheel, missing transfer pin, loaded main, and geothermal dependency.

One high-value handoff remains absent. The Empty Bridge Vigil makes withdrawal
of three shelter grants the price of the bridge miracle, but no owner says what
an active shelter grant did to a shelter, its materials, its workers, or its
capacity. The downstream record can name withdrawn beneficiaries while the
withdrawal itself remains materially inert. No other mechanism finding
survives the evidence threshold.

## Findings

### MLE39-01 — The withdrawn shelter grants have beneficiaries but no material function

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Spoilers/Events/The Empty Bridge Vigil.md`, **Evidence
  inside the hierarchy**, **The river changes outside it**, and **Four
  preserved records**; `Kalsa/Spoilers/Institutions/The Potential Economy.md`,
  **The Empty Bridge consequence**; `Kalsa/Spoilers/Institutions/God-Cult
  Covenants.md`, **The Empty Bridge consequence**; `Kalsa/Public/Sources/The
  Empty Bridge Vigil.md`, **The cult account** and **The ridge account**.
- **Evidence:** The cult maintains three approach shelters and minor flood rites.
  Its steward says the finite reserve cannot both reinforce the bridge and keep
  all three shelter grants active. The intermediary withdraws those grants.
  Refugees later reach the shelters; two open ordinary stores and fires, while
  the third lacks workers who went to the bridge. The institutional notes call
  the shelters “unprotected” and preserve the withdrawals as the price of the
  successful bridge grant. No note states what an active grant changed: whether
  it held a roof or approach, excluded water or wind, sustained heat or light,
  extended medical capacity, protected stores, or did some other bounded work.
  The Public omission is not compression; the event owner and both institutional
  consequences omit the same handoff.
- **Material chain:** Finite potential enters recognized miracle routes;
  shelters, workers, stores, and fires form the infrastructure; the grants are
  withdrawn and the reserve is reassigned to bridge piers and deck. The broken
  handoff is between withdrawal and shelter capacity. The event shows hardship
  from the longer ridge route and from the third shelter's absent workers, but
  it cannot show which recovery labor, environmental exposure, or capacity loss
  followed from the grants stopping.
- **Effect:** The ledger gives the withdrawal moral and institutional weight
  without a material payer, place, or recovery mechanism. A steward cannot
  compare the allocation except as “bridge versus three miracles,” and a
  shelter claimant recovering the ledger cannot say what service or protection
  was actually lost. This is the packet's one instance of an automatic misery
  ledger: the cost is named and preserved, but not caused on the page.
- **Repair direction:** In the event owner, give the shelter grants one bounded,
  observable lower-world function and the material lever or labor they assisted.
  The three grants need not be identical. Show what shelter workers had to do,
  could no longer do, or could do only at reduced capacity after withdrawal.
  Carry only the minimum situated consequence into the Public record and the
  institutional summaries. The exact divine mechanism, quantities, and
  counterfactual casualty comparison may remain unresolved.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not make potential create fuel, food, dry clothing, or
  intact structures from nothing. Do not make ordinary stores and fires
  decorative once the grant is named. Do not assign every ridge death to the
  withdrawal, decide that the God chose wrongly, or universalize this shelter
  rite across cults.
- **Dependency:** The event note owns the factual function and failure. The
  Potential Economy owns how stewards record it; God-Cult Covenants owns the
  procedural inheritance; Public owns a source-bounded presentation.
- **Confidence:** High. The complete packet repeats the allocation four times
  while never connecting an active shelter grant to a material change.

## Pattern or dependency map

`MLE39-01` is local to the Empty Bridge chain. The other adopted collisions
connect magical or informational intervention to a specific brace, valve,
road, depot, gate, cart, field, current, shelter list, or labor transfer. The
repair should fill this single missing handoff, not impose a new universal

## Leave-alone guidance

- Leave the divine style, exact miracle effect, reserve quantity, flood volume,
  and casualty distribution unresolved. A concrete shelter dependency does not
  require a complete metaphysics or engineering specification.
- Leave the bridge grant successful and materially real. Its piers, deck,
  scour marks, repair record, and unreachable approach already form a coherent
  chain.
- Leave the upstream spill cut morally and materially mixed. It saves the mill
  settlement, floods the bridge approach, and does not need pollution or an
  additional downstream disaster added for balance.
- Leave the Second Gate's quantities and bell duration unresolved. Its active
  chain already distinguishes cart order, hand movement, mechanism wear,
  labor, ration timing, and custody well enough for its function.
- Protect magical abundance and spectacle where the packet earns them. The
  material standard does not require every miracle to reassert scarcity through
  soot, disease, or exhaustion.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not invent a standard “divine shelter field” used by every cult and then
explain it as a force wall. One local rite with one observable dependency is
enough. A universal package would flatten divine styles, overexplain the vigil,
and turn a missing material handoff into generic fantasy infrastructure.
