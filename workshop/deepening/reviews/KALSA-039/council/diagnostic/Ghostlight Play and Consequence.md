# Ghostlight Play and Consequence

## Concise diagnosis

**Pass with findings.** The reconstructed 40-file council manifest matches
40/40 raw SHA-1 digests, including both replacement bodies. The Second Gate is
a real current Ghostlight situation rather than an oracle-ecology essay wearing
a dungeon label: characters begin with bounded evidence, a public clock changes
actor behavior, material operation remains independent of magical reports, and
several supported courses leave different claims and damage behind. Historic
record recovery also remains live across the five adopted collision cases.

One high-value seam remains in the current site. The party's written courier
charge names duties and forbidden powers but no issuing owner, consideration,
or return forum. The world reacts concretely to what happens to the grain,
report, gate, and public slate, yet it has no equally concrete owner for what
happens to the party's bargain.

## Protected successes

- `Kalsa/Public/Sources/At the Second Gate.md` is an honest player threshold.
  It exposes the waiting grain, reduced ward issue, sealed report, first bell,
  two routes, Pera's refusal to invent a cause, and Daro's independent stop
  authority without leaking the hidden response table.
- `Kalsa/Spoilers/Dungeons/The Second Gate Understore.md`, `## Three bells`,
  makes information consequential. A first-bell route slate moves crowds; a
  second-bell office order moves the levy shift; a third-bell shortage and
  requisition change custody. Delay is a course with costs, not empty table
  time.
- Iven, Pera, Nerin, Daro, and Sela possess different useful evidence. The
  house comparison, shamanic target pressure, opening on the weigh mechanism,
  technical stop, and ward need do not collapse into a shared “oracle check.”
- `## What the party can change` and `## Reachable state changes` support
  information, route, target, and material intervention without prescribing a
  righteous solution. Intact delivery, witnessed split, watch custody,
  mechanical closure, fresh comparison, and withdrawal all leave distinct
  consequences.
- The historic Public sources remain usable play carriers in their own right.
  Their ledgers, route books, signal copies, bridge works, gate records, and
  old claims support recovery play without turning every oracle collision into
  a present-tense quest advertisement.

## Findings

### GPC39-01 — The courier charge has no owner who can answer for it

- **Finding ID:** GPC39-01
- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/Sources/At the Second Gate.md`, paragraph
  beginning “Your written charge is short”; `Kalsa/Spoilers/Dungeons/The
  Second Gate Understore.md`, `## Site card` row `Party custody`, `## What the
  party can change`, and `## Continuing consequences`.
- **Evidence:** The charge tells the party to deliver the sealed comparison to
  Sela, escort rather than own the load, and preserve new orders, stops, and
  routes. The site then permits disclosure beyond the recipient list,
  first-bell delay, hand carriage outside the buyer's pay record, use of
  claimed equipment, unsafe-system intervention, and contest over
  requisition. No note identifies who issued the courier charge, who owes its
  consideration or support, where the party returns its copies, or which owner
  answers breach, extraordinary labor, injury, and equipment claims.
- **Effect:** The main state changes remain playable, but the party's own
  bargain floats outside the otherwise excellent owner map. Opening or
  misdelivering the report, spending a bell moving grain by hand, damaging
  Daro's equipment, refusing the charge, or suffering injury requires the
  resolver to invent a patron and settlement path. The written charge is nearly
  a durable receipt and then stops one relation short of consequence.
- **Repair direction:** Give the existing charge the minimum local owner and
  settlement path needed for play: identify which already-adopted actor or
  compact issued it, where the party's copies and objections return, and who
  initially answers ordinary pay plus extraordinary labor, injury, or claimed
  equipment. Keep later reassignment and dispute available. This needs a
  bounded line or site-card row, not a new institution.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not turn the charge into ownership of the grain,
  authority to open either gate, salvage title, immunity for disclosure, or a
  universal adventurers' guild contract. Do not publish the hidden clock or
  intended-response list merely to explain the bargain.
- **Dependency:** The Second Gate dungeon owns the live charge and outcome;
  the Public source owns only the courier-facing projection. Institutional
  Causality may test the chosen existing issuer and forum, while Material Life
  and Ecology may test the bounded support and liability terms.
- **Confidence:** High; the charge and its limits are explicit, and no issuing
  or settlement owner appears anywhere in the complete current-site bodies.

## Pattern or dependency map

The playable trace already holds:

**Observable cue:** grain carts wait, Reed Ward has one reduced issue, the
sealed comparison names Sela, and the first bell approaches. -> **Attempt:**
deliver, disclose, delay, change the route, seek a witnessed retarget, or alter
the understore mechanism. -> **Resistance or misreading:** publication changes
traffic, the levy follows the first signed slate, Pera refuses unauthorized
splitting, Daro stops unsafe entry, and the watch attempts third-bell
requisition. -> **Responding owners:** Sela chooses for the buyer, Nerin's
office moves its crew, Pera's witnesses own a new target, Daro owns operation,
and the watch owns its scoped custody attempt. -> **Durable consequence:**
route slates, signed corrections, stops, bond witnesses, report expiry, damage,
and the party's copies carry claims forward.

GPC39-01 concerns the missing outer edge of that trace: no owner receives the
party's copies or answers the courier charge after those consequences occur.

## Tempting edit to resist

Do not repair the missing issuer by giving the party the hidden dossier, a
complete oracle map, or a universal menu of legal actions. The site works
because asking and disclosure change the field, each actor knows a different
slice, and even a successful delivery can strengthen levy, coercion, or later
claim. Preserve that ignorance. Also resist adding every developed Gift to the
live gate; the historic sources already carry the wider ecology, and a
six-oracle encounter would become a taxonomy parade.

## Ranked actions

1. **High-value:** Anchor the courier charge to one existing issuer and return
   forum, with the smallest usable responsibility for ordinary pay,
   extraordinary labor, injury, and claimed equipment.
2. **Leave alone:** Preserve the Public threshold's bounded knowledge, the
   three-bell response clock, the four intervention surfaces, all six reachable
   states, unresolved Pera pressure, scoped material authority, and the
   consequence-bearing historic records.
3. **Optional:** No new quest hooks, oracle actors, factions, or explanatory
   action catalogue are warranted.
