## Concise diagnosis

**Return to Critic.** The full sanity check fails. KALSA-039 reintroduces the
institutional essay machinery that KALSA-038 had removed from Public: the six
new source routes disproportionately use the same archive-case structure,
several end by explaining the approved uncertainty, and `When futures crowd`
returns to an evenly weighted Gift chorus. The hidden owner surface repeats
the same event recaps and correction frames often enough to reinforce the
voice rather than contain it.

This is a prose diagnosis, not an authorship claim. The underlying oracle
ecology, events, procedures, and current dungeon are worth preserving.

## Findings

### AIP39-01 — The new Public source shelf shares one archive-case skeleton

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Public/Sources/The Empty Bridge Vigil.md`, `The Grain at
  Two Gates.md`, `The Narrow Shield.md`, `The Road That Returned.md`, and `The
  Three Orders at Sunwall.md`; compare the new source routes in
  `Kalsa/Public/index.md`.
- **Evidence:** Five new histories announce their preservation medium before
  their human pressure: “Four records survive,” “Merchants still copy this
  delivery record,” “Circle tenders keep,” “road books and Selza'a recitations
  agree,” and “Sunwall stewards copy ... House comparers copy.” Their bodies
  then proceed through titled accounts, copies, a correction or settlement,
  later practice, and a final limit on what the records prove. `The Empty
  Bridge Vigil` uses four constituency accounts; `The Three Orders at Sunwall`
  moves from the steward copy to “What later readers kept”; `The Grain at Two
  Gates` ends in “The copied correction.” This architecture recurs across most
  of the material added to a previously saturated source shelf.
- **Effect:** Distinct cultures, media, and events sound as though one neutral
  institutional essayist filed them on the same form. The reader anticipates
  the evidentiary reconciliation before discovering the event.
- **Repair direction:** Preserve the adopted facts and the genuinely archival
  nature of the cases, but choose which one or two sources actually need the
  complete archive-case form. Let the others be governed by their peculiar
  carrier—a watch order, merchant delivery dispute, road recitation, steward
  copy, or immediate scene—with unequal section shapes and without a neutral
  survey of every later lesson.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not erase provenance, the surviving records, or the
  institutional reason each account exists. Do not convert every source into
  dialogue merely to create surface variety.
- **Dependency:** Reader Experience and Navigation for route identity only;
  factual event ownership remains outside this remit.
- **Confidence:** High. The recurrence spans five of six new Public sources and
  is structural rather than lexical.

### AIP39-02 — Evidentiary checksum endings explain the uncertainty

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** Endings of `The Empty Bridge Vigil`, `The Grain at Two Gates`,
  `The Road That Returned`, and `The Three Orders at Sunwall`; also the new
  `When futures crowd` section in `World/Magic and Miracles.md` and the closing
  memory chorus in `The Narrow Shield`.
- **Evidence:** The sources close with “Joining the copies does not supply the
  missing dead or tell a god what it should have done,” “Neither clause can
  stop an unnamed person from entering the route,” “No surviving copy
  identifies a bearer of Luck” followed by a five-part approved set of
  explanations, and “None of those copies gives its owner the other three
  orders.” Magic supplies the matching survey checksum: “Each order changed
  the future the others expected. None became the city's complete account.”
  Narrow Shield gives Circle leads, outer settlements, and Sarxe route keepers
  one balanced final lesson each.
- **Effect:** After action and objects have already established partial
  knowledge, the narrator steps forward to certify exactly what the reader may
  conclude. Repetition turns metaphysical openness into managed ambiguity.
- **Repair direction:** Keep exact evidence limits where a court, copyist, or
  source tradition must state them. Elsewhere end on the surviving object,
  unpaid loss, blocked route, or faction's action and omit the universal
  correction. Break the three-position chorus where one concrete aftermath can
  carry the disagreement.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not resolve Luck, divine intent, casualty counts, or
  rival custody. The defect is the explanatory checksum, not uncertainty.
- **Dependency:** None.
- **Confidence:** High. The same closing function appears across four new
  histories, a survey, and the Narrow Shield coda.

- **Dependency:** Culture and Situated Knowledge only if the repair changes who

### AIP39-04 — `Oracle Ecology` argues by serial pre-emptive correction

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** `Kalsa/Spoilers/Foundations/Oracle Ecology.md`, especially `No
  view from outside`, `Density without a census`, `Differently shaped oracles`,
  `Crowded horizons`, `Luck and large attractors`, `Timeline capture`, and
  `Ghostlight consequences`.
- **Evidence:** The foundation repeatedly defines by anticipated
  misinterpretation: “not every possible self ... It is any relation,” “The
  Gifts are not interchangeable,” “No universal anti-magic follows,” “These
  are relationships, not weather,” “More oracles do not merely make prediction
  noisier. They change,” “It is still not global destiny,” “A forecast changes
  risk; it does not announce the resolver's result,” and “The meaningful choice
  is often not ... It is ....” Long actor, lever, archive, and failure lists
  repeatedly follow those corrections.
- **Effect:** The core author model feels debated against an invisible reader
  sentence by sentence. Precision accumulates as rhetorical insulation rather
  than as a small set of memorable mechanisms, and the final Ghostlight
  checksum explains the choice after the site and examples already show it.
- **Repair direction:** State each hard invariant once, then let paired
  collisions, capture mechanisms, and named historical cases demonstrate its
  consequences. Consolidate repeated anti-omniscience and anti-determinism
  qualifications instead of paraphrasing them in every section. Retain lists
  that are actual adjudication inventories.
- **Collateral risk:** This is an author-facing foundation; do not sacrifice
  resolver constraints, asymmetric Gift mechanics, or unresolved Intuition for
  a lyrical voice. Do not replace it with an inhabitant theory.
- **Dependency:** Oracle Ecology and Timeline Competition for mechanical
  invariants.
- **Confidence:** High. The issue is sustained architecture across the note,
  not an isolated `not` construction.

### AIP39-05 — Historical consequence blocks duplicate one neutral voice

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** Added consequence/stress sections in `God-Cult Covenants`, `The
  Potential Economy`, `Stormshield Circles`, `Prophetic Houses`, `Ju'onai
  Hegemony`, `Luck — Selza'a`, and `Shamanism — Papsenai`; compare the five new
  Spoiler event owners.
- **Evidence:** The receiving notes repeatedly begin by announcing that the
  event note “owns” the facts, recap the event in compressed neutral prose,
  state what “some” institutions copied, and finish by restricting the lesson.
  Empty Bridge is retold in both covenant and potential notes; Grain at Two
  Gates in both house and Papsenai notes; Road That Returned in both Ju'onai
  and Selza'a notes; Narrow Shield in the event, Circle, and Oracle Ecology
  notes. The additions repeatedly use `owns`, `preserve`, `record`, `bounded`,
  and `does not prove` as narrative connective tissue rather than only as
  authority metadata.
- **Effect:** Ownership hygiene becomes audible as house prose, while the same
  history arrives several times in nearly identical moral and evidentiary
  packaging. Cultural difference is reduced to which constituency receives
  the final sentence.
- **Repair direction:** Keep the owner link, but make each receiving note carry
  only the local procedure, practice, or political use it actually owns. Avoid
  re-summarizing the whole event and let culturally specific objects or
  formulations carry disagreements where they exist.
- **Collateral risk:** Do not merge canonical ownership, remove useful
  cross-links, or force one event interpretation to win. Functional owner
  statements in author notes remain valid.
- **Dependency:** Institutional Causality and Culture and Situated Knowledge
  where a local consequence needs a clearer owner or voice.
- **Confidence:** High. The repeated blocks are visible across seven receiving
  owner notes and four event families.

Second Gate Understore`; keep the Oracle Ecology adjudication inventories; keep
the Empty Bridge's four actual jurisdictions; keep the Ash-Valve inspection
independent of the shrine knocks. The repair is to stop unrelated surfaces
from sharing one rhetorical architecture, not to perform lexical cleansing.
