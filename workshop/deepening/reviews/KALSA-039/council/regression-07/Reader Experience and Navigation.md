# Reader Experience and Navigation Final Regression

## Concise diagnosis

**Pass.** The nine-file Public projection provides a clear curiosity route from
the index into the Divided City model and then into distinct collisions where
foreknowledge, authority, labour, shelter, and loss can be inspected. I
understand Kalsa as a world where magical knowledge changes what its recipients
do and therefore changes the conditions it described; institutions preserve
bounded records, while households, workers, lineages, and officials keep
incompatible accounts of the cost.

I would most readily play the escort at Reed Ward's second gate, a claimant or
reviewer after the narrow shield, or someone following the unpaid guide claims
up the returned road. Pera Sai's hand on the marked cart, the first bell rope,
wet seed outside the refuge list, extinguished shelter flames, and the turned
numbered stone remained in memory. I skimmed portions of the broad polity
survey, but its headings and concrete source links restored momentum. No page
became morally pre-digested or instructional, and no link in the inspected
route felt unsafe or unearned.

The candidate-07 ending of `The Three Orders at Sunwall` succeeds. The steward
copy's undersized refuge and the present workers who begin with the bodies it
can hold give the material test a situated carrier. The line does not settle
which teaching, account, or response is correct, and it does not sound like an
author speaking over the sources. No substantive reader or navigation repair
remains.

## Pattern or dependency map

`Kalsa/Public/index.md` supplies the invitation and choice hierarchy. `Magic
and Miracles` supplies a situated explanatory model owned by Divided City house
comparers. `The Three Orders at Sunwall` tests that model through separately
owned teaching, administrative, and historical carriers. `At the Second Gate`
and `The Grain at Two Gates` turn the same pressure into a bounded player
charge and an inspectable consequence. `The Narrow Shield`, `The Empty Bridge
Vigil`, and `The Road That Returned` show that the pattern does not collapse
into one institution's vocabulary: other workers and record traditions keep
different signals, claims, losses, and endings.

## Tempting edit to resist

through an omniscient glossary, disclose the sealed comparison, reconcile the
casualty counts, or reveal why every recipient acted. Those gaps are playable
uncertainty. Also resist expanding every source into the same symmetrical set
of viewpoints; their unequal forms and surviving records are part of what makes
the route feel inhabited.
