# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** Public already reproduces the shield's material mechanism unusually
well. It does not reproduce the authority, refusal, evidence, or settlement
path that made the Shield Levy Winter institution-forming.

## Protected successes

- Incoming contact precedes outgoing release; observers compare the handoff.
- Agriculture and shielding consume real reserves, labor, care, and time.
- Circle operation, tribal provision, and city decisions remain separate in
  hidden canon.

## Findings

### IC36-01 — The double demand has no authority handoff

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** final Public Papsenai paragraph.
- **Evidence:** local authorities released grain; campaign collectors took it;
  a cohort and attendants were reassigned; Circle leads owned safe overlap.
- **Effect:** the failure reads as generic overreach rather than conflicting
  authorities crossing their limits.
- **Repair direction:** one source carrying demand, release, diversion, warning,
  review, and bounded settlement with each acting owner.
- **Collateral risk:** no universal council, court, or emergency law.
- **Dependency:** event and Circle owners.
- **Confidence:** High.

### IC36-02 — Separate evidence becomes one proof bundle

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** `Store marks, shift records, and household testimony...`.
- **Evidence:** stores establish extraction; Circle records establish lost
  overlap/exhaustion; households establish reserve loss; military need remains
  a political claim.
- **Effect:** no reader can tell which evidence changes which decision.
- **Repair direction:** expose the evidence-owner collision in sequence.
- **Collateral risk:** no record may own the whole finding.
- **Dependency:** Culture and Ghostlight.
- **Confidence:** High.

### IC36-03 — Public omits what settlement changed

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** end of Papsenai section.
- **Evidence:** reserve identification, reviewable receipts, shield-worker levy
  limits, consent/replacement, and operational refusal followed the event.
- **Effect:** institutions remember but do not reproduce correction.
- **Repair direction:** carry bounded changes and unpaid losses through source.
- **Collateral risk:** procedure is one settlement, not a constitution.
- **Dependency:** History and Material Life.
- **Confidence:** High.
