# Critic Report

## Concise diagnosis

**Block.** `Papsenai accounts` makes tribes, households, stores, Circle workers,
and campaign authorities share one memory and interest. The event already owns
asymmetrical carriers that can safely displace that consensus.

## Protected successes

- Bounded shamanism remains material and incomplete.
- Circle overlap and relief produce situated working knowledge.
- Wartime need remains audible without becoming technical proof.

## Findings

### CSK36-01 — `Papsenai accounts` is unowned collective memory

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** final Papsenai paragraph.
- **Evidence:** households, store workers, Circle witnesses, political users,
  and military accounts preserve different parts.
- **Effect:** plurality collapses into narrator consensus.
- **Repair direction:** route to an incomplete source with declared carriers.
- **Collateral risk:** no invented archive, council, or native event name.
- **Dependency:** event owner.
- **Confidence:** High.

### CSK36-02 — Evidence loses useful domain and blind spot

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** evidence list in the final sentence.
- **Evidence:** store, Circle, household, military, and later review records
  establish different consequences.
- **Effect:** documents become a frictionless bundle.
- **Repair direction:** let each object enter when its claim is tested; avoid an
  equal-voice symposium.
- **Collateral risk:** no carrier becomes complete truth.
- **Dependency:** Institutional Causality.
- **Confidence:** High.

### CSK36-03 — The military account disappears

- **Priority:** Essential
- **Location:** current Shield Levy summary.
- **Evidence:** campaign authority claimed the demands served wider protection;
  challengers disputed compounding, not wartime need itself.
- **Effect:** refusal becomes narrator-approved politics.
- **Repair direction:** preserve the claim and its inability to decide local
  reserve sufficiency or shield safety.
- **Collateral risk:** hearing the claim must not excuse missing records or
  unsafe handoff.
- **Dependency:** History.
- **Confidence:** High.

### CSK36-04 — Institutional reproduction has no carrier

- **Priority:** High-value
- **Location:** all three Public Papsenai paragraphs.
- **Evidence:** household/store copies, Circle trainers, failure records, and
  later assessments reproduce practice in hidden canon.
- **Effect:** institutions appear as stable administrative nouns.
- **Repair direction:** show how the event's records changed later reserve,
  receipt, and handoff practice while families retain claims.
- **Collateral risk:** do not make this the origin of all Papsenai practice.
- **Dependency:** polity and event owners.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Tempting edit to resist

No universal archive, equal-voice dialogue, Alliance participant, or full Circle
primer.

## Ranked actions

1. Publish one incomplete, carrier-owned reconstruction.
2. Keep military and local knowledge asymmetrical.
3. Preserve missing terms and actors.
