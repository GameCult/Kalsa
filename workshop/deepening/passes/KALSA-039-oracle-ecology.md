## Objective

Make Kalsa answer its fundamental causal question:

> What does a world become when causality is inhabited by innumerable competing
> oracles, none privileged, whose different Gifts perceive and steer possibility
> through different shapes?

The repair must not reduce this to a large population of Prophets. Luck,
Sorcery, Shamanism, Analysis, Prophecy, Channeling, Intuition, individual
Projections, Chaos Beasts, and God hierarchies are differently shaped
participants in the same adversarial ecology.

## Source inventory

- The immutable seed says observing the future changes it and oracular
  information exposes present levers in open causal systems.
- Every person occupies an Individual Projection; prescient folding can produce
  Chaos Beasts, and some Beasts bind others into God hierarchies.
- Luck acts constantly, invisibly, and at greatest reach toward survival;
  Sorcery selects focused improbable interventions; Shamanism binds work toward
  distant outcomes; Analysis reads bounded causal systems; Prophecy compares
  long-range possible states; Channeling predicts and redirects active flows;
  Intuition remains underdesigned.
- Existing canon recognizes rival closure, forecast deformation, prophetic
  competition, divine recruitment, Sorcerous submission, ideological divine
  hierarchy, and Luck that cannot be directly identified.
- Existing institutions develop Prophetic evidence deeply but do not yet make
  heterogeneous oracle competition the world's explanatory spine.
- The governing direction records “oracular spectacle plus hidden capability
  limits” but does not name this fundamental question or require every
  cross-cutting repair to answer it.

## Unknowns and protected limits

- Exact Gift frequencies, population totals, inheritance, multistyle
  expression, and Intuition mechanics remain unresolved.
- No oracle gains author omniscience. No forecast creates matter, energy,
  consent, jurisdiction, or an otherwise unreachable branch.
- Different styles must remain asymmetric. “Interference” cannot become a
  universal cancel-magic rule.
- Independent futures are not automatically just or safe. Coordination is not
  automatically domination.
- Public presentation must use situated cultural evidence rather than author
  dimensions, signatures, or oracle-ecology terminology.

  binding. No canonical note owns interactions among heterogeneous oracles or
  local timeline capture.

  aggregation, capture, release, and ordinary consequence.
- **Cut line:** remove the implicit equation `oracle = Prophet` and the notion
  that interference is merely a caveat attached to forecast evidence.

## Intended authority after repair

  interaction invariants among differently shaped oracles and the conditions
  of local timeline capture.
- **Inputs:** operations and limits owned by `Kaos`, style distinctions owned by

  emitted by situated people and institutions.
- **Outputs:** constraints consumed by divine hierarchy, conquest, forecast
  practice, law, markets, kinship, infrastructure, migration, and play.

  Public sources interpret partial evidence without becoming universal truth.
- **Forbidden writers:** Oracle Ecology cannot invent a polity's law, declare a
  moral preference for plurality, or replace style-specific mechanics.
- **Shared path:** oracular act -> altered reachable field -> partial detection
  and rival response -> convergence, displacement, occlusion, or escape ->
  institutionally distributed consequence.
- **Deletion line:** prophecy ceases to be the implicit sole owner of future
  knowledge; institutional procedure ceases to substitute for the causal
  ecology it is responding to.

## Decision record

- The operator clarified that Luck's reach and Luck Gods are closest to the
  timeline-collapse pressure experienced by Paul Atreides. Dune's collapse
  depends upon a singular oracle; Kalsa instead contains innumerable competing
  oracles of heterogeneous shapes, many of which never manifest as conscious
  Prophecy.
- `Oracle ecology` and `timeline capture` are author-facing working terms. Each
  inhabitant model requires its own evidence, institutions, interests, and
  characteristic limits.
