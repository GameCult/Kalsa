# KALSA-017 — Divine Institutional Reproduction

## Objective

Repair the divine cluster's missing careers, support, discipline, audit, removal, succession, exit, factions, and owned historical pressure without allowing mortal offices to write metaphysics.

## Authority map and repair

- **Covenants** own cult formation, living entry, office reproduction, discipline, and contractual exit.
- **Potential stewards** own estimation, material support, allocation, ledgers, audit, appeal, and removal for allocative misconduct.
- **Death law** owns death declaration, mortuary custody, post-mortem claims, and mortuary severance, not living contract exit.
- **Soiru'i sects** own champion and steward formation, shrines, stores, discipline, succession, aid, and organized resistance, not the dead's identity.
- **Foundations** retain Projection, binding, potential, identity, and release mechanics.
- [[Kalsa/Events/The Three Ledgers Schism|The Three Ledgers Schism]] alone owns the adopted event facts; each institution keeps only its jurisdictional consequence.

## Falsification and decision

The repair tests defeated communities, unaffordable exit, apostates, unregistered dead, allocative fraud, schism, sect sanctuary, and archives split from food or divine access. It adopts reproducible mortal institutions and one explicit adopted history. It does not adopt clerical metaphysical authority or one universal cult law.
