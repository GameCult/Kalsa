# KALSA-015 — Sarxe Households and Refuge

## Objective

Give Sarxe communal life, displacement, and refuge durable institutions without using “hive,” “insanity,” mercenary diaspora, or magical proximity to erase personhood and consent.

## Source inventory

- `seed/original/page-2026-08-20-10-40-04.md` establishes Channeling, Ju'onai annexation, static Saxfoldi, the later Terjamna Ji'esti massacre, mobile aftermath, and an outside-facing mercenary strand.
- Foundations retain Channeling mechanics. The two displacement histories remain successive, and the external diaspora is not the whole society.
- Exact Sarxe language, route, offices, membership law, and Ji'esti metaphysics remain unresolved.

## Authority map before repair

- **Owner:** the polity stub preserved sequence but lacked a lawful person-scale social unit or refuge path.
- **Inputs:** consent, bounded shared attention, labour, care, route knowledge, warnings, provisions, testimony, custody records, and host claims.
- **Outputs:** membership and use claims, work rotations, privacy boundaries, safe conduct, admission, care, separation and recovery records, appeal, departure, and handoff.
- **Forbidden writers:** communal awareness cannot write bodily consent; refuge cannot write lifelong custody; mobile use cannot write universal land title; diaspora work cannot write authentic Sarxe identity.
- **Cut line:** replace the hive/nonperson frame with bounded households and reviewable refuge procedure.

- **Stress case:** the successive annexation, static consolidation, Ji'esti breach, massacre, and flight test whether identity, records, children, dependents, and obligations survive when neither proximity nor a host polity can safely own them.
