# KALSA-007 — Techpriesthoods and Relic Custody

## Objective

Turn seed references to priestly maintenance into institutions that can keep inherited machinery alive without quietly owning every relic, settlement, or dependent person.

## Source inventory

- `seed/original/page-2026-08-20-10-39-12.md` establishes surviving technical machinery, incomplete knowledge, priesthoods, and Promethean language inheritance.
- [[Kalsa/Foundations/Ark and Fall|Ark and Fall]], [[Kalsa/Foundations/Prometheus|Prometheus]], and [[Kalsa/Foundations/Language and Reckoning|Language and Reckoning]] own the inherited substrate.
- The seed does not establish a single order, central hierarchy, universal tribunal, or continent-wide law.

## Authority map before repair

- **Owner:** no canonical institutional owner existed.
- **Inputs:** dangerous inherited systems, partial procedures, community support, local political permission, and relic claims.
- **Outputs:** working systems, trained successors, maintenance records, emergency warnings, custody handoffs.
- **Forbidden writers:** competence could not become land title, political rule, divine standing, personhood, or universal ownership.
- **Cut line:** remove the implied singular priesthood and separate operational competence from custody and title.

## Critique and repair

The generic guild default hid who feeds specialists, who may refuse them, and what happens when a competent lineage splits. [[Kalsa/Institutions/The Techpriesthoods|The Techpriesthoods]] now owns plural training and maintenance lineages; [[Kalsa/Institutions/Relic Custody|Relic Custody]] owns possession, access, configuration, and handoff distinctions. Site authority, recovery labour, sacred claims, community dependency, title, and operational custody remain separable.

## Falsification

- **Marginal perspective:** communities paying for repair still retain land and output; finders and carriers do not gain automatic sale or activation rights.
- **Stress case:** emergency intervention expires after stabilization, anomalous relic speech preserves evidence rather than forcing a metaphysical verdict, and rival competent lineages can remain legitimate after schism.
