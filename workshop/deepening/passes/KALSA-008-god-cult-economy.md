# KALSA-008 — God-Cult Economy

## Objective

Make worship, finite potential, welfare, binding, death, and succession produce ordinary obligations and disputes without letting clerical records rewrite metaphysics.

## Source inventory

- `seed/original/page-2026-08-20-10-39-03.md` and `page-2026-08-20-10-39-12.md` establish God Beasts, worship, binding, potential, and magical predation.
- [[Kalsa/Foundations/Projections, God Beasts, and Thaumavores|Projections, God Beasts, and Thaumavores]] owns the metaphysics.
- The seed distinguishes relationships of worship and submission but does not supply a universal church or single divine law.

## Authority map before repair

- **Owner:** metaphysics existed; social entry, allocation, leakage, funerary procedure, and succession had no bounded owners.
- **Inputs:** covenant routes, material stores, labour, potential estimates, testimony, bodies, records, and competing kin/cult claims.
- **Outputs:** recognized obligations, welfare, rationing, audits, funerary custody, severance findings, and schism settlements.
- **Forbidden writers:** offices, ledgers, and verdicts cannot prove divine identity or redefine Projection mechanics.
- **Cut line:** separate covenant, economy, and mortuary law rather than giving one temple a total account.

## Critique and repair

[[Kalsa/Institutions/God-Cult Covenants|God-Cult Covenants]] distinguishes voluntary covenant, inherited attachment, and coerced submission. ## Falsification

- **Marginal perspective:** defeated communities may have coercion laundered into covenant; nominal exit may be unaffordable; apostates, Null witnesses, concealed descendants, and unregistered war dead remain socially vulnerable.
- **Stress cases:** an anchor death can split records, stores, champions, and routing; miracle shortages and thaumavore leakage expose false accounting; continuation after death may remain evidentially uncertain.
