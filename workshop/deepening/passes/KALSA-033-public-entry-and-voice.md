# KALSA-033 — Public Entry and Narrator Voice

## Objective

Make Kalsa's casual reader path lead through a live high-fantasy problem before
asking readers to absorb the whole setting. Repair repeated authorial
correction, moral-ledger, list-clause, and checksum prose where concrete
inhabitant evidence already carries the meaning. Preserve spoiler friction,
setting strangeness, culturally owned technical language, and unresolved
mystery.

## Source inventory

### Seed evidence

- The immutable teenage export remains evidence for setting facts but is not a
  prose or navigation owner. Exact witnesses will be named in the review packet
  only where a proposed Public change depends on them.

### Adopted canon

- `Kalsa/Public/index.md` owns Public navigation and reader invitation.
- Each note under `Kalsa/Public/` owns its situated presentation, not the hidden
  facts it summarizes.
- Subject notes under `Kalsa/Spoilers/` retain the setting claims consumed by
  Public pages. KALSA-026 and KALSA-030 own the adopted publication and
  inhabitant-model decisions.
- Low Sere and both situated source texts are protected working examples rather
  than default rewrite targets.

### Provisional, contested, rumored, or deprecated material

- A new contrasting situated branch, traveller itinerary, and additional
  polity pages remain proposals. This pass may not manufacture them to satisfy
  link symmetry.
- The coldward Intuition people remain unresolved and cannot be clarified here.

### Contradictions and unknowns

- Public can state culturally owned disagreement but cannot settle backstage
  mechanics or use an authorial narrator to simulate consensus.
- The reader needs routes without receiving an omniscient map or exhaustive
  one-page-per-people catalogue.

## Institutional model

- **Function:** Public is a knowledge commons encountered through inhabitants,
  places, offices, texts, and disputes, with an inviting first playable route.
- **Authority and enforcement limits:** Public may present attributed knowledge
  and common observation; it cannot settle author truth or grant institutions
  more authority than their hidden owners.
- **Material support:** nine reader notes, their links, the Obsidian consent
  gate, Quartz navigation/search, and later bounded additions.
- **Doctrine versus practice:** the declared inhabitant-facing design currently
  opens with five broad surveys before reaching concrete place and voice.
- **Beneficiaries, costs, exclusion, refusal:** casual readers gain a safe
  route; survey-first hierarchy and abstract caution impose entry cost and make
  several peoples feel like unreachable catalogue entries.
- **Dependencies and neighboring institutions:** all source subject owners,
  publication checks, vault enclosure, and Ghostlight knowledge projection.
- **Variation by class, region, lineage, language, magic:** must remain in
  situated sources rather than one narrator's balanced institutional dialect.
- **Historical formation and path dependence:** reader-facing history remains
  culturally owned; this pass invents no origin event.
- **Internal contradiction and factions:** useful caution against essentialism
  has become rhetorically uniform enough to flatten the differences it protects.
- **Player-facing consequences:** the entrance should expose a concrete problem,
  available evidence, competing actors, and unresolved danger before the survey.

## Critique

- **Generic fantasy defaults:** encyclopedic world-first navigation and
  symmetrical polity summaries.
- **Missing material constraints:** abstract cost lists can name burdens without
  showing a handoff or consequence.
- **False consensus or absent subordinate perspectives:** a narrator distributes
  correct caveats to every people in one register.
- **Consequences that stop at the page edge:** several polity glimpses raise
  curiosity without an honest onward route.
- **Duplicate or ambiguous owners:** narrator-level doctrine repeats decisions
  already embodied by Low Sere and the source texts.

prevent essentialism and accidental authoritarian claims. Repair must preserve
the actual distinctions while removing their repeated essay cadence.

must accept that new source without rewriting every overview or promoting one
universal explanation.
