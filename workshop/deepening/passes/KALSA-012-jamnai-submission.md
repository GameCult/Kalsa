# KALSA-012 — Jamnai Submission and Soiru'i

## Objective

Turn the seed's conquest hierarchy into a polity that can run a geothermal arcology, extract tribute, reproduce office, shelter resistance, and fail without making victory identical to competence or consent.

## Source inventory

- `seed/original/page-2026-08-20-10-39-38.md` establishes Jamnai, Terjamna, the geothermal arcology, submission hierarchy, and Soiru'i.
- `page-2026-08-20-10-40-04.md` establishes the later Ji'esti wound.
- Foundations retain Sorcery and Projection mechanics; sect doctrine cannot become author metaphysics.

## Authority map before repair

- **Owner:** the polity stub owned history but lacked civic, material, and resistance mechanisms.
- **Inputs:** branch records, oaths, magical contests, geothermal services, labour, tribute, protection, testimony, and funerary practice.
- **Outputs:** obligations, protected shares, office, succession, technical stop orders, appeals, sanctuary, and external tribute.
- **Forbidden writers:** victory cannot write technical competence; civic oath cannot write magical binding; rank cannot write ownership of dependents; sect account cannot write cosmology.
- **Cut line:** split polity, civil hierarchy, and sect authority while preserving their conflict.
