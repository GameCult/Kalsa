# KALSA-010 — Dungeon Provenance and Salvage

## Objective

Give Ghostlight Dungeon a setting-native procedure for entering, interpreting, surviving, and taking material from layered ruins without making “adventurer” a magic word for ownership.

## Source inventory

- The seed establishes fallen Rings, spokes, Ark systems, evolutionary arenas, terraforming works, dead Gods, thaumavores, post-Fall construction, and incomplete simulation language.
- Foundation notes retain ownership of history and metaphysics; Techpriest, relic, cult, kin, and mortuary notes retain their bounded authorities.
- No seed evidence supports a universal guild, court, dungeon type, or finder-keeps rule.

## Authority map before repair

- **Owner:** no dungeon provenance or admission owner existed.
- **Inputs:** site layers, local jurisdiction, public danger, custody records, kin/cult claims, labour, route knowledge, and expedition agreements.
- **Outputs:** classified evidence, admission scope, safety orders, claim holds, post-expedition adjudication, rescue and share obligations.
- **Forbidden writers:** provenance cannot grant title; a compact cannot bind non-signatories; operational competence cannot own a site.
- **Cut line:** replace the generic dungeon/salvage default with layered consequence and residual title.

## Critique and repair

[[Kalsa/Dungeons/Dungeon Provenance|Dungeon Provenance]] owns evidence about how a site formed and changed. [[Kalsa/Institutions/Relic and Ruin Law|Relic and Ruin Law]] models local admission, danger, claims, removal, review, and appeal. [[Kalsa/Institutions/Delving Compacts|Delving Compacts]] binds expedition parties around labour, records, rescue, costs, breach, and only the residual movable salvage left after superior claims and hazard costs.

## Falsification

- **Marginal perspective:** porters can bear concealed hazard costs; residents may have only continued use as evidence; kin and cult claims may be dismissed by titled outsiders; emergency closures can become extraction.
- **Stress cases:** one depth can have Ark, war, refuge, burial, and later-use layers; active infrastructure can cross a purported site boundary; sealed works and anomalous remains preserve uncertainty rather than becoming loot categories.
