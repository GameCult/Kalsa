# KALSA-038 — Public Prose Saturation

## Objective

Run the version-controlled AI-Isms and Prose critic over the complete reader
surface, repair its supported findings without changing lore authority, and
repeat on frozen candidates until the critic returns an unqualified **Pass**
with no remaining prose finding.

This is a presentation-maintenance pass, not an institutional deepening pass.
By explicit operator direction, only the prose critic speaks. Other council
seats neither diagnose nor certify this work.

## Scope and provenance

- **In scope:** `Kalsa/index.md` and every Markdown note under `Kalsa/Public/`.
- **Canon boundary:** Public notes own situated presentation only. Hidden
  subject and event notes retain every setting fact.
- **Protected:** spoiler friction, inhabitant ownership, source asymmetry,
  functional evidence chains, exact uncertainty, peculiar seed detail,
  navigational clarity, and lists that genuinely carry play or procedure.
- **Forbidden additions:** new lore facts, names, dates, offices, quantities,
  causal answers, universal institutions, author taxonomy, or generic fantasy
  texture introduced merely to vary prose.

## Authority map before repair

- **Owner:** each Public note owns its own presentation; no critic or packet
  owns prose globally.
- **Inputs:** current Public Markdown, the root consent gate, the shared critic
  prompt, and `critics/AI-Isms and Prose.md`.
- **Outputs:** narrower reader prose, preserved links and claims, frozen review
  packets, critic reports, and a complete audit trail.
- **Derived state:** pattern counts, hashes, critic judgments, diffs, and
  rendered Quartz pages can locate pressure but cannot write canon.
- **Forbidden writers:** the prose critic diagnoses only; the coordinator may
  alter presentation but may not infer hidden facts or use stylistic repair to
  change an institution's authority.
- **Shared path:** full Public snapshot -> critic diagnosis -> exact finding
  disposition -> smallest cross-note repair -> new full snapshot -> same critic
  regression, repeated until no finding remains.
- **Cut line:** no other council seat, hidden-lore expansion, prose detector,
  word blacklist, mechanical regex rewrite, or generic style-normalization pass.

## Repair

Five repair rounds changed ten Public notes and the Public index. The work:

- broke the repeated surviving-record/missing-metadata opening form by placing
  source limits where a copy, roll, or later use actually encounters them;
- removed the managed-ambiguity machine around uncanny events and technical
  findings, including its paired skeptic, neutral chorus, and explanatory
  checksum;
- varied consequence placement so institutional correction and residual harm
  no longer arrive in the same polished order;
- replaced authority matrices with books, stores, bodies, worktables, civic
  desks, warrants, handoffs, and other acts of custody;
- broke survey comparison rows and exhaustive route blurbs through uneven
  emphasis, concrete cases, and links;
- retained functional inventories, source provenance, material costs,
  metaphysical uncertainty, deliberate dialogue, and exact institutional terms.

## Falsification

- **Reader pressure:** a casual reader moves among overview, place, dialogue,
  and reconstructed-source notes. Voice and rhythm should change with the
  carrier instead of exposing one tireless institutional essayist.
- **Overcorrection pressure:** a zealous cleanup removes functional evidence,
  flattens precise limits, invents ornament, or replaces one formula with
  clipped faux-human prose.

## Authority map after repair

- **Owner:** unchanged. Each Public note owns presentation; hidden owner notes
  retain setting truth.
- **Inputs:** unchanged. The repair introduced no new fact source and no new
  institution.
- **Outputs:** the complete Public surface now varies by carrier and action
  without concealing provenance or material consequence.
- **Derived state:** five frozen candidate packets and six critic reports record
  the loop. Their verdicts remain review evidence, not prose or canon authority.
- **Forbidden writers:** unchanged. The critic diagnosed and never edited lore;

- **Shared path:** the same full-surface hash, diagnosis, disposition, repair,

- **Cut line:** no detector, blacklist, synonym purge, new lore, hidden-owner
  change, or second critic was introduced.

## Decision record

The operator's discomfort was correct at the corpus level even where each
paragraph would pass an isolated classroom reading. The recurring six-beat
sequence—uncanny event, believer, skeptic, neutral record, material finding,
scope checksum—was a house mechanism, not cultural depth. Candidate 05 is
accepted because the same critic reread the complete surface and found no
remaining substantive prose defect. The loop stops on that judgment, not on a
round count or a favorable local excerpt.

This pass exhausts the AI-Isms and Prose critic on its complete Public review
surface. Its exact certified baseline is `KALSA-038-candidate-06`. The seat now
operates as a Guardian: future passes send it the exact diff, complete changed
notes, and affected context rather than an untouched full-vault baseline. Its
relevant-review counter starts at zero; the fifth relevant review is a full
sanity check. The earlier triggers and return-to-Critic rule are owned by
`workshop/review-council/README.md`.

The current AI-Isms and Prose specialist-prompt calibration, versioned in
commit `198a14b`, distills examples from this completed loop and belongs to the
transition baseline. Later material changes to either the specialist or
Guardian prompt trigger an early sanity check.
