# KALSA-034 — Selza'a Windtrap Knowledge

## Objective

Give readers an inhabited route into Selza'a knowledge without making Luck
detectable, family custom uniform, or outsiders the foil in another polished
correction paragraph. The Broken Windtrap Settlement should show how family
signs, prophecy, work records, inspection, emergency water, accusation, and
restitution interact—and where a correct finding still fails a person.

## Source inventory

### Seed evidence

- The immutable witnesses establish Luck as constant undetectable causal bias,
  strong family and territorial organization, fog, windtraps, dimna or
  seltanzda identity, no central government, and uncertain entry or exit.
- The seed does not name the Broken Windtrap Settlement, its keeper, its
  families, or a universal Selza'a evidentiary procedure.

### Adopted canon

- `Luck — Selza'a` owns the polity, isolation, distributed government, and the
  inability to infer a Luck bearer from outcomes.
- `Selza'a Family Territories` owns family membership, shared works, boundary
  compacts, water, records, inspection, emergency baselines, and exit.
- `Luck Harm and Restitution` owns rescue-first response, material evidence,
  bounded prophetic reports, risk pools, findings, restitution, and corrected
  records.
- `The Broken Windtrap Settlement` alone owns the adopted event facts.
- Public owns presentation only.

### Unknowns and protected uncertainty

- The families, keeper, prophet, worker, site, date, native legal terms, and
  exact windtrap mechanism remain unnamed.
- No record proves Luck caused the failure or that the cleared keeper carried
  any magical relation.
- No Public source may universalize this settlement, name a Luck detector, or
  turn the city into one tribunal or constitution.

## Authority map before repair

- **Owner:** hidden polity, institution, and event notes own facts. Public
  currently summarizes Selza'a difference through an outsider/insider
  correction and generic family claims.
- **Inputs:** accident pattern, forecast bias, wreckage, maintenance records,
  shared-work compact, emergency stores, water baseline, injury, lost work,
  family standing, and corrected finding.
- **Outputs:** bounded accusation, preserved evidence, physical inspection,
  emergency service, scoped liability, restitution, record correction, and a
  situated Public route.
- **Derived state:** Public prose, family reputation, prophetic bias, Luck
  allegation, and council reports cannot identify a magical cause or person.
- **Forbidden writers:** fortune cannot write guilt; prophecy cannot name the
  bearer; work competence cannot own water allocation; a corrected record
  cannot claim to have restored livelihood or belonging.
- **Shared path:** harm -> emergency water and restraint -> evidence
  preservation -> forecast and material inspection kept distinct -> scoped
  finding -> pooled repair and shifted contribution -> record correction and
  incomplete social recovery.
- **Cut line:** remove the outsider/insider rhetorical hinge. Do not replace it
  with a Selza'a slogan, named family, universal court, or modern procedural
  transcript.

## Institutional model

- **Function:** keep shared water working while invisible-cause accusations can
  ignite territorial feud.
- **Authority:** families and compacts own named relations; keepers own safe
  operation; water users retain allocation claims; prophets report forecast
  bias; domain workers inspect matter; case tables assign only supported harm.
- **Material support:** windtrap, cistern, water stores, routes, maintenance
  labor and records, repair pool, food, care, and lost-work provision.
- **Historical formation:** the Broken Windtrap Settlement supplies one
  adopted precedent and its later limits.
- **Contradiction:** distributed restraint prevents feud and restores water
  while family dependence lets cleared people lose work, affiliation, and
  standing beyond the remedy.
- **Play:** evidence, wreckage, routes, stores, witness protection, water
  delivery, accusation, and affiliation change all offer consequential contact
  without a Luck answer key.

## Falsification

### Hostile or marginal perspective

The cleared adopted keeper says the procedure valued water and inter-family
peace more than the livelihood of the person it exposed to suspicion. A poor
household says emergency rations cannot replace work lost while private-cistern
families waited comfortably.

### Historical stress case

The Broken Windtrap tests whether rescue, restraint, prophecy, inspection,
liability, and restitution remain distinct under water shortage and threatened
feud.
