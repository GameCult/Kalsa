# KALSA-031 — Divine Witness Practice

## Objective

Replace an unowned Public theology of “delegated attention” with distinct
inhabitant practices for testing divine claims. Covenant witnesses, potential
stewards, mortuary witnesses, hostile chroniclers, clergy, and intermediaries
may preserve different evidence and act within different limits. The reader
should be able to encounter this disagreement without receiving the authorial
divine taxonomy or a newly invented universal cult school.

## Authority map before repair

- **Owner:** divine mechanics own the backstage limit; Public currently turns
  that limit into an unowned comparison among generic “cult schools.”
- **Inputs:** covenant origins and witness statements; petition, material-input,
  grant, failure, and beneficiary records; mortuary inventories; manifestations;
  intermediary claims; hostile chronicles; the Schism's adopted event record.
- **Outputs:** bounded witness statements, allocation findings, mortuary
  findings, doctrine, warnings, appeals, and situated Public explanation.
- **Derived state:** comparative Public prose, teaching labels, and the council
  packet may summarize evidence but cannot create a cult or metaphysical fact.
- **Forbidden writers:** divine testimony cannot become author truth; miracle
  allocation cannot decide divine intention; covenant, allocation, and
  mortuary records cannot inherit one another's authority.
- **Shared path:** observed manifestation or promise -> role-attributed record
  -> rival interpretation -> bounded material/covenant/mortuary action ->
  adverse evidence and review.
- **Cut line:** remove or owner-back “cult schools” and “delegated attention.”
  Do not solve the fault with a synonym, universal theology, or one merged
  divine evidence office.

## Institutional model

- **Function:** keep divine power consequential while making claims about its
  knowledge inspectable by people who must distribute food, enforce terms,
  tend bodies, and survive mistaken revelation.
- **Authority:** witnesses own recorded terms and observations; clergy own
  doctrine; stewards own allocation records and audits; mortuary offices own
  bodies and death evidence; gods and intermediaries remain powerful witnesses,
  not author narrators.
- **Material support:** archives, duplicate copies, petition routes, granaries,
  healers, guards, burial grounds, local delegates, witness protection, and
  survival support during appeal.
- **Historical formation:** the Three Ledgers Schism supplies adopted pressure
  for separated custody without making that settlement universal.
- **Contradiction:** separation prevents total succession while slowing action;
  centralizers and reformers inherit different lessons from the same crisis.
- **Play:** records can be recovered, compared, carried, hidden, challenged,
  or used to reopen relief and severance claims without revealing a god's true
  mind.

## Falsification

### Hostile or marginal perspective

A household denied relief says every office protects its own clean record while
hunger crosses all three jurisdictions. A centralizing cleric says separated
custody turns every emergency into paralysis and lets rival patrons forge the
copy most convenient to them.

### Historical stress case

The Three Ledgers Schism tests whether a Public account preserves separated
authority, incomplete restitution, and competing memories without becoming a
factual transcript or a universal law.
