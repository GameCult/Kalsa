## Falsification

### Hostile reader

A reader who wants every answer remains free to inspect the repository. The design must not claim security it does not possess.

### Unwillingly spoiled player

A fresh site visitor uses navigation, search, sitemap, RSS, and backlinks. None may expose author ontology or an answer key before that visitor chooses the repository spoiler path.

### Character with an informed player

The human may know the complete model. The character agent still receives only its bounded projection; human spoiler choice cannot widen runtime retrieval.

## Authority map after repair

- **Owner:** author subject notes own truth; public notes own situated presentation; Ghostlight owns runtime projection and adjudication.
- **Inputs:** author canon, situated source text, explicit reveal decisions, character and campaign state.
- **Outputs:** spoiler-safe reader site plus open author/GM vault; future compiler artifacts remain a Ghostlight responsibility.
- **Derived state:** Quartz and later compiled packets lower owned state without acquiring truth authority.
- **Forbidden writers:** public prose cannot rewrite hidden facts; author truth cannot enter character context by proximity; resolver outcomes cannot grant causal understanding automatically.
- **Shared paths:** author canon → audience/reveal review → public source or compiler input → bounded consumer → receipt → explicit reveal/canonization.
- **Deleted or demoted authority:** `contentDir Kalsa` no longer decides public eligibility.
