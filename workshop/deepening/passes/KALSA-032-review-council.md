# KALSA-032 — Deepening Review Council

## Objective

Make every critique/repair pass answer to several independent editorial
faculties before completion. Add one critic dedicated to recurring AI-shaped
prose habits, grounded in the current Vault rather than a generic banned-word
list.

## Source inventory

### External and neighboring evidence

- Wikipedia's `Signs of AI writing` field guide is descriptive rather than
  prescriptive. It identifies clusters such as superficial significance,
  vague attribution, negative parallelism, rule-of-three lists, inflated
  copular substitutes, abstract associations, repeated formatting templates,
  and excessive rhetorical symmetry. It warns that signs are not proof and
  that detectors and human guesses produce false positives.
- AetheriaLore's live `Vault Cleanup` contract separates Modeling, Imagination,
  fresh-reader questioning, source-bounded lore research, Hands, and Soul.
  Its `Revision Council Master Prompt`, `Revision Council Specialist Prompts`,
  and `Revision Council Passes` provide the reusable independent-report,
  coordinator-adjudication, bounded-repair, regression, and fresh-reader shape.
  Kalsa does not copy its novel-specific specialties.

### Kalsa evidence

- Public and author prose repeatedly uses corrective antithesis, especially
  `not X; Y`, `rather than`, and `can ... and still ...`.
- Public summaries often praise a wonder, immediately balance it with social
  costs, and close with a list-shaped moral checksum. The Ti'asantatca passage
  in `Public/World/Peoples and Polities.md` is the clearest operator-identified
  example.
- Lists of four to eight nouns recur as a signal of comprehensiveness even when
  one concrete consequence would carry more force.
- Authoring vocabulary such as `authority`, `bounded`, `material`, `legible`,
  `claim`, `record`, `preserve`, and `consequence` is load-bearing in workshop
  maps but can flatten culture, setting prose, and character speech into one
  institutional essay voice.
- Existing Soul review is strong at truth, ownership, and boundary checks but
  has no durable prompt surface for prose uncanniness or reader fatigue.

## Authority map before repair

- **Owner:** the pass coordinator informally decides which reviews to request
  and how to interpret them.
- **Inputs:** seed evidence, owner notes, pass records, diff, benchmark, and
  occasional ad hoc specialist findings.
- **Outputs:** one synthesis and a final Soul verdict.
- **Derived state:** commentary, agent reports, and grep inventories may reveal
  pressure but have no standard artifact or completion gate.
- **Forbidden writers:** reviewers may not write canon, but this is convention
  rather than an explicit council contract.
- **Shared paths:** no required path guarantees that every dedicated critic
  receives the same candidate or speaks before repair.
- **Cut line:** retire ceremonial `Soul review` checkboxes that can be satisfied
  by one undifferentiated opinion.

## Process model

- **Function:** seven independent lore critics inspect one immutable review packet;
  the coordinator adjudicates their findings; the same seven receive the
  repaired candidate for bounded regression.
- **Authority and enforcement limits:** critics diagnose and recommend. They do
  not edit canon, vote truth into existence, or compel a rewrite by majority.
- **Material support:** stable prompts, one shared report contract, raw reports,
  synthesis, dispositions, regression reports, and the pass record.
- **Variation:** Reader Experience receives reader-visible prose without
  workshop intention or hidden canon. Other critics receive only the context
  necessary for their remit.
- **Internal contradiction:** requiring all voices can become ceremony. A
  critic may return `no finding`; the coordinator must not manufacture work to
  justify the role.

## Repair

- Add `workshop/review-council/` as the process owner.
- Define seven dedicated critics: institutional causality; culture and situated
  knowledge; material life and ecology; history, conflict, and change;
  Ghostlight play and consequence; AI-isms and prose; and reader experience
  and navigation.
- Give the AI-Isms and Prose critic a Kalsa-specific calibration without
  converting examples into forbidden strings.
- Require raw independent reports, coordinator dispositions, and post-repair
  regression from every critic in each deepening loop.
- Integrate the council into the deepening README, pass template, benchmark,
  Body map, project instructions, and project memory.

## Falsification

### Hostile perspective

A competent human writer uses antithesis, a three-item list, or the word
`bounded` because the sentence needs it. The critic must be able to protect the
passage and explain why local function outweighs statistical resemblance.

### Stress case

Seven critics disagree. The coordinator preserves every raw report, rules on
each substantive finding, and repairs only accepted defects. Absence of
consensus does not create split ownership; absence of a critic report blocks
completion.

## Authority map after repair

- **Owner:** `workshop/review-council/README.md` owns council membership,
  sequencing, packet boundaries, and report requirements. The pass coordinator
  owns adjudication. Canonical subject notes retain lore authority.
- **Inputs:** one immutable candidate packet, protected strengths, target
  audience, exact source authority, and bounded context per critic.
- **Outputs:** seven raw reports, one synthesis with dispositions, seven
  regression responses, and a finite repair list.
- **Derived state:** pattern counts, detector scores, consensus, and critic
  confidence are evidence only.
- **Forbidden writers:** no critic, report, detector, word list, or majority
  may edit canon or decide truth.
- **Shared paths:** packet → seven independent reports → coordinator
  synthesis and dispositions → owner repair → seven regressions → final
  verification and record.
- **Deleted or demoted authority:** an unchecked or generic `Soul review` label
  no longer stands in for the council.
