## Institutional model

- **Function:** the Study of Openings makes sudden systemic comprehension and
  learned causal records teachable, disputable, and reusable without claiming
  one complete metaphysics.
- **Authority and enforcement limits:** scholarly lineages own teaching,
  commentary, archives, publication, and internal discipline. Houses of
  Attestation own certification; Measured Offices own government; courts own
  verdict; cults own appointment; custodians own safe operation.
- **Recruitment, training, rank, compensation, discipline, succession, exit:**
  temple schools, public colleges, family tutors, client academies, office
  apprenticeships, work crews, and commentary circles recruit differently.
  Teacher-to-student lineages transfer archive duties and unfinished students,
  not blood authority, openings, certificates, office, or truth.
- **Material support, records, places, tools, maintenance, supply:** food,
  lodging, stipends, copied cases, witnesses, teachers, test rooms, diagrams,
  scale mechanisms, survey tools, translation, travel, field access, temple
  estates, public levies, patron trusts, and safely kept Crown interfaces.
- **Doctrine versus practice:** every lineage claims disciplined limits while
  patronage, archive custody, prestige speech, divine standing, and access to
  consequential cases shape what becomes publishable knowledge.
- **Beneficiaries, costs, exclusion, refusal:** opened readers and archive-owning
  families receive prestige; learned readers, workers, clerks, and clients make
  comparison possible while often losing authorship. Readers can refuse unsafe
  extension, witness erasure, or confidence presented as verdict.
- **Dependencies and neighboring institutions:** High Lojban traditions,
  concept cults, Houses of Attestation, Measured Offices, Techpriests, relic
  custodians, courts, work crews, client governments, and petition archives.
- **Variation by class, region, lineage, language, magic:** Concordance Temples,
  Trial Boards, Four-Because Chairs, and Named-Edge Circles privilege different
  evidence and reproduce through different material bodies. Translated names do
  not pretend one descendant register governs them all.
- **Historical formation and path dependence:** trial practice descends or
  claims descent from Crown exercises; temple schools join opening to divine
  concepts; causal philology develops around law and translation; Named-Edge
  reform grows through client failures such as Santabi'u. Competing antiquity
  claims remain unresolved.
- **Internal contradiction and factions:** the field depends on ordinary
  observation while prestige treats firsthand opening as authorship; every
  school's strongest method creates a predictable blind spot.
- **Player-facing consequences:** disputed records, suppressed adverse cases,
  contested authorship, dangerous teaching interfaces, rival patrons, and
  models that are locally useful without deciding which authority may act.

## Critique

- Generic fantasy defaults: a single correct magical academy would merely put
  author omniscience in robes.
- Missing material constraints: schools and lineages presently lack named
  archives, teaching cases, patronage, instruments, and succession.
- False consensus or absent subordinate perspectives: imperial certification
  is overrepresented relative to workers, clients, low-expression scholars,
  cult interpreters, and competing regional methods.
- Consequences that stop at the page edge: Public prose names examination but
  does not yet let a reader encounter a Ju'onai argument as an inhabitant would.
- Duplicate or ambiguous owners: a new scholarship note must own traditions
  and models without stealing magical mechanics, certification, or government.

## Falsification

### Hostile or marginal perspective

A low-expression keeper supplies the marks, work history, and omitted lateral
that every school needs. In `Four Readings of a Broken Sluice`, she rejects the
role of anonymous witness, demands attribution and material repair, and exposes
how separated scholarly authority can still become collective evasion.

### Historical stress case

The Santabi'u test correctly described the prepared gates and became false when
used for the valley. Trial reformers demand transfer tests; Four-Because Chairs
separate mechanism, motive, justification, and entailment; Named-Edge Circles
preserve omitted people and review horizons; Concordance Temples split rather
than receiving a convenient common verdict.
