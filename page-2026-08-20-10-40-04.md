## VI – Channeling

Step by step, Prometheus has been nearing the end of his road. That end was already in sight at this point, but before his grand breeding plan could come to its natural conclusion another step had to be taken. Both the ability to affect the world and the ability to understand it had been created, and both had variants that were able to act on larger scales. In preparation for the next step, Prophecy had been successfully created using a mixed heritage of Analysis and Shamanism. The next step would be to mix Analysis and Sorcery, in an attempt to create the ultimate offensive magic.

## Evolution

To make this next step succeed, it was necessary to create an evolutionary game which forced its individuals to adopt a combative nature, but which would not move the entirety of the conflict into the mental domain. A delicate balance was needed in order to mix the virtual metagame of Analysis with the gladiatorial fitness of Sorcery. This was achieved by giving them the same virtual interface used by Analysis but no arena for them to use it in. Instead, individuals could only interface with each other when they were close enough for their implants to resonate, creating an environment where combat in the physical world had its simultaneous parallel in an augmented space. This very direct battle of wits allowed a very precise strategy to dominate the field: in order to survive a warrior needed to attain an intimate understanding of the forces that determine the outcome of a battle, and realign those forces without providing a mental opening for the enemy. After many generations the bionic crutch of the implants was removed, but it was no longer necessary. The trait itself had changed in character to replace the implants. Channeling was born.

## Magic

Channeling is not an easy magic for its performers. It is in fact so difficult to properly harness that attaining the balance necessary is enough to redefine the channeler. A Channeler sees the forces around him as consisting of various kinds of flows. He senses the motions of energy and its inertia, and in feeling them he changes the way they flow. He does not perform prophecy, but is excellent at prediction. In combat, the Channeler is the natural enemy of the Sorcerer, whose attacks take the form of directed energy which only provide the Channeler with greater power. Since a Channeler performs his magic by taking psychic grasp of various flows in his environment, he is a natural candidate for the application of anti-magic in all its forms. A barrier woven by Channelers can fence in the effect of any magic user, since it doesn’t allow the lines of influence to spread.

## Attributes

Channelers were descended from both Analysis and Sorcery stock, allowing them to overcome the physical limitations of Analysis without the bulky ursine features of Shamanism. A pacifistic outlook and a nocturnal foraging lifestyle prompted physical changes including a small body, large eyes and head, prominent incisors and other features of rodent herbivores. They are unsightly thin by human standards and can communicate with each other through the creative and subtle application of their magic, allowing the ability to vocalize to become vestigial. Contrary to the urban legend, they do not communicate by squeaking as bats do; their squeak has meaning only as an exclamation.

## Culture

Channelers identify themselves as “Sarxe,” “the harmonious”. Just as their magic system revolves around synergistic collaboration, so does their society. With their extraordinary ability to communicate with each other and their natural sensitivity to environmental changes, they are able to live and work together so well as to form a type of hive-civilization. Sarxe are notoriously social, those that are capable of being alone without feeling great discomfort are by definition insane, and there is very little incentive to communicate outside of their species. An insane Sarxe is still Sarxe however, and to better serve society they are relegated to those roles which involve communicating with other civilizations. As a result, it is only these individuals which humans tend to come into contact with.

## Wonders

The Sarxe Hive, Saxfoldi (harmonious field), contains the main body of their civilization. It is completely mobile and is always on the move, but its existence is carefully kept secret by the Sarxe. The Saxfoldi was originally static, a gathering place for their people and their sole, massive population center. When the Terjamna broke through, Sarxe lands were the first to be hit. They were able to put up a significant resistance, but one of their Soiru’i Gods infiltrated Saxfoldi and sacrificed himself there, creating a magical beacon that allowed the Terjamna to overwhelm Sarxe resistance and slaughter vast numbers of civilians. The slaughter is remembered as Ji’esti (Extinction), and ever since that day they have never made the mistake of settling their capitol, to ensure that Ji’esti can never happen again. Ji’esti is commemorated every cycle.
