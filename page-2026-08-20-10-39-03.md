## Chaos Magic and the Birth of Gods

## Introduction

The premise for chaos magic is really quite a simple one. It originated with speculation regarding the true nature of prescient ability. After all, the future itself changes through the act of observing it. I would not deign to attribute to time a will of its own, preferring to keep the universe roughly deterministic. Therefore prescience, in order to allow for a multitude of futures, must exist on a level beyond our 4-dimensional timeline, in a way that is easiest described by the visualization of a fifth dimension.

But, if observing time allows you to steer the present, how specific could the path be that the oracle leads you down? Surely with prescience it would be possible to manipulate open systems – because the ability would tell one exactly which levers to pull in order to bring about a given event. Systems where small variations domino into greater effects (chaotic systems) would provide particularly fertile ground, and the prescient would be able to manipulate these systems to do their bidding, depending on what sort of information can be gleaned from the future, and how much.

## Taxonomy

There are several different steps in the process of using chaos magic, and these lead to a rough categorization of the different ways to use it, using three binary variables:

-   Clarity, the ability to glean information from time which can be used to perceive causal chains and how to influence them
-   Depth, the ability to find the levers which manipulate a system to bring about a given solution – sort of like working backwards from the desired effect
-   Scope, the scale on which prescience operates – there is chaos to be used on every scale and different styles require

None of these is truly binary, but thinking of them that way gives rise to 7 different kinds of chaos magic, a good variety considering it has many applications, sorcery being only one of them. The 7 different styles and the societies that gave them rise are outlined in Hierarchy of Chaos Magic

## Origin of Magical Power

When you imagine 4-dimensional space as being a timeline (each point being a single universe-state), a line perpendicular to that occupies a fifth dimension, containing all the possible states of the world. In order to fold between points on the plane formed by 4D and 5D, the object must occupy space in the sixth dimension. The variations introduced by random fluctuations give individuals a curious shape in the upper dimensions, they and other chaotic systems seem to dominate the landscape, because small changes domino quickly in them. Allowing for the fantasy of free will (saying that people have some control over their future), every person occupies a small volume in the sixth dimension, which I will refer to as their Individual Projection.

Some people have the capacity to develop oracular (prescient) powers – according to the presence of the genetic marker and the individual’s pressing need for the power.

## The Upper-Dimensional Food Chain

When a person’s Individual Projection folds in on itself along the 4D – prescient information transfer –  this triggers a violent expansion in 5D, as the fold opens doors to new futures. The exploded 5D being has an extended consciousness that inhabits the upper dimensions, referred to as a Chaos Beast. Chaos Beasts manifest as a purposeful twisting in the pattern of the world, and each one has its own personality according to which type of genetic marker gave birth to it (there are 7) and the personality of the individual that created it.

Some Chaos Beasts have the potential to become God Beasts, by absorbing the identities of lesser Chaos Beasts and Individual Projections and redistributing the chaos magic that they all produce. God Beasts grow in power by absorbing other Projections and Beasts. When an Individual Projection is absorbed, the God Beast takes the chaos it generates and converts it to its will, which can manifest itself wherever the absorbed Projections exist.

That is to say, all the chaos magic absorbed by the God Beast is available to those directly beneath it in the absorption hierarchy, subject to the God’s whims. Magic flows upwards into the hierarchy, and downwards through the God Beast to the projections it controls. What flows, however, is only the potential for magic, the upper-dimensional “body” of the God Beast which can move and spread like an amoeba. Magical potential is information obtained from the future, the fold along 4D. That is why 6D volume is also called potential, because that volume is what the information flows through. Chaos magic comes in a variety of exciting flavors, as the 7 cities has its own version of the marker, each of which produces a different 4D fold and a different 6D volume.

One important aspect to note is that Projections never really disappear unless they willingly loose their grip on the world, after which the space occupied by the Projection is taken up by a reborn individual. The remaining identities have no magical power, since they no longer inhabit the lower dimensions, but in the presence of a large magical potential (like the passing of a God Beast) they can steal some of that potential and use it to manifest in the real world. In this way, lost souls and the carcasses of unascended Chaos Beasts are thaumavores, appearing only through the magic they consume from other bodies. The magical potential they steal is used however they will, but the majority of thaumavores want recognition more than anything else, one final attempt to survive through ascension, and so they behave more often as poltergeists, though ancestral spirits have been known to shield their descendants with the magic they steal.

The parasitic presence of thaumavores means that God Beasts are fundamentally limited in how much of their power they can concentrate in one place and for how long, because an extended miracle attracts all of the nearby thaumavores, which promptly begin feasting on the magical potential. The God Beast must retract its amoebic body before it is consumed, or risk converting its entire power base into a magical fountain. When this happens, however, long-dead gods and sleeping ghosts come back to life for as long as the fountain lasts. The death of a God Beast, then, can mean the birth of many others depending on the visibility of the event.

## Nations

### 000(0) – Null, no magical power

Formed settlements outside of the 7 Great Cities before the Fall, expanded early on to fill the spaces between and flood the Great Cities with population, so that their bloodlines are strong everywhere and genes which are conducive to magic (descended from Ring inhabitants) are rare. Tribal religion largely disappeared with the Fall, taking on more of a monotheistic flavor, which was itself supplanted by polytheistic beliefs of the two great religious powers.

### 001(1) – Scope only, manifests as luck

Isolated city, generates God Beasts but no information flows outwards. Users have no conscious control, and life in the city is wild and crazy all the time. Some enter, but none may leave. Pretty much self-sufficient, in the mountains coldward, isolationist and anti-expansionistic.

### 010(1) – Clarity only, user can analyse chaotic systems with conscious effort

One of the two connected cities capable of generating God Beasts. Type II’s (sunward) empire is grown through caraful analysis and tactical expansion, religion focuses on transcendence and enlightenment. Sunward, on the very edge, very tan and therefore to the mind of the twilight cultures more civilized and aristocratic.

### 011(0) – Clarity/Scope, user can ‘see’ through time and over distances, with predictive effect

Consciously precognitive, prophetic capacity lends great dimension to a God Beast’s powers and thus is greatly sought after by the religious cities. City is divided between the two empires, prophets are promptly snatched up and people are used to living in fear. Somewhere between the two empires. Since the Fall, their city has been infected by the religious beliefs of the native populations. All the more reason to resist religious takeover.

### 100(1) – Depth only, wildly improbably events happen often (e.g. spontaneous combustion)

Militaristic society, coldward with little in the way of arable farmland. Capable of generating godbeasts. However, in possession of the iron and chaos powers conducive to said militaristic lifestyle. Expansionistic by necessity. They are placed close to an isolated area (where they at some point discover Prometheus, who takes over their pantheon).

### 101(0) – Depth/Scope, user’s will manifests over time and space (e.g blessings or curses)

Agrarian society, who due to the nature of their shamanistic powers have cultivated patience and a sense of inevitability. Unable or unwilling to defend themselves, they seek the protection of the technologically-oriented Type VII civilization. Sunward, plenty of arable farmland, isolated enough from the two major religious powers to not be taken over.

### 110(0) – Depth/Clarity, user can consciously manipulate chaotic systems

Got forcibly evicted from their city, now fighting mercenary wars at the pay of other cities, as the nature of their power gives them a particular talent for that form of action. They are capable of feeling their environment and consciously shifting it, granting them prowess as hunters and assassins. Technological scavengers in the service of their alliance with Type V and VII. Their original city was coldward and close to the Type IV nexus (although more sunward), as the Type IV civilization took over early on and pretty much absorbed it.

### 111(0) – Depth/Clarity/Scope, manifests as intuition: user has unusual insights about situations

A city of very sharp traders, politicians, and pretty much everything else. Not particularly expansionistic, but entirely capable of defending themselves. Possessed of a great national ego, founded on a very real history of success. Allied with type V civilization as a supply base. Coldward of Type V but close by and isolated from the two main religious powers.
