## II – Sorcery

The first ring to receive the trait as a result of Prometheus’ breeding program, his goal here was to make the effects of Luck more focused, spontaneous. To do this, he engineered a society geared entirely around warlike tribal conquest.

## Evolution

The population was divided into small tribes, all given the task of expanding their influence as quickly as possible by dominating the opposition militarily. After total conquest they would use that power to genocide the losing tribes and expand the winning tribe. Then the game would restart, with the children of the winning tribe divided against each other. Generation after generation, this struggle shortened the scope of the trait to form a more focused type of magic, one that was useful in fighting and little more.

## Magic

Sorcery is, unlike Luck, intermittent. A sorcerer is not subject to a constant fortune, but his unconscious mind feels the capacity of the universe to generate chaos. The untrained mind uses this awareness automatically at times of need, to cull highly specific information from the void and use it to bring advantage to a situation. Battles between sorcerers are more like gunfights than wrestling, simply because their magical power is so focused that the chaos they control rarely overlaps. When it does, the magical potential stored in the warring beasts drains quickly until the weaker beast submits.Submission, as one would expect, results in the complete subordination of magical power to the winner. A battle between sorcerers, therefore, is a careful balance between offense and defense where blows must be parried but shields cannot be held, for doing so invites the final blow of submission. Even for those that are knowingly superior it is unwise to bring a battle to its end so quickly, for the end drains one of magical potential equal to the amount left over in the losing party. The transition to godhood is completely natural for sorcerers, since conquest is so deeply bred into the trait. Sorcerer Godbeasts seek to use their power to bring ever more competitors into submission, but they rarely survive long after the death of their earthly anchor, for the raw threat of force disappears and is usually dissolved in the struggle to fill the power-void that forms after the death of a sorcerer.

## Attributes

Terjamna have the blood of warriors, as such they are generally of great stature and bulk, but not particularly bright. There is very little dilution of their ratial features in the core society, but conquered peoples are subject to much interbreeding with the conquerors. They are hardy and resourceful, their ring having been dropped far coldward in an area of terrible weather; passage to the sunny lands was impeded by thunder and hailstorms.

## Culture

Following the trend of their magic, terjamna (people of Jamnai) are deeply militaristic. Each and every terjamna is locked into the characteristic unending power struggle, which arranges their society into a strict hierarchy. Victory in combat determines who is capable of leading, and the alpha male is the one who has succeeded in forcing the most submissions. This does not necessarily require magic, but magic tends to help a lot. Even in a society where power is so simple, loyalty has its place. Effective leaders are tolerated, and champions often spring up to defend the hierarchy from instability. As submission brings magical power to the victors, they can use that power to increase their standing in the hierarchy. Sideways conquest is considered a direct threat to those higher up (since it cuts power-branches off), so Jamnai culture is fiercely protective of underlings and loyalties run strong and deep. Soiru’i (warrior god) is the residual power of a dead terjamna, it can be championed just as a living terjamna would be, and there are several powerful religious sects that do just that.

## Wonders

Jamnai places much cultural significance on the artifact that allowed them to survive the biting cold before they discovered ways through the storms. Like many baltcadu (great cities), they had access to extensive technology immediately after the Fall, which they used to build a geothermal generator powering a self-sustaining arcology, an isolated oasis of light and warmth in the midst of darkness and cold. They had long lost the knowledge of how it worked by the time they found their way through the weather, so it is more of a religious artifact than anything else.
