# Kalsa Project Memory

This file preserves judgment that should bend future work. It is not a lore
database and does not grant canon status to any setting claim.

## Durable judgments

- `memory-authority-boundary` — Canonical notes under `Kalsa/` own setting truth; memory, workshop records, retrieval indexes, and Quartz output are evidence or projections only.
- `memory-seed-witness` — The teenage export must remain byte-identical evidence. Repair happens in new canonical owner notes, never by laundering edits back into the witnesses.
- `memory-depth-standard` — Institutional parity with AetheriaLore means comparable causal density across authority, reproduction, material support, contested legitimacy, dependencies, history, and playable consequences; page and word counts are diagnostics only.
- `memory-language-history` — Prometheus's imposed logical forms should become living culture through inheritance, misuse, repair, ritual, class performance, and regional change rather than remaining frozen design specifications.
- `memory-pass-discipline` — Deepening work uses one bounded target, one explicit owner map, one hostile or marginal perspective, one historical stress case, and one verified repair before moving on.
- `memory-no-persona-yet` — Kalsa currently needs project memory and work discipline, not an invented public repo Persona. Persona birth requires a separate operator-approved identity and jurisdiction.
- `memory-authority-follows-consequence` — Magical insight, technical competence, office, custody, possession, land use, divine standing, and civic judgment are separate authorities. Each owns only the consequence it can legitimately inspect and answer for.
- `memory-evidence-not-verdict` — Forecasts, analytical models, lineage records, provenance classifications, and clerical testimony are contestable evidence. None becomes a verdict or author truth merely because an institution recorded it.
- `memory-residual-salvage` — Dungeon recovery composes prior site, dependency, custody, kin, cult, personhood, and hazard claims. A delving compact allocates only residual movable salvage and binds only its parties.
- `memory-candidate-quarantine` — An `adopted-candidate` is non-factual design pressure. It cannot establish present actors, obligations, history, or consequences in canonical neighbors until an explicit adoption decision gives those claims an owner.
- `memory-event-owner` — Each adopted named history has one factual event owner. Institution, polity, place, and dungeon notes consume it through links and retain only their current evidence, procedures, and consequences.
- `memory-parity-scope` — Depth parity applies to the adopted high-impact institutional set and is judged by causal density, not bulk. Candidates and unresolved or preservation stubs remain visibly excluded rather than being inflated into false completeness.
- `memory-play-proof` — Abstract institutional machinery is not enough for Ghostlight use. At least one concrete threshold, route, clock, claimant collision, and consequence surface must prove that the setting can be entered and played.
- `memory-open-spoiler-friction` — Kalsa's author vault remains open and inspectable. `Kalsa/index.md` is a reader-first consent gate, `Kalsa/Public/` owns the fantasy-first reader path, and all author/GM material is physically enclosed under `Kalsa/Spoilers/`; warnings and deliberate navigation provide opt-in friction rather than pretending the material is secret.
- `memory-epistemic-split` — Author truth, reader lore, character knowledge, and adjudicator affordances are different projections with different owners. Proximity in the vault does not grant a reader or character access.
- `memory-public-epistemic-ownership` — `Kalsa/Public/` is an inhabitant-facing knowledge commons, not an abbreviated author encyclopedia. Explanatory models require a culture, institution, lineage, text, witness, or named synthesis; translated terms must carry evidence, material reproduction, interests, and characteristic limits rather than smuggling backstage taxonomy into player knowledge.
- `memory-divine-uncertainty` — Gods are powerful, situated witnesses rather than authorial oracles. Divine confidence, memory, doctrine, and testimony remain evidence shaped by binding, rivalry, self-conception, and blind spots.
- `memory-play-canon` — Persistent play creates receipts and canon candidates. Runtime outcomes become shared setting canon only when an owning canonical note explicitly adopts them.
- `memory-independent-lore-review` — Every deepening pass routes all eight Lore Review Council seats according to their version-controlled modes, including Oracle Ecology and Timeline Competition. Active Critics independently diagnose the baseline and regress the repaired candidate. Guardians independently review the exact diff, complete changed notes, and affected context; only periodic or triggered sanity checks reopen their full surface. The coordinator adjudicates and subject notes own canon.
- `memory-exhausted-critic-guardian` — Exhaustion is an earned, surface-bound transition from Critic to Guardian: a dedicated immutable-packet loop must resolve every finding, pass the exact shipping body with nothing left at any priority, and receive a fresh final full-surface confirmation. One quiet report is not exhaustion. Guardians inspect new changes, run a full sanity check every fifth relevant review or earlier on recurrence, broad surface change, prompt/architecture change, operator evidence, or baseline doubt, and return to Critic mode when systemic pressure reappears. AI-Isms and Prose entered Guardian mode on KALSA-038 candidate 06, then returned to Critic mode when the KALSA-039 full-surface sanity check found systemic repeated-frame pressure. A later clean pass certifies only its candidate; it does not re-exhaust the seat.
- `memory-oracle-ecology-spine` — Kalsa's fundamental causal pressure is innumerable heterogeneous oracles competing inside the same reachable field, not a large population of Prophets. Luck has the widest natural reach and its Gods most closely produce the life-shaping attractor associated with singular prescience, but rival Luck, unlike Gifts, material exhaustion, escape, and divided authority keep capture local and contested. `Oracle Ecology` owns only the author-facing interaction invariants; each inhabitant theory needs an evidence-bearing culture, institution, lineage, text, witness, or named synthesis.
- `memory-temporal-transaction` — A Gift's oracular role must be stated as its own temporal transaction and tested through action: Luck accumulates survival pressure without a report; Sorcery receives the first local departure its embodied person-Beast act can reach; Shamanism holds a named target; Analysis receives consequence as present relevance inside an opening; Prophecy compares branches; Channeling receives the imminent continuation of active flow. Cross-Gift work must preserve those asymmetries instead of distributing one prophecy-shaped sentence per Gift.
- `memory-public-provenance-granularity` — Public cultural and historical specificity cannot exceed its hidden owner merely because a more particular carrier sounds vivid. Timing, transmission, recitation form, institutional continuity, and later reform each need an adopted owner. When canon owns only an account or a present citation, Public must not promote it into an ancient lineage, marching tradition, immediate reform, or universal later practice.
- `memory-ai-ism-judgment` — AI-shaped prose is diagnosed by recurring construction and reading effect, never detector scores, word bans, or authorship claims. The unit of failure may be the corpus: locally polished paragraphs become alienating when different cultures repeatedly pass through the same disclaimer, balanced dispute, institutional repair, and unresolved-invoice sequence. Kalsa's known pressure includes correction theatre, symmetrical wonder-then-cost ledgers, list-clause congestion, repeated frames, checksum conclusions, viewpoint choruses, workshop-dialect leakage, and generic smoothing. Repair the repeated argumentative architecture through source carrier, action, custody, omission, and uneven emphasis; functional lists, deliberate rhetoric, exact uncertainty, and precise technical terms remain protectable. The version-controlled `AI-Isms and Prose` critic prompt carries an actual Kalsa before/after example and false-positive guidance for every named pressure.
- `memory-public-entry-and-omission` — Public navigation should offer a concrete situated problem before optional surveys. An overview is allowed to omit traditions: shuffling every hidden family into a new order still reconstructs the author taxonomy. Let source-owned mechanisms, witnesses, and disagreements carry ethical limits instead of adding narrator-level correction ledgers and checksum conclusions.
- `memory-situated-divine-evidence` — Public divine knowledge should follow the
  record and office responsible for each consequence: covenant origin,
  miracle delivery, and mortuary evidence remain distinct. A god's claim is
  consequential evidence, never author narration. Protected historical blanks
  stay blank even when a reader would prefer a named cult or deity; situated
  ownership is provenance, not cosmetic naming.
- `memory-situated-memory-carriers` — A Public historical source needs owners
  for later memory as well as owners for evidence and action. Councils,
  victims, households, sects, and offices may preserve incompatible lessons
  from one event without becoming duplicate fact owners. If one neutral record
  carries every later meaning, the culture has flattened even when the event
  sequence remains correct.
- `memory-projection-completeness` — Search, sitemap, RSS, and HTML routes are
  exact projections of every admitted Public note. A renderer's default item
  limit is not permission to silently drop older reader sources as the vault
  grows.
- `memory-institution-through-handoff` — Public should make institutional
  distinctions visible through evidence-bearing handoffs among people,
  records, tools, stores, and consequences. An exhaustive narrator taxonomy
  remains author ontology even when every distinction is correct. A situated
  case may omit the complete system while showing exactly who can act, what
  they can inspect, and what their correction leaves unpaid.
- `memory-latent-capacity` — Reserve and relief are institutional state, not
  background texture. A field, barrier, service, or office can still produce a
  visible result after the seed, replacement body, recovery time, or household
  support needed for its next result has already been spent. Public sources
  should make that delayed failure legible through owned evidence.
- `memory-correction-is-not-remedy` — Correcting an official finding and
  repairing the harm it caused are separate institutional outputs. A source
  should identify the record owner, harmed carrier, material evidence, remedy
  owner, and enforcement path without treating a corrected file as restored
  work, shelter, standing, care, or trust.
