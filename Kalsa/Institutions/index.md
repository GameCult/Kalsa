# Institutions

Kalsa's institutions grew around needs that survived the [[Foundations/Ark and Fall#The Fall|Fall]]: keeping inherited systems alive, distributing scarce service, making dangerous knowledge transmissible, and deciding who may touch relics on which other lives depend. Their authority is rarely complete. A city authority, temple, town, lineage, guild, household, and god may each hold a different claim over the same structure. Their canonical scope follows [[Reference/Canon and Provenance|Canon and Provenance]].

## Maintenance, ruins, and recovery

- [[Institutions/The Techpriesthoods|The Techpriesthoods]] — plural transborder lineages that preserve procedural competence and bargain for the support needed to keep inherited infrastructure working.
- [[Institutions/Relic Custody|Relic Custody]] — the bounded claims, records, duties, and handoffs surrounding machines that cannot be treated as ordinary portable wealth.
- [[Institutions/Relic and Ruin Law|Relic and Ruin Law]] — local admission, public-danger, claim, removal, refusal, and appeal procedures at layered sites.
- [[Institutions/Delving Compacts|Delving Compacts]] — expedition work, rescue, record, cost, breach, and residual-salvage obligations among signatories.

## Gods, potential, and death

- [[Institutions/God-Cult Covenants|God-Cult Covenants]] — voluntary, inherited, and coerced routes into reciprocal cult obligation.
- [[Institutions/The Potential Economy|The Potential Economy]] — material support, estimation, allocation, rationing, and leakage around finite divine capacity.
- [[Institutions/Death and Binding Law|Death and Binding Law]] — testimony, severance, funerary custody, and succession where persons and divine structures may continue after bodily death.

## Evidence, language, and administration

- [[Institutions/Prophetic Houses|Prophetic Houses]] and [[Institutions/Forecast Evidence and Courts|Forecast Evidence and Courts]] — training, forecast service, disclosure, admissibility, civic judgment, and appeal in the divided city.
- [[Institutions/Ju'onai Examiners|Ju'onai Examiners]] and [[Institutions/Analyst Administration|Analyst Administration]] — scoped causal certification and the class-bearing government that consumes it.
- [[Institutions/High Lojban and Descendant Speech|High Lojban and Descendant Speech]] — teaching, certification, translation, public notice, treaty use, and elite gatekeeping.
- [[Institutions/Measures, Formulae, and Machine Speech|Measures, Formulae, and Machine Speech]] — technical notation, conversion, machine-recognized sequences, operational refusal, and review.

## Polity institutions

- [[Institutions/Stormshield Circles|Stormshield Circles]] — maintained weather protection, consent, rest, handoff, warning, and failure response. [[Institutions/The Alliance|The Alliance]] is a non-factual `adopted-candidate` awaiting an Intuition-side owner.
- [[Institutions/Jamnai Submission Hierarchy|Jamnai Submission Hierarchy]] and [[Institutions/Soiru'i Sects|Soiru'i Sects]] — civil submission, geothermal obligation, funerary memory, welfare, sanctuary, and resistance.
- [[Institutions/Saxfoldi Households|Saxfoldi Households]] and [[Institutions/Ji'esti Flight and Refuge|Ji'esti Flight and Refuge]] — consent-preserving communal life, mobility, warning, admission, care, recovery, and departure.
- [[Institutions/Selza'a Family Territories|Selza'a Family Territories]] and [[Institutions/Luck Harm and Restitution|Luck Harm and Restitution]] — distributed land and membership claims, mutual defense, material evidence, risk pools, and restitution without detectable Luck.

These notes distinguish technical competence, evidence, office, custody, possession, land use, divine standing, personhood, and judgment. Each authority stops at the consequence it can inspect and answer for. Further institutions remain unresolved until the seed or later adopted canon gives them enough pressure to deserve an owner note.
