# The Narrow Shield

A gulf storm turned while a Sarxe convoy and its households were passing below
Ti'asantatca. Papsenai workers held the cultivated slopes and road approach.
Farther inward, other cohorts held the city and its pressure refuges.

Wind and magical pressure crossed the Sarxe camp. Its Channelers raised a
boundary and turned the crossing away from the road. The nearest shield workers
felt the changed force enter their target and pressed it toward an empty
drainage line. Another cohort received that movement against the city and
strengthened its own hold.

Each group protected the people and place already in its hands. Pressure began
returning through the outer stations faster than their separate signals could
explain it. Relief arrived carrying instructions for conditions that had
passed.

## One watch under one signal

The active Circle lead placed the watch under a narrowing order. Shield signals
went through the central station. Outer field and road bonds were released.
Cohorts, attendants, stores, and shelter access moved to the central refuges.
The Sarxe boundary answered the Circle signal until the watch ended.

The changing corrections stopped. The central cohorts held one target until
the worst crossing passed.

The main barrier and refuges survived. Wind and water crossed the released
road and slopes. The Sarxe convoy lost its departure. People outside the
central shelter claim were separated from those admitted. Papsenai households
lost seed, dwellings, fields, and stored work. Surviving copies record injuries
and deaths without agreeing upon a number.

## After the watch

The narrowing order expired with the watch. Control of the storehouse, shelter
list, and central signal copies remained with the Circle. Relief workers,
outer households, and Sarxe witnesses brought their own records to the review.

Later orders named which signals entered the central account, which targets
would be released, what stores and boundaries came under the lead, who would
bear the transfer, and what ended the command.

The road did not reopen in time for the convoy's departure. Outside the refuge
list, families recovered wet seed, broken shelter frames, and the names of
people separated during admission.
