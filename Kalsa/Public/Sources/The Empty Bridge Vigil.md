# The Empty Bridge Vigil

The shelter flames went out one by one when a divine intermediary called their
flood grants into the high bridge. Watchers had reported damaged approach
works. A low-settlement household had begun evacuation, and a Prophet warned
of deaths if the deck failed during the crossing.

Each shelter grant had kept driven rain and runoff out of roof seams, smoke
vents, and thresholds. It made no food or fuel. It kept ordinary stores,
bedding, and fires usable while the water rose. The available reserve could
hold those three workings or reinforce the bridge through the night.

The bridge received it. The deck stood.

## Water from the mill

Upstream, a Channeler felt a debris dam turning the current toward homes and a
burial terrace. The Channeler cut a spill and sent the first surge through an
older channel. No warning from the cult or its Prophet had reached the mill.

The old channel crossed the bridge approach.

## Along the ridge

The evacuees turned toward the shelters. At two, workers trenched thresholds,
covered roof seams, shifted bedding, and bailed until ordinary stores and fires
could be used. The third shelter had sent workers to the bridge. Its fuel was
wet, its floor stores had to be moved, and it could not receive everyone.

Route households from the low settlement still keep departure times, missing
names, and claims for the people who carried the injured along the ridge. They
do not agree with the cult copies about how many died.

## After the water

Bridge keepers found scour marks and work that would not have survived the
night unaided. The mill settlement kept its homes and burial terrace. The low
settlement carried its missing names and shelter claims to three different
jurisdictions.
