# Intuition — Unresolved

Intuition is signature 111 and Sequence VII, combining Depth, Clarity, and Scope. The seed gives it no dedicated article. This note therefore preserves only the surviving claims and refuses to counterfeit a complete culture from a paragraph.

## Preserved source claims

- Intuition manifests as unusual situational insight.
- Its people are described as exceptionally effective traders, politicians, and general problem-solvers.
- Their polity is not strongly expansionist but can defend itself.
- It possesses a strong national ego grounded in a real history of success.
- It is technologically oriented.
- It is coldward of, near, and allied with the Shamanism polity, for which it serves as supply base and protector in the early sketch.
- Intuition does not normally found an independent God Beast.

These are source strands, not yet an adopted explanation of how a society works. “Sharp traders” is especially at risk of becoming generic clever-merchant wallpaper if expanded without material pressures and institutions.

## Unresolved

- People, polity, Crown City, region, and language names.
- Prometheus's evolutionary game and the relation of all three Kaos axes to intuitive experience.
- Magic limits, observability, training, cost, and interaction with other styles.
- Technology, production, trade, defense, government, class, kinship, and religion.
- The Alliance with [[Polities/Shamanism - Papsenai|Papsenai]]: authority, exchange, mutual aid, coercion, and exit.
- Crown City Wonder and post-Fall history.
- Whether the early national sketch survives at all after institutional deepening.

Any future repair must begin with the polity's material role and an authority map, not a personality adjective.
