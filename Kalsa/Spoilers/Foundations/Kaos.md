# Kaos

Kaos is magic that uses information from possible futures to change what happens now. A mage's Gift reveals or selects an intervention—a gesture, pressure, timing, word, route, or refusal—that can bring one of those futures about. Declaring an outcome does not make it possible: the intervention must work through causes already available in the world.

Small changes have the greatest reach in chaotic systems, where their effects can grow as they propagate. Weather, stressed geology, markets, battles, ecologies, and human decisions can all carry Kaos. What a mage can achieve through each depends on what that system contains.

`Kaos` is an author-facing name. This note sets the constraints for deciding what magic can do; it does not supply Kalsa's cultures with a shared vocabulary or theory.

## Author comparison: three axes

Authors compare Gifts through three separate capabilities:

- **Clarity:** how precisely the mage consciously understands the causes at work. It distinguishes variables, their dependencies, and the available ways to intervene.
- **Depth:** how far the mage or Beast can push an outcome away from what would probably happen without help. It governs selecting and committing to a less probable future, not reaching farther into time.
- **Scope:** how far across space and time information or influence can remain coherent.

These terms are distilled from the seed for backstage comparison. Promethean records may preserve related classifications, but living cultures do not know this table innately. Their scholars classify evidence through their own instruments, work, doctrines, and histories of failure.

The seed's inconsistent descriptions of “forward” and “backward” perception are retired. In the current model, distinguishing consequences uses Clarity, selecting a possible course uses Depth, and extending reach across place or time uses Scope.

## Hard constraints

Kaos creates neither matter nor energy. Every successful working needs all seven conditions:

1. The desired outcome must have a causally reachable path with a nonzero possibility.
2. The necessary matter and energy must be available.
3. The mage, their allies, or their bound power must be able to reach something they can change: a causal lever.
4. The working must have enough Clarity, Depth, and Scope for its style and task.
5. It must have sufficient magical potential.
6. There must be enough time for its causes to produce their effects.
7. No stronger rival, barrier, or God Beast may close the required route.

A large miracle therefore needs a large system susceptible to intervention. Rending a mountain requires stressed geology and exceptional coordination. Heating a lake requires an existing thermal, geological, or technological source. When a tale names no source, it may be compressing the causal account or exaggerating for religious purposes.

Working magic can deplete magical potential and expose the mage to rival Beasts. Competing interventions can corrupt forecasts, while concentrated power can attract [[Spoilers/Foundations/Projections, God Beasts, and Thaumavores|thaumavores]]. Each style also has practical costs of its own, such as trance, preparation, vulnerability, submission, or dependence on a suitable flow.

## Divine-scale processing

A God hierarchy can attempt larger workings because it has more magical potential, more relationships to act through, more preparation, and a wider range of causal systems within reach. It can coordinate different Gifts alongside long records, delegates, people doing material work, and prepared sites. Each contributor still has its own limits. Combining their partial views does not give the hierarchy one complete faculty.

Where a causal route remains open, divine magic can locate, gather, separate, refine, transform, move, and assemble existing material. The work still draws on available physical processes: pressure, heat, gravity, metabolism, machinery, weather, geology, or another source. Prescience also allows preparation before the need is apparent. Matter, people, instructions, and stored energy may already be in place by the time observers recognize what they are for.

This can look like creation or an instantaneous arrival. Witnesses may reasonably describe raiment closing around a champion in a flash as **materialization**. They may call the timely arrival of a concealed or long-guided entity **summoning**. Those words accurately describe their experience. To adjudicate the event, the author must still account for the source matter, energy, preparation, accessible lever, propagation time, and rival actions that made it reachable.

Levitation, radiance, sudden armament, and catastrophic destruction are possible outcomes. They are not a standard list of spells available to every god. Each established occurrence needs its causal account in the note that owns the event or site.

## Observing Kaos

Different Gifts reveal different evidence. None provides a universal ability to detect magic, and witnesses need not agree on what their evidence means. The distinctions here are backstage constraints on perception, not a classification all inhabitants share.

- **Analysis** reveals which present connections will matter to later consequences within an active, bounded system. During that opening, the mage can understand which connection will carry a change through the system. This can expose local signs of intervention, but it does not reveal a soul or Beast directly.

- **Sorcery** gives the user no observation or forecast. The coupled person and Beast have an active local orientation: the immediate change toward which their relation is directed. That change presses backward upon unconscious motor choice, selecting a lever that can produce the first reachable departure from the current course. A superior's strategic aim is a different thing, even when obedience has shaped the local orientation. Other witnesses can encounter only the lever used, the change forced, and any lasting causal scar.

- **Prophecy** can notice where actors working across large horizons deform or obscure possible futures. It may therefore infer that an actor is present without identifying who they are or which style they use.

- **Channeling** senses where an active flow is about to go and how it will respond to deflection, before it finishes crossing the sensed boundary. The Channeler can redirect or contain that crossing. This does not locate a source that is remote or dormant.

- **Shamanic bonding** senses future conflict or pressure affecting the bonded target.

- **Luck** has no distinct activation signal and cannot be detected directly. A prophet may infer persistent Luck when forecasts fail in a recurring pattern. Magical sight alone cannot identify the luck-user.

A trail left by a Chaos Beast or God Beast is therefore a pattern of obscured forecasts or repeated causal scars. It is not a track that every magical observer can see.

## Oracles among oracles

Every Gift uses an oracular relation: information from possible futures affects present action, even when the user sees no conscious forecast. The user, their Beast, their intended response, and the material intervention they make are also parts of the world that other Gifts can encounter and change.

These encounters work differently in each direction. One act blocks another only by closing its causal route, consuming or redirecting its potential, removing a needed lever, changing the system being observed, or making the original question obsolete.

[[Spoilers/Foundations/Oracle Ecology|Oracle Ecology]] owns the explanation of those interactions, including crowded horizons and local timeline capture. This note owns the narrower question of whether an individual intervention is physically and causally reachable.

## Unresolved

- What the quantitative limits are, and whether magical potential can be measured at all.
- Whether Scope limits perception and influence in the same way.
- What to make of the seed's omitted fourth faculty, which concerns perceiving farther across possibility itself.
- What Intuition perceives, and whether Analysis can identify a magical style rather than merely an intervention.
- Where the exact boundary lies between an event with physically zero probability and one that a particular Beast simply cannot reach.
