# Projections, God Beasts, and Thaumavores

A person's existence in Kalsa extends across possible futures and can persist after bodily death. That continuing identity is called a Projection. Repeated magic can also produce something connected to the person that can act in its own right: a Chaos Beast. A Beast can become a god by binding other Beasts and Projections beneath it. Some surviving dead instead draw on passing magical potential to appear and act again.

This note owns the author model for those beings and relations, keeping the consequences of magic, divine power, and death consistent. `Projection`, `Chaos Beast`, `God Beast`, and `thaumavore` are author labels unless a local owner explicitly adopts a related term. Inhabitants encounter manifestations, covenants, inherited relations, divine claims, and dangerous dead. Their institutions preserve records of these encounters; that does not give them the complete taxonomy described here.

## Individual Projections

An **Individual Projection** is a person's continuing identity across the possible selves and futures that remain attached to them. The body visible in ordinary time shows only one sequence of states within that larger volume of possibility.

Magic uses information about possible outcomes to reach a future that would otherwise remain unrealized. A mage's Gift exposes or selects an intervention—a gesture, a word, a change in timing—that can make a reachable outcome happen. The information passes between points in the person's volume of possible selves and futures; this connection is called a **prescient fold**.

The Projection is the continuing person-pattern. A [[#Chaos Beasts|Chaos Beast]] is a separate kind of agency that can form in connection with it.

## Chaos Beasts

A **Chaos Beast** can form through repeated prescient folds. It remains coupled to the person through whom it formed, but is capable of acting; it is not simply another name for that person's continuing identity. The model places this agency in upper dimensions, beyond the bodily sequence visible in ordinary time. Both the person and their Kaos strain—the kind of magic they express—shape its character.

In the lower world, a Beast acts through purposeful patterns of coincidence, perception, intervention, blessing, or force. It does not appear as an ordinary animal.

The seed leaves several boundaries unsettled: whether every mage with an expressed Gift forms a distinct Beast, when the coupled agency becomes self-aware, and what changes when one person expresses more than one magic style.

## God Beasts

Magical intervention requires **magical potential**. It can be supplied, diverted, or depleted, and a divine hierarchy determines who can draw on it. This does not identify it with physical energy or settle what substance, if any, is being conserved. Those questions remain open.

A **God Beast** is a Chaos Beast that has bound other Beasts and Projections beneath it in a hierarchy. The bound identities continue to exist. What is sometimes called *absorption* gives the God priority in directing their magical potential; it does not annihilate them.

The God can draw on potential generated by those beneath it. It may also send potential down the hierarchy, granting protection, authority, or miracles. Its direct children are the Beasts or Projections bound immediately beneath it. A mortal's high clerical title does not, by itself, make them a direct child.

Bindings can arise through the following relations:

- An explicit covenant establishes the relation.
- An attachment is inherited, though the exact mechanism remains unresolved.
- Conflict ends in coerced submission.

These bindings may have similar structures in upper dimensions. Their moral and legal differences remain real.

The God's magic style also shapes the hierarchy:

- **Luck Gods** pursue survival and territory.
- **Sorcerer Gods** reproduce relations of conquest and submission. They often collapse when their living anchor dies.
- **Analysis Gods** embody ideas and feelings, competing through ideological hierarchies.

The survival rules for other styles remain unresolved.

A God can use its routing priority to coordinate potential and responses across many bound actors. If the same hierarchy also controls information, the material means of intervention, and rivals' movement, nearly every reachable future within a limited horizon may come to include its anticipated response. [[Spoilers/Foundations/Oracle Ecology#Timeline capture|Oracle Ecology]] calls this **local timeline capture**.

This control remains contested. Bound identities still exist, outside rivals still act, and different Gifts reveal pressures the God may miss. Each style therefore narrows futures differently: a Luck God through the broad pressure toward its survival, a Sorcerer God through submission, and an Analysis God through its ideological domain.

## Divine knowledge and performed certainty

A God Beast knows the world through what its hierarchy can reach: its relations, magical potential, manifestations, memories, and active conflicts. This can give it extraordinary evidence. It may recognize patterns beyond mortal reach, retain perspectives across deaths, compare distant dependents, and infer causes from centuries of intervention. It still does not possess the complete author model.

What a god can tell someone depends on several limits:

- Its bound people and Beasts can perceive only what their relations expose.
- Memories may or may not survive binding, death, schism, delegation, and divine collapse.
- Its style and scale of intervention determine how it encounters the lower world.
- Rivals, thaumavores, broken routes of potential, and evidence too dangerous to approach restrict its access.
- Its doctrine, self-image, appetites, and political need to appear consistent shape its explanation.

Gods can consequently disagree about their own origins or mistake a stable relation for a universal law. Some conceal doubt and deliver inference as revelation because worship, obedience, or a negotiated covenant depends on their certainty. Others take care to distinguish memory, vision, doctrine, and guesswork. Age and power give a divine claim evidential weight; they do not make it author truth.

Reaching a god can therefore transform a mortal investigation without settling it. The encounter may reveal whole layers of hidden history, but the god explaining an event might be its witness, beneficiary, prisoner, perpetrator, or surviving fragment. Its place in that history still matters when reconstructing what happened.

## Thaumavores

Bodily death does not simply erase a Projection. Some surviving Projections lack independent magical power but can consume potential passing nearby and use it to manifest. These beings are called **thaumavores**. They include lost souls, ancestral presences, and the carcasses of Chaos Beasts that never ascended.

Many want recognition or remembrance, seek to protect descendants, or pursue another way to continue through divine existence. Feeding gives them a means to act, and it makes concentrated miracles dangerous. When a God extends too much potential through one place for too long, thaumavores whose Projections or existing footholds can reach that route gain an opportunity to feed.

A catastrophic divine collapse can release a **magical fountain**. Old ghosts and dead gods may manifest there for as long as diverted potential remains available.

### Acquired potential bodies

Repeated feeding can give a thaumavore an **acquired potential body**: an organized capacity to manifest, act, and maintain a foothold in the lower world. The potential it has taken supports that active form.

Feeders do not all organize this capacity alike. A lost person, a remembered ancestor, and an unascended Beast's carcass may differ in memory, appetite, Gift, and agency. Evidence can establish what the active form does. It does not yet settle whether a substance is conserved through these changes or how complete a person survives within the form.

Destroying an acquired body has immediate consequences. The capacity taken to support it is lost, the manifestation collapses, and its present agency ends. Relations that depended on that foothold can fall silent.

The underlying Individual Projection nevertheless retains its grip on the lower world until voluntary release. That persistence does not establish what else survives the destruction. Memory, commitments, personhood, possible dormancy, and the possibility or form of return remain unresolved.

### Feeding, interception, and ascent

To feed, a thaumavore must reach part of a route carrying magical potential. Its diversion can change how much of a grant arrives, where it goes, when it arrives, or how coherently it holds together.

Someone can detect the feeding only if their relations expose that disruption. Acting against it likewise requires a means of reaching the affected route or feeder. Possible responses include withdrawing the grant, dispersing it, redirecting it, containing it, negotiating, or binding the feeder.

Forceful interception may strip away the acquired body before the feeder gains control. A failed response can leave a loss that goes unnoticed, an overfed manifestation, or a magical fountain.

Captured flow can also allow a thaumavore to act independently on a lasting basis, opening a route toward godhood. It may later stabilize that agency and bind other Beasts or Projections into a God hierarchy. The model leaves open how stabilization works, how the first binding happens, whether this ascent has occurred historically, how common such origins are among present Gods, and whether any of them reliably remember their earlier hunger.

A feeding collision may produce crackling, faces, voices, cold, shadow, or light. None of these appearances supplies a diagnosis on its own. The event, the route involved, and the surviving evidence must establish what happened.

## Release

According to the seed, a Projection persists until it willingly releases its grip on the lower world. A reborn individual can then take up the possibility it occupied.

Current canon retains voluntary release. It treats the account of rebirth as a cosmological interpretation, not a mechanism that an institution has verified.

## Institutional pressure

Worship, submission, apostasy, funerals, ancestral claims, miracles, and divine succession can all change real relations of power under this model. The model does not establish a universal law governing those practices.

The institution notes that develop them must explain who controls a rite, what performing it changes, who can refuse, and who faces the danger when it attracts thaumavores.

## Unresolved

- Whether magical potential is conserved, can be divided, or can be measured independently of a particular intervention that someone can actually reach.
- Which memories, commitments, or parts of personhood survive the destruction of an acquired potential body.
- Whether a thaumavore stripped of that body becomes dormant, can rebuild the same agency, or returns in a meaningfully different manifestation.
- How captured divine flow becomes stable independent agency, and how the first binding establishes a God hierarchy.
- How many present Gods began through thaumavore ascent, and which, if any, remember it reliably.
- Which visible signs can distinguish feeding, interception, binding, ordinary manifestation, and unrelated material effects.
