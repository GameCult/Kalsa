# The Three Orders at Sunwall

The Three Orders at Sunwall is an adopted episode from the contest that divided
the [[Spoilers/Polities/Prophecy - The Divided City|City of Prophets]]. This
note owns its factual sequence. The exact date, gate, houses, commanders, wards,
and relation to a formal armistice remain unresolved.

The episode occurred after Ju'onai tribute rule had been established and while
a Terjamna force was trying to convert military access into permanent custody.
The Sunwall was already feeding wards claimed by both powers.

## The prepared victory

A Ju'onai command assembled a defense around a cargo gate and adjacent lift
routes. Opened readers mapped the loaded structures, movement intervals,
garrison supplies, and approaches available to a Terjamna assault. The command
shifted grain and defenders until its officers judged every useful approach
answerable.

Two Prophetic house teams received different briefings. The Ju'onai-sponsored
team compared futures after the defensive movement. A house under Terjamna
protection was briefed for a seizure led by a champion whose Sorcery had not yet
selected its point of attack. Both reports contained heavy crowding and short
expiry. Neither sponsor delivered its complete planned response to the civic
Sunwall office.

The Ju'onai order held the gate and prepared a counter-movement through its
cargo galleries. The Terjamna order instructed the champion to take the first
opening created by that movement. Each plan had made the other plan's success
one of its own causes.

## The third order

Sunwall stewards saw the altered loads, grain concentration, and armed movement
through galleries whose pressure refuge could not carry the bodies assigned to
it. They closed the cargo configuration under their existing operational
authority. The closure was not proposed in either surviving sponsor briefing.
It removed the counter-movement's route and the champion's expected opening at
the same time.

The closure did not defeat either army. It stranded grain, split work crews,
and placed lower wards under ration pressure. Civic order officers could serve
the closure but could not command either garrison away. House messengers carried
new questions across lines already changing as soldiers and residents moved.

The three orders remained simultaneously effective: Ju'onai forces held their
claimed defense, the Terjamna champion retained the capacity to punish a
reopening, and the Stewardship kept the unsafe configuration shut.

## Settlement

The immediate settlement divided movement into smaller loads through more than
one route. House reports entering civic use thereafter traveled with the
sponsor's intended response, while Sunwall operating changes were recorded as
independent interventions rather than treated as scenery inside a military
forecast.

Neither empire surrendered its claim. The houses did not become civic offices,
and the Stewardship did not gain authority over war or tribute. The episode
instead became one reason the city preserves several owners across forecast,
judgment, material operation, and force.

Later Ju'onai teaching presents the closure as a missing boundary in the
original system. Terjamna accounts emphasize that the champion forced a larger
enemy to abandon its prepared movement. House comparers cite the shortened
horizons. Steward copies begin with refuge capacity and record how long grain
remained stopped. These traditions inherit different evidence from the same
event.

## Consequences

- A strategic forecast offered for civic action must name the sponsor's planned
  response and the material owner whose intervention could change the field.
- A Sunwall operational order is not a forecast and cannot decide tribute or
  sector title.
- A prepared victory can become the lever another oracle requires.
- Preventing both predicted actions does not establish that the third actor saw
  farther; the stewards acted upon a material system the sponsors had omitted.
- Dividing authority can prevent capture while imposing delay, shortage, and
  duplicated coercion upon residents.

## Adventure consequences

The two house reports, three orders, load maps, messenger copies, and later
settlement may survive under different custodians. A party recovering one copy
can alter a present claim that an empire, house, or Stewardship authored the
whole outcome. Reopening the old route can restore capacity and recreate the
military crossing the closure removed.
