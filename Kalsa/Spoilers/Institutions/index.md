# Institutions

Kalsa's institutions organize work and settle claims that people's lives depend on: maintaining inherited systems, allocating scarce services, teaching dangerous knowledge, and deciding who may handle essential relics. These needs persisted after the [[Spoilers/Foundations/Ark and Fall#The Fall|Fall]] and shaped the institutions listed here.

Anyone seeking access, help, or a ruling may face several authorities. A city authority, temple, town, lineage, guild, household, and god can each claim a different say over the same structure. Few have complete authority. Technical competence, evidence, office, custody, possession, land use, divine standing, personhood, and judgment remain distinct: authority in one does not settle the others. Each authority is limited to consequences it can inspect and answer for.

The linked notes define those responsibilities and limits under [[Spoilers/Reference/Canon and Provenance|Canon and Provenance]].

## Maintenance, ruins, and recovery

- [[Spoilers/Institutions/The Techpriesthoods|The Techpriesthoods]] — multiple lineages working across borders to preserve the practical knowledge of how inherited infrastructure works. They bargain for the support needed to maintain it.
- [[Spoilers/Institutions/Relic Custody|Relic Custody]] — who may claim responsibility for a machine, what records and duties that claim requires, and how custody passes to someone else. These machines cannot be treated as ordinary wealth that an owner can simply carry away.
- [[Spoilers/Institutions/Relic and Ruin Law|Relic and Ruin Law]] — local procedures for sites where claims overlap: who may enter, how public danger is handled, whose claims are recognized, what may be removed, and how to refuse or appeal a decision.
- [[Spoilers/Institutions/Delving Compacts|Delving Compacts]] — agreements that bind expedition signatories to duties concerning work, rescue, records, costs, breaches of the agreement, and salvage left over.

## Gods, potential, and death

- [[Spoilers/Institutions/God-Cult Covenants|God-Cult Covenants]] — reciprocal obligations between gods and cult members, including obligations entered voluntarily, inherited, or imposed by coercion.
- [[Spoilers/Institutions/The Potential Economy|The Potential Economy]] — how finite divine capacity is materially supported, estimated, allocated, and rationed, and how potential leaks away.
- [[Spoilers/Institutions/Death and Binding Law|Death and Binding Law]] — how people handle testimony, attempts to sever bindings, custody during funerals, and succession when persons and divine structures may continue after bodily death.

## Evidence, language, and administration

- [[Spoilers/Institutions/Prophetic Houses|Prophetic Houses]] and [[Spoilers/Institutions/Forecast Evidence and Courts|Forecast Evidence and Courts]] — how the divided city trains forecasters, provides forecasts, requires disclosure, decides what forecast evidence is admissible, and reaches and hears appeals against civic judgments.
- [[Spoilers/Institutions/Ju'onai Schools of the Opening|Ju'onai Schools of the Opening]], [[Spoilers/Institutions/Houses of Attestation|Houses of Attestation]], and [[Spoilers/Institutions/Measured Offices|Measured Offices]] — rival scholarly explanations of cause and effect, certification valid within specified limits, and a government that uses both while carrying class distinctions into its administration.
- [[Spoilers/Institutions/High Lojban and Descendant Speech|High Lojban and Descendant Speech]] — how languages are taught, certified, translated, and used in public notices and treaties, and how elites use those requirements to control access.
- [[Spoilers/Institutions/Measures, Formulae, and Machine Speech|Measures, Formulae, and Machine Speech]] — technical notation, conversion between measures, sequences that machines recognize, and procedures for refusing an operation and reviewing that refusal.

## Polity institutions

- [[Spoilers/Institutions/Stormshield Circles|Stormshield Circles]] — how weather protection is maintained, with provisions for consent, rest, transfer of responsibility, warnings, and response to failure. [[Spoilers/Institutions/The Alliance|The Alliance]] remains a non-factual `adopted-candidate`: a design proposal awaiting an owner note on the Intuition side of the setting, not an established institution.
- [[Spoilers/Institutions/Jamnai Submission Hierarchy|Jamnai Submission Hierarchy]] and [[Spoilers/Institutions/Soiru'i Sects|Soiru'i Sects]] — civil submission and obligations tied to geothermal systems, alongside funerary memory, welfare, sanctuary, and resistance.
- [[Spoilers/Institutions/Saxfoldi Households|Saxfoldi Households]] and [[Spoilers/Institutions/Ji'esti Flight and Refuge|Ji'esti Flight and Refuge]] — communal life that preserves consent, including how people move, give warning, gain admission, receive care, recover, and leave.
- [[Spoilers/Institutions/Selza'a Family Territories|Selza'a Family Territories]] and [[Spoilers/Institutions/Luck Harm and Restitution|Luck Harm and Restitution]] — land and membership claims held across multiple parties, mutual defense, and ways to use material evidence, pool risk, and provide restitution when Luck itself cannot be detected.

Further institutions remain unresolved. They receive owner notes only when the seed or later adopted canon provides enough substance to define them.
