# Dungeons

Kalsa's dungeons are places where several histories occupy the same structure. A Crown City underwork may begin as a [[Spoilers/Foundations/Ark and Fall|Ring]] service corridor, pass through [[Spoilers/Foundations/Prometheus|Promethean]] experimentation, become a post-Fall refuge or fortress, receive generations of burials, and still carry water or heat to people above. A dead god or [[Spoilers/Foundations/Projections, God Beasts, and Thaumavores#Thaumavores|thaumavore]] concentration can add another live authority without erasing the older ones.

## Owners

- [[Spoilers/Dungeons/Dungeon Provenance|Dungeon Provenance]] — identifies the source layers, alterations, dependencies, claims, and uncertainty attached to a site. Provenance describes; it does not grant entry or title.
- [[Spoilers/Institutions/Relic and Ruin Law|Relic and Ruin Law]] — governs admission, public danger, claims notice, removal, refusal, and appeal through the authorities that actually exist at the site.
- [[Spoilers/Institutions/Delving Compacts|Delving Compacts]] — bind expedition participants to work, rescue, record, cost, and residual-salvage terms without governing outsiders or pre-existing claims.

## Launch site

- [[Spoilers/Dungeons/The Ashen Intake|The Ashen Intake]] — a direct-entry Ghostlight dungeon beneath [[Spoilers/Places/Low Sere|Low Sere]], with a controlled threshold, three-shutter clock, contested custody, mortal remains, a language-and-measure failure, recoverable records, and consequences that continue after ascent.

## Adjacent authority

- [[Spoilers/Institutions/The Techpriesthoods|The Techpriesthoods]] own bounded operational competence at named systems.
- [[Spoilers/Institutions/Relic Custody|Relic Custody]] owns the custody and handoff distinctions around particular relics.
- [[Spoilers/Institutions/Death and Binding Law|Death and Binding Law]] preserves claims involving bodies, Projections, divine remains, and the unquiet dead.

There is no adopted universal adventurers' guild, finder-keeps title, or single dungeon law. Descent reveals a site; it does not make the site socially empty.
