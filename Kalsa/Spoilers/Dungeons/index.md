# Dungeons

A dungeon in Kalsa may still supply water or heat to the people living above it. It may also hold their dead, preserve earlier inhabitants' work, and contain powers that remain active. Entering it does not give an expedition ownership or cancel anyone else's claims.

These uses can accumulate in one structure. The [[Spoilers/Foundations/Ark and Fall|Rings]] were inhabited parts of the colony ship; their wrecks became the Crown Cities. A service corridor might have housed [[Spoilers/Foundations/Prometheus|Promethean]] coercive evolutionary experiments, then served as a refuge or fortress after the Fall, then received generations of burials. It may still carry water or heat today. A dead god or a concentration of [[Spoilers/Foundations/Projections, God Beasts, and Thaumavores#Thaumavores|thaumavores]] can introduce another active power. The older uses and claims still matter.

## Understanding a site and deciding what may happen there

Three references cover different parts of a descent:

- [[Spoilers/Dungeons/Dungeon Provenance|Dungeon Provenance]] — records where a site's layers came from, what changed, what depends on it, and which claims or uncertainties remain. That account grants neither entry nor title.
- [[Spoilers/Institutions/Relic and Ruin Law|Relic and Ruin Law]] — explains how the authorities present at a site govern admission, public danger, notice of claims, removal, refusal, and appeal.
- [[Spoilers/Institutions/Delving Compacts|Delving Compacts]] — set the expedition participants' obligations for work, rescue, records, costs, and salvage left after prior claims are addressed. They bind the participants; they do not govern outsiders or override pre-existing claims.

Kalsa has no adopted universal adventurers' guild, rule granting ownership to the finder, or single dungeon law.

## Sites for play

- [[Spoilers/Dungeons/The Ashen Intake|The Ashen Intake]] — a direct-entry Ghostlight dungeon beneath [[Spoilers/Places/Low Sere|Low Sere]]. Entry is controlled, custody is contested, and three remaining shutter indicators count down toward failure of the heat and water works. Mortal remains, a failure involving language and measurement, and recoverable records make decisions below matter after the party returns.
- [[Spoilers/Dungeons/The Second Gate Understore|The Second Gate Understore]] — an active urban Ghostlight situation beneath a Divided City cargo gate. The party escorts grain while several kinds of knowledge and obligation affect its delivery: a house report compares two routes, a Papsenai bond names the promised load and recipient, and a Ju'onai opening reveals how the understore mechanism will bear its load. Public route announcements move people and officials; rival claims and changing conditions can upset the delivery.
- [[Spoilers/Dungeons/The Ash-Halo Court|The Ash-Halo Court]] — a reopened Jamnai furnace court preparing divine armour for a champion, using staged materials and machinery. Three horns mark the rite's progress. A sealed answer roll records signs of earlier manifestations without proving their identities. A dead claimant can reach the divine grant through a connected route, putting the armament and possible negotiation at risk.

## Specialist responsibilities and claims

Other authorities cover particular systems, objects, and remains:

- [[Spoilers/Institutions/The Techpriesthoods|The Techpriesthoods]] — have operational competence at specified systems, within defined limits.
- [[Spoilers/Institutions/Relic Custody|Relic Custody]] — distinguishes the forms of custody and handoff that apply to particular relics.
- [[Spoilers/Institutions/Death and Binding Law|Death and Binding Law]] — preserves claims involving bodies, Projections, divine remains, and the unquiet dead.
