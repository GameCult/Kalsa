# The Ashen Intake

The Ashen Intake is a buried terraforming work feeding warm water and heat to [[Spoilers/Places/Low Sere|Low Sere]]. It is the canonical direct-entry launch dungeon for Kalsa: the entrance is open, the compact is disputed but usable, the settlement is visibly dependent, and three of eight shutter indicators remain before the relic fails.

The site reveals no simulation truth. Its marks, formulae, machinery, manifestations, and records remain things characters can inspect without discovering an omniscient explanation of Kalsa.

[[Spoilers/Events/The Grey Scald|The Grey Scald]] owns the prior failure's factual sequence. This dossier owns the evidence still present below, the current route and clock, and what the party can change now.

## Direct entry

Begin at the drained west basin of Low Sere's Cistern House.

Warm ash mist hangs below the stone lip. Maro Seln has lowered the basin and unlocked the iron grate. Sera Venn kneels at the black pressure door with an eight-tooth spindle in both hands. Three pale marks remain on the indicator above her. A fourth goes dark while the party watches.

Tavi Kes ties two name cloths to the lead recorder's pack. Ressa Orr asks who has authority to order withdrawal. Former custodian Teren Vey waits behind the household witnesses and says the compact uses the wrong translation.

The party can descend as soon as it accepts or contests the terms below. If discussion consumes one full work interval, advance the pressure clock.

Normal lawful entry requires both surface admission and technical release. Theft of either key creates possession, not authority. Emergency rescue may justify forced entry under [[Spoilers/Institutions/Relic Custody|Relic Custody]], with a complete record afterward.

## Present claimants

### Low Sere's basin table

The table claims site admission, warm-water output, public records, and restoration because its households maintain and depend on the basin. It does not claim technical restart. Maro Seln wants the relic working before review can close it.

### Sera Venn

Sera holds provisional operational custody of the pressure seal and degraded intake. She may inspect, isolate, and stop. Her appointment does not authorize the final calibration without the missing Measure or competent review. She wants the party to recover both and preserve the site state.

### Teren Vey

The removed custodian claims his emergency action during the Grey Scald was technically bounded and his later removal incoherent. He admits hiding the Ashen Measure and folio below to prevent unqualified restart. He offers the Relief Crawl route and a safer formula gloss if the compact records that recovery reopens his appeal.

### The Eighth Baffle lineage

Ressa Orr can compare the intake practice, Sera's formation, and Teren's intervention. Her lineage claims no present title but will seek a formal support compact if it supplies the review that saves Low Sere. Ressa retains stop authority at the Intake Crown.

### The Ash Names

Kin of Jori Kes and Pell Am claim the Memorial Sump, identified remains, bone-name tags, and all records of the Grey Scald. Tavi Kes requires a kin witness, no removal of fixed memorial objects, and no concentrated miracle in the sump. The fellowship does not own the intake beyond those mortuary claims.

### The delving crew

The party claims wages, care, rescue, accurate hazard notice, its expedition record, and residual movable salvage after prior claims. Recovery labor does not create title to the Measure, folio, fixed systems, remains, or public output.

## Compact at the threshold

The offered [[Spoilers/Institutions/Delving Compacts|Delving Compact]] contains these terms:

- Low Sere supplies six work-watches of provisions, surface rescue, injury care, and witnessed compensation in water, heat, stores, or trade goods according to the settlement's available forms.
- The crew enters through the Cistern House, may traverse the seven recorded zones, and may open only the Custodian Gallery and Intake Crown seals named in the objective.
- Sera owns technical stops at the pressure seal and known intake boundary. Ressa owns an independent stop at the Crown. Tavi owns a mortuary hold on disturbing identified remains.
- The delving lead owns movement, defense, route, and withdrawal within those stops.
- The recorder makes three copies: Low Sere, custodian review, and crew.
- Fixed components, the Ashen Measure, handoff folio, bone-name tags, remains, and custody records are reserved.
- Residual movable salvage is divided among the working crew after repair, mortuary, and public-restoration costs.
- If no residual salvage remains, wages and care survive.
- The compact expires on return, clock failure, or withdrawal. It grants no later access.

Pressure points before descent:

- Teren offers a private route only if his appeal is named in the return record.
- Tavi will hold the threshold if the party refuses a kin witness.
- Ressa refuses final actuation if the party treats the service phrase as self-executing proof.
- Maro wants authority to override a stop at the final shutter. The offered compact does not grant it.
- Poor lower-step households supplied much of the expedition food. A treasure-first retreat leaves them carrying the loss.

## The pressure clock

Start with **three pale shutters**. Mark one closed after each pressure pulse.

A pressure pulse occurs when:

- the party takes an extended rest below;
- a service formula is attempted in the wrong order;
- a major gate is forced or broken;
- the Relief Throat discharges uncontrolled;
- the table has been stalled for roughly one work-watch of play without a stabilizing action.

### Three pale

The intake coughs grey water but Low Sere maintains ordinary minimum service. Steam reveals active seams. Old walkways are hot rather than scalding.

### Two pale

Ash enters the lower cistern. Low Sere cuts Grey Bed flow. Visibility falls in the Settling Walk. Manifestations in the Memorial Sump gain enough potential to become clearer and more dangerous.

### One pale

Relief valves hammer. Unshielded metal burns. The lower-step channels are evacuated. Every forced actuation risks an immediate pulse.

### None pale

Untreated hot slurry enters the Relief Throat. One final pulse remains before the lower cistern ruptures. The party can still:

- open the full bypass, saving lives while ending Low Sere's warm-water economy;
- close the surface feed from the Cistern House if a message reaches Maro, buying evacuation and making later restart harder;
- install the Measure and complete a correct zero-pressure sequence;
- withdraw before the rupture seals or changes routes.

At rupture, Low Sere survives as people if warned. The lower basin, Warm Steps, and dungeon entrance become a scalded disaster site.

## Language and formula problem

The Intake Crown bears four octal triplets:

`110 — 001 — 000 — 111`

The inherited high service phrase is rendered by Teren as:

> Six feed. One bears witness. At zero, seven are released.

Low Sere's old public instruction rendered the same sequence as “feed six; wait one watch; release at empty.” The Grey Scald followed that interval reading.

The formula is not solved by choosing the prestigious translation. Its physical referents must be reconstructed:

- **110 / six:** six feed baffles show matching grey abrasion and must align with six long teeth on the Ashen Measure.
- **001 / one:** the seventh relief baffle must be held manually; its handle bears glove burns and a watcher mark.
- **000 / zero:** the differential gauge, not the cistern, must reach zero before release.
- **111 / seven:** six feed baffles and the held relief baffle release together. The eighth shutter remains the isolation boundary.

Clues appear in the Meter Stair gauges, Settling Walk wear, Memorial Sump testimony, Custodian Gallery folio, and the Measure itself. [[Spoilers/Institutions/Measures, Formulae, and Machine Speech|Formula practice]] owns the accountable comparison. A machine accepting the sequence proves operational response, not political legitimacy or linguistic purity.

## Route overview

```text
Cistern House
    |
1. Ash Door and Meter Stair
    |----------------------\
2. Settling Walk         4. Relief Crawl
    |          \             |       \
3. Eight Screens --- 5. Memorial Sump  |
    |                   |               |
6. Custodian Gallery ---+---------------+
    |
7. Intake Crown
```

The Settling Walk is slower and preserves more evidence. The Relief Crawl is faster, hotter, and relies on Teren's disputed knowledge. The Memorial Sump can be approached from either route. No zone is a simulation; each is an inherited work with later human use.

## Zone 1 — Ash Door and Meter Stair

### Threshold

The black door opens inward after Maro drains the basin and Sera turns the spindle. A toothed track records the exact opening. Forcing it damages the record and advances the clock.

### Features

Eight shallow channels run beside the descending stair. Six carry warm flow, one hammers intermittently, and one remains dry behind a black isolation mark. Triplet labels appear beside them. A differential gauge shows pressure on two opposed arcs.

### Hazards

- Sudden ash coughs make the stair slick and obscure sight.
- Touching the hammering seventh channel without insulation burns.
- Opening the dry eighth channel bypasses the admission seal and creates a direct uncontrolled route to Low Sere.

### Evidence

The six-one-zero-seven formula has physical referents here. Ressa or a careful observer can record them. An Analyst can map the local relations but cannot certify operation without the missing calibration dimension.

### Exits

The broad stair leads to the Settling Walk. A maintenance hatch marked in Teren's hand enters the Relief Crawl.

## Zone 2 — Settling Walk

### Body

A gridded bridge crosses three ash chambers. Warm flow settles mineral dust beneath it. Low Sere's current basins imitate this older sequence on a smaller scale.

### Hazards

- Corroded grids fail under concentrated weight.
- At two pale shutters, mist conceals missing sections.
- Falling into slurry causes burns, choking ash, and gear damage; the flow carries a victim toward the Memorial Sump screen.

### Claims and clues

Worker scratches record maintenance rotations, including missed shifts before the Grey Scald. Six baffle housings show repeated use. A removable brass hand tool is residual salvage if documented; cutting the screen weights is not.

### Choice

The party can spend time bracing the bridge for safer return or move quickly. Bracing does not advance the clock unless it becomes an extended rest; it consumes compact tools owed back to Low Sere.

### Exits

Forward reaches the Eight Screens. A side ladder reaches the Memorial Sump's upper lip.

## Zone 3 — Eight Screens

### Body

Eight vertical filter frames divide the intake. Six are grey with use. The seventh is scorched. The eighth is black and nearly clean. A rack awaits a calibration comb.

### Hazards

- Cleaning or moving a screen without isolating its paired flow advances the clock.
- A compacted ash mass can tear free and strike like a moving wall.
- Valuable filter mesh tempts removal; each cut worsens Low Sere's output.

### Problem

The frames cannot be synchronized without the Ashen Measure. Temporary wedges can hold one baffle for a pulse, allowing retreat or access to the Custodian Gallery. They cannot complete restoration.

### Evidence

The six long wear lines match the formula's first term. The seventh handle's witness mark matches the bone-name tag cord used by platform workers.

### Exits

A service door leads to the Custodian Gallery. A lower screen passage enters the Memorial Sump but disturbs its seal.

## Zone 4 — Relief Crawl

### Body

The disputed bypass is a narrow hot passage around the Settling Walk. Teren used it during the Grey Scald. White mineral scale makes recent flow visible.

### Hazards

- Only one armored or burdened person can pass at a time.
- Pressure knocks can become a scalding jet with little warning.
- A manual brace delays one clock pulse while held, but the holder suffers escalating heat and cannot defend effectively.
- Cutting the brace opens the full bypass and commits Low Sere toward evacuation.

### Evidence

Teren's route marks show that he expected the maintenance platform cleared before opening. A later scratch in ordinary Low Sere speech gives the shorter interval. Neither proves who received which warning.

### Exits

One hatch opens above the Memorial Sump. The crawl continues to an emergency port at the Intake Crown.

## Zone 5 — Memorial Sump

### Body

Post-Fall workers turned a dead settling pocket into a maintenance platform. The Grey Scald filled it with mineral ash. The Ash Names later sealed its upper grate and tied two name cloths above the flow.

### Remains and claim

Jori Kes's and Pell Am's bone-name tags lie beyond the seal near mineral-coated human remains. Tavi's compact permits recovery of tags and identification without moving fixed remains unless immediate danger requires it.

### Manifestations

Knocks answer some spoken names. Ash briefly takes human posture where warm potential passes. The presences may be Jori and Pell, other dead, thaumavores, pressure effects interpreted through grief, or several causes. They are not a simulation message and do not deliver unquestionable testimony.

At two pale shutters, manifestations can pull heat, extinguish lamps, repeat fragments of warning, or block the route toward the Custodian Gallery. Recognition, a correctly spoken name, reducing potential, or material repair may calm them. Force disperses a form and advances attention elsewhere; it does not prove the dead were defeated.

### Hazards

- Mineral crust hides unstable depth.
- Concentrated miracles feed manifestations and can attract more.
- Removing remains without support collapses ash into the lower screen.

### Evidence

One tag carries a work-watch notch matching the longer interval. The other is tied to a platform-clearance token issued after the valve had already begun to turn. Together they support both Teren's expected procedure and the workers' late notice.

### Exits

A sealed archive crawl leads to the Custodian Gallery. The Relief Crawl hatch returns upward.

## Zone 6 — Custodian Gallery

### Body

A dry service room holds layered records, discarded tools, a wall diagram, and a locked calibration chest. Teren placed the chest behind a mechanical refusal: it opens only when the current differential gauge reading is copied onto three octal wheels.

### Recoverable objective

The chest contains:

- the **Ashen Measure**, a black ceramic comb with six long teeth, one hooked tooth, and one solid isolation edge;
- the last handoff folio, including Teren's intervention record, Sera's incomplete formation list, formula variants, and support failures;
- two sealed maintenance notices never delivered to lower-step households;
- a small pouch of portable calibration beads that counts as disputed but potentially residual salvage after review.

The Measure and folio are reserved. Taking the beads without recording them turns later formula comparison into a custody dispute.

### Hazards

- Wrong wheel settings open an ash purge and advance the clock.
- The room's dry air masks heat exhaustion.
- A desperate character can break the chest, damaging the Measure and making only bypass outcomes available.

### Exits

The main service door leads to the Intake Crown. A narrow return connects to the Eight Screens. The archive crawl returns to the Memorial Sump.

## Zone 7 — Intake Crown

### Body

Seven operating baffles surround a black isolation shutter. A central rack accepts the Ashen Measure. The differential gauge from the Meter Stair is repeated here. No voice explains the machine.

### Final procedure

To restore the intake:

1. preserve the black eighth shutter as isolation;
2. insert the Ashen Measure and align its six long teeth with the six feed baffles;
3. assign one person to hold the hooked seventh relief baffle;
4. bleed pressure through the marked channel until the differential gauge reaches zero;
5. release the six feed and one relief baffle together;
6. record response before removing or leaving the Measure as the competent custodians decide.

Sera can perform the sequence with the recovered folio. Ressa can compare and stop. Teren can advise remotely or accompany the crew only if the compact was amended. A player character can execute the physical acts without thereby acquiring custody.

### Alternatives

- **Full bypass:** open the Relief Throat and route slurry away from the cistern. Low Sere gains evacuation time and loses normal output.
- **Surface shutdown:** signal Maro to close the settlement feed. The clock pauses after one final pulse; later restart requires excavation and a new compact.
- **Partial wedge:** restore one or two baffles. This buys several work-watches and leaves a more dangerous later failure.
- **Removal:** take the Measure without calibrating. This preserves a valuable object and abandons the settlement claim.

### Final hazard

Releasing above zero recreates the Grey Scald at greater scale. Releasing with the eighth isolation shutter open sends the surge directly toward Low Sere. Waiting “one watch” instead of holding one baffle advances the clock.

## Objective and recovery

The expedition succeeds materially when it returns with:

- the Ashen Measure intact or installed under recorded custody;
- the last handoff folio and copies;
- a recorded configuration and outcome at the Intake Crown;
- living crew and notice to Low Sere;
- bone-name tags if recovered within the mortuary hold.

Killing a manifestation, opening every door, or maximizing salvage is not the objective. The dungeon is an institution under pressure, not a sealed purse with architecture around it.

## Consequences above the threshold

On return, the party must surrender reserved objects for witnessed inventory without surrendering its record. Low Sere owes wages and care before dividing residual salvage. Sera and Teren receive separate hearings. Tavi receives the tags and disturbance account. Ressa submits a bounded comparison. Maro must disclose any override he ordered.

A technically successful descent can still fail socially if the crew conceals context, lets one claimant take every copy, or accepts payment contingent on a preferred verdict. A failed descent can preserve legitimacy if it warns Low Sere, respects stops, and returns evidence explaining why the objective was unreachable.

## Unresolved

- The original Ark function beyond evidence that the Intake participates in heat, water, filtration, and pressure control.
- Exact age and builder of every post-Fall modification.
- Whether the Memorial Sump manifestations are the named dead, other thaumavores, mechanical effects, or mixed causes.
- Final adjudication of Sera's appointment, Teren's appeal, and Eighth Baffle involvement.
- Long-term repair after any outcome and whether the Ashen Measure can be reproduced.
- Wider polity and external claim over Low Sere.
