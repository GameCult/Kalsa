# Intuition — Unresolved

Intuition is a magical style described as unusual insight into a situation. The seed also sketches a people and polity associated with it, but gives them no dedicated article. This note preserves that sketch; it does not establish a complete culture.

In the author classification, Intuition has signature **111**, combining Depth, Clarity, and Scope, and occupies **Sequence VII** in the Promethean development sequence. How those three axes produce intuitive experience remains unresolved. Intuition does not normally found an independent God Beast.

## The seed's polity sketch

The seed describes Intuition's people as exceptionally effective traders, politicians, and general problem-solvers. Their polity emphasizes technology and has strong national pride supported by a real history of success. It has little drive to expand, but can defend itself.

The early sketch places it near and coldward of the Shamanism polity, allied with that neighbor and acting as its supply base and protector. The proposed Alliance with [[Spoilers/Polities/Shamanism - Papsenai|Papsenai]] remains unresolved: the sketch does not establish who makes decisions, what either side supplies, or what obligations bind them.

These descriptions survive as source claims, not an adopted account of how the society works. Calling its people capable traders does not explain what they trade, what pressures they face, or which institutions make their success possible.

## What still needs an answer

- **Names:** the people, polity, Crown City, region, and language.
- **Magical origins and experience:** Prometheus's evolutionary game, and how Depth, Clarity, and Scope relate to intuition.
- **Magical practice:** what the style can and cannot do, what observers can detect, how people train, what using it costs, and how it interacts with other styles.
- **Social and material life:** technology, production, trade, defense, government, class, kinship, and religion.
- **The Alliance:** who holds authority, what the partners exchange, how mutual aid works, where coercion may enter, and whether either partner can leave.
- **The Crown City and history:** its Wonder and what happened after the Fall.
- **The sketch itself:** whether the early national description survives examination of the institutions needed to support it.

Future development must begin with what this polity produces, supplies, and depends on, then identify who controls those resources and decisions. A personality description cannot do that work.
