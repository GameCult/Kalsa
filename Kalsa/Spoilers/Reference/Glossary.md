# Glossary

This glossary explains terms used in the author notes. It draws its definitions from the notes that own each subject; it does not establish a shared dictionary for Kalsa's inhabitants. English Gift names and formal descriptions of magical capabilities let authors compare those subjects. The relevant polity and institution notes explain what people in Kalsa call them and how they understand them.

| Term | Meaning in the author notes |
|---|---|
| **Analysis** | Gift with signature 010. It lets a person consciously distinguish causes and understand how they connect. See [[Spoilers/Polities/Ju'onai Hegemony|Ju'onai Hegemony]]. |
| **acquired potential body** | Author shorthand for the stolen magical capacity a thaumavore has organized into its current means of appearing and acting. Destroying it does not establish whether anyone has been released, whether memory remains, or whether personhood survives. |
| **answer roll** | A record prepared around a concentrated mortuary rite. It lists familiar signs, witnesses who recognize them, sites, and last appearances. |
| **baltcadu** | A seed term glossed as “Great City.” Its exact language and modern use remain unresolved. |
| **Chaos Beast** | Something capable of acting in upper dimensions, coupled to a person's **Individual Projection**: their continuing pattern of possible states, defined below. The person is prescient—their magic uses information about possible futures. The Beast and the continuing person-pattern are distinct. See [[Spoilers/Foundations/Projections, God Beasts, and Thaumavores#Chaos Beasts|Chaos Beasts]]. |
| **Children of Fire** | Populations of the Ark's Rings shaped by Prometheus's worship and evolutionary games. Their descendants do not constitute a single present-day ethnicity. |
| **Clarity** | The ability to consciously distinguish the variables that affect an outcome and the causal paths connecting them. |
| **Control** | The experimental population Prometheus deliberately left without Gifts. Its history is distinct from that of the Spokers. |
| **Crown City** | Any of the eight cities formed from fallen Ark Rings. |
| **Depth** | The ability to make a less probable possible course of events become actual. That course must still be reachable; Depth measures this counterfactual reach. |
| **dimna** | A seed term for the Luck culture's God Beasts. Its linguistic analysis remains unresolved. |
| **Gifted Cities** | The seven Crown Cities that carry Prometheus's seven Kaos strains. |
| **God Beast** | A Chaos Beast that binds other Projections or Beasts and routes their potential. |
| **God-foundation** | A root strain's capacity to establish a stable, independently acting **God Beast**, as defined above. “Stabilize” names the result; the mechanism is not explained here. |
| **High Lojban** | An author-facing label for conservative, prestigious forms of speech descended from the language Prometheus imposed. It does not name one present-day language under universal governance. |
| **Individual Projection** | The continuing range of possible states a person could occupy, attached to that person's identity. |
| **Intuition** | The seventh Gift, with signature 111. Its workings remain incompletely documented. |
| **Ji'esti** | The Sarxe name or gloss for the massacre remembered as Extinction. |
| **Kaos** | The use of prescience to steer events along causal branches that remain possible from the present. |
| **Luck** | Gift with signature 001. It constantly and unconsciously biases events toward the bearer's survival or benefit, across a broad reach. |
| **Null** | Signature 000: no Kaos Gift is expressed. This describes a condition, not an ethnicity. |
| **oracle ecology** | An author-facing term for how people with different forms of prescience, Beasts, Gods, and institutions interact and affect one another's possible futures. It is not common inhabitant vocabulary. See [[Spoilers/Foundations/Oracle Ecology|Oracle Ecology]]. |
| **Promethean Sequence** | The development order N, I–VII. These labels record sequence and are distinct from the binary Kaos signatures. |
| **Prophecy** | Gift with signature 011. It allows conscious perception of possible futures over long horizons. |
| **Saxfoldi** | The hidden, mobile center of Sarxe civilization. The seed gloss is “harmonious field.” |
| **Scope** | How far through space and time magical perception or influence can remain coherent. |
| **seltanzda** | A seed term for a magical identity or Beast among the selza'a. Exactly what it corresponds to remains unresolved. |
| **selza'a** | The Luck-descended people or culture of the isolated, fogbound Crown City. |
| **Shamanism** | Gift with signature 101. Its practices include blessings and curses with long scope, and trance through a bond. |
| **Soiru'i** | A seed classification for the power remaining from a dead terjamna warrior, glossed as “warrior god.” What this power is in metaphysical terms remains unresolved. |
| **Sorcery** | Gift with signature 100. It applies focused force to bring about a different reachable outcome over a short horizon. |
| **Spokers** | Descendants of the embodied people who inhabited the Ark's Spokes and were excluded from Prometheus's sacred program. |
| **Sunwall** | The vast wall facing the sun in the City of Prophets, together with its vertical-farm complex. |
| **thaumavore** | A Projection that persists, or a Beast carcass, that consumes passing magical potential in order to manifest. |
| **Techpriesthoods** | Maintenance lineages that developed after the Fall. There are multiple priesthoods, each with limits to what it can competently operate and maintain. See [[Spoilers/Institutions/The Techpriesthoods|The Techpriesthoods]]. |
| **timeline capture** | An author-facing term for a bounded situation in which one oracle or hierarchy controls enough information, means of intervention, and rivals' ability to act that nearly every reachable outcome within a specified horizon depends on its response. |
| **Ti'asantatca** | The Papsenai Crown City beside a storm front. The seed gloss is “stormshield.” |

Seed glosses record supplied translations, not settled meanings. See [[Spoilers/Foundations/Language and Reckoning|Language and Reckoning]] for their limits.
