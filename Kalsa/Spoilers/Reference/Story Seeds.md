# Story Seeds

The exported history ends with a proposed protagonist and conflict. These claims are preserved here without becoming standing setting fact.

## The escaped Luck bearer

A person from the isolated Luck city chooses to leave with Techpriests. The coincidences surrounding him grow visibly wrong outside the system that had contained his ancestors.

## The Cabal

The seed proposes an order of magi called the Cabal that seeks to control civilization and notices the escaped Luck bearer. The Cabal's existence, reach, age, and authority are not current setting canon.

## The sleeping God's return

The seed proposes that Prometheus seeks to reclaim the Children whose ancestors severed and crashed his Ark. [[Spoilers/Foundations/Prometheus|Prometheus]] may have survived the Fall, but active return is not current setting fact.

## Use

A campaign or fiction project may adopt, alter, or reject these premises. When adopted, it should name its era and distinguish campaign state from the reusable world vault.
