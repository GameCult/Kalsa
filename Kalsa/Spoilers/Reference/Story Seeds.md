# Story Seeds

The exported history closes with a proposed story: someone leaves the isolated Luck city, attracts the attention of an order of magi, and may face the return of Prometheus. These premises are preserved for possible use in fiction or play. They are not established facts of the shared setting.

## The escaped Luck bearer

A man from the isolated Luck city chooses to leave with Techpriests. Outside the system that had contained his ancestors, the coincidences surrounding him become visibly wrong. The proposal does not explain what those coincidences look like or how the system had contained them.

## The Cabal

The proposed Cabal is an order of magi that wants to control civilization and notices the escaped Luck bearer. Whether it exists in the shared setting remains undecided, as do its reach, age, and authority.

## The sleeping God's return

In this proposed story, [[Spoilers/Foundations/Prometheus|Prometheus]] seeks to reclaim the Children—the descendants of those who severed and crashed his Ark. He may have survived the Fall, but his active return is not an established event in the shared setting.

## Use

A campaign or fiction project may adopt, change, or reject any of these premises. If it adopts one, it should specify the era and make clear which events and conditions belong to that particular story. Adoption does not make the premise a fact of the reusable world vault.
