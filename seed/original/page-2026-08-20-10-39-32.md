## I – Luck

Luck is the ur-form of the magic trait; it developed naturally in one of Prometheus’ accelerated-evolution populations, causing Prometheus to change the goal of his breeding program entirely.

## Magic

Luck is the most powerful and the least controlled of all the forms of magic. It causes changes so subtle that they cannot be detected on short timescales, but acts constantly that these changes can accumulate into vastly powerful effects. Its influence is not felt by either the user nor can it be detected by any of the more perceptive forms of magic. The undetectable influence of Luck manifests by subtly bending the universe’s chaos to the user’s benefit. The chaos beasts that control Luck are not very sophisticated and work at their survival singly and simply. Chaos beasts of this type have the capacity to become Godbeasts, who are also beasts of little sophistication: they live for little more than survival, expanding only incidentally and protecting territory before idealistic goals. Fights between luck users in the short term look much like the regular kind, but aligning oneself against the survival of a Luck beast is bad for one’s own fortunes.

## Attributes

The ring harboring the Luck trait landed far coldward, in a desert plateau nestled in the northern coastal mountain chain. Their city is always shrouded in fog, forcing them to use windtraps to generate precipitation. Because of this, they have also learned to rely on senses other than sight for input. They weren’t heavily bred by Prometheus, so their phenotype has plenty of natural variation. Their isolation also means no interbreeding has occurred between them and any of the other groups.

## Culture

As with the other cultures, selza’a derive much of their identity from the propagation of their seltanzda, called dimna. They are very territorial and loyal mostly to their family units. With power being distributed along genetic lines, and dimna being extremely primitive seltanzda, no distinct government structure was ever able to form in their city. Territory is controlled by mutually competitive families that answer threat with threat and band together to combat perceived external threats. This lends great stability to the overall structure, but at personal scales the large amount of magic being slung about causes constant disturbances along territorial lines. Wildly improbable events occur often, and families often know exactly who to blame when some misfortune befalls them, as misfortune is indicative of attack. Families locked into permanent war are common, and the resulting magical battles look highly chaotic to the untrained eye.
