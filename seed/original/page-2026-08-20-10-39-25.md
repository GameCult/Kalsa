## N – Control

No good experiment is complete without a control group, and Prometheus saw this need despite his contempt for the scientific method.

## Evolution

One group of humans was set aside at the moment Prometheus decided to create his breeding program. The appearance of chaos-manipulation didn’t change this, and the magical trait was never bred into this population. They were bred not for any particular trait, but for uniformity and ease of handling – like lab rats. They would also be used in the occasional genetic experiment by Prometheus, mostly focusing on specific uses for prescience (e.g. the neurobiological interface used to breed Analysis).

## Attributes

The control group is healthy and competent, bred for universality. Properly trained, they can be applied effectively to any task. Their blood has mixed with that of the ring-dwellers extensively to form mixed-heritage ethnic groups that have traits from both ancestor groups, including magical ability.

## Culture

When Prometheus’ terraforming plan required manual labor on the surface, Prometheus would prepare a well-indoctrinated group and drop them down to the surface. They were conditioned to depend on a religion focusing on enlightenment through purity, a mindset intended to avoid decadence and promote loyalty to Prometheus. They were conditioned against their reproductive urges and kept sterile through their diet, but both of these proved unstable and some splinter races branched off in time, forming independent settlements that, while still heavily religious, didn’t follow the laws of Prometheus’ thralls. Their religions developed monotheistic overtones, which found easy spread into godless Great Cities. When the Great Cities dropped, the self-ruled thrall-descendants found it easy to impose their own order on many ring-dwellers, who had suddenly lost the only direction they had, and were generally unbalanced specimens (having evolved into a very specific niche).
